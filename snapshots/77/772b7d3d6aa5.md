据多方行业数据显示，目前国内[公共充电桩](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=273921340&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%85%AC%E5%85%B1%E5%85%85%E7%94%B5%E6%A1%A9&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODAsInEiOiLlhazlhbHlhYXnlLXmoakiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNzM5MjEzNDAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.bgPYPrp0aPxy3jHp25v5nhWlbz7KgDvEVXp26f8BhGQ&zhida_source=entity)整体利用率不到10%，在北京、[上海](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=273921340&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%B8%8A%E6%B5%B7&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODAsInEiOiLkuIrmtbciLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNzM5MjEzNDAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.fGJO8cmfpS5tffnJns4TK6B-n5fWC5CdLyPLqce5iV0&zhida_source=entity)等竞争最激烈的一线城市，利用率甚至不足2%。这意味着，每天大部分时间，超过九成的公共充电桩处于闲置状态。

这个数字背后有一个关键的[商业逻辑](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=273921340&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%95%86%E4%B8%9A%E9%80%BB%E8%BE%91&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODAsInEiOiLllYbkuJrpgLvovpEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNzM5MjEzNDAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.7_tuvl79WCPRyGbpZa6rRd2HNF1w9SJfEMCblvTkR7Q&zhida_source=entity)：根据行业测算，若以无政府补贴8%的[内部回报率](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=273921340&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%86%85%E9%83%A8%E5%9B%9E%E6%8A%A5%E7%8E%87&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODAsInEiOiLlhoXpg6jlm57miqXnjociLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNzM5MjEzNDAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.HRCN66zkxXJ4X5FP8AAJZKfGuoSRtvNMC-TEGhdt_EY&zhida_source=entity)为基准，单桩日均利用率至少要超过8.5%，即每天累计充电超过2小时，方可实现正常盈利。而当前大多数运营商的利用率，远低于这条[盈亏平衡线](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=273921340&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%9B%88%E4%BA%8F%E5%B9%B3%E8%A1%A1%E7%BA%BF&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODAsInEiOiLnm4jkuo_lubPooaHnur8iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNzM5MjEzNDAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.mNxCHZ4G3O-tPtw1sCNPKsfRLHIRVMaGOuJTirv_yMw&zhida_source=entity)。

## **走通了吗？三条可能的出路**

行业不是没有出路，而是出路还没有被大规模验证。目前有三个方向值得关注。
