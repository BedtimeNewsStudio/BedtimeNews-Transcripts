# How Turkey quietly became a power broker in Africa

Politics[Africa](/en/africa/s-12756)

[Luisa von Richthofen](/en/luisa-von-richthofen/person-69896115)

July 21, 2026

Turkey has spent two decades building ties with Africa — with embassies, mosques, airline routes, trade deals and development and military support. Now it's building a space base in Somalia, raising concerns in Israel.

https://p.dw.com/p/5HWPP

Advertisement

[Turkey](/en/turkey/t-65619354) is building a $350 million spaceport on Somalia's coast, a project that highlights Ankara's growing influence in Africa. The launch site near Warsheikh is intended for satellite launches and could support Turkey’s ambitions for a future lunar program. The development comes after years of Turkish investment, military cooperation and economic deals in [Somalia](/en/somalia/t-64337951), where Ankara has become a key partner. The project has also drawn scrutiny amid regional rivalries, with some [Israeli](/en/israel/t-19067044) analysts viewing it as a potential strategic concern.
