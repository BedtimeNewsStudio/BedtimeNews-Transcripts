The [Gambling Survey for Great Britain (GSGB)](/about-us/statistics-and-research/gambling-survey) now gathers the latest data on gambling behaviours.

This statistical release covers the quarterly gambling participation and problem gambling prevalence data for the year to March 2023.

The data being released today is based on the Gambling Commission’s quarterly telephone survey conducted by Yonder Consulting. A nationally representative sample of 4002 adults aged 16 and over were interviewed via telephone in June 2022, September 2022, December 2022 and March 2023. Further details on the quarterly telephone survey methodology can be found on the front cover of the main publication.

## Key facts

## Details

This is the last edition of the quarterly participation and prevalence statistics which will be taken from the quarterly telephone survey. On behalf of the Commission, Yonder Consulting have created a report summarising key trends from March 2015 to March 2023. The report reviews the results of the last 8 years, exploring overall key trends found in gambling participation, behaviour and attitudes across that period.

Read the [Gambling Behaviour 2015 to 2023: Quarterly telephone survey trends report](/about-us/guide/gambling-behaviour-2015-to-2023-quarterly-telephone-survey-trends).
