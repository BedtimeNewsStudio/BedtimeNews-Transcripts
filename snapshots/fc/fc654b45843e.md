# 2024 1. Population 

1.1 Population Size (1) 

The population size of the Emirate of Dubai is estimated by 4,248 ,200 individuals at the end of 2024 , 68. 5% of them are males 

with a total of 2, 911 ,500 individuals, and 31. 5% of them are females with a total of 1, 336 ,700 individuals. The higher proportion 

of males in Dubai community (218 Males per 100 Females) is referred to the external workers who are arriving individually 

without their family members. On the other hand, there is a balance in the distribution of each ge nder within the households, 

which is one of the unique characteristics of Dubai’s demographic structure as the male and female ratio is balanced at the 

household level. Table 1 shows the distribution of the Emirate of Dubai population by gender at the end of 2022 -2024 .

1.2 Estimated Active Individuals During Peak Hours “Day time ”

The usual active individuals during the day in the peak hours are estimated with a number of 5,937 ,800 individuals which 

includes residents of Dubai and the employees residing outside the emirate who are working in all type of sectors: local 

government, federal government and private sector. Moreover, it includes the average number of tourists and sailors, in addition 

to dynamic movement of people coming into Dubai through the peak hours and heading back home in the other emirates. Peak 

hours in Dubai start s from 6:30 am to 8:30 pm. 

There are two main reasons for that: 

• Dubai is a commercial and tourist hub that attracts residents from neighboring emirates for shopping, tourism, 

business management, or employment. 

• A portion of employees in both the government and private sectors reside in neighboring emirates due to relatively 

lower housing rental costs .

2. Population Distribution by Age Groups in Dubai 

The age and gender structure of Dubai population usual residents is considered as unbalanced (unnatural). The reason behind 

this is that there is a large proportion of male foreign workers which is a result of rapid economic growth witnessed by the 

emirate. This is shown clearly by Figure 2 which displays the population pyramid of Dubai at the end of 2024 .

The majority of the residents are aged between 25 to 44 years, representing 56 .72 % of the population in Dubai. 15 .44 % of 

them are in 30 -34 age group, representing the highest population by age with a total of 655, 447 individuals. The second highest 

age group is 35 -39 represented by total of 638 ,861 individuals and a percentage of 15 .04 % of the population. The third highest 

is the 25 -29 age group with a total of 615 ,043 individuals representing 14 .48 % of the total Dubai population.

5 with a total of 610 ,687 residents representing 14 .38 % of total population. Moreover, sectors 7 -9 reached 106 ,789 residents

The population distribution by communities allows to get a realistic idea about the population size in each community , which 

helps the planners and decision makers for better planning and generating necessary plans needed for each community. This 

helps in maintaining each community’s privacy and insuring that it has its own public facilities and services. 

As described in table 5 below, Jabal Ali Industrial First (599) has the highest population with 205 ,079 individuals that 

represents 4.83 % of Dubai’s population. The second highest is Muhaisanah Second (264) with 160 ,831 individuals and a 

percentage of 3. 79 %. AlGouz Industrial Second (365) comes in the third place with a population size of 144 ,327 individuals 

and a percentage of 3. 40 %. After that, comes Warsan First Area (621) that has a population of 120 ,353 individuals and a 

percentage of 2.83 %. They totally represent 14 .85 % of Dubai’s population with a total of 630 ,590 individuals. The reason 

behind the high population in these communities is that they contain several labor camps and collective households.
