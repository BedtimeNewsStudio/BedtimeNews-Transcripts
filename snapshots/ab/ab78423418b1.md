# About UPI

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is a system that empowers users to add multiple bank and other allowed accounts (of any participating bank) into a UPI App, merging several banking features, seamless fund routing & merchant payments under one hood.

The pilot launch on UPI was done on 11th April 2016 by Dr. Raghuram G Rajan, Governor, RBI at Mumbai.

## Participants in UPI

*   UPI App
*   Payer PSP
*   Remitter Bank
*    Payee PSP
*   Beneficiary bank
*   Users and Merchants

![Image 12: benifits-participants](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/about_UPI_48b642f8cb.png)

## Benefits to Participants

Users

Merchants

Banks

*   Round the clock availability
*   Single Application for accessing different bank accounts
*   Use of UPI ID is more secure, no credential sharing
*   Two-factor authentication
*   Raise Complaint from Mobile App directly

Financial Transactions

![Image 14: Next Slide](https://www.npci.org.in/static/media/blcak%20arrow%20right.542a123ccf60a0009d4e736220fb815d.svg)

*   Immediate money transfer through mobile device 24*7 and 365 days.
*   Single mobile application for accessing different bank accounts.
*   2 Factor Authentication – Aligned with the Regulatory guidelines and provides for a very secure and seamless payment.
*   Virtual address of the customer for Pull & Push provides for incremental security with the customer not required to enter the details such as Card no, Account number; IFSC etc.
*   QR Code and intent based payments
*   Best answer to Cash on Delivery hassle, running to an ATM or rendering exact amount.
*   P2P and merchant payments
*   Grievance redressal from Mobile App directly.
