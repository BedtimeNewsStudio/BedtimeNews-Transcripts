# Terrenganu datukship for Ferrari’s Todt

* * *

Thursday, 20 Jul 2006

\| 12:00 AM MYT

bookmark

**KUALA TERENGGANU:** Ferrari Formula One Racing Team director Jean Todt **_(top left)_** is among 379 people to be conferred awards and medals in conjunction with the 44th birthday of Sultan Mizan Zainal Abidin of Terengganu today.

He will receive the Darjah Dato’ Paduka Mahkota Terengganu (DPMT), which carries the title “Datuk”.

Malacca Yang di-Pertua Negeri Tun Mohd Khalil Yaakob **_(top right)_** heads the list of recipients. He is to be bestowed the Darjah Seri Utama Sultan Mizan Zainal Abidin Terengganu (SUMZ), which carries the title “Datuk Seri Utama”

A member of the Terengganu Royal Court Datuk Abdul Rahman Nasir will be the sole recipient of the Darjah Seri Setia Sultan Mizan Zainal Abidin Terengganu (SSMZ) award, which also carries the title “Datuk Seri.”

Five people will receive the Darjah Dato’ Setia Sultan Mizan Zainal Abdidin Terengganu (DSMZ), which also carries the title “Datuk.”

Besides Todt, 19 others will also be conferred the DPMT.

Eleven people will receive the Darjah Setia Sultan Mizan Zainal Abidin Terengganu, 28 the Darjah Setia Mahkota Terengganu, 13 the Darjah Ahli Sultan Mizan Zainal Abidin Terengganu and 39 the Darjah Ahli Mahkota Terengganu.
