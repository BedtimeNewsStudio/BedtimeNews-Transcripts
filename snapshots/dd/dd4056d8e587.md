**Qingdao West Coast Football Club** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters "Simplified Chinese characters"): 青岛西海岸足球俱乐部; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin "Pinyin"): *Qīngdǎo Xīhǎi'àn Zúqiú Jùlèbù*) is a Chinese professional [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football "Association football") club based in [Qingdao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qingdao "Qingdao"), [Shandong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong "Shandong"), that competes in the [Chinese Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Super_League "Chinese Super League"), the top tier of [Chinese football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_football_league_system "Chinese football league system"). Qingdao West Coast plays its home matches at the [Qingdao West Coast University City Sports Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guzhenkou_University_City_Sports_Center "Guzhenkou University City Sports Center"), located within [Huangdao District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huangdao_District "Huangdao District").

## History

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Qingdao_West_Coast_F.C.&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")]

Qingdao Kangtaiyuan F.C. was founded in 2007 by Qingdao Kangtine Commercial Concrete Co., Ltd. as a company football team for their employees.[[1]](#cite_note-1) The club would initially participate in local amateur and [futsal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futsal "Futsal") tournaments until the 2011 league season when they participated in the Qingdao Football Association Cup and were knocked out in the last sixteen.[[2]](#cite_note-2) The following season, they joined the Chinese football league pyramid in the second tier of the Qingdao League or seventh tier overall.

In 2017, after a third-place finish in the [Qingdao City League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qingdao_City_League?action=edit&redlink=1 "Qingdao City League (page does not exist)"), they changed their name to Qingdao Kangtine and participated in the [China Amateur Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Amateur_Football_League "China Amateur Football League") for the first time. They advanced to the national play-offs after finishing first in their group but were eventually eliminated by [Qinghai Zhuangbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qinghai_Zhuangbo?action=edit&redlink=1 "Qinghai Zhuangbo (page does not exist)").

In 2018, they were crowned champions of the Qingdao City League, and participated in the [Chinese Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Champions_League "Chinese Champions League") for a second time. However, they were eliminated by [Taizhou Yuanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taizhou_Yuanda_F.C. "Taizhou Yuanda F.C.") in the regional group stage play-offs and failed to advance to the national play-offs.

In 2019, they changed their name to Qingdao Zhongchuang Hengtai. In their third attempt at the highest level of amateur football league, they were eliminated once again by [Shandong Wangyue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_Wangyue?action=edit&redlink=1 "Shandong Wangyue (page does not exist)") in the group stage play-offs, but were later admitted into the national stage after the withdrawal of [Huizhou Huixin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huizhou_Huixin_F.C.?action=edit&redlink=1 "Huizhou Huixin F.C. (page does not exist)"). They eventually finished 8th and were promoted to [China League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_League_Two "China League Two") for the first time in the club's history.

In January 2021, they changed their name to Qingdao Youth Island.[[3]](#cite_note-3) They finished 4th this season and entered the promotion-relegation play-offs against [Beijing BIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Institute_of_Technology_F.C. "Beijing Institute of Technology F.C."), who finished 17th in the [2021 China League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_China_League_One "2021 China League One"). Qingdao won 5–4 on penalties after drawing 2–2 on aggregate, gaining promotion to [China League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_League_One "China League One") for the first time in the club's history.

## Name history

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Qingdao_West_Coast_F.C.&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Name history")]

* 2007–2017: **Qingdao Kangtaiyuan F.C.** (青岛康太源)
* 2017–2018: **Qingdao Kangtine F.C.** (青岛康太源)
* 2019–2020: **Qingdao Zhongchuang Hengtai F.C.** (青岛中创恒泰)
* 2021–2022: **Qingdao Youth Island F.C.** (青岛青春岛)
* 2023–: **Qingdao West Coast F.C.** (青岛西海岸)
