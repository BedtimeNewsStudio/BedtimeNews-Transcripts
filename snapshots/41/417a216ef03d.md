## Swiss watchmaking in December 2017 Timid growth

Despite a relatively low comparison base, watch industry exports reported only weak growth in December. 
Their value rose by 0.7% against December 2016 to reach a total of 1.7 billion francs. With this result, the 
industry has seen 2.7% export growth over the year 2017 as a whole, just short of the 20 billion francs mark 
(19,924 million francs precisely).

Sales of watches priced at less than 500 francs (export price) fell in December, especially in the <200 francs
segment, where volumes were down by one-fifth. The 500-3000 francs range made significant gains while 
the most expensive timepieces remained flat.

The main Asian markets generally saw steep increases in the final months of the year. Hong Kong (+11.8%) and 
China (+12.3%) maintained their sustained dynamic, while Japan (+7.0%) experienced slower growth, although 
this remained high throughout the fourth quarter. Ranking in eleventh place worldwide, South Korea (+33.3%) 
advanced very strongly month on month. The United States (-9.5%) still seem to be in the doldrums and posted 
the steepest fall since May. The trend of Swiss watch industry exports in Europe varied from country to country 
(-1.5% on average). The United Kingdom (-3.9%), Germany (-10.0%) and Spain (-9.0%) declined, while France 
(+10.7%) and Italy (+8.8%) advanced strongly.
