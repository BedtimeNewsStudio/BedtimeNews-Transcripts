The **1974–75 Australian bushfire season** is a series of [bushfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bushfires_in_Australia "Bushfires in Australia"), also known around the world as [wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildfire "Wildfire"), that burned across [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia "Australia"). Fires that summer burned up an estimated 117million [hectares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hectare "Hectare") (290million [acres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre "Acre"); 1,170,000 [square kilometres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometre "Square kilometre"); 450,000 [square miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_mile "Square mile")).[&#8202;](#ref-back-AIDR-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-AIDR)[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3) Approximately 15% of Australia's land mass suffered "extensive fire damage"[&#8202;](#ref-back-AIDR-1)[\[2\]](#refnote-AIDR)[&#8202;](#ref-back-abs1995-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-abs1995) including parts of [New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_South_Wales "New South Wales"), the [Northern Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Territory "Northern Territory"), [Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queensland "Queensland"), [South Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Australia "South Australia") and [Western Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Australia "Western Australia").

## Statistics

The fires killed six people, approximately 57,000 farm animals, farmers' crops, and destroyed nearly 10,200 kilometres (6,300mi) of fencing.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Ellis-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-Ellis):339–345

The [Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Bureau_of_Statistics "Australian Bureau of Statistics") attributed the extent of the fires to "exceptionally heavy rainfall in the previous two years".[&#8202;](#ref-back-abs1995-1)[\[4\]](#refnote-abs1995)

[Stephen J. Pyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_J._Pyne "Stephen J. Pyne") qualified the fire season as the most destructive event in terms of hectares burned among historical fires in Australia, but added that "the 1974/75 fires had almost no impact and much of the damage was found by satellite after the fact."[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6) Nonetheless, fire historian [Danielle Clode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danielle_Clode "Danielle Clode") notes that these fires remain significant for being the biggest bushfire event by area ever recorded.[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7) In 2011, retired Australian government scientist David Packham warned that "we are in for one big season" that could repeat the 1974 summer fires.[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8)

## Areas impacted

Australia, being a [federation of States and territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_Australia "States and territories of Australia"), breaks up the 1974–1975 fires by state or region:

New South Wales

Six people killed. Area burned: 3.5million hectares (8.6million acres). 50,000 livestock lost, 10,170 kilometres (6,320mi) of fencing destroyed. [Bourke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourke,_New_South_Wales "Bourke, New South Wales") to [Balranald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balranald "Balranald"), [Cobar Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobar_Shire "Cobar Shire"), Moolah–Corinya—most of the [Western Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_West,_New_South_Wales "Far West, New South Wales").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Ellis-1)[\[5\]](#refnote-Ellis):341 Lost crops, and widespread damage to infrastructure, including communications, roads and railways.[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9)

The Moolah-Corinya fire was "the largest fire ever contained by man in New South Wales without the help of the weather." It burned 1.166million hectares (2.88million acres) and its perimeter was over 1,000 kilometres (620mi).[&#8202;](#ref-back-10-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-10)

Northern Territory

Area burned: 45million hectares (110million acres). The fire reached [Barkly Tableland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barkly_Tableland "Barkly Tableland"), Victoria River district, near [Newcastle Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_Waters "Newcastle Waters").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Ellis-2)[\[5\]](#refnote-Ellis):339

Queensland

The bushfire season was defined as October 1974 to February 1975. Area burned: 7.3million hectares (18million acres). 95 cattle, 6,850 sheep lost. Areas damaged: Thargomindah, Bulloo Shire, Boulia Urandangie, McKinlay Shire.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Ellis-3)[\[5\]](#refnote-Ellis):340

South Australia

Area burned: 15million hectares (37million acres). The areas affected were the north-west of the state (arid and semi-arid zones), and the [Adelaide Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelaide_Hills "Adelaide Hills").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Ellis-4)[\[5\]](#refnote-Ellis):344

Western Australia

Area burned: 29million hectares (72million acres) of fires, damaging east and north-east of [Kalgoorlie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalgoorlie "Kalgoorlie").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Ellis-5)[\[5\]](#refnote-Ellis):344
