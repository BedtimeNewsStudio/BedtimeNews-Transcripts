# FIDIC Awards 2018 - Results

## [BANNER\_facebook-twitter-awards\_banner.jpg](https://fidic.org/file/bannerfacebook-twitter-awardsbannerjpg-0)

![](https://fidic.org/sites/default/files/styles/right-side-image2/public/BANNER_facebook-twitter-awards_banner.jpg?itok=sWqyXNVE)

FIDIC is pleased to recognise the following projects which received an Award at the FIDIC Awards Gala Dinner celebrated on Monday, the 10th of September in Berlin, Germany. The award categories recognised were, "Outstanding Project of the Year", "Award of Special Merit" and "Award of Merit". Congratulations to all projects awarded!
