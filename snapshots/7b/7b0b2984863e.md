# Most prolific cow

![Most prolific cow ](/images/css/gwr-ph.jpg)

Who
:   Big Bertha

Where
:   Ireland (Kerry)

When
:   31 December 1993

The most prolific cow is Big Bertha (Ireland) who produced 39 calves, verified on 31 December 1993.

Big Bertha is the most prolific cow ever.
