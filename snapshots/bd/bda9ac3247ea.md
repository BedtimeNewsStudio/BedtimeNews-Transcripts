# Timeline: Wrongful deportation of Kilmar Abrego Garcia to El Salvador

The alleged MS-13 member was sent to a Salvadoran prison before being returned.

By[Laura Romero](https://abcnews.com/author/laura_romero), [Peter Charalambous](https://abcnews.com/author/peter_charalambous), [James Hill](https://abcnews.com/author/james_hill), [Ely Brown](https://abcnews.com/author/ely_brown), [Armando Garcia](https://abcnews.com/author/armando_garcia), and [Katherine Faulders](https://abcnews.com/author/katherine_faulders)

August 18, 2026, 7:13 PM

Share

Follow ABC News on Google

Kilmar Abrego Garcia, a Salvadoran native living Maryland, was [mistakenly deported](https://abcnews.com/Politics/ice-admits-administrative-error-after-maryland-man-el/story?id=120359991) in March to a mega-prison in his home country of El Salvador -- despite a 2019 court order barring his deportation to that country due to fear of persecution -- after the Trump administration claimed he was a member of the criminal gang MS-13, which he denies.

After a series of court battles in which the Trump administration said it could not bring him back despite the Supreme Court ordering them to facilitate his return, Abrego Garcia was indicted for allegedly transporting undocumented migrants within the United States, and [brought back to the U.S.](https://abcnews.com/US/kilmar-abrego-garcia-back-us-face-criminal-charges/story?id=121333122) to face charges.

Here is how the case has unfolded.

### March 15, 2025

The Trump administration, as part of its immigration crackdown, [deports three planeloads](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-expected-invoke-wartime-alien-enemies-act-carry/story?id=119769090) of alleged migrant gang members to El Salvador, where they are incarcerated in the notorious CECOT mega-prison under an agreement with the Salvadoran government. The alleged gang members include Kilmar Abrego Garcia, whose protected legal status precludes him from being deported to that country.

### March 18, 2025

Families of several of the deported men tell ABC News that their detained relatives have [no criminal record](https://abcnews.com/US/families-migrants-relatives-deported-ice-gang-members/story?id=119892593) in the United States -- a fact that's acknowledged by an [Immigration](https://abcnews.com/alerts/immigration) and Customs Enforcement official, who nonetheless says that "the lack of specific information about each individual actually highlights the risk they pose" and "demonstrates that they are terrorists with regard to whom we lack a complete profile."

### April 1, 2025

In a [sworn declaration](https://abcnews.com/Politics/ice-admits-administrative-error-after-maryland-man-el/story?id=120359991) as part of a lawsuit brought by Abrego Garcia's attorneys, a U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement official says Abrego Garcia was mistakenly deported to El Salvador as a result of an "administrative error" -- but that he is still under a "final order of removal" for deportation, after the government rescinded his protected legal status due to his purported gang ties as alleged by a confidential police informant.

Court documents say that on March 12, Abrego Garcia was detained by ICE officers who "informed him that his immigration status had changed" due to the MS-13 allegations, after which he was transferred to a detention center in Texas and then deported to El Salvador.

### April 4, 2025

U.S. District Judge Paula Xinis, at a hearing in Maryland, grants a preliminary injunction and orders the government to " [facilitate and effectuate](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-hearing-arguments-deportation-maryland-man-el-salvador/story?id=120491918)" the return of Abrego Garcia to the United States by midnight on April 7.

Asked by Judge Xinis under what authority law enforcement officers seized Abrego Garcia, DOJ attorney Erez Reuveni says he does not have the answer, telling the judge, "Your honor, my answer to a lot of these questions is going to be frustrating and I'm also frustrated that I have no answers for you on a lot of these questions."

### April 5, 2025

A day after the hearing, the Justice Department places DOJ attorney Erez Reuveni on [indefinite paid leave](https://abcnews.com/Politics/doj-places-attorney-leave-after-struggling-maryland-migrant/story?id=120526466) over a "failure to zealously advocate" for the government's interests.

### April 7, 2025

Supreme Court Chief Justice John Roberts issues a [temporary administrative stay](https://abcnews.com/Politics/appeals-court-affirms-judges-order-return-wrongly-deported/story?id=120562513) putting off Judge Xinis' midnight deadline for the government to return Abrego Garcia from El Salvador, in order to give the court more time to consider the arguments presented by both sides.

### April 8, 2025

After the Trump administration, in a court filing, argues that a judge can't demand Abrego Garcia's return because a federal court can't order a president to engage in foreign diplomacy, an attorney for Abrego Garcia [tells ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/lawyer-man-deported-error-el-salvador-expects-returned/story?id=120602266) he believes the Supreme Court will rule in his client's favor.

### April 9, 2025

In an interview with ABC News, Abrego Garcia's wife, a U.S. citizen, [denies](https://abcnews.com/US/wife-maryland-man-deported-error-el-salvador-denies/story?id=120650722) the Trump administration's allegation that her husband is a member of the criminal gang MS-13.

![](https://i.abcnewsfe.com/a/4feffd28-584a-45e8-8102-531172dd119c/Jennifer-Vasquez-Sura-2-rt-gmh-240415_1744750228949_hpMain.jpg)

Jennifer Vasquez Sura, wife of Kilmar Abrego Garcia, speaks during a press conference on the day of a hearing in the case related to Kilmar Abrego Garcia outside U.S. District Court in Greenbelt, Maryland, April 15, 2025.

### April 10, 2025

The U.S. Supreme Court [unanimously rules](https://abcnews.com/US/live-updates/trump-admin-live-updates-securing-trump-3rd-term/?id=120551202&entryId=120698619) that Judge Xinis "properly requires the Government to 'facilitate' Abrego Garcia's release from custody in El Salvador and to ensure that his case is handled as it would have been had he not been improperly sent to El Salvador."

"The intended scope of the term 'effectuate' in the District Court's order is, however, unclear, and may exceed the District Court's authority," the court writes. "The District Court should clarify its directive, with due regard for the deference owed to the Executive Branch in the conduct of foreign affairs."

The Trump administration, interpreting the ruling as prohibiting the district court from ordering the executive branch to take any action that would violate the separation of powers, celebrates the order as a victory for the administration.

### April 11, 2025

Saying "the Supreme Court has spoken quite clearly" that Abrego Garcia should be released from El Salvador, Judge Xinis [slams](https://abcnews.com/US/after-supreme-court-ruling-judge-sets-hearing-maryland/story?id=120703441) the government's handling of the case and orders the Justice Department to provide her with "daily updates" on Abrego Garcia's status and their efforts to bring him back.

### April 12, 2025

A State Department official, in response to Judge Xinis' demand for daily updates, [tells the judge](https://abcnews.com/Politics/state-department-reveals-status-man-erroneously-deported-el/story?id=120752503) that Abrego Garcia is "alive and secure" in El Salvador's CECOT prison, but provides no information about their efforts to return him to the United States.

### April 14, 2025

Attorney General Pam Bondi, in an [Oval Office meeting](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-hosts-el-salvadors-bukele-amid-deportation-controversy/story?id=120788481) with President Trump and the visiting El Salvador President Nayib Bukele, says that Abrego Garcia's return is "up to El Salvador," after which Bukele says, "I don't have the power to return him to the United States."

![](https://i.abcnewsfe.com/a/79828221-c4e0-480a-ad53-1b919bde2558/donald-trump-12-gty-gmh-250414_1744645888224_hpMain.jpg)

President Donald Trump meets with El Salvador President Nayib Bukele in the Oval Office at the White House in Washington, April 14, 2025.

### April 15, 2025

Judge Xinis, in a hearing, takes Justice Department attorneys to task over the Trump administration's inaction and orders government officials to [testify under oath](https://abcnews.com/US/trump-administration-digs-case-wrongly-deported-maryland-man/story?id=120822855) through expedited discovery in order to resolve Abrego Garcia's wrongful deportation.

Prior to the hearing, a DHS official says the Trump administration is "prepared to facilitate Abrego Garcia's presence in the United States ... if he presents at a port of entry" -- but that DHS does not have the authority to forcibly extract him "from the domestic custody of a foreign sovereign nation."

### April 16, 2025

The Department of Justice files notice that it will appeal Judge Xinis' ruling ordering the government to facilitate Abrego Garcia's return, and [releases two documents](https://abcnews.com/US/doj-appeal-ruling-ordering-administration-return-abrego-garcia/story?id=120885785) that were previously used to allegedly tie Abrego Garcia to MS-13.

The developments come on the same day that DHS, in a social media post, shares court records showing that Abrego Garcia's wife had a month-long [order of protection](https://abcnews.com/US/wife-deported-maryland-man-abrego-garcia-hit-scratched/story?id=120882240) against him in 2021, in which she cited being slapped, hit with an object, and being detained against her will.

Meanwhile, Sen. Chris Van Hollen of Maryland, where Abrego Garcia and his family live, [travels to El Salvador](https://abcnews.com/Politics/democratic-senator-heads-el-salvador-find-man-deported/story?id=120865596) in the hope of meeting with Abrego Garcia to get answers about his deportation and check on his condition.

### April 17, 2025

Slamming the government for "asserting a right to stash away residents of this country in foreign prisons without the semblance of due process," the U.S. Court of Appeals for the 4th Circuit [denies](https://abcnews.com/US/slamming-trump-administration-appeals-court-denies-request-appeal/story?id=120921685) the Trump administration's effort to appeal Judge Xinis' order requiring it to facilitate Abrego Garcia's return.

Late in the day, Sen. Van Hollen shares a photo on social media of him [meeting with Abrego Garcia](https://abcnews.com/US/maryland-sen-van-hollen-shares-photo-kilmar-abrego/story?id=120932263) in El Salvador. Abrego Garcia's wife is told that the meeting was set up by El Salvador President Nayib Bukele, a source close to the family tells ABC News.

Sen. Chris Van Hollen shares photo of meeting Kilmar Abrego Garcia in El Salvador.

### April 18, 2025

Sen. Van Hollen, at a news conference following his return from El Salvador, [provides details](https://abcnews.com/Politics/maryland-senator-greets-abrego-garcias-family-return-us/story?id=120954585) of his meeting with Abrego Garcia, saying that authorities brought Abrego Garcia to his hotel for the meeting and that Abrego Garcia told him he had been transferred nine days ago to another facility and was no longer being held at the CECOT mega-prison -- although he still has no access to news and no ability to communicate with the outside world.

### April 20, 2025

Trump administration border czar Tom Homan tells ABC News' "This Week" co-anchor Jonathan Karl that the administration [properly used](https://abcnews.com/Politics/homan-trump-administration-thing-deporting-abrego-garcia/story?id=120978761) the Alien Enemies Act when it deported Abrego Garcia.

Sen. Van Hollen, discussing Abrego Garcia in a separate "This Week" interview, [tells Karl](https://abcnews.com/Politics/van-hollen-defending-man-defending-rights-man-due/story?id=120978764), "I am not defending the man. I'm defending the rights of this man to due process."

### April 21, 2025

The Salvadoran government [rejects](https://abcnews.com/US/el-salvador-government-rejects-lawmakers-request-visit-abrego/story?id=120910107) a request from four Democratic lawmakers to visit Abrego Garcia in El Salvador.

### April 22, 2025

Judge Xinis, overruling objections raised by the Trump administration, orders the government to [more fully answer](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-attorneys-decry-administrations-failure-comply-court/story?id=121046788) and respond to discovery requests from Abrego Garcia's attorneys.

### April 23, 2025

Judge Xinis, at the Trump administration's request, [temporarily pauses](https://abcnews.com/US/trump-administration-asks-judge-pause-discovery-abrego-garcia/story?id=121083991) discovery in the case for seven days.

### April 29, 2025

In an exclusive interview with ABC News to mark his 100th day in office, President Trump says he ["could" secure the return](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-abrego-garcia-back-el-salvador/story?id=121298276) of Abrego Garcia, and "if he were the gentleman that you say he is, I would do that" -- before adding, "I'm not the one making this decision."

### April 30, 2025

Multiple sources tell ABC News that Secretary of State Marco Rubio has [been in touch](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-denies-dojs-request-delay-discovery-abrego-garcia/story?id=121323475) with El Salvador President Nayib Bukele about Abrego Garcia, although details of their contact are not immediately clear.

Kilmar Abrego Garcia speaks with a Tennessee state trooper on the side of I-40 in 2022.

### May 2, 2025

Newly released video shows the moments when Abrego Garcia was [pulled over](https://abcnews.com/US/newly-released-video-shows-abrego-garcias-2022-tennessee/story?id=121384956) by state troopers in Tennessee in 2022.

### May 6, 2025

Sources tell ABC News that the Justice Department has spoken with a convicted felon in an Alabama prison about potential connections to the [2022 Tennessee traffic stop](https://abcnews.com/Politics/justice-department-investigating-2022-abrego-garcia-traffic-stop/story?id=121492776) involving Abrego Garcia.

### May 7, 2025

Judge Xinis orders attorneys for Abrego Garcia and the DOJ to submit briefs by May 12 and schedules a hearing for May 16 after the Trump administration invokes the rarely used [state secrets privilege](https://abcnews.com/US/live-updates/trump-admin-live-updates-trump-threatens-100-tariff?id=121461146&entryId=121575617) to shield information about its handling of the matter.

### May 8, 2025

Attorneys for Abrego Garcia, saying they're "still in the dark" after deposing three government officials in the case, ask Judge Xinis to authorize the depositions of [three additional officials](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-lawyers-dark-governments-efforts-facilitate-return/story?id=121602607).

### May 13, 2025

Lawyers for Abrego Garcia, in a court filing, [push back](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-lawyers-challenge-trump-administrations-invocation-state/story?id=121749339) on the Trump administration's invocation of the state secrets privilege, saying that the government has produced no evidence that it's made "the slightest effort" to facilitate his release.

### May 16, 2025

Under orders from Judge Xinis following a hearing on the matter, the Trump administration releases Secretary of State Marco Rubio's May 5 declaration supporting the invocation of the state secrets privilege in the case. Rubio, in the declaration, says that the disclosure of certain information requested by Abrego Garcia's attorneys "could be expected to cause [significant harm](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-abrego-garcia-case-hear-arguments-administrations-claim/story?id=121839025) to the foreign relations interests" and national security interests of the U.S.

### June 6, 2025

Abrego Garcia is [brought back](https://abcnews.com/US/kilmar-abrego-garcia-back-us-face-criminal-charges/story?id=121333122) to the U.S. to face criminal charges for allegedly participating in a yearslong conspiracy to haul undocumented migrants from Texas to the interior of the United States. Attorney General Pam Bondi, after the administration had said for months that it could not bring him back, says, "Our government presented El Salvador with an arrest warrant and they agreed to return him to our country."

Abrego Garcia makes his initial court appearance in the Middle District of Tennessee, answering "Yes, I understand" in Spanish when U.S. Magistrate Judge Barbara Holmes asks him if he understands the charges against him.

### June 9, 2025

Arguing that Abrego Garcia poses "a serious risk of flight," federal prosecutors in Tennessee ask Judge Holmes for a pre-trial [detention hearing](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-lawyers-argue-maryland-case-government-held/story?id=122652258) in his human smuggling case.

The filing comes a day after Abrego Garcia's attorneys, in a separate filing in federal court in Maryland, argue that their wrongful deportation case is not over until the government is "held accountable" for "its blatant, willful, and persistent violations of court orders at excruciating cost to Abrego Garcia and his family."

### June 10, 2025

Calling Abrego Garcia's attorneys' request to continue their Maryland case "desperate and disappointing," Justice Department lawyers argue in a court filing that the Trump administration [complied with the court's order](https://abcnews.com/US/trump-administration-argues-complied-court-order-return-abrego/story?id=122700932) to facilitate Abrego Garcia's return.

### June 11, 2025

Attorneys for Abrego Garcia ask Judge Xinis to [impose sanctions](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-attorneys-judge-sanctions-trump-administration/story?id=122782568) on the Trump administration for what they call "repeated violations of its discovery obligations" in Abrego Garcia's wrongful deportation case.

### June 13, 2025

One week after being brought back to the U.S. from detention in El Salvador, Abrego Garcia [pleads not guilty](https://abcnews.com/US/kilmar-abrego-garcia-scheduled-arraigned-charges-human-smuggling/story?id=122791176) to human smuggling charges at his arraignment in Tennessee.

Judge Holmes, after hearing arguments over Abrego Garcia's pre-trial detention, says she will wait to issue a decision on whether he should be released from custody pending trial.

### June 22, 2025

Judge Holmes denies the government's motion to detain Abrego Garcia while he awaits trial on human smuggling charges -- but acknowledges that, if released, he will likely be [placed in ICE custody](https://abcnews.com/Politics/judge-denies-governments-motion-detain-kilmar-abrego-garcia/story?id=123105021) due to an immigration detainer the government has on him.

### June 25, 2025

Judge Holmes outlines Abrego Garcia's conditions of release for his release on bond, but he [remains in custody](https://abcnews.com/US/kilmar-abrego-garcia-set-court-tennessee-after-judge/story?id=123173632) after the judge orders his attorneys and attorneys for the Justice Department to file briefs on whether the government has the ability to prevent his deportation while he awaits trial.

### June 26, 2025

Abrego Garcia's attorneys file an [emergency motion](https://abcnews.com/US/trump-administration-plans-deport-abrego-garcia-3rd-country/story?id=123242998) with Judge Xinis seeking to have him returned to Maryland as he awaits trial, but a government attorney tells the judge the administration will seek to deport him to a country other than his native El Salvador.

### July 2, 2025

Attorneys for Abrego Garcia argue in a court filing that he could "face persecution or torture if removed directly to various other countries," saying that he had been subjected to [severe mistreatment](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcia-subjected-severe-mistreatment-cecot-lawyers/story?id=123432720) while he was held in CECOT.

### July 7, 2025

Judge Xinis, in a hearing, orders the Trump administration to [produce witnesses](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-hear-arguments-transfer-abrego-garcia-maryland-tennessee/story?id=123535334) who can testify about the government's plans should Abrego Garcia be released from custody in Tennessee.

### July 10, 2025

On the second day of a hearing in Maryland, an ICE official testifies about the [process the government might undertake](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-hear-dojs-deportation-plan-abrego-garcia-released/story?id=123644298) to deport Abrego Garcia should he be released from custody in Tennessee -- but the official says that he himself has had no involvement in Abrego Garcia's case and little experience handling similar deportation processes.

### July 11, 2025

On Day 3 of a hearing in Maryland, Abrego Garcia's legal team [asks Judge Xinis](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-attorneys-judge-require-72-hours-notice/story?id=123682707) to order that he not be removed from the United States without at least 72 hours notice should he be released on bond from detention in Tennessee.

### July 16, 2025

A federal judge in Tennessee says he expects to rule within the next week on Abrego Garcia's pre-trial detention, after government attorneys [argue during a hearing](https://abcnews.com/US/federal-judge-hear-arguments-abrego-garcias-detention-pending/story?id=123795148) that Abrego Garcia poses a public danger and flight risk too great to allow him to be released on bail.

### July 21, 2025

Abrego Garcia's attorneys ask a federal judge for a [30-day stay](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-attorneys-request-30-day-stay-ordered/story?id=123926884) if he is ordered released from custody while awaiting trial.

### July 23, 2025

Judge Xinis orders the Trump administration to [return Abrego Garcia to Maryland](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-blocks-trump-administration-detaining-abrego-garcia-release/story?id=123958126) upon his release from criminal custody, shortly before a magistrate judge in Tennessee, responding to Abrego Garcia's attorneys' request, pauses Abrego Garcia's release for 30 days.

### Aug. 19, 2025

Abrego Garcia's attorneys file a motion [seeking to dismiss](https://abcnews.com/Politics/kilmar-abrego-garcias-attorneys-accuse-doj-vindictive-selective/story?id=124796903) the Tennessee criminal charges against him, accusing federal prosecutors of "vindictive and selective prosecution."

### Aug. 22, 2025

Abrego Garcia is [released from criminal custody](https://abcnews.com/Politics/kilmar-abrego-garcia-released-friday-criminal-custody/story?id=124874189) in Tennessee into the custody of his brother in Maryland, with what sources say are orders to report to the ICE office in Baltimore on Monday morning. The Trump administration may seek to deport Abrego Garcia [to Uganda](https://abcnews.com/US/costa-rica-accept-abrego-garcia-conclusion-criminal-sentence/story?id=124897725) despite the government of Costa Rica indicating it is willing to accept him "upon the conclusion of any criminal sentence he may serve in the U.S," according to sources.

### Aug. 25, 2025

Abrego Garcia is [taken into immigration custody](https://abcnews.com/US/kilmar-abrego-garcia-check-ice-maryland-deportation-threat/story?id=124944566) upon checking in with ICE at its office in Baltimore and is transferred to a detention center in Virginia, where his attorneys say ICE has notified them that he may be deported to Uganda after he rejected a plea deal to be deported to his preferred destination of Costa Rica in exchange for pleading guilty to human smuggling charges.

Judge Xinis says at a habeas hearing that the federal government is "absolutely forbidden" from deporting him for the time being.

### Aug. 26, 2025

Attorneys for Abrego Garcia [file an emergency motion](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-attorneys-blast-government-unilaterally-rearresting/story?id=124991217) to reopen his immigration case in order to seek asylum and to allow Abrego Garcia to designate Costa Rica as the country of removal should he be deported.

### Aug. 26, 2025

Judge Xinis [temporarily blocks](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-blocks-administration-deporting-kilmar-abrego-garcia-october/story?id=125024099) the Trump administration from deporting Abrego Garcia until at least early October when his latest deportation court challenge is expected to be resolved.

### Aug. 28, 2025

Saying that "numerous government officials have vilified" their client in the media, lawyers for Abrego Garcia [ask a court in Tennessee](https://abcnews.com/US/abrego-garcias-lawyers-judge-order-trump-administration-stop/story?id=125081801) to order all DOJ and DHS officials to "refrain from making extrajudicial comments that pose a substantial likelihood of materially prejudicing" the court proceedings against him.

### Sept. 5, 2025

After the Trump administration says in a court filing that it will again remove Abrego Garcia to El Salvador if a judge grants his attorneys' request to reopen his immigration case, the government says in an email to his lawyers that -- following Abrego Garcia's claims of fear of prosecution if he's deported to countries that include El Salvador, Mexico, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras and Haiti -- it now plans to deport him to [Eswatini, Africa](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-administration-warns-deport-kilmar-abrego-garcia-el/story?id=125302355).

### Oct. 1, 2025

An immigration judge [denies the motion](https://abcnews.com/US/doj-seeks-postpone-deadlines-abrego-garcias-deportation-case/story?id=126132382) to reopen the immigration case of Abrego Garcia, who is now being held in a detention center in Pennsylvania. The DOJ, meanwhile, moves to postpone all the deadlines in the case due to the [government shutdown](https://abcnews.com/Politics/live-updates/trump-admin-live-updates/?id=126029955).

### Oct. 6, 2025

In a status hearing, Judge Xinis [denies the government's request](https://abcnews.com/US/kilmar-abrego-garcias-deportation-case-back-maryland-judge/story?id=126237666) to delay proceedings in Abrego Garcia's deportation case because of the government shutdown. After government attorneys are unable to provide information on potential plans to remove Abrego Garcia to Eswatini, the judge gives the government two days to file any such evidence.

### Oct. 9, 2025

DHS notifies Abrego Garcia that it plans to deport him to Ghana, before it says the [notice was "premature"](https://abcnews.com/International/dhs-now-plans-deport-abrego-garcia-ghana/story?id=126382679) andshould be disregarded.

### Oct. 10, 2025

In a hearing in front of Judge Xinis, an ICE official testifies that Abrego Garcia could be removed from the U.S. within 72 hours if the agency gets [approval from a third country](https://abcnews.com/US/attorneys-kilmar-abrego-garcia-return-court-deportation-saga/story?id=126381740) that is willing to accept him. An attorney for Abrego Garcia says there is a "clear pathway" for his removal to Costa Rico, as that country has indicated it would receive him.

### Oct. 24, 2025

DHS says in a court notice that the West African nation of Liberia has [agreed to accept](https://abcnews.com/US/dhs-pressing-deport-kilmar-abrego-garcia-liberia-agreed/story?id=126831679) Abrego Garcia and that the agency expects "to be able to effectuate removal as soon as October 31."

### Oct. 27, 2025

Judge Xinis, in a hearing, [presses DOJ attorneys](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-presses-doj-intention-deport-kilmar-abrego-garcia/story?id=126909166) to justify their decision to designate Liberia as Abrego Garcia's country of removal, when Abrego Garcia's lawyers have said there is a "clear pathway" for his removal to Costa Rico.

On the same day, U.S. District Judge Waverly Crenshaw orders officials with the Department of Homeland Security and Department of Justice to refrain from [making prejudicial statements](https://abcnews.com/US/federal-judge-orders-doj-dhs-stop-prejudicial-statements/story?id=126918933) about Abrego Garcia in his human smuggling case in Tennessee.

### Nov. 8, 2025

In court filings, the government asks Judge Xinis to [dissolve the ban](https://abcnews.com/US/trump-administration-moves-dissolve-ban-abrego-garcias-removal/story?id=127329912) on Abrego Garcia's removal to Liberia, saying it has received assurances from the Liberian government that he will not be persecuted or tortured, while Abrego Garcia's attorneys ask that his removal continue to be blocked until an immigration judge reviews the administration's denial of his reasonable fear claim.

### Nov. 20, 2025

During an evidentiary hearing on the government's plans to deport Abrego Garcia to Liberia, a top ICE official [struggles to answer questions](https://abcnews.com/US/ice-official-struggles-answer-questions-abrego-garcias-potential/story?id=127714915) about Abrego Garcia's potential removal.

### Dec. 11, 2025

Abrego Garcia is [released from immigration detention](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-orders-immediate-release-kilmar-abrego-garcia-immigration/story?id=126511576) after Judge Xinis orders his immediate release, ruling in Abrego Garcia's habeas case that he has been detained "without lawful authority," in part because he had not been issued a formal order of removal during his immigration proceedings in 2019.

### Dec. 12, 2025

After an immigration judge appears to add a removal order to Abrego Garcia's immigration record following the ruling ordering his release, Judge Xinis issues a [temporary restraining order](https://abcnews.com/US/federal-judge-blocks-government-detaining-kilmar-abrego-garcia/story?id=128339058) prohibiting Abrego Garcia from being re-arrested at his required immigration check-in.

### Dec. 30, 2025

U.S. District Judge Waverly Crenshaw,after postponing the trial for Abrego Garcia's human smuggling case in Tennessee, says in an unsealed order that Abrego Garcia has a "due process right to a [non-vindictive prosecution](https://abcnews.com/Politics/judge-documents-suggest-top-doj-officials-pushed-prosecute/story?id=128792629)," and schedules a follow-up hearing.

### Feb. 26, 2026

The Tennessee prosecutor who brought criminal human smuggling charges against Abrego Garcia, testifying at a hearing over whether Abrego Garcia is being [vindictively prosecuted](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-hear-arguments-kilmar-abrego-garcia-vindictively-prosecuted/story?id=130494679), tells Judge Waverly Crenshaw that no one from the White House or the Department of Justice directed him to seek the charges.

### March 20, 2026

Trump administration officials, seeking to swiftly removeAbrego Garciato Liberia as soon as a court allows, ask a judge to [dissolve a preliminary injunction](https://abcnews.com/US/trump-administration-seeks-move-ahead-removing-abrego-garcia/story?id=131272186) that bars them from re-detaining Abrego Garcia and deporting him.

### April 7, 2026

Judge Xinis, during a hearing, presses the DOJ over its [push to deport Abrego Garcia](https://abcnews.com/US/judge-questions-dojs-push-deport-abrego-garcia-criminal/story?id=131802873) while his criminal human smuggling case in Tennessee is still pending.

### May 22, 2026

Judge Crenshaw [dismisses](https://abcnews.com/US/federal-judge-dismisses-tennessee-criminal-case-kilmar-abrego/story?id=133232491) Abrego Garcia's criminal human smuggling case in Tennessee, finding that the government's investigation had been tainted "with a vindictive motive."

### June 3, 2026

Department of Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin says during testimony that his agency "will be [happy to send](https://abcnews.com/US/mullin-us-happy-send-kilmar-abrego-garcia-costa/story?id=133560658)"Abrego Garciato Costa Rica -- contradicting what DHS officials and DOJ attorneys have said in court for months.

### June 22, 2026

The Justice Department files notice that it [intends to appeal](https://abcnews.com/US/doj-appeal-judges-order-dismissing-kilmar-abrego-garcias/story?id=134110051) Judge Crenshaw's dismissal of Abrego Garcia's criminal human smuggling case.

### Aug. 18, 2026

The Justice Department asks an appeals court to [reinstate the criminal human smuggling case](https://abcnews.com/US/doj-asks-appeals-court-reinstate-human-smuggling-case/story?id=135736924) against Abrego Garcia, arguing the decision to indict him was supported by "legitimate reasons."
