## Historical rankings

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Opinion_polling_on_the_Narendra_Modi_premiership&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Historical rankings")\]

The _Mood of the Nation_ survey conducted annually between 2016 and 2021 consistently listed Modi as the most popular prime minister among Indian public.[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-28)[&#8202;](#ref-back-29-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-29) A YouGov-Mint-CPR survey in 2022 also ranked Modi first, with [Jawaharlal Nehru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawaharlal_Nehru "Jawaharlal Nehru") and [Atal Bihari Vajpayee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atal_Bihari_Vajpayee "Atal Bihari Vajpayee") being in the second and third places respectively.[&#8202;](#ref-back-30-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-30)
