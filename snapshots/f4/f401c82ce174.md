The **13th Politburo of the Communist Party of Vietnam (CPV)**, formally the _13th Political Bureau of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam_ ( [Vietnamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_language "Vietnamese language"): _Bộ Chính trị Ban Chấp hành trung ương Đảng Cộng sản Việt Nam Khoá XIII_), was elected at the 1st Plenary Session of the [13th Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Vietnam "13th Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam") (CC) in the immediate aftermath of the [13th National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_National_Congress_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Vietnam "13th National Congress of the Communist Party of Vietnam"). [Nguyễn Phú Trọng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Ph%C3%BA_Tr%E1%BB%8Dng "Nguyễn Phú Trọng") was re-elected for his third term as [General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Secretary_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Vietnam "General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam"), a position he has held since 2011.[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1)

On 30 December 2022, at the 13th CC's 2nd Extraordinary Plenary Session, [Phạm Bình Minh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph%E1%BA%A1m_B%C3%ACnh_Minh "Phạm Bình Minh") offered his resignation as a member of the 13th Politburo; the plenum approved the resignation, and he was removed from the 13th CC as well.[&#8202;](#ref-back-PBMremoval-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-PBMremoval) [Nguyễn Xuân Phúc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Xu%C3%A2n_Ph%C3%BAc "Nguyễn Xuân Phúc"), the [President of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Vietnam "President of Vietnam") and the second-ranked member of the 13th Politburo, voluntarily resigned from all state and party offices at the 3rd Extraordinary Plenary Session on 17 January 2023.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NXPremoval-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-NXPremoval)
