# Retraction: Kweichow Moutai ameliorates alcohol‐induced liver fibrosis in mice by targeting the NFκB pathway

Collection date 2023 Nov.

[PMC Copyright notice](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/copyright/)

PMCID: PMC10630787  PMID: [37970389](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37970389/)

This retracts the article " [Kweichow Moutai ameliorates alcohol‐induced liver fibrosis in mice by targeting the NFκB pathway](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7455936/)" in volume 8 on page 4214.

## Abstract

Yang, T, Feng, Y, Zhang, Y, et al. Kweichow Moutai ameliorates alcohol‐induced liver fibrosis in mice by targeting the NFκB pathway. _Food Sci Nutr_. 2020; 8: 4214–4222. [https://doi.org/10.1002/fsn3.1716](https://doi.org/10.1002/fsn3.1716).

The above article, published online on June 15, 2020 in Wiley Online Library ( [wileyonlinelibrary.com](http://wileyonlinelibrary.com/)), has been retracted by agreement between the journal Editor in Chief Y. Martin Lo, and Wiley Periodicals LLC. The retraction has been agreed because of concerns that the figures were duplicated, affecting the interpretation of the data and results presented.
