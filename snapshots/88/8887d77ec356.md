# Will the Sagrada Família ever be finished?

Written and fact-checked by

[The Editors of Encyclopaedia Britannica\\
\\
Encyclopaedia Britannica's editors oversee subject areas in which they have extensive knowledge, whether from years of experience gained by working on that content or via study for an advanced degree....](https://www.britannica.com/editor/The-Editors-of-Encyclopaedia-Britannica/4419)

The Editors of Encyclopaedia Britannica

Yes, construction of the Sagrada Família’s structure is expected to be completed in 2026, the centennial of the death of [Antoni Gaudí](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Antoni-Gaudi), its architect. However, work will continue into the 2030s on the construction of a stairway to the church’s entrance and on the decoration of the church, which will include the carving of sculptures.
