# BSNL cancels multi-crore 4G tender for network upgrade

State-owned BSNL on Wednesday cancelled a multi-crore tender for 4G telecom network upgrade after the government asked the company not to use any Chinese equipment.

![BSNL cancels multi-crore 4G tender for network upgrade](https://static.toiimg.com/thumb/msid-76743937,imgsize-809778,width-400,resizemode-4/76743937.jpg)

(File photo)

NEW DELHI: State-owned [BSNL](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/topic/bsnl) on Wednesday cancelled a multi-crore tender for 4G telecom network upgrade after the government asked the company not to use any Chinese equipment.

The move comes against the backdrop of a growing tirade against Chinese goods and services after the violent clash at the border with China. BSNL, which is upgrading to 4G technology, issued a notice for cancellation of the tender.

A new tender is likely to be floated that will have an emphasis on ‘Make in India’ gear. The notice for cancellation has been “issued with the approval of competent authority”, it added.

Meanwhile, sources said that the government had earlier directed BSNL not to use Chinese equipment in the 4G upgrade, and implementation of this direction meant that the company now has to issue a new tender.

Keeping in view developing Indian capacity and in-house technology, a new tender will be floated, the source said, adding this will have emphasis on preference to ‘Make in India’.
