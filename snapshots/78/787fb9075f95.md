# Group D: Defeat to England ends China PR's campaign

Tue, 01 August, 2023Share

Adelaide: China PR crashed out of the FIFA Women’s World Cup Australia & New Zealand 2023 as Shui Qingxi’s side were handed a 6-1 thrashing by European champions England in Adelaide’s Hindmarsh Stadium on Tuesday.

Alessia Russo, Lauren Hemp and Lauren James gave England a three-goal cushion at half time and, while Wang Shuang pulled one back from the penalty spotin the 57th minute, James’ second of the game eight minutes later was followed by strikes from Chloe Kelly and Rachel Daly.

The result leaves the Chinese in third place in Group D and means the Steel Roses will not qualify for the knockout rounds of the FIFA Women’s World Cup for the first time in eight appearances at the finals.

Shui selected Wang in her starting line-up as a result of Zhang Rui’s suspension but within four minutes the Chinese were chasing the game, Russo scoring into the bottom corner after James’ perfectly weighted through ball.

The English dominated the opening exchanges, Hemp shooting wide in the 13th minute after Russo’s pass and a further 13 minutes later they doubled their advantage. Millie Bright won possession at half way and released James, who fed the ball forward for Hemp to strike.

Lucy Bronze hit the post with a header in the 33rd minute and then pulled the rebound wide of the opposite side of the goal as England’s dominance increased.

![image content](https://assets.the-afc.com/Fifa_Womens_World_Cup_2023/Finals/Matches/Group_D/MD3/China_PR_v_England/China-England-2.jpg?d=1880x0)

It came as little surprise that Sarina Wiegman’s side scored a third, the goal coming when James, the game’s dominant force, swept in from the edge of the area after Alex Greenwood’s free kick.

James then thought she had scored her side’s fourth deep into injury time, only for referee Casey Reibelt to hand the Chinese a reprieve when she ruled the strike out after consulting the pitch-side monitor.

Shui’s side had been unable to push deep into English territory, Li Mengwen providing a rare glimpse of the opposition goal when she burst down the right to send in a cross that a straining Wu Chengshu was unable to meet.

![image content](https://assets.the-afc.com/Fifa_Womens_World_Cup_2023/Finals/Matches/Group_D/MD3/China_PR_v_England/China-England-3.jpg?d=1880x0)

There was to be greater success for China PR in the second half, Mary Earps making a fine save to deny Wu before referee Reibelt decided Bronze had handled in the area and Wang struck her 57th minute penalty down the middle.

Buoyed by the goal the Chinese went in search of a second, Yang Lina thumping her shot from distance just over the bar to the delight of the red-clad fans within Hindmarsh Stadium.

![image content](https://assets.the-afc.com/Fifa_Womens_World_Cup_2023/Finals/Matches/Group_D/MD3/China_PR_v_England/China-England-5.jpg?d=1880x0)

That was to prove a brief flurry of excitement for China PR, however, as James scored again, volleying Jessica Carter’s lofted ball into the area past Zhu with her instep with 25 minutes remaining.

Kelly added the fifth when she slotting into an empty net in the 77th minute after Zhu had misread James’ pass forward while Daly put the seal on the emphatic win when she smashed her shot in with six minutes remaining.
