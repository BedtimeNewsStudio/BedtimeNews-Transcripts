LONDON (Reuters) - David Cameron has been leader of the Conservative Party since December 2005. Here are five facts about him:

\- Born in October 1966, he was educated at the elite school Eton west of London and studied Philosophy, Politics and Economics at Oxford University where he graduated with first class honours.

\- Elected to parliament in 2001, he is credited with making the Conservatives electable again after a decade in the political wilderness when it was regarded, in the words of one-time chairman Theresa May, as the "nasty party".

\- He has promoted "Compassionate Conservatism", partly influenced by the experience of caring for his oldest child Ivan, who was born in 2002 with cerebral palsy and extreme epilepsy.

\- When Britain crashed out of the European Exchange Rate Mechanism on Black Wednesday in 1992 Cameron was working as an advisor to then Conservative Chancellor Norman Lamont. The debacle scuppered the Conservative party's reputation for economic competence for years afterwards.

\- Cameron has refused to comment on allegations he took illegal drugs in his youth, saying he was allowed to keep private the details of his life before he entered politics. "We are all human and we err and stray," he said.
