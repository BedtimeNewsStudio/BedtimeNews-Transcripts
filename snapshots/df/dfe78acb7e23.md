The **Kouré shooting** was a [mass shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_shooting "Mass shooting") that occurred in [Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger "Niger") on 9 August 2020.[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1) The attack left at least 8 [civilians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilian "Civilian") dead, six [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people "French people") and two Nigeriens.[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) The attack took place in [Kouré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kour%C3%A9,_Niger "Kouré, Niger"), a rural community in [Tillabéri Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tillab%C3%A9ri_Region "Tillabéri Region").[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3)

## Background

Tillabéri Region, which is in the west of Niger and borders [Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mali "Mali") and [Burkina Faso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkina_Faso "Burkina Faso"), is largely unstable and has been used as a hideout by various [Sahel jihadist groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamist_insurgency_in_the_Sahel "Islamist insurgency in the Sahel") including the [Islamic State in the Greater Sahara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_State_in_the_Greater_Sahara "Islamic State in the Greater Sahara") (ISGS).[&#8202;](#ref-back-auto-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-auto) Since 2018, these terrorist groups have spread throughout the Sahel region, resulting in the deaths of hundreds of military personnel and civilians in sporadic attacks. As a result of the attacks, the region has seen extensive developments in security, such as the construction of a new [U.S. airbase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force "United States Air Force").[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-:0) In an effort to combat the terrorists, the [Nigerien government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerien_government "Nigerien government") also restricted the use of motorcycles in January 2019.[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6) Despite this, parts of Tillaberi and Tahoua Regions remain under a [state of emergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_emergency "State of emergency") and the [French government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_government "French government") warns people against visiting vast areas of Niger.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-1)[\[5\]](#refnote-:0)[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7)

## Shooting

The shooting occurred around 11:30am (10:30 GMT) six kilometres (four miles) east of the town of Kouré, [Kollo Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kollo_Department "Kollo Department"), Tillabéri Region, where a [wildlife park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_park "Wildlife park") is located. The region is [visited by tourists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Niger "Tourism in Niger") and purportedly hosts the last [West African giraffes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_African_giraffe "West African giraffe").[&#8202;](#ref-back-auto-1)[\[4\]](#refnote-auto) Eight people were killed in the attack. The victims were identified as two Nigeriens, of whom one was the president of the guides at the park while the other was the driver of the group, both aged 50; the other six were French citizens, all of whom aid workers aged between 25 and 30.[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8) The Islamic State claimed responsibility for the attack. Seven of the victims were shot dead, while the French woman who initially escaped was later kidnapped and murdered; an empty [magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magazine_(firearms) "Magazine (firearms)") was found at the scene. "They \[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[the attackers\] came on motorcycles through the bush and waited for the arrival of the tourists" said the governor.[\[9\]](#refnote-9)
