# ANVF INNOVATION 2026

![Caixin](https://img.caixin.com//2026-06-05/178065543970369.jpg)

![China ECONOMY News - Caixin Global](https://img.caixin.com/2026-07-29/178529990410679.jpg)

![亚洲愿景论坛2024-1](https://promote.caixin.com/upload/0530anvf/biaoti-pc.png)[News Reports](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026/asia-new-vision-forum-2026/) [会议报道](https://topics.caixin.com/2026/asia-new-vision-forum_2026/?cxw=IOS&Sfrom=Wechat)

从人工智能到新能源，我们正步入一个以“突破性变革”为特征的十年。然而，在创新加速之际，造就这一进程的全球稳定格局却日趋瓦解。2026亚洲愿景论坛创新峰会（ANVF Innovation 2026）的主题“变革与韧性”正是一次行动号召，它呼吁企业与政策制定者寻找韧性，坚守共同利益的底线，确保当下的爆发式增长造福人类未来，而非动摇其根基。本届峰会将汇聚来自政商学界深具远见卓识的领导者，就此进行开放和建设性的对话，探索创新解决方案，打造一个兼具积极对话与可落地成果的动态平台。

From AI to new energy, we are entering a decade of "breakthrough disruption." While innovation is accelerating, the global stability that made it possible is fracturing. The theme of ANVF Innovation 2026 - Disruption Meets Resilience - is a call to action. It is about businesses and policymakers finding the resilience to hold the line for the collective good, ensuring that today's explosive growth serves humanity's future rather than undermining it. This summit will bring together visionary leaders from government, business, academia, and society for open and constructive dialogues. Together, they will explore innovative solutions and build a dynamic platform that translates inspiring insights into actionable outcomes.
