**Captain Nemo**, fictional character, the megalomaniacal captain of the submarine _Nautilus_ in [Jules Verne](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jules-Verne)’s novel _Vingt Mille Lieues sous les mers_ (1869–70; _[Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Twenty-Thousand-Leagues-Under-the-Sea)_), and also a character in the [subsequent](https://www.britannica.com/dictionary/subsequent)_L’Île mystérieuse_ (1874; _[The Mysterious Island](https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Mysterious-Island)_).
