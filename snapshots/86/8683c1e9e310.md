Lesotho

**AGOA Status**

Lesotho is eligible for [AGOA](https://ustr.gov/trade-topics/trade-development/preference-programs/african-growth-and-opportunity-act-agoa) this year. It also qualifies for textile and apparel benefits.

**Trade Agreements**

The U.S. and the Southern Africa Customs Union (SACU), which includes Lesotho, signed a Trade, Investment, and Development Cooperative Agreement (TIDCA) in 2008. The TIDCA establishes a forum for consultative discussions, cooperative work, and possible agreements on a wide range of trade issues, with a special focus on customs and trade facilitation, technical barriers to trade, sanitary and phytosanitary (SPS) measures, and trade and investment promotion.

**Lesotho Trade Summary**

U.S. goods and services (exports plus imports) trade with Lesotho totaled an estimated $205.0 million in 2025, down 25.4 percent ($69.9 million) from 2024.

U.S. goods trade (exports plus imports) with Lesotho totaled an estimated $173.0 million in 2025.  U.S. goods exports to Lesotho in 2025 were $2.8 million, up 1.2 percent ($0.03 million) from 2024. U.S. goods imports from Lesotho totaled $170.2 million in 2025, down 28.2 percent ($67.0 million) from 2024. The U.S. goods trade deficit with Lesotho was $167.4 million in 2025, a 28.6 percent decrease ($67 million) over 2024.

U.S. services trade (exports plus imports) with Lesotho totaled an estimated $32.0 million in 2025. U.S. services exports to Lesotho in 2025 were $22.0 million, down 4.3 percent ($1.0 million) from 2024. U.S. services imports from Lesotho in 2025 were $10.0 million, down 16.7 percent ($2.0 million) from 2024. The U.S. services trade surplus with Lesotho was $12 million in 2025, unchanged from 2024.
