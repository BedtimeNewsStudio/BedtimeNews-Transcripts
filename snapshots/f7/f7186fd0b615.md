    India

    Urban: Statutory places with a municipality, corporation, cantonment board or notified town area committee, and places satisfying all three of the following criteria: 5,000 inhabitants or more, at least 75 per cent of the male working population engaged in non-agricultural pursuits, and at least 400 inhabitants per square kilometre.

02. [\[2\]](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/population-by-country/#population-note-ref-us-urban)

    United States

    Urban: Areas with at least 5,000 people, classified by housing unit density (425 housing units per square mile) rather than population density.

    Russia

    Urban: Settlements approved by legislative acts as cities and urban-type settlements (working, resort and summer cottages).

    Japan

    Urban: Cities (shi), in general municipalities with 50,000 or more inhabitants, 60 per cent or more of houses in the main built-up areas, and 60 per cent or more of the population (including dependants) engaged in manufacturing, trade or other urban types of business.

05. [\[5\]](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/population-by-country/#population-note-ref-germany-urban)

    Germany

    Urban: Localities with 2,000 or more inhabitants.

06. [\[6\]](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/population-by-country/#population-note-ref-kenya-urban)

    Urban: Built-up and compact settlements with a population of at least 2,000 people, and some trading centres with fewer than 2,000 people.
