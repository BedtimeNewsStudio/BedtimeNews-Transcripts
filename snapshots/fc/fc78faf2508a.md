# SAG-AFTRA Board Votes to Extend NED Duncan Crabtree-Ireland’s Contract; Updates Title to National Executive Director and Chief Executive Officer | SAG-AFTRA

[Skip to main content](#main-content)

##### https://www.sagaftra.org/sag-aftra-board-votes-extend-ned-duncan-crabtree-irelands-contract-updates-title-national-executive

![](https://www.sagaftra.org/sites/default/files/styles/thumbnail/public/2025-04/APP_News_v2.jpg?itok=Ub-f95Fm)

October 24, 2026

The SAG-AFTRA National Board met today and overwhelmingly voted to extend National Executive Director Duncan Crabtree-Ireland's contract through June 20, 2032. Crabtree-Ireland’s current contract was originally set to expire June 21, 2028.

The extended term provides for added stability and continuity for the union during a time of unprecedented industry disruption.

Additionally, Crabtree-Ireland’s title has been updated to include chief executive officer to better reflect the breadth of his responsibilities. He continues as chief negotiator for the union.

The agreement was approved with a 98.34% yes vote. Details of the agreement are confidential and were not disclosed.

SAG-AFTRA President Sean Astin said, “To battle giant global transnational corporations, we need expert leadership with a track record of successful bargaining and significant operational management expertise. That is Duncan. He is a powerful executive who has led our union at the bargaining table, in the halls of congress and in boardrooms and conference rooms across the industry and the world. We are thrilled to have guaranteed Duncan's continued service to the members of SAG-AFTRA.”

Crabtree-Ireland said, “Leading SAG-AFTRA’s staff is the honor of my career, and I’m grateful for the opportunity to keep doing this work. As we continue fighting for human creativity, the coming years will be crucial for charting the path forward. I intend to fight alongside our leadership to make sure our members control their image, likeness, voice, and destiny in the workplace, and that they are compensated fairly in the process. I thank President Astin, the national officers, and the National Board for their trust, and our extraordinary staff team for everything they make possible.”

### About SAG-AFTRA

SAG-AFTRA represents approximately 160,000 actors, announcers, broadcast journalists, dancers, DJs, news writers, news editors, program hosts, puppeteers, recording artists, singers, stunt performers, voiceover artists and other entertainment and media professionals. SAG-AFTRA members are the faces and voices that entertain and inform America and the world. A proud affiliate of the AFL-CIO, SAG-AFTRA has national offices in Los Angeles and New York and local offices nationwide representing members working together to secure the strongest protections for entertainment and media artists into the 21st century and beyond.
