# 2023 Hugo Awards | The Hugo Award

[Skip to content](#content)

[![](https://www.thehugoawards.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/HugoHeaderv2.jpg)](https://www.thehugoawards.org/)

[![2023 Hugo Award Trophy](https://www.thehugoawards.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/2023HugoAwardTrophySmall-e1709099086152.jpg)](https://www.thehugoawards.org/2023-hugo-award-trophy/)**Presented at:** [2023 Chengdu Worldcon](https://en.chengduworldcon.com/), Chengdu, China on October 21, 2023

**Hosts:** Zhou Weiyi, Yuan Li, Li Bo

**Base design:** Liu Cheng

**Lodestar trophy design:** Sara Felix

**Awards Administration:** Dave McCarty, Ben Yalow, Ann Marie Rudolph, Diane Lacey, Shi Chen, Joe Yao, Tina Wang, Dongsheng Guo, Bo Pang

1847 nominating ballots (1843 electronic and 4 paper) were received and counted from members of the 2022 and 2023 World Science Fiction Conventions for the 2023 Hugo Awards. 1674 final ballots were received. The nominating and final ballot statistics are [here](https://www.thehugoawards.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/2023-Hugo-Award-Stats-Final.pdf) (PDF).

The names of the winners are highlighted below. Currently, the other finalists are listed in the order they were listed on the original final ballot, not in the order of finish after the final ballot.

Please direct all questions about the administration of the 2023 Hugo Awards/Lodestar Award/ _Astounding_ Award to the [2023 Hugo Award Administrators](mailto:hugoteam@chengduworldcon.com), not to the Hugo Awards web site. The Hugo Awards web site does not administer the Hugo Awards. All questions about the administration of the current Awards should go to the administrators.
