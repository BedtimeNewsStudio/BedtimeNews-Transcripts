## Female labor force participation rate, 2025:

The average for 2025 based on 38 countries was 55.55 percent. The highest value was in Iceland: 70.01 percent and the lowest value was in Turkey: 37.15 percent. The indicator is available from 1990 to 2025. Below is a chart for all countries where data are available.   
  
 Measure: percent; Source: [The World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/)
