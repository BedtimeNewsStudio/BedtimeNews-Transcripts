**Ng Chee Peng** [PPA(P)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingat_Pentadbiran_Awam_(Tentera) "Pingat Pentadbiran Awam (Tentera)") [LOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Merit "Legion of Merit") is a Singaporean civil servant and former two-star [rear-admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear-admiral "Rear-admiral") who served as [Chief of Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Navy_(Singapore) "Chief of Navy (Singapore)") between 2011 and 2014.

## Education

Ng was awarded the [President's Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President's_Scholar "President's Scholar") and [Singapore Armed Forces Overseas Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Armed_Forces_Overseas_Scholarship "Singapore Armed Forces Overseas Scholarship") in 1989. He graduated from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford") with a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts "Bachelor of Arts") with first class honours degree in [philosophy, politics and economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy,_Politics_and_Economics "Philosophy, Politics and Economics") (PPE).

He was awarded the Singapore Armed Forces Postgraduate Scholarship (General Development) in 2002, before going on to complete a [Master of Public Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Public_Administration "Master of Public Administration") degree at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University "Harvard University").[&#8202;](#ref-back-SAF_Leadership-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-SAF_Leadership)

## Career

### Military career

Ng enlisted in the [Singapore Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Armed_Forces "Singapore Armed Forces") (SAF) in December 1988 and served in the [Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Singapore_Navy "Republic of Singapore Navy"). He spent his early days with the Missile Gun Boats, first as a junior officer and later as the Executive Officer on board the RSS _Sea Tiger_. He was also the Anti-Submarine Warfare Officer on board the RSS _Valiant_. He assumed his first command on board the Missile Corvette RSS _Victory_. He also held various staff appointments in the headquarters of the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_(Singapore) "Ministry of Defence (Singapore)") (MINDEF), including: Head, Operations Planning Branch, Naval Operations Department; Head, Long Term Planning Secretariat, Office of the Chief of Defence Force.[&#8202;](#ref-back-SAF_Leadership-1)[\[1\]](#refnote-SAF_Leadership)

After returning from his studies in Harvard University in 2002, Ng was appointed as the Commanding Officer of the Missile Corvette Squadron in July 2003, and concurrently served as the Commander of the First Flotilla from January 2004. He held the following appointments later: Director (Policy), MINDEF (2005–2007); Fleet Commander (2007–2009); Chief of Staff, Naval Staff (2009–2010); Chief of Staff, Joint Staff (2010–2011). Ng succeeded [Chew Men Leong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chew_Men_Leong "Chew Men Leong") as the Chief of Navy on 29 March 2011[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) and was promoted to the rank of two-star [rear-admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear-admiral "Rear-admiral") on 1 July.[&#8202;](#ref-back-SAF_Leadership-2)[\[1\]](#refnote-SAF_Leadership)

Ng stepped down from his position as the Chief of Navy on 1 August 2014 and was succeeded by [Lai Chung Han](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai_Chung_Han "Lai Chung Han").[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3)

### Public career

Ng was appointed as the chief executive officer of the [Central Provident Fund Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Provident_Fund "Central Provident Fund") on 15 March 2015. Prior to that, he served as Deputy Secretary (Special Projects) at the [Ministry of Manpower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Manpower_(Singapore) "Ministry of Manpower (Singapore)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Ng_Chee_Peng_appointed_new_chief_executive_officer_of_CPF_board-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-Ng_Chee_Peng_appointed_new_chief_executive_officer_of_CPF_board) Ng stepped down as the chief executive officer of the CPF Board on 1 April 2019.[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5)

Ng remained a senior advisor at the MOM and CPF Board, as well as on the board committee for transformation at [Public Utilities Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Utilities_Board_(Singapore) "Public Utilities Board (Singapore)"), as of March 2021. He was appointed as a non-executive and independent director of Great Eastern Holdings.[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6)

## Personal life

Ng has two elder brothers. [Ng Chee Khern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ng_Chee_Khern "Ng Chee Khern"), a former [major-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major-general "Major-general") who served as [Chief of Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Singapore_Air_Force#List_of_chiefs_of_Air_Force "Republic of Singapore Air Force") between 2006 and 2009. [Ng Chee Meng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ng_Chee_Meng "Ng Chee Meng"), a politician and [lieutenant-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant-general "Lieutenant-general") who served as [Chief of Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Singapore_Air_Force#List_of_chiefs_of_Air_Force "Republic of Singapore Air Force") between 2009 and 2013, [Chief of Defence Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_Force_(Singapore) "Chief of Defence Force (Singapore)") between 2013 and 2015, and [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Singapore "Parliament of Singapore") (MP) for [Pasir Ris–Punggol GRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasir_Ris%E2%80%93Punggol_GRC "Pasir Ris–Punggol GRC") between 2015 and 2020.[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7) Ng and his brothers are [Chinese Singaporean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Singaporean "Chinese Singaporean") of [Teochew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_people "Teochew people") descent.[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8)

Ng is married to Valerie Low.[&#8202;](#ref-back-SAF_Leadership-3)[\[1\]](#refnote-SAF_Leadership)
