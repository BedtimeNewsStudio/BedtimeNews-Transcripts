Société québécoise du cannabis

Quebec Cannabis Corporation

The **Société québécoise du cannabis** ( **SQDC**; lit. Quebec Cannabis Corporation) is the [Crown corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_corporations_of_Canada "Crown corporations of Canada") which operates the [legal monopoly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_monopoly "Legal monopoly") on [recreational cannabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recreational_cannabis "Recreational cannabis") sales in the Canadian province of [Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec "Quebec").

SQDC is incorporated as a subsidiary of the [Société des alcools du Québec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9_des_alcools_du_Qu%C3%A9bec "Société des alcools du Québec") (SAQ). It was tabled in the [National Assembly of Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Quebec "National Assembly of Quebec") on November 16, 2017, and was officially adopted on June 12, 2018.[&#8202;](#ref-back-cannabis_law,_ctv-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-cannabis_law,_ctv)[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2)

## Financing

Under the Act respecting the Société des alcools du Québec,[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3) the SQDC's revenues from the sale of cannabis must be used for the following purposes:

- The elimination of any deficit that the SQDC may incur;
- The transfer to be made by the Minister of Finance each year to the Cannabis Prevention and Research Fund, and also the prevention of the use of psychoactive substances, as well as the fight against related harms.\[ _[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_\]
