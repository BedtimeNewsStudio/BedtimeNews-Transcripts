## Summary of the Results - Current Population Estimates as of October 1, 2024

### Population for Japan

**[Total Population]**

* The total population was 123,802 thousand, a decrease of 550 thousand compared with the previous year. The rate of decrease was 0.44 percent. The total population decreased for the fourteenth year in a row.
* The male population was 60,233  thousand, a decrease of 260  thousand (0.43 percent), while the female population was 63,569  thousand, a decrease of 290   thousand (0.45 percent).
* The natural change of the male population was negative for the twentieth year in a row, and that of the female population was negative for the sixteenth year in a row.
* The migration change of the Japanese population was negative for the first time in two years, and that of the foreign population was positive for the three years in a row.

**[Population by Age Group]**

* The population under 15 years  old was 13,830 thousand (11.2  percent of the total population).
* The population aged 15 to 64 was 73,728 thousand (59.6 percent of the total population).
* The population aged 65 years old and over was 36,243 thousand (29.3 percent of the total population).

### Population by Prefecture

**[Population]**

* The top five prefectures in population were Tokyo-to, Kanagawa-ken, Osaka-fu, Aichi-ken and Saitama-ken. These prefectures account for 37.9 percent of the total population.
* Tokyo-to had the largest population in 2024 (11.5 percent of the total population).

**[Rates of Population Change]**

* The population increased in Tokyo-to and Saitama-ken. Tokyo-to had the highest rate of increase in population.
* The population decreased in 45 prefectures. Akita-ken had the highest rate of decrease in population.

**[Population by Age Group]**

* The percentage of the population under 15 years old was the highest in Okinawa-ken and lowest in Akita-ken.
* The percentage of the population aged 15 to 64 was the highest in Tokyo-to and lowest in Akita-ken.
* The percentage of the population aged 65 years old and over was the highest in Akita-ken and lowest in Tokyo-to, and it increased in 44 prefectures.
