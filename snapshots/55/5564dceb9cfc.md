# Weekly schedule of President Charles Michel

**Sunday 4 April 2021**

**Tripoli –Libya**

13.30 Meeting with President of the Presidential Council Mohamed Yunus al-Menfi

15.00 Meeting with Prime Minister Abdul Hamid Mohammed Al-Dabaiba and with Minister for Foreign Affairs Najla Mangoush

**Monday 5 April 2021**

**Tunis – Tunisia (local time)**

11.00 Meeting with President Kais Saied

12.30 Working lunch hosted by President Kais Saied

**Tuesday 6 April 2021**

**Ankara – Turkey (local time)**

10.00 Roundtable with representatives from IOM, UNHCR, UNICEF, UN Women

13:30 Meeting with Turkey President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and EU Commission President Ursula von der Leyen

17.00 Joint press conference with EU Commission President Ursula von der Leyen

**Thursday 8 April 2021** 12.30 Meeting with Deputy Prime minister and minister for Foreign Affairs of Belgium Sophie Wilmès and Louise Mushikiwabo, Secretary-General of the International Organisation of La Francophonie (OIF)
