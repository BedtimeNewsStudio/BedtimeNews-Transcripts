# Press release, CRPF — English release (PDF page 1)

## 第1页

Press release,  CRPF
CRPF lost forty men in uniform in the dastardly terror attack. As a mark of
solidarity and respect to the family of  40 CRPF brave Jawans who attained
martyrdom in a terror attack at Pulwama on 14/02/2019, North Eastern
Sector, CRPF has decided not celebrate the festival of Color Holi this year.
The Officers and Men of CRPFvisited at Mother TeresaMissionaries of
Charity Home in Shillong and distributed sweets among the people. The IGP
Sh. Prakash. D said that this is a true tribute to the departed souls.
