**Nimrod Aloni** ( [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language "Hebrew language"): נמרוד אלוני; born 12 July 1973) is a major-general in the [Israeli Defence Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Defence_Force "Israeli Defence Force").[\[1\]](#ref-ground)

He has held a number of important posts in the IDF, including commander of the [Depth Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depth_Corps "Depth Corps"), commander of the [143rd 'Gaza' Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Division "Gaza Division"), and led operations in the [Ramallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramallah "Ramallah") area.[\[1\]](#ref-ground)

## Biography

Aloni was born in the [Ein Karem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ein_Karem "Ein Karem") village on the outskirts of [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem "Jerusalem").\[ _[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_\] In November 1991, he enlisted in the IDF, volunteering for the [35th Paratroopers Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paratroopers_Brigade "Paratroopers Brigade") where he was appointed as a commander after completing an infantry commander course. He continued in various commander positions until June 2002 when he was promoted to Major General of the [Central Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Command_(Israel) "Central Command (Israel)").[\[2\]](#ref-:1)

In 2012, at the end of his term as the commander of the Samaria Regional Brigade, he took responsibility for the failures that led to the deaths of 5 members of the [Fogel family during an attack on their Itamar home in March 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Itamar_attack "2011 Itamar attack").[\[3\]](#ref-3)

In 2017 he was appointed the [Depth Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depth_Corps "Depth Corps") chief of staff, and in July 2019, he was appointed commander of the [143rd 'Gaza' Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Division "Gaza Division").[\[2\]](#ref-:1) On 16 July 2023 an exchange ceremony was held in which Aloni assumed the office of commander of the Depth Corps, replacing outgoing Major General [Itai Veruv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itai_Veruv "Itai Veruv").[\[4\]](#ref-4)

In 2023, on the day of the [October 7 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_7_attacks "October 7 attacks"), [Hamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas "Hamas") falsely proclaimed to have captured Aloni as a prisoner.[\[5\]](#ref-5)[\[6\]](#ref-telegraph) An IDF spokesperson denied the claims, and the claim was proven false with an IDF video on 8 October showing Aloni speaking with other Israeli military officials.[\[7\]](#ref-:0)
