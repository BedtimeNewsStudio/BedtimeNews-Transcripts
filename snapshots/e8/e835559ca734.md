Circa Date

Oct 1962

Description

The first U-2 photographs of Soviet missiles in Cuba were taken on October 14 and include the one identified on October 15 by the National Photographic Interpretation Center (NPIC) as showing Soviet medium-range ballistic missiles (MRBM) near Los Palacios at San Cristobal and another showing a second MRBM site nearby, subsequently named San Cristobal no. 1.

[![ First U-2 photo Cuba missiles 1962](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/styles/small/public/2022-10/first-u-2-photo-cuba-missiles-1962.jpg?itok=NpsvzfHW)](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/first-u-2-photo-cuba-missiles-1962.jpg)

_October 14, 1962: U-2 photograph of a truck convoy approaching a deployment of Soviet MRBMs near Los Palacios at San Cristobal. This photograph was the first one identified by NPIC on October 15 as showing Soviet medium-range ballistic missiles in Cuba._

[![Second U-2 photo Cuba missiles 1962](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/styles/small/public/2022-10/second-u-2-photo-cuba-missiles-1962.jpg?itok=GRhQU2b8)](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/second-u-2-photo-cuba-missiles-1962.jpg)

_October 14, 1962: U-2 photograph of MRBM site two nautical miles away from the Los Palacios deployment—the second set of MRBMs found in Cuba. This site was subsequently named San Cristobal no. 1 (the photo is labeled October 15 for the day it was analyzed and printed)._

[![CIA reference photo SS-4 missile in Red Square](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/styles/small/public/2022-10/cia-reference-photo-ss-4-missile-in-red-square.jpg?itok=Kpwt4cdE)](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/cia-reference-photo-ss-4-missile-in-red-square.jpg)

_CIA reference photograph of Soviet medium-range ballistic missile (SS-4 in U.S. documents, R-12 in Soviet documents) in Red Square, Moscow._

[![U-2 pilot Richard Heyser at White House Oct 1962](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/styles/small/public/2022-10/u-2-pilot-richard-heyser-at-white-house-oct-1962.jpg?itok=1FiPgMeS)](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/u-2-pilot-richard-heyser-at-white-house-oct-1962.jpg)

_U-2 pilot Richard Heyser (second from left), who flew the October 14 mission, waits to meet President Kennedy at the White House on October 30, 1962, with Col. Ralph Steakley (left), fellow U-2 pilot Joe O'Grady, and Air Force Chief of Staff Gen. Curtis LeMay (right). (Source: JFK Library, White House Photographs, Robert Knudsen.)_

Source

Dino Brugioni Collection at the National Security Archive and JFK Library, White House Photographs, Robert Knudsen for the last photo.
