**Anna Paulina Luna** (née **Mayerhofer**; born May 6, 1989) is an American politician and [Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force "United States Air Force") veteran serving as the [U.S. representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._representative "U.S. representative") for [Florida's 13th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida's_13th_congressional_district "Florida's 13th congressional district") since 2023. A member of the [Republican Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_(United_States) "Republican Party (United States)"), she is the first [Mexican-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican-American "Mexican-American") woman elected to Congress from Florida.

Luna served as airfield management specialist in the [Air National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_National_Guard "Air National Guard") from age 19, serving from 2009 to 2014. For her service, she was awarded the Air Force Achievement Medal.[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1)
Luna graduated from the [University of West Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_West_Florida "University of West Florida") in 2017. In her first campaign, Luna challenged, and lost to, incumbent Democratic congressman [Charlie Crist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Crist "Charlie Crist") in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida#District_13 "2020 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida"). Luna was subsequently elected to the U.S. House of Representatives in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida#District_13 "2022 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida"), with the endorsement of then-former president [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump "Donald Trump") in the Republican primary. Luna was re-elected in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida#District_13 "2024 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida").

Luna serves on the House Committee on Foreign Affairs and the House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.

## Early life and family

Anna Paulina Mayerhofer was born on May 6, 1989, to George Mayerhofer and Monica Todd, an elementary school [teacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacher "Teacher") and stay-at-home mother, in [Santa Ana, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Ana,_California "Santa Ana, California").[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Cramer_2022-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-Cramer_2022)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)[&#8202;](#ref-back-time-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-time) Luna has said that her father raised her to follow [Messianic Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messianic_Judaism "Messianic Judaism"), an evangelical movement that incorporates Jewish traditions into Protestant Christianity, and that she is "a small fraction [Ashkenazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi "Ashkenazi")".[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-1)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Deutch_2022-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-Deutch_2022)[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7)[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8) Members of her extended family have denied this claim, saying her father was Catholic, and that "they were not aware of him practicing any form of Judaism while Luna was growing up".[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-2)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) Her mother has said that Luna's father was a Christian who "embraced the Messianic faith" after getting clean from drug addiction. Her grandfather, Heinrich Mayerhofer, identified as Catholic when he immigrated to Canada from Germany in 1954.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-3)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9)

Her father had Mexican and German ancestry. Her paternal grandmother was born in [Hidalgo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidalgo_(state) "Hidalgo (state)"), Mexico. Her paternal grandfather was born in Germany and was in the [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht "Wehrmacht") during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-4)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) Her mother has Mexican-American ancestry,[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-5)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) although a maternal great-grandfather was the son of an [American immigrant to Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_immigration_to_Mexico "American immigration to Mexico") and a native of [Colima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colima "Colima"), Mexico.[&#8202;](#ref-back-10-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-10) Her two maternal great-grandfathers served in the [United States Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Armed_Forces "United States Armed Forces") during World War II.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-6)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)

Luna's parents never married or lived together;[&#8202;](#ref-back-time-1)[\[5\]](#refnote-time) her father had a drug addiction problem,[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-7)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) her mother married another man when Luna was nine years old but divorced four years later.[&#8202;](#ref-back-time-2)[\[5\]](#refnote-time) Luna was raised in the [Orange County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County,_California "Orange County, California") cities of [Santa Ana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Ana,_California "Santa Ana, California"), [Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvine,_California "Irvine, California"), and [Aliso Viejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliso_Viejo "Aliso Viejo"), as well as [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), and has called [Santa Monica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica "Santa Monica") her hometown.[&#8202;](#ref-back-11-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-11) She attended high school in Los Angeles.[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-1)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-8)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)

Luna said that at the age of 10, she found her father's bag of [meth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meth "Meth").[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-2)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay) Her maternal grandmother was a long-time heroin user and was HIV positive.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-9)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)[&#8202;](#ref-back-people-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-people) By 2020, she (Anna) and her mother (Monica) had chosen the Luna surname as "an homage" to that woman's maiden name.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-10)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)[&#8202;](#ref-back-people-1)[\[12\]](#refnote-people)

Luna has said that she "grew up in the welfare system",[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13) raised by her mother on government assistance with "no family to rely on", and was raised in a "broken home mentality".[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-11)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) Luna has said that both she and her mother lacked "a strong extended network of people" who could help care for them,[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-12)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) and that she had attended "over six high schools" before graduating.[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-3)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay) Luna's cousin has said, "The whole family kind of raised her—my dad was a part of her life when she was younger and we all kind of coddled her ... She was always a part of everything, all these family gatherings and activities". Luna's aunt said, "She had everything. What she needed and more ... And not only did \[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-13)[Luna's mother\] provide for her, but \[Luna's grandfather\] did, too."[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) Luna has disputed these accounts, saying that she "barely spent any time with them in her entire life". Luna's mother said that she (Monica) had to rely on welfare for periods of time, especially while she was putting herself through college at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine "University of California, Irvine"), and then at the [UCLA School of Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_School_of_Law "UCLA School of Law"), and that she was the only source of meaningful financial support for the family.[&#8202;](#ref-back-people-2)[\[12\]](#refnote-people)

Luna's campaign website biography says that throughout her childhood and teenage years, her father "spent time in and out of incarceration", and that she communicated with him "through letters to jail and collect calls". Luna's mother and aunt said that he served several short stints in jail for not paying child support. Luna's mother also said that he spent at least one year in jail for a drug-related charge.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-14)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) Luna's father was sentenced to [Orange County jail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County_Sheriff's_Department#Jails "Orange County Sheriff's Department") on at least five different occasions. He was convicted for crimes including carrying a loaded gun in public, violating probation, and driving with a suspended license. He was charged but not convicted for [assault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assault "Assault") and [battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_(crime) "Battery (crime)") and possession of illegal drugs.[&#8202;](#ref-back-time-3)[\[5\]](#refnote-time) The drug possession case indicated that Luna's father was in custody around the time of two court appearances. The case information did not specify the length of his confinement; the court proceedings lasted about three months.[&#8202;](#ref-back-people-3)[\[12\]](#refnote-people)

## Military service, education, and early career

Luna served in the [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Air_Force "U.S. Air Force") from 2009 to 2014 as an airfield management specialist, at [Whiteman Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whiteman_Air_Force_Base "Whiteman Air Force Base") in [Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri "Missouri") and [Hurlburt Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurlburt_Field "Hurlburt Field") in [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida "Florida"). Her decorations included the [Air Force Achievement Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Achievement_Medal "Air Force Achievement Medal"). She subsequently joined the [Oregon Air National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Air_National_Guard "Oregon Air National Guard").[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-4)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay)

While in the Air Force, she began studying at a local college.[&#8202;](#ref-back-time-4)[\[5\]](#refnote-time) She also modeled during this period, appearing in 2013's _[Sports Illustrated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Illustrated "Sports Illustrated")'s_ online site, and briefly worked as a [cocktail waitress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocktail_waitress "Cocktail waitress") in a [strip club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strip_club "Strip club").[&#8202;](#ref-back-time-5)[\[5\]](#refnote-time)[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14) She later appeared as a swimsuit model in _[Maxim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_(magazine) "Maxim (magazine)")_ magazine in 2014.[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-5)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay)[&#8202;](#ref-back-15-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-15)[&#8202;](#ref-back-16-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-16)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Cramer_2022-1)[\[3\]](#refnote-Cramer_2022) Luna gained a following as an [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram") influencer.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-15)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)

Luna said that she experienced a 2010 early morning home invasion by her landlord while stationed at [Whiteman Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whiteman_Air_Force_Base "Whiteman Air Force Base").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-16)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) Luna's roommate said she did not remember such an incident. Instead, the roommate recalled a daytime break-in when Luna was not home. A [Warrensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrensburg,_Missouri "Warrensburg, Missouri") Police Department report described the July 2010 incident as a "burglary not in progress".[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-17)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)

In 2017, Luna earned a Bachelor of Science in biology from the [University of West Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_West_Florida "University of West Florida").[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-6)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay)

Luna became the director of Hispanic engagement for [Turning Point USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turning_Point_USA "Turning Point USA") in 2018.[&#8202;](#ref-back-17-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-17) In a November 2018 [Fox News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_News "Fox News") segment, she compared [Hillary Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton "Hillary Clinton") to [herpes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herpes "Herpes"), leading the network to cut the segment short and host [Rick Leventhal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Leventhal "Rick Leventhal") and anchor [Arthel Neville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthel_Neville "Arthel Neville") to apologize to viewers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-18-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-18) In 2020, she appeared at a [We Build the Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Build_the_Wall "We Build the Wall") event as vice president of Bienvenido, an organization dedicated to conservative Hispanic outreach.[&#8202;](#ref-back-19-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-19)

### Elections

#### 2020

Main article: [2020 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida §District 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida#District_13 "2020 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida")

Luna decided to run for Congress in 2018. She entered the Republican primary for [Florida's 13th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida#District_13 "2020 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida") in September 2019, and [Matt Gaetz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Gaetz "Matt Gaetz") endorsed her in November 2019.[&#8202;](#ref-back-20-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-20) She was also endorsed by [Charlie Kirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Kirk "Charlie Kirk"), [Elise Stefanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elise_Stefanik "Elise Stefanik"), [Students for Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Students_for_Trump "Students for Trump"), and former mayor of [St. Petersburg, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg,_Florida "St. Petersburg, Florida"), [Bill Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Foster_(mayor) "Bill Foster (mayor)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-21)[&#8202;](#ref-back-22-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-22) In July 2020, she and her husband purchased a house in St. Petersburg, near [MacDill Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacDill_Air_Force_Base "MacDill Air Force Base"), where her husband was stationed.[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-7)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay) Luna won the Republican primary, but lost to incumbent Democrat [Charlie Crist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Crist "Charlie Crist") in the general election.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Solomon_20200818-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-Solomon_20200818)[&#8202;](#ref-back-24-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-24)

#### 2022

Main article: [2022 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida §District 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida#District_13 "2022 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida")

Luna was elected as the U.S. representative for [Florida's 13th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida's_13th_congressional_district "Florida's 13th congressional district") in the [2022 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida#District_13 "2022 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida"), defeating Democratic nominee Eric Lynn, a former senior advisor for [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama "Barack Obama"). Before the Republican primary, another Republican candidate privately threatened to have her assassinated by a hit squad.[&#8202;](#ref-back-25-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-25) In June 2021, Luna was granted a temporary [stalking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalking "Stalking") injunction against her primary opponent, William Braddock, after a friend of Luna's recorded Braddock threatening to make Luna "disappear" and claiming he had "access to a hit squad, too, Ukrainians and Russians". Braddock dropped out of the race after the judge granted the temporary injunction. The judge dismissed the request for a permanent injunction, saying she found one instance of harassment when the law required two.[&#8202;](#ref-back-26-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-26)

[Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump "Donald Trump") endorsed Luna,[&#8202;](#ref-back-27-0)[\[27\]](#refnote-27) and [Marjorie Taylor Greene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marjorie_Taylor_Greene "Marjorie Taylor Greene") campaigned for her in Florida.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Deutch_2022-1)[\[6\]](#refnote-Deutch_2022) She is the first [Mexican-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican-American "Mexican-American") woman elected to Congress from [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida "Florida").[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-28)

#### 2024

Main article: [2024 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida §District 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida#District_13 "2024 United States House of Representatives elections in Florida")

Luna was challenged in the general election by Whitney Fox, a moderate Democrat and former communications director for the Pinellas Suncoast Transit Authority.[&#8202;](#ref-back-29-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-29) Though seen as the only Florida house seat being in striking distance for Democrats, Luna won re-election with 55% of the vote, compared to Fox's 45%.[&#8202;](#ref-back-30-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-30)

### Tenure

On May 23, 2023, Luna co-sponsored resolutions to [impeach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_impeachment_in_the_United_States "Federal impeachment in the United States") Attorney General [Merrick Garland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrick_Garland "Merrick Garland"),[&#8202;](#ref-back-31-0)[\[31\]](#refnote-31) [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI "FBI") director [Christopher Wray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_A._Wray "Christopher A. Wray"),[&#8202;](#ref-back-32-0)[\[32\]](#refnote-32) [Secretary of Homeland Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_Homeland_Security "Secretary of Homeland Security") [Alejandro Mayorkas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Mayorkas "Alejandro Mayorkas"),[&#8202;](#ref-back-33-0)[\[33\]](#refnote-33) and U.S. attorney for D.C. [Matthew M. Graves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_M._Graves "Matthew M. Graves").[&#8202;](#ref-back-34-0)[\[34\]](#refnote-34)

### Agriculture

In March 2024, Luna led a letter signed by 10 House Republicans to the [House Agriculture Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_Agriculture_Committee "House Agriculture Committee") opposing the inclusion of the Ending Agriculture Trade Suppression (EATS) Act in the 2024 [farm bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farm_bill "Farm bill").[&#8202;](#ref-back-76-0)[\[76\]](#refnote-76) The EATS Act aimed to prevent state and local governments from enforcing regulations on the production of agricultural goods sold across state lines. The letter expressed concern that the legislation would negatively impact American farmers and cede influence over the U.S. agricultural system to entities owned by foreign adversaries, particularly China. It was noted by agricultural industry observers and advocacy organizations for signaling conservative Republican opposition to federal preemption of state-level [animal welfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_welfare "Animal welfare") laws such as [California's Proposition 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_California_Proposition_12 "2018 California Proposition 12").[&#8202;](#ref-back-77-0)[\[77\]](#refnote-77)[&#8202;](#ref-back-78-0)[\[78\]](#refnote-78)

On January 9, 2026, Luna claimed that [Christians were being persecuted in Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_religion_in_Ukraine "Freedom of religion in Ukraine").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Luna8015465-0)[\[102\]](#refnote-Luna8015465) She announced she would contact the [State Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Department "State Department").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Luna8015465-1)[\[102\]](#refnote-Luna8015465) Luna also proclaimed that the " [Vatican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_See "Holy See") has a responsibility to ensure that they are educating the international community on what is happening across the world, specifically with regard to [Christian persecution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persecution_of_Christians "Persecution of Christians")."[&#8202;](#ref-back-Luna8015465-2)[\[102\]](#refnote-Luna8015465) Luna based her opinion on a letter from [Ukrainian MPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_MP "Ukrainian MP") [Oleksandr Dubinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleksandr_Dubinsky "Oleksandr Dubinsky"), [Artem Dmytruk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artem_Dmytruk "Artem Dmytruk") and [Oleksandr Kunytskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleksandr_Kunytskyi "Oleksandr Kunytskyi"), in which they claimed that in Ukraine the "number of [political prisoners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_prisoner "Political prisoner") has already exceeded 42,000, along with the criminal prosecution of 400,000 Ukrainian men who refused to go to frontline and fight."[&#8202;](#ref-back-Luna8015465-3)[\[102\]](#refnote-Luna8015465) According to [Olha Stefanishyna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olha_Stefanishyna "Olha Stefanishyna"), the ambassador of Ukraine to the United States, there were no political prisoners in the entire [Ukrainian penitentiary system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisons_in_Ukraine "Prisons in Ukraine"), out of a total 34,600 inmates.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Luna8015465-4)[\[102\]](#refnote-Luna8015465)

#### Middle East

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6d/George_Simion_%26_Anna_Paulina_Luna.jpg/500px-George_Simion_%26_Anna_Paulina_Luna.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:George_Simion_&_Anna_Paulina_Luna.jpg) Luna with [Romanian presidential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Romanian_presidential_election "2025 Romanian presidential election") candidate [George Simion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Simion "George Simion") in June 2025

In 2023, Luna was among 47 Republicans to vote in favor of H.Con.Res. 21, which directed President [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden "Joe Biden") to remove U.S. troops from [Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria "Syria") within 180 days.[&#8202;](#ref-back-103-0)[\[103\]](#refnote-103)[&#8202;](#ref-back-104-0)[\[104\]](#refnote-104) That same year Luna was among 52 Republicans who voted in favor H.Con.Res. 30, which would remove American troops from Somalia.[&#8202;](#ref-back-105-0)[\[105\]](#refnote-105)[&#8202;](#ref-back-106-0)[\[106\]](#refnote-106)

#### Germany

In August 2026 Luna wrote a letter to the Committee for Justice of the _[Thüringer Landtag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Th%C3%BCringer_Landtag "Thüringer Landtag")_, the parliament of the German state [Thuringia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thuringia "Thuringia"), in reaction to the motion[&#8202;](#ref-back-107-0)[\[107\]](#refnote-107) by [The Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Left_(Germany) "The Left (Germany)") parliamentary group on initiating proceedings to ban the [Alternative for Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_for_Germany "Alternative for Germany") (AfD), the biggest [opposition party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opposition_party "Opposition party") in the [federal parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_Bundestag "21st Bundestag") and in multiple [state parliaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Parliaments_of_Germany "State Parliaments of Germany") as well as leading party in [opinion polling for the next German federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_for_the_next_German_federal_election "Opinion polling for the next German federal election"). Luna referred to the Western tradition of the free [constitutional state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_state "Constitutional state"), stating that "criticism of the government does not equate hostility toward the Constitution, nor a successful opposition with a threat to democracy". In the letter she expressed her impression, that there was a lack of "convincing evidence" that the AfD aims to "abolish the [free democratic basic order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_democratic_basic_order "Free democratic basic order")" as well as a lack of any realistic possibility that such a plan could be implemented" and that she rather has the impression that the [domestic intelligence agencies on state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Office_for_the_Protection_of_the_Constitution "State Office for the Protection of the Constitution") and [federal level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Office_for_the_Protection_of_the_Constitution "Federal Office for the Protection of the Constitution") are being instrumentalized against the AfD. She announced that, in the event of an AfD ban, she would speak to the [US Secretary of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_State "United States Secretary of State") and request a travel ban for all the [MPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_parliament "Member of parliament") who introduced the motion, as well as for those who support it. "Something like this must never happen in a [democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy "Democracy"), and it should not happen in this case either."[&#8202;](#ref-back-108-0)[\[108\]](#refnote-108)

## Personal life

Luna identifies as a [Messianic Jewish Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messianic_Judaism "Messianic Judaism").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-18)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b) She is married to Andrew Gamberzky, a [U.S. Air Force combat controller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force_Combat_Control_Team "United States Air Force Combat Control Team") whom she met while serving in the [United States Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force "United States Air Force").[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-9)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay) In 2019, she took her maternal grandmother's maiden name, Luna, to represent her Hispanic heritage.[&#8202;](#ref-back-TampaBay-10)[\[2\]](#refnote-TampaBay)[&#8202;](#ref-back-109-0)[\[109\]](#refnote-109) Her mother had also changed her own surname to Luna following a finalized divorce.[&#8202;](#ref-back-people-4)[\[12\]](#refnote-people) Gamberzky later served with the [Oregon Air National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Air_National_Guard "Oregon Air National Guard") but resigned after his application to be exempt from the [COVID-19 vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_vaccine "COVID-19 vaccine") mandate for religious reasons was rejected. Luna and Gamberzky subsequently sued the Department of Defense, alleging that Gamberzky's [First Amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Amendment "First Amendment") rights had been violated.[&#8202;](#ref-back-110-0)[\[110\]](#refnote-110)

In 2023 family members told _[The Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post "The Washington Post")_ that Luna's paternal grandfather, Heinrich Mayerhofer, served as a soldier in [Nazi Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany "Nazi Germany")'s military during the 1940s, and they disputed her claim of Jewish heritage, stating that her father was Catholic and that none of them remember him practicing Judaism.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Alemany_2023b-19)[\[4\]](#refnote-Alemany_2023b)[&#8202;](#ref-back-KATV-0)[\[111\]](#refnote-KATV) The _Post_ reviewed a photo of Mayerhofer in a Nazi uniform, which the [Simon Wiesenthal Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Wiesenthal_Center "Simon Wiesenthal Center") confirmed matched that of the [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht "Wehrmacht").[&#8202;](#ref-back-KATV-1)[\[111\]](#refnote-KATV) Luna's office declined to comment on the matter.[&#8202;](#ref-back-KATV-2)[\[111\]](#refnote-KATV)

Luna announced that she had given birth to a son on August 27, 2023.[&#8202;](#ref-back-112-0)[\[112\]](#refnote-112)

In mid-August 2025, Luna said on _[The Joe Rogan Experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Joe_Rogan_Experience "The Joe Rogan Experience")_ that she had heard testimony about non- [human beings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_being "Human being") on Earth. She said that "Congress has seen proof of 'interdimensional beings'", and that "I think that they can actually operate through the time spaces that we currently have".[&#8202;](#ref-back-Gustaf-0)[\[113\]](#refnote-Gustaf)[&#8202;](#ref-back-114-0)[\[114\]](#refnote-114) She was interviewed in _[The Age of Disclosure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Age_of_Disclosure "The Age of Disclosure")_, a 2025 documentary film about [UFOs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFOs "UFOs"), claiming that U.S. government programs were in place for the recovery of [alien technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraterrestrial_life "Extraterrestrial life") that had crashed on Earth.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Age_of_Disclosure-0)[\[115\]](#refnote-Age_of_Disclosure)
