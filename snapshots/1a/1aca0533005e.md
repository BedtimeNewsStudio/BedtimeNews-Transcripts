The **King of the Kingdom of Bahrain** ( [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language "Arabic language"): ملك مملكة البحرين_Malik Mamlakat al-Baḥrayn_) is the [monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarch "Monarch") and [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state "Head of state") of the [Kingdom of Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain "Bahrain"). The [House of Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Khalifa "House of Khalifa") has been the ruling family of the country since the [1783 Arab invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bani_Utbah_invasion_of_Bahrain "Bani Utbah invasion of Bahrain") that led to the end of [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zand_dynasty "Zand dynasty") rule in Bahrain. Between 1783 and 1971, the Bahraini monarch held the title of [hakim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakim_(title) "Hakim (title)"), and, from 1971 until 2002, the title of [emir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir "Emir"). On 14 February 2002, the emir of Bahrain, [Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamad_bin_Isa_Al_Khalifa "Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa"), declared Bahrain a kingdom and proclaimed himself the first king.[&#8202;](#ref-back-kingdom-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-kingdom) The king enjoys wide-ranging powers, which include appointing the [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Bahrain "Prime Minister of Bahrain") and the [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Bahrain "Cabinet of Bahrain"), holding supreme command over the [Defence Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_Defence_Force "Bahrain Defence Force"), chairing the [Higher Judicial Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary_of_Bahrain "Judiciary of Bahrain"), appointing the [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_(Bahrain) "National Assembly (Bahrain)")'s [upper house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consultative_Council_(Bahrain) "Consultative Council (Bahrain)") and dissolving its elected [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Representatives_(Bahrain) "Council of Representatives (Bahrain)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Fr_15.2.2011-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-Fr_15.2.2011)
