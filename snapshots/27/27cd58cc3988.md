# ITTF Executive Board Acknowledges CAS Termination Order on 2025 Presidential Election Appeal

15 Apr 2026

![](https://www.ittf.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/KV-ITTF.png)

Meeting online on 15 April 2026, the ITTF Executive Board acknowledged that the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) has formally terminated the proceedings regarding the outcome of the ITTF Presidential election of 27 May 2025 through a Termination Order, thereby bringing the legal process to a definitive close.

CAS’s Termination Order, issued on 14 April 2026, follows Mr. Khalil Al-Mohannadi and the Qatar Table Tennis Association withdrawing their appeal before the CAS on 7 January 2026. The appeal to CAS followed an earlier dismissal by the ITTF Tribunal in its decision of 1 September 2025.

The election of Ms. Petra Sörling as ITTF President for the term 2025-2029 stands confirmed at every level.

The ITTF welcomes this moment as an opportunity to look ahead. The Federation has noted with appreciation the new direction of the Qatar Table Tennis Association, and looks forward to a constructive and positive collaboration in the years to come.

It is also noted that the operationally independent Integrity Unit’s separate inquiry into misconduct surrounding the AGM of 27 May 2025 remains ongoing. It is conducted independently, in accordance with the applicable statutes.

The ITTF’s focus is now on its centenary year, delivering its full programme of events and celebrating one hundred years of table tennis together with the entire table tennis family, in a spirit of unity and looking resolutely ahead.
