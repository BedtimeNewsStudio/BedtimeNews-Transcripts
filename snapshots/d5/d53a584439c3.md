**Kaesang Pangarep** (born 25 December 1994) is an Indonesian politician, entrepreneur, and YouTuber. He is the third and youngest child of former Indonesian president [Joko Widodo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joko_Widodo "Joko Widodo"). In September 2023, he was appointed as chairman of the [Indonesian Solidarity Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_Solidarity_Party "Indonesian Solidarity Party") (PSI).[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1)

He is currently the owner of [Liga 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_1_(Indonesia) "Liga 1 (Indonesia)") football club [Persis Solo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persis_Solo "Persis Solo") along with [Erick Thohir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erick_Thohir "Erick Thohir").[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2)

## Early life and education

He graduated from [ACS International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Chinese_School#Anglo-Chinese_School_(International)_Singapore "Anglo-Chinese School") in [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore "Singapore") with an [International Baccalaureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IB_Diploma_Programme "IB Diploma Programme") diploma,[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3)[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5) and [Singapore University of Social Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_University_of_Social_Sciences "Singapore University of Social Sciences").[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6)

## Entertainment career

He became popular as a blogger called 'diary anak kampung' or 'mister kacang' in 2011. His writing style is considered creative and funny, which explores his personal life and his relation with his family.[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7)

Since 2016, Kaesang has been active on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube"), and many of his videos went viral because of his sense of humor. His YouTube [vlogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vlog "Vlog") became popular; as of May 2017, he had more than 270,000 subscribers. Because of Kaesang's influence, Jokowi created his own YouTube vlog called #JKWVLOG, which gained popularity after posting Jokowi's lunch with [King Salman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salman_of_Saudi_Arabia "Salman of Saudi Arabia").[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8) Kaesang also appeared in the Indonesian television show [_Mata Najwa_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mata_Najwa?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mata Najwa")\[ [id](https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mata%20Najwa "id:Mata Najwa")\] to explain his life as the son of Indonesia's president. In 2017, Kaesang released a single song called 'Bersatulah' to promote unity and tolerance among Indonesia's citizens.[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9)[&#8202;](#ref-back-10-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-10) Kaesang Pangarep made a cameo appearance in the Indonesian movie _[Cek Toko Sebelah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cek_Toko_Sebelah "Cek Toko Sebelah")_.[&#8202;](#ref-back-11-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-11)[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12)[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13)

## Business career

Kaesang is active in the [culinary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culinary_arts "Culinary arts") business, owning a chain of banana-centered outlets operating in several cities in Indonesia.[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14) He also created a clothing line, featuring images of tadpoles.[&#8202;](#ref-back-15-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-15) In addition, he is active as a marketer in a 2017-launched startup called Madhang, which works with culinary [SMEs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_and_medium-sized_enterprises "Small and medium-sized enterprises") and households.[&#8202;](#ref-back-16-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-16) In 2019, he co-founded GK HEBAT, a [business incubator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_incubator "Business incubator") focusing on SMEs in the culinary sector. He became the company's CEO. Among other investments, the firm took an 8 percent stake in a publicly listed frozen shrimp products company for Rp 92 billion (~USD 6.5 million).[&#8202;](#ref-back-17-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-17)

## Political career

### 2023 Depok mayoral election

Further information: [2024 Depok mayoral election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Depok_mayoral_election "2024 Depok mayoral election")

In 2023, Kaesang stated his intention to run as mayor of [Depok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depok "Depok") in the [2024 mayoral election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Depok_mayoral_election "2024 Depok mayoral election").[&#8202;](#ref-back-18-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-18)

### Indonesian Solidarity Party

The [Indonesian Solidarity Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_Solidarity_Party "Indonesian Solidarity Party") (PSI) voiced its support for Kaesang shortly afterwards, along with the [Gerindra Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerindra_Party "Gerindra Party").[&#8202;](#ref-back-19-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-19)[&#8202;](#ref-back-20-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-20) He officially became a member of PSI on 23 September 2023, and was appointed as party chairman two days later.[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-21)

### 2024 Indonesian general election

PSI took part in the [2024 Indonesian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Indonesian_general_election "2024 Indonesian general election") under Kaesang's leadership. The party failed to secure any seats in the national [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_(Indonesia) "House of Representatives (Indonesia)"), although they managed to secure over double the number of [Regional Houses of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_House_of_Representatives "Regional House of Representatives") seats won in 2019.[&#8202;](#ref-back-22-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-22)

### 2024 potential gubernatorial candidacy

Main articles: [2024 Indonesian local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Indonesian_local_elections "2024 Indonesian local elections") and [2024 Indonesian local election law protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Indonesian_local_election_law_protests "2024 Indonesian local election law protests")

After the general election, Kaesang was named as a potential candidate for mayoral elections in [Bekasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Bekasi_mayoral_election "2024 Bekasi mayoral election") and [Surabaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Surabaya_mayoral_election "2024 Surabaya mayoral election"), in addition to Depok.[&#8202;](#ref-back-23-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-23) He was also initially considered as a candidate for the [Jakarta gubernatorial election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Jakarta_gubernatorial_election "2024 Jakarta gubernatorial election"), but did not meet the age requirements – Kaesang would be 29 years and 11 months on the date of the election at 27 November 2024, while the minimum age was 30.[&#8202;](#ref-back-24-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-24) However, on 30 May 2024, the [Supreme Court of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Indonesia "Supreme Court of Indonesia") ruled that the age limitation applied to the date of taking office, not the date of election.[&#8202;](#ref-back-25-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-25)[&#8202;](#ref-back-26-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-26)

## Personal life

Further information: [Family of Joko Widodo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_of_Joko_Widodo "Family of Joko Widodo")

Kaesang married Indonesian model [Erina Gudono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erina_Gudono "Erina Gudono") in [Yogyakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogyakarta "Yogyakarta"), [Special Region of Yogyakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Region_of_Yogyakarta "Special Region of Yogyakarta"), on 10 December 2022.[&#8202;](#ref-back-27-0)[\[27\]](#refnote-27) Their first child, a daughter named Bebingah Sang Tansahayu, was born on 15 October 2024.[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-28)

## Controversies

### Blasphemy report

After creating a video responding to children chanting 'kill [Ahok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basuki_Tjahaja_Purnama "Basuki Tjahaja Purnama")' in a [Ramadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramadan "Ramadan") rally, Kaesang was reported to the police for blasphemy and hate speech.[&#8202;](#ref-back-29-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-29)[&#8202;](#ref-back-30-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-30) However, the police found no evidence of blasphemy and dropped the case.[&#8202;](#ref-back-31-0)[\[31\]](#refnote-31)

### Private Jet

In August 2024, Kaesang faced significant public scrutiny on social media for using a private jet while travelling to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States"). The incident was discovered by chance on social media, after an inquiry online regarding the cabin windows from Erina Gudono's post on social media.[&#8202;](#ref-back-32-0)[\[32\]](#refnote-32) Kaesang private jet usage were coincided with a period of widespread [Indonesian local election law protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Indonesian_local_election_law_protests "2024 Indonesian local election law protests") against the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_(Indonesia) "House of Representatives (Indonesia)") attempt to amend the regional law election in August 2024.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-0)[\[33\]](#refnote-:0) [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") reported that the amend is an effort to find a way around existing [Constitutional Court of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Indonesia "Constitutional Court of Indonesia") court decision[&#8202;](#ref-back-35-0)[\[a\]](#refnote-35) to uphold the current minimum age limit of 30-year-old for candidates, which pivotal for Kaesang candidacy in [2024 Central Java gubernatorial election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Central_Java_gubernatorial_election "2024 Central Java gubernatorial election").[&#8202;](#ref-back-36-0)[\[35\]](#refnote-36)

This public scrutiny made Erina in the public spotlight for her social media posts that are said to show off a luxurious lifestyle, with netizens called her behavior like [Marie Antoinette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Antoinette "Marie Antoinette").[&#8202;](#ref-back-37-0)[\[36\]](#refnote-37) Prior to her US trip, Erina announced that she had received a scholarship to study social policy at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania "University of Pennsylvania").[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-1)[\[33\]](#refnote-:0) The private jet is known to be [Gulfstream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulfstream_Aerospace "Gulfstream Aerospace") [G650ER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G650ER "G650ER") type with [tail number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_registration "Aircraft registration") N588SE, [FAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAA "FAA") registration shows that airplane belongs to [Garena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garena "Garena"), a holding company that in the past few years already have relationship with Kaesang and his brother, [Gibran Rakabuming Raka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibran_Rakabuming_Raka "Gibran Rakabuming Raka") in various development projects in [Surakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surakarta "Surakarta").[&#8202;](#ref-back-38-0)[\[37\]](#refnote-38)[&#8202;](#ref-back-39-0)[\[38\]](#refnote-39)

Kaesang then was reported to the [Corruption Eradication Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corruption_Eradication_Commission "Corruption Eradication Commission") (KPK) by Indonesian Anti-Corruption Society (MAKI).[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-2)[\[33\]](#refnote-:0) On 17 September 2024, Kaesang visited KPK, and told the media that he and his wife was hitching their friend on the jet.[&#8202;](#ref-back-40-0)[\[39\]](#refnote-40)

Kaesang's August 2024 is not the only trip, another video from the past were circulated where Kaesang and Erina went directly to a car after getting off from same N588SE private jet while bringing numerous shopping bags without going through proper airports customs procedure.[&#8202;](#ref-back-41-0)[\[40\]](#refnote-41)
