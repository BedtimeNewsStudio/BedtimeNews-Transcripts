**Environment Statistics**

**Natural Resource and Land**

*   Uganda has a total area of 241,553 square kilometres (sq. kms), of which 37,552 sq. kms are open water.
*   Forest area (Ha) has increased from 2.20 million hectares in 2010 to 2.48 million Hectares in 2021.
*   Agriculture area has increased by 16% percent in the same period from 9.9 million Hectares in 2010 to 11.5 million hectares in 2021
**Climate Change and Water resources**

*   Uganda received a bi-modal rainfall pattern in 2023. November recorded the highest amount of rainfall with 227.7 mm, followed by March with 219.5 mm
*   During 2023, Buginyanya ZARDI had the most rain days with an annual average of 144 days, followed by Bulindi ZARDI with an annual average of 136 days. Nabuin ZARDI had the least number of rain days with an annual average of 61 days, followed by Mbarara ZARDI with an annual average of 114 days
*   During the FY2023/24, water supplied by both NWSC and DWD was 169.4 million cubic meters compared to 167.3 million cubic meters supplied in the FY2022/23, hence an increase of about 1.3 percent, which is less than the increment registered between FY2021/22 and FY2022/23 (2.1%).
*   In FY2023/24, NWSC produced 175.5 million cubic meters of water, an increase of 2.8 percent from 170.8 million cubic meters in FY2022/23
*   In FY2022324, 5.8 million cubic meters of water were supplied, registering a 2 percent increase

*   The Manufacturing Sector (C),
*   Electricity Generation (D), and
*   Water Supply, Sewerage, Waste Management, and Remediation Activities (E).

**Agro-Industrialization – Fish Production**

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics is pleased to present the final report for Aquaculture Census (UAC) 2025. The report provides comprehensive information on the characteristics of the aquaculture sector in Uganda. The information will provide a basis for guided planning, decision-making and monitoring of the sector. **Aquaculture Farms** The UAC 2025 established a total of 9,463 aquaculture farms in the country between July 2024 and June 2025. Findings further indicate that 67.7 percent of the farms were in rural areas. By nature of production activities, 97.1 percent of the aquaculture farms practiced grow-out only, 2.1 percent practiced both hatchery and grow-out while 0.8 percent practiced hatchery only. **Fish Species Cultured** Tilapia, Catfish, and Mirror Carp were the most cultured species. Overall, 80.5 percent of the farms cultured Tilapia, 48.7 percent cultured Catfish, and 2.5 percent cultured Mirror Carp. **Production Methods used** Ponds were the dominant aquaculture production method, used by 92.3 percent of aquaculture farms, while 4.5 percent used cages and 2.1 percent used tanks. **Ponds:** A total number of 24,348 ponds were found, 70.3 percent of which were stocked. Majority (67.0%) of the ponds were in rural area. **Cages:** A total number of 3,135 cages were found, 58.3 percent of which were stocked. More than half (58.9%) of the cages were in urban area. **Tanks:** A total number of 699 tanks were found, 71.1 percent of the which were stocked. About 71.6 percent of the tanks were in urban areas. **Production** The total aquaculture production was 65,444.0 MT between July 2024 and June 2025. Findings further indicate that 47,050.7 MT (71.9%) of the total production was from farms in the urban areas. Of the total aquaculture production, 82.4 percent was Tilapia, 17.3 percent was Catfish, and 0.2 percent was Mirror Carp. **Main Purpose of Production** Nearly eight (8) in every ten farms culturing a species did it mainly for sale. More specifically; * i) Tilapia, 88.6 percent produced Mainly for Sale and 9.7 percent produced Only for Own Consumption. * ii) Catfish, 81.5 percent produced Mainly for Sale and 14.5 percent produced Only for Own Consumption and * iii) Mirror Carp, 77.3 percent produced Mainly for Sale and 21.7 percent produced Only for Own Consumption. **Disposition**: 87.8 percent of the total production was sold, 10.2 percent was consumed by the farmer, 1.6 percent processed, and 0.4 percent was lost. **Extension Services** Extension services were received by 53.3 percent of the aquaculture farms. There was no significant difference seen for farms in the rural and urban areas. View this link for more details,[https://statistics.ubos.org/aquaculture/](https://statistics.ubos.org/aquaculture/)
