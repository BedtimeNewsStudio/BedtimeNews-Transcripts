With all the talk of unmanned drones it's not surprising a retail company is trying to get a slice of the action.

A video of Domino's new "DomiCopter," a pizza-delivering multicopter drone, was [posted on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=on4DRTUvst0) and has received a lot of attention.

The one-minute video of the remote-controlled drone shows it wisp two pizzas into the air and deliver them to a customer.

According to [The Huffington Post](https://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/06/04/domicopter_n_3383815.html), the video was filmed in the UK as a marketing tool for the pizza chain and there no plans to actually use drones to deliver pizzas.

Currently, civilian companies may not operate a UAS as part of a business without obtaining a Special Airworthiness Certificate - Experimental Category (SAC-EC), [according to the FAA.](https://www.businessinsider.com/faa-ban-on-commercial-drones-2013-3)
