# Casey Wasserman Is Putting His Talent Agency Up for Sale After Epstein Fallout

## Wasserman has said he ‘never had a personal or business relationship with Jeffrey Epstein’ and told staff he felt that he had ‘become a distraction’ to the firm’s work

By

[Jessica Toonkel](https://www.wsj.com/news/author/jessica-toonkel)

Feb. 13, 2026 10:45 pm ET

[839](#comments_sector)

* * *

Listen

(2 min)

![Casey Wasserman](https://images.wsj.net/im-77710715?width=700&height=466)Casey WassermanPatrick R. Fallon/AFP/Getty Images

Casey Wasserman is putting his eponymous talent and marketing agency up for sale, he said in a memo to staff Friday night. The move follows the release of [suggestive emails](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/flirty-emails-and-chummy-photos-show-how-far-epstein-reached-into-business-world-5e150c7c?mod=article_inline) he exchanged years ago with [Jeffrey Epstein](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/jeffrey-epstein) associate Ghislaine Maxwell and the departure of high-profile talent from his firm.

Wasserman told staff he felt that he had “become a distraction” to the firm’s work and had begun the process of selling the company.
