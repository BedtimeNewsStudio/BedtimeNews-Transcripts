State Political Directorate

The **State Political Directorate** ( [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language "Russian language"): Государственное политическое управление, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian "Romanization of Russian"):_Gosudarstvennoe politicheskoe upravlenie_), abbreviated as **GPU** (ГПУ), was the [secret police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_police "Secret police") of the [Russian Socialist Federative Soviet Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Socialist_Federative_Soviet_Republic "Russian Socialist Federative Soviet Republic") (RSFSR) from February 1922 to November 1923. It was the immediate successor of the [Cheka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheka "Cheka"), and was replaced by the [Joint State Political Directorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_State_Political_Directorate "Joint State Political Directorate") (OGPU).

The full official designation was the State Political Directorate (also State Political Administration) under the [People's Commissariat for interior affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NKVD "NKVD") of the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic (Russian: Государственное политическое управление при Народном комиссариaте внутренних дел РСФСР, romanized:_Gosudarstvennoe politicheskoe upravlenie pri Narodnom komissariate vnutrennikh del RSFSR_; abbr.ГПУ при НКВД РСФСР, _GPU pri NKVD RSFSR_).

## Establishment and functions

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=State_Political_Directorate&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Establishment and functions")\]

Formed from the [Cheka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheka "Cheka"), the original Russian state security organization, on February 6, 1922, it was initially known under the Russian abbreviation **GPU**—short for "State Political Directorate under the [NKVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NKVD "NKVD") of the RSFSR" (Russian: Государственное политическое управление при НКВД РСФСР, romanized:_Gosudarstvennoe politicheskoe upravlenie pri NKVD RSFSR_). Its first chief was the Cheka's former chairman, [Felix Dzerzhinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Dzerzhinsky "Felix Dzerzhinsky").

### Internal security

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=State_Political_Directorate&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Internal security")\]

On paper, the new agency was supposed to act with more restraint than the Cheka. For example, unlike the Cheka, it did not have the right to shoot suspected " [counter-revolutionaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-revolutionaries "Counter-revolutionaries")" at will. All those suspected of political crimes had to be brought before a judge in normal circumstances.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Dictators-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-Dictators)

### Foreign intelligence

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=State_Political_Directorate&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Foreign intelligence")\]

The 'Foreign Department' of the GPU was headed by a former Bolshevik and party member, [Mikhail Trilisser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Trilisser "Mikhail Trilisser").[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) The Foreign Department was placed in charge of intelligence activities overseas, including espionage and liquidation of 'enemies of the people'. Trilisser himself was later liquidated by [Joseph Stalin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Stalin "Joseph Stalin") during the [Great Purge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Purge "Great Purge") in 1940.

## Reorganization

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=State_Political_Directorate&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Reorganization")\]

With the creation of the USSR in December 1922, a unified organization was required to exercise control over [state security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_security "National security") throughout the new union. Thus, on November 15, 1923, the GPU left the Russian NKVD and was reorganized as the all-union [Joint State Political Directorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_State_Political_Directorate "Joint State Political Directorate"). Its official name was "Joint State Political Directorate under the [Council of People's Commissars of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_People's_Commissars_of_the_USSR "Council of People's Commissars of the USSR")" (Russian: Объединённое государственное политическое управление при СНК СССР, romanized:_Obyedinyonnoe gosudarstvennoe politicheskoe upravlenie pri SNK SSSR_), or OGPU (ОГПУ).
