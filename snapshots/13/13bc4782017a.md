American software conglomerate

[Cloud Software Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_Software_Group) ( **CSG**) is an American software conglomerate formed by [Vista Equity Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vista_Equity_Partners "Vista Equity Partners") and Evergreen Coast Capital through the merger of [Citrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citrix "Citrix") and [TIBCO Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TIBCO_Software "TIBCO Software").

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cloud_Software_Group&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

On January 31, 2022, it was announced that Citrix had been acquired in a $16.5 billion deal by affiliates of [Vista Equity Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vista_Equity_Partners "Vista Equity Partners") and Evergreen Coast Capital. Citrix would merge with [TIBCO Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TIBCO_Software "TIBCO Software"), a Vista portfolio company, to form Cloud Software Group.[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2)[&#8202;](#ref-back-crn-csg-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-crn-csg) In July 2022, Citrix announced that [Tom Krause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Krause_(business_executive) "Tom Krause (business executive)") would succeed Robert Calderoni as CEO following the merger. It was also reported that Citrix would go private as part of the deal.[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Bloomberg_July2022-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-Bloomberg_July2022) As part of the merger, in September 2022, Citrix announced a $4.55 billion-equivalent cross-border term loan to back its $16.5 billion buyout by Vista Equity and Evergreen Coast Capital. Following the merger, the Citrix Hypervisor product-line was spun-off into a standalone business unit under Cloud Software Group and rebranded as XenServer.[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7)

In July 2022, Krause became chief executive officer of the Cloud Software Group. After Krause joined Cloud Software group it laid off 15 percent of its employees.[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8) A year later it laid off an additional 12 percent.[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9)

In January 2025, Krause became involved with the [U.S. Treasury Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Treasury_Department "U.S. Treasury Department") as part of the [Department of Government Efficiency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Government_Efficiency "Department of Government Efficiency") (DOGE). He was assigned as a [special government employee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_government_employee "Special government employee") to review the federal payment system and was granted limited access as a liaison between the Treasury and DOGE.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:2-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-:2)[&#8202;](#ref-back-11-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-11) On February 7, 2025, he was appointed as the [Fiscal Assistant Secretary of the Treasury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiscal_Assistant_Secretary_of_the_Treasury "Fiscal Assistant Secretary of the Treasury"), succeeding [Matthew Garber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Garber_(American_civil_servant) "Matthew Garber (American civil servant)"), who had been acting in the role since [David Lebryk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lebryk "David Lebryk")'s resignation.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:2-1)[\[10\]](#refnote-:2) Krause continued to work at Cloud Software Group despite his employment by DOGE, which has been cited as a potential conflict of interest.[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12)

In August 2025, Cloud Software acquired Arctera from [The Carlyle Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Carlyle_Group "The Carlyle Group"). Arctera held the product lines from the legacy [Veritas Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veritas_Technologies "Veritas Technologies") that The Carlyle Group did not sell to [Cohesity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohesity "Cohesity"). The portfolio includes [BackupExec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backup_Exec "Backup Exec"), InfoScale, and other data-management, -integrity, and -governance products.
