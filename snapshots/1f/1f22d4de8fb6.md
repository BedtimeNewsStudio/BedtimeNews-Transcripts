# El Salvador Poverty and Equity Brief : October 2026  (English)

According to official figures, poverty declined over the past two years, reversing the increase observed between 2019 and 2023. From 2023 to
2025, the share of households living below the national poverty line decreased by 4.7 percentage points to 22.5 percent. Extreme poverty
