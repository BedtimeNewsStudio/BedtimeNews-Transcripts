From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

(Redirected from [Advisory Committee on the Future of the U.S. Space Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Advisory_Committee_on_the_Future_of_the_U.S._Space_Program&redirect=no "Advisory Committee on the Future of the U.S. Space Program"))

Group established by Dan Quayle to study US human spaceflight's future

For the 2009 committee, see [Review of United States Human Space Flight Plans Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_of_United_States_Human_Space_Flight_Plans_Committee "Review of United States Human Space Flight Plans Committee").

The **Advisory Committee on the Future of the United States Space Program**, commonly known as the **Augustine Committee**, was a 1990 [space policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_policy "Space policy") group requested by Vice President [Dan Quayle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Quayle "Dan Quayle"), chairman of the [National Space Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Space_Council "National Space Council"). The objective of the committee was to evaluate the long-term future of [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA "NASA") and the United States civilian space program.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustine_Committee#cite_note-1) The committee's final report (known as the **Augustine Report**) recommended that the space program should comprise five activities— [space science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_science "Space science"), [Earth science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth_science "Earth science"), [human spaceflight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_spaceflight "Human spaceflight"), [space technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_technology "Space technology") and [space transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_transportation "Space transportation")—with space science as the highest priority for funding. It also proposed an unmanned launch vehicle to replace some [Space Shuttle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle "Space Shuttle") launches, and a scaled-back redesign of space station [Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Station_Freedom "Space Station Freedom").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustine_Committee#cite_note-2)

## Original recommendations

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Augustine_Committee&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Original recommendations")\]

In its original report, the committee ranked five space activities in order of priority:

1. Space science
2. Technology development
3. Earth science
4. Unmanned launch vehicle
5. Human spaceflight

At a dinner with Vice President Quayle and committee members, [Office of Management and Budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Management_and_Budget "Office of Management and Budget") director [Richard Darman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Darman "Richard Darman") argued that the low priority projects would be eliminated during the budget process. The committee members decided to change their report. Space science was still given first priority, but the other activities were assigned equal priority behind space science.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustine_Committee#cite_note-3)

After discussing the [Space Shuttle _Challenger_ disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Challenger_disaster "Space Shuttle Challenger disaster") the executive summary of the committee's report recommended, "saving the Space Shuttle for those missions requiring human presence."

## Members

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Augustine_Committee&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Members")\]

The committee had twelve members in total, with one chairman and one vice chairman.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustine_Committee#cite_note-4)

- [Norman Augustine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Augustine "Norman Augustine") (chairman), CEO of [Martin Marietta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Marietta "Martin Marietta")
- [Laurel L. Wilkening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurel_L._Wilkening "Laurel L. Wilkening") (vice chairman), provost of the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington "University of Washington")
- [Edward Aldridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Aldridge "Edward Aldridge"), president of the [McDonnell Douglas Electronic Systems Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonnell_Douglas_Electronic_Systems_Company?action=edit&redlink=1 "McDonnell Douglas Electronic Systems Company (page does not exist)")
- [Joseph Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_P._Allen "Joseph P. Allen"), former [astronaut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronaut "Astronaut") and president of [Space Industries International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Industries_International?action=edit&redlink=1 "Space Industries International (page does not exist)")
- [D. James Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._James_Baker "D. James Baker"), president of [Joint Oceanographic Institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Oceanographic_Institutions?action=edit&redlink=1 "Joint Oceanographic Institutions (page does not exist)")
- [Edward Boland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Boland "Edward Boland"), former Massachusetts congressman
- [Daniel Fink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Fink?action=edit&redlink=1 "Daniel Fink (page does not exist)"), former senior vice president of [General Electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Electric "General Electric")
- [Don Fuqua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Fuqua "Don Fuqua"), president of the [Aerospace Industries Association of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_Industries_Association_of_America?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aerospace Industries Association of America (page does not exist)")
- [Robert Herres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Herres "Robert Herres"), former commander of the [United States Space Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Space_Command "United States Space Command")
- [David Kearns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Kearns "David Kearns"), chairman of [Xerox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xerox "Xerox")
- [Louis Lanzerotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Lanzerotti "Louis Lanzerotti"), chairman of the [Space Studies Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Studies_Board "Space Studies Board")
- [Thomas Paine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_O._Paine "Thomas O. Paine"), former [NASA administrator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA_administrator "NASA administrator")
