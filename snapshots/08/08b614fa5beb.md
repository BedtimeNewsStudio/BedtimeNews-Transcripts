**Vicente Gonzales Duterte** (Tagalog pronunciation:[\[dʊˈtɛɾtɛ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Tagalog "Help:IPA/Tagalog"); November 23, 1911 – February 21, 1968), also known by his nicknames **Nene** and **Teti**, was a Filipino politician, lawyer, and father of [Rodrigo Duterte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigo_Duterte "Rodrigo Duterte"), the [16th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_the_Philippines "List of presidents of the Philippines") [president of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Philippines "President of the Philippines"). He was governor of the then-unified province of [Davao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_(province) "Davao (province)") in 1959, succeeding [Alejandro Almendras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Almendras "Alejandro Almendras") who was elected senator.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-1)

## Early life and career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Vicente_Duterte&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life and career")\]

Duterte was born in 1911 in [Danao, Cebu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danao,_Cebu "Danao, Cebu"), to Facundo Buot Duterte and Zoila Gonzáles from northern [Cebu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebu_island "Cebu island"), who also traces her roots in [Iloilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iloilo "Iloilo").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-mm-2) He had four siblings and a half-brother.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-bloodline-3)

Duterte was appointed mayor of [Danao, Cebu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danao,_Cebu "Danao, Cebu") in an acting capacity by President [Sergio Osmeña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Osme%C3%B1a "Sergio Osmeña") during the [liberation of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines_campaign_(1944%E2%80%931945) "Philippines campaign (1944–1945)") in 1945.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-5) He and his family moved back forth between [Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindanao "Mindanao") and [the Visayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visayas "Visayas"), finally settling in [Davao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_City "Davao City") in 1949. In 1959, Duterte was elected governor of [Davao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_(province) "Davao (province)"), succeeding his distant cousin and political ally [Alejandro Almendras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Almendras "Alejandro Almendras").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-6)

In 1965, Duterte was appointed [Secretary of General Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_the_Interior_and_Local_Government "Secretary of the Interior and Local Government") by President [Ferdinand Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos "Ferdinand Marcos"). He unsuccessfully ran for Congress in the [1967 special elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967_Philippine_House_of_Representatives_special_elections "1967 Philippine House of Representatives special elections") for the [lone district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_del_Sur's_at-large_congressional_district "Davao del Sur's at-large congressional district") of the newly-created province of [Davao del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_del_Sur "Davao del Sur").[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-7) While defending himself in a court case, Duterte collapsed and died of [heart failure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_failure "Heart failure") on February 21, 1968.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-8)

## Personal life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Vicente_Duterte&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Personal life")\]

Duterte was married to [Soledad Duterte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soledad_Duterte "Soledad Duterte"), a teacher whom he first met at the [Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_administration "Public administration") of [Public Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_school "State school"). The couple had five children: Eleanor, [Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigo_Duterte "Rodrigo Duterte"), Jocelyn, Emmanuel, and Benjamin. He was the paternal grandfather of [Representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_the_Philippines "House of Representatives of the Philippines") [Paolo Duterte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Duterte "Paolo Duterte"), [Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_Philippines "Vice President of the Philippines") [Sara Duterte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Duterte "Sara Duterte") and [Davao City Mayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Davao_City "Mayor of Davao City") [Sebastian Duterte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Duterte "Sebastian Duterte").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Duterte#cite_note-rafi-9)
