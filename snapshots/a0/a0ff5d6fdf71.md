# Apple announces the iPhone X with a 5.8" flexible Super AMOLED

![Share Story to LinkedIn](/img/icons/social/linkedin-icon.png)

Yesterday [Apple](https://www.oled-info.com/apple-oled) announced its new iPhone lineup which includes the flagship [iPhone X](https://www.oled-info.com/apple-iphone-x) that, as expected, features a 5.8" 1125x2436 (458 PPI) flexible [Super AMOLED](https://www.oled-info.com/super-amoled) display that covers almost the entire front of the phone. Apple's first OLED iPhone will ship in November starting at $999.

![Apple iPhone X photo](/sites/default/files/story/storyApple-iPhone-X-img_assist-400x322.jpg "Apple iPhone X photo")

![Apple iPhone X photo](/sites/default/files/story/storyApple-iPhone-X-img_assist-400x322.jpg "Apple iPhone X photo")

Some reports suggest that [SDC is only able to supply a limited number of displays to Apple](https://www.oled-info.com/digitimes-sdc-will-only-be-able-supply-3-4-million-flexible-oleds-apple-upon-iphone-8-launch) which will severely limit iPhone X shipments.
