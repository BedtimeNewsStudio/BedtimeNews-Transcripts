# Voters appear to reject Measure A in early returns

Homes in Pacific Beach. Photo: Kevin Carter/Getty Images

A majority of voters said "no" to the city's [vacant home tax](https://www.axios.com/local/san-diego/2026/05/13/san-diego-measure-a-non-primary-homes-tax-realtors-mailers) proposal, according to [early returns](https://www.livevoterturnout.com/ENR/sandiegocaenr/24/en/Index_24.html) Tuesday night.

**The big picture:** 58% of voters so far opposed Measure A, which would cost owners of second homes $8,000 annually in 2027 and $10,000 every year after.

- The city said the measure would bring in $9 million to $21 million each year to the [general fund](https://www.axios.com/local/san-diego/2026/04/15/san-diego-mayor-gloria-draft-city-budget-2027-arts-police-fire-spending).

**If the measure fails,** the city will have to do without, and find other ways to continue to make up its [budget deficit](https://www.axios.com/local/san-diego/2026/04/15/san-diego-mayor-gloria-draft-city-budget-2027-arts-police-fire-spending), which sits at $146 million next fiscal year.

**Follow the money:** Over the past few months, the measure drew sizable spending from outside organizations that opposed it.

- The [California Association of Realtors](https://www.car.org/) shelled out more than $1 million to defeat the measure, according to [campaign filings](https://efile.sandiego.gov/public/search/campaign?query_string=&state_id=&start_date=2025-06-02&end_date=2026-06-02&filing_types=fppc400,fppc401,fppc402,fppc410,fppc425,fppc450,fppc460,fppc461,fppc465,fppc470,fppc495,fppc496,fppc497,fppc497lcr,fppc497lcm,fppc498,fppc501,EC:PEC-DPM&committee_types=Candidate%20or%20Officeholder,Primarily%20Formed%20Candidate,Primarily%20Formed%20Ballot%20Measure,General%20Purpose,Major%20Donor,Independent%20Expenditure,470%20Officeholder,470%20Person,Slate%20Mailer&current_page=undefined&page_size=undefined).

**Zoom in:** The measure would apply to about 5,100 homes locally, Councilmember Sean Elo-Rivera told Axios last month.

- It would exempt some situations, like when someone has moved into a care facility or is away for military service.

**Meanwhile,** other races across the region are narrowing down to two candidates who will advance to a November runoff. In early returns:

- **City Council [District 2](https://www.axios.com/local/san-diego/2026/05/22/san-diego-district-2-council-race-police-budget-city-services-deficit):** Richard Bailey had 39% of the vote, and Nicole Crosby 31%. Josh Coyne trailed with 11% of the vote.
- **City Council [District 8](https://www.axios.com/local/san-diego/2026/05/26/san-diego-district-8-election-candidates-sdge-housing-budget):** Antonio Martinez led with 29% of the vote, but less than 300 votes separated the two candidates vying for second place, Gerardo Ramirez and Venus Molina.
- **[48th Congressional District](https://www.axios.com/local/san-diego/2025/10/09/how-prop-50-could-change-elections-in-san-diego):** Republican Jim Desmond and Marni von Wilpert advanced to the November election, nabbing 42% and 20% of the vote, respectively.

**Caveat:** The Registrar of Voters will continue to count ballots throughout the week, so numbers could change. The next big vote count update will come Thursday at 6pm.
