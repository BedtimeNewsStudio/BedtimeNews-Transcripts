Although the football-league season has not officially concluded, Sassuolo Calcio has mathematically secured its promotion to Serie A. MAPEI proudly celebrates this remarkable achievement for the Black & Green team, which, after a successful season, is set to return to Italy's top football league following just one year in Serie B.
