# Scores of BJP workers quit party in Valley following Bari’s murder

![Scores of BJP workers quit party in Valley following Bari’s murder](https://static.toiimg.com/thumb/msid-47529300,imgsize-110164,width-400,resizemode-4/47529300.jpg)

[SRINAGAR](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/topic/srinagar): Scores of BJP workers in [Kashmir](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/topic/article-35a) have quit the party in the last one week following the brutal murder of prominent party functionary Waseem Bari by terrorists in Bandipora on July 8. Party insiders said it all started with the resignation of Baramulla Bharatiya Janata Yuva Morcha (BJYM) president Marouf Bhat on July 11, followed by [Kupwara](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/topic/kupwara-attack) district vice president Asif Ahmad on July 14.

![1x1 polls](https://static.toiimg.com/photo/25581306.cms)

Though none of these functionaries and party workers has ascribed their move to fear of terrorists, the string of resignations is widely being seen to have been triggered by the fear factor.

While The Resistance Front, an amalgam of [Lashkar-e-Taiba](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/topic/lashkar-e-taiba), Jaish-e-Mohammad and Hizbul Mujahedeen, had claimed responsibility for killing Waseem Bari along with his father and brother, terror outfits Tahreek-ul-Mujhadeen and Al Badr had issued a warning to all BJP leaders and workers in Kashmir to quit the party or else meet the same fate as Bari’s.

Among the party workers who tendered their resignations on Thursday is Mubeena Bano from Handwara in Kupwara district. A prominent social activist, Mubeena had joined BJP a couple of months ago, the insiders said.

“I have been working for redressal developmental issues of people since 2015. I had joined BJP recently to get people’s problems resolved effectively due to my affiliation with BJP, but I failed to deliver or get the expected response from the administration for the good of people. Therefore, I decided to quit BJP (sic),” Mubeena said in her resignation.

Another party worker who resigned on Thursday is Bashir Ahmad Malik of Thokerpora in Handwara. Bashir had joined BJP last month.

Earlier, in his resignation letter, Marouf Bhat had said: “I hereby resign from the BJYM as a member and any post I held as part of it. I thank you for giving me the opportunity to work for the party.” He did not assign any reason for his decision to quit BJP.

On his part, Asif Ahmad said in his resignation: “I am from a well-off family. I did not join BJP to make money but to work for my people. But we are not allowed to work by officials. BJP has no value on the ground. Officials are not bothered to listen to us and address issues of people (sic).”

Reacting to the spate of resignations, BJP UT general secretary Ashok Koul said these workers or functionaries, who quit the party, had come from other political parties and were not comfortable with BJP
