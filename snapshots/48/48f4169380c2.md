# Top Maoist leader Basavraju among 27 killed in Chhattisgarh’s Narayanpur

One District Reserve Guard member also killed during the encounter in Bastar region; Basavaraju, general secretary of the banned CPI (Maoist), is third member of the organisation’s top body to be killed this year

Updated  - May 22, 2025 02:14 am IST

Twenty seven Maoists, including Nambala Keshav Rao, alias Basavaraju, the general secretary of the banned Communist Party of India (Maoist), were gunned down in an anti-Naxal operation in Chhattisgarh’s Narayanpur district on Wednesday (May 21, 2025), announced Union Home Minister Amit Shah.

A landmark achievement in the battle to eliminate Naxalism. Today, in an operation in Narayanpur, Chhattisgarh, our security forces have neutralized 27 dreaded Maoists, including Nambala Keshav Rao, alias Basavaraju, the general secretary of CPI-Maoist, topmost leader, and the…

![](https://www.thehindu.com/theme/images/th-online/1x1_spacer.png)

A member of the District Reserve Guard (DRG) of Bastar was killed in the encounter while a few others sustained injuries, the police added.

Describing Basavraju as the backbone of the ‘Naxal’ movement in a post on X, Mr. Shah described the encounter that took place in the border region of Narayanpur, Bijapur, and Dantewada districts of Chhattisgarh’s Bastar region, a landmark achievement. He also reiterated that March 2026 was the deadline the Centre had set for the elimination of Maoism in the country.

“A landmark achievement in the battle to eliminate Naxalism. Today, in an operation in Narayanpur, Chhattisgarh, our security forces have neutralized 27 dreaded Maoists, including Nambala Keshav Rao, alias Basavaraju, the general secretary of CPI-Maoist, topmost leader, and the backbone of the Naxal movement. This is the first time in three decades of Bharat’s battle against Naxalism that a general secretary-ranked leader has been neutralized by our forces. I applaud our brave security forces and agencies for this major breakthrough.

“Also glad to share that after completion of Operation Black Forest, 54 Naxalites have been arrested and 84 Naxalites have surrendered in Chhattisgarh, Telangana, and Maharashtra. The Modi government is resolved to eliminate Naxalism before the 31st of March 2026,” Mr. Shah’s post read.

![](https://www.thehindu.com/theme/images/th-online/1x1_spacer.png)

Prime Minister Narendra Modi reposted Mr. Shah’s post with the message “Proud of our forces for this remarkable success. Our Government is committed to eliminating the menace of Maoism and ensuring a life of peace and progress for our people”.

Chhattisgarh Chief Minister Vishnu Deo Sai also took to X and wrote that despite difficult geographical conditions and many challenges, this decisive operation against left wing extremism was being carried out with full commitment and determination by the DRG unit of Chhattisgarh Police in Narayanpur.

A senior police officer said that Maoists opened fire when teams of District Reserve Guards of police from four districts were out on an operation in the Abhujmad area, a large unsurveyed jungle area spread across Narayanpur, Dantewada and Bijapur districts. The operation, the police added, was launched following intelligence inputs about the presence of “Central Committee and Politburo members as well as senior Maad Division cadres and PLGA cadres”.

Central Committee or CC is the main political wing and the highest decision making body of CPI (Maoist) and Basavraju is the third member to have been killed this year after Prayag Manjhi alias Vivek was killed in Jharkhand in April and Ramachandra Reddy Gari Pratap Reddy alias Chalapathi was killed in Chhattisgarh’s Gariaband district along the Odisha border in January.

According to the police officers, Basavraju had taken over the reins of the CPI (Maoist) in 2018. R.K. Vij, a retired Director General-rank IPS officer of the Chhattisgarh cadre, said that Basavraju’s killing was a major blow to the Maoist movement. “The general secretary is the top rank in the Maoist hierarchy and they are guarded by armed cadres carrying sophisticated weapons. So, if he has been gunned down, those lower down the hierarchy will feel more vulnerable. Secondly, they will have to soon elect a new leader,” said Mr. Vij, who has headed the Naxal Operations wing of the Chhattisgarh police in the past.

Chhattisgarh Deputy Chief Minister Vijay Sharma said the search operation was going on for 50 hours during which the encounter took place.

The latest operation comes close on the heels of another major one along the Karegutta Hills in Bijapur, the one Mr. Shah referred to in his post on X, in which 31 Maoists were killed. That operation was 21-day long concluded on May 11, and officers had claimed that it resulted in the top Maoist leaders getting dislocated from their safe hideout in the inaccessible hilly terrain. A Chhattisgarh government official added that from December 1, 2023 to 20 May 2025, 401 Naxalites were killed in the State while 1,355 surrendered. “The highest number of incidents were recorded in Bijapur, Dantewada and Sukma. A total of 682 Naxalite incidents, 199 encounters and 516 weapons were seized. 214 soldiers were injured, 87 civilians lost their lives,” said the official.
