- #### [Description](https://www.rct.uk/collection/2004230/queen-of-denmark-arrives-for-state-visit-to-britain-1974\# "Description")

Queen Margrethe II of Denmark escorted by Prince Philip walks down the pontoon at the Royal Naval College, Greenwich to begin her 3 day State visit to Britain. Behind the Queen, Prince Henrik of Denmark salutes.
