## How We Track Cancelled Data Center Projects

### Cancelled

We consider a data center construction project “cancelled” when a developer withdraws it or the local/state government issues a final denial.

### What Is Not Counted

Projects that are paused, delayed, tied up in court or re-filed at the same site are not counted. If a developer moves a project to a different site, the original site is counted. Local bans and pauses are covered in our [data center moratorium tracker](https://programs.com/resources/data-center-moratoriums-tracker/).

### Size

The tracker covers projects of at least 100 MW or at least $1 billion. Where neither figure was published, a project is included if the site is at least 200 acres or the plan is at least 1 million square feet.

### Capacity and Investment

MW and dollar figures are announced by the developer or reported at the time. Where a range was given, we use the low end.

### Reasons

The reason is the one given in the source. “Local opposition” is used only when the source cites the outcome as resulting from the pushback from residents.

### Dates

The date shown is the month the project was withdrawn, denied or dropped. The tracker covers cancellations from January 2024 onward.
