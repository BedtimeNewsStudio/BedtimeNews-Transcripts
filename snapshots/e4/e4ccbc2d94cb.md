# 哈萨克斯坦交通部：AZAL事故阶段性报告

INTERIM STATEMENT ON THE INVESTIGATION OF THE AVIATION ACCIDENT INVOLVING EMBRAER 190 4K-AZ65 AIRCRAFT OF AZERBAIJAN AIRLINES Date of issue: December 25, 2025 Basis: This Interim Statement is issued on the anniversary of the aviation accident in accordance with Standard 6.6 of Annex 13 to the Convention on International Civil Aviation (hereinafter – ICAO) and paragraph 94 of the Rules for the Submission of Data and Investigation of Aircraft Accidents and Incidents in Civil and Experimental Aviation of the Republic of Kazakhstan, approved by the Minister of Investment and Development of the Republic of Kazakhstan on July 27, 2017, No. 505 (hereinafter – Rules). 1. General information On December 25, 2024, near the city of Aktau, an aviation accident occurred involving an Embraer 190 4K-AZ65 aircraft belonging to Azerbaijan Airlines, which was operating passenger flight J2-8243 on the route Baku (UBBB) – Grozny (URMG). As a result of the aviation accident, 38 persons were fatally injured (including 3 crew members) and 29 persons sustained injuries of varying severity. The Commission investigating the accident, appointed by order of the acting Minister of Transport of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 433 dated December 26, 2024, continues to work to establish the causes and contributing factors of the aviation accident. Accredited representatives of the Republic of Azerbaijan, the Russian Federation, the Federative Republic of Brazil, and an observer from ICAO are participating in the work of the Commission investigating the aviation accident. On February 4, 2025, in accordance with Standard 7.4 Annex 13 of ICAO and paragraph 77 of the Rules, a Preliminary Report was issued. 2. Investigation progress At the time of preparation of the Interim Statement, the Commission had collected and analyzed factual data, including information about the crew, Air Traffic Controllers, and the operator's regulatory documentation, flight preparation and flight execution documentation, meteorological information, and maintenance materials records. Copies of the aircraft logbook and other flight-related documentation were obtained. Work was carried out on the flight recorders: the flight parameters from Flight Data Recorder (FDR) were retrieved, identified and decoded, and the Cockpit Voice Recorder (CVR) was decoded. page 2 ACCIDENT AND INCIDENT INVESTIGATION ON TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT OF THE MINISTRY OF TRANSPORT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KAZAKHSTAN The Commission conducted a detailed on-site examination of the accident site, recording the spatial position of the aircraft structural elements. Photo and video documentation were performed, and aircraft components were collected and removed from the accident site. The following examinations were conducted as part of the investigation into the aviation accident: – Comprehensive (traceological, ballistic, explosive-technical and fire) forensic examination of the identified foreign metallic objects (“foreign objects”). The examination revealed no traces of explosive substances. There were no traces of an explosion on the three oxygen cylinders submitted for examination. The damage to the aircraft was presumably caused by striking elements of a warhead, but it was not possible to establish their origin. The aircraft damage was preliminary assessed as having been caused by damaging elements of a warhead; however, their origin could not be established; – Traceological examination of part of the hydraulic system pipe No. 2. The examination revealed that the tubing exhibits damage. The mechanism of damage is classified as metal rupture resulting from contact with hard objects. Through-type damage may have been caused by metallic fragments (foreign objects) composed of iron-based alloys (steel-based materials); – Examination of the Central Maintenance Computer cassette removed from the Modular Avionics Unit at the «Honeywell International Inc.» manufacturing facility. Due to thermal exposure, it was not possible to extract data from the cassette. To retrieve the data, «Honeywell International Inc.» recommended contacting the memory card manufacturer «Delkin Devices Inc.». The Commission continues to coordinate with the Accredited Representative of the United States of America concerning arrangements for the conduct of an examination at «Delkin Devices Inc.»; – Examination of Global Positioning System (GPS1 and GPS2) cassettes, removed from Avionics Modular Units No. 1 and No. 3 manufactured by «CMC Electronics». Work completed, analysis in progress. The Investigation Commission formed two working groups: – Working group on Risk Assessment analysis flying over or near conflict zones in accordance with ICAO Doc 10084. The Commission is analyzing the documentation submitted by the aviation authorities of the Russian Federation and the Republic of Azerbaijan, as well as by Azerbaijan Airlines, concerning the organization and implementation of risk assessment procedures. This work also examines issues of civil and military sectors in ensuring flight safety. The analysis of this issue is one of the significant elements of the investigation, aimed at establishing the compliance of the measures taken with the requirements of current regulatory documents and identifying any possible deviations that could have influenced the development of the circumstances of the aviation accident; page 3 ACCIDENT AND INCIDENT INVESTIGATION ON TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT OF THE MINISTRY OF TRANSPORT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KAZAKHSTAN – Working group on layout (wreckage) reconstruction. In order to restore the spatial relationship between the fragments of the hydraulic system, a layout (reconstruction) of the elements of hydraulic system No. 2 was carried out in Aktau. Work completed, analysis in progress. In consideration of the above, the Commission is currently awaiting the completion of the examination of the Central Maintenance Computer cassette, as well as the report of the Working Group on the analysis of risk assessment for flights over or near conflict zones. The Investigation Commission is taking a balanced approach, and all conclusions will be objective and based solely on facts. The final report on the results of the investigation into the aviation accident will be published on the website of the Ministry of Transport of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
