**Manaus** (Portuguese:[\[maˈnaws,mɐ-\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Portuguese "Help:IPA/Portuguese")[Play audio](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/b/b2/Br-Manaus.ogg/Br-Manaus.ogg.mp3 "Play audio")[ⓘ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Br-Manaus.ogg "File:Br-Manaus.ogg")) is the [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_capitals_of_subdivisions_of_Brazil "List of capitals of subdivisions of Brazil") and largest city of the [Brazilian state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Brazil "States of Brazil") of [Amazonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_(Brazilian_state) "Amazonas (Brazilian state)"). It is the [seventh-largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_cities_in_Brazil "List of largest cities in Brazil") in Brazil, with an estimated 2024 population of 2,279,686 distributed over a land area of about 11,401km2 (4,402sqmi). Located at the east centre of the state, the city is the centre of the [Manaus metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Manaus "Greater Manaus") and the largest metropolitan area in the [North Region of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Region,_Brazil "North Region, Brazil") by urban landmass. It is situated near [the confluence of](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meeting_of_Waters "Meeting of Waters") the [Negro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_(Amazon) "Rio Negro (Amazon)") and [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_River "Amazon River") rivers. It is one of the two cities in the Amazon rainforest with a population of over 1 million people, alongside [Belém](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel%C3%A9m "Belém").

The city was founded in 1669 as the Fort of São José do Rio Negro. It was elevated to a [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town "Town") in 1832 with the name of "Manaus", an altered spelling of the indigenous Manaós peoples, and legally transformed into a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City "City") on October 24, 1848, with the name of _Cidade da Barra do Rio Negro_, [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language "Portuguese language") for "The City of the Margins of the Black River". On September 4, 1856, it returned to the name "Manaus".[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)

Manaus is located in the center of the [Amazon rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_rainforest "Amazon rainforest"), and home to the [National Institute of Amazonian Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Amazonian_Research "National Institute of Amazonian Research"), being the most important center for scientific studies in the Amazon region and for international sustainability issues.[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5) It was known at the beginning of the century as Heart of the Amazon and City of the Forest.[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6) Its main economic engine is the Industrial Park of Manaus, a Free Economic Zone.[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7) The city has a [free port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Manaus "Port of Manaus") and an [international airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Gomes_International_Airport "Eduardo Gomes International Airport"). Its manufactures include electronics, [chemical products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_substance "Chemical substance"), and soap; there are distilling and ship construction industries. Manaus exports [Brazil nuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_nuts "Brazil nuts"), [rubber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubber "Rubber"), [jute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jute "Jute"), and [rosewood oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosewood_oil "Rosewood oil"). It has a [cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Cathedral_of_Our_Lady_of_the_Conception,_Manaus "Metropolitan Cathedral of Our Lady of the Conception, Manaus"), the [Amazon Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Theatre "Amazon Theatre") opera house, [zoological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoology "Zoology") and [botanical gardens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botanical_garden "Botanical garden"), an eco-park, and regional and native peoples [museums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum "Museum").[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8)

The Solimões and Negro rivers meet just east of Manaus and join to form the [Amazon River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_River "Amazon River") (using the Brazilian definition of the river; elsewhere, Solimões is considered the upper part of the Amazon).[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9) Rubber made it the richest city in South America during the late 1800s. Rubber helped Manaus earn its nickname, the Paris of the Tropics. Many wealthy European families settled in Manaus and brought their love for sophisticated [European art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_of_Europe "Art of Europe"), [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_architecture#Europe_to_1600 "History of architecture"), and [culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Europe "Culture of Europe") with them. Manaus was one of the twelve Brazilian host cities of the [2014 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_World_Cup "2014 World Cup"), as well as one of the six hosts of [football matches at the 2016 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics "Football at the 2016 Summer Olympics").

### Early settlement of Manaus

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Early settlement of Manaus")\]

The history of the European colonization of Manaus began in 1499 with the Spanish arrival at the mouth of the Amazon River. The Spanish then continued to colonize the region north of Brazil. Development continued in 1668–1669 with the building of the Fort of São José da Barra do Rio Negro by the Portuguese in order to ensure its predominance in the region, especially against the [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands "Netherlands"), at that time headquartered in what is today [Suriname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suriname "Suriname"). The fort was constructed in rock and clay, with four [cannons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannon "Cannon") guarding the curtains.[&#8202;](#ref-back-calvarymanaus.com-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-calvarymanaus.com) It continued to function for more than 100 years. Next to the fort there were many indigenous [mestizos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo "Mestizo"), who helped in its construction and began to live in the vicinity.[&#8202;](#ref-back-calvarymanaus.com-1)[\[11\]](#refnote-calvarymanaus.com)

The population grew so much that, in 1695, the missionaries ( [Carmelite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmelite "Carmelite"), [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit "Jesuit"), [Franciscan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franciscan "Franciscan")) built a nearby [chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapel "Chapel") dedicated to _Nossa Senhora da Conceição_ (Our Lady of the Conception), who, in time, became the patron saint of the city.[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12) A Royal Charter of March 3, 1755 created the captaincy of São José do Rio Negro, with capital in Mariuá (now [Barcelos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelos,_Amazonas "Barcelos, Amazonas")), but with the governor, Lobo D'Almada, fearing a Spanish invasion, the seat went back to Lugar de Barra in 1791. Being located at the confluence of the Rio Negro and Amazon Rivers, it was a strategic point. On November 13, 1832, Lugar da Barra was elevated to [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town "Town") status and named Manaus. On October 24, 1848, under Law 145 of the Provincial Assembly of Para, it was renamed the City of Barra do Rio Negro. On September 4, 1856, the governor, Herculano Ferreira Pena, finally gave it the name "Manaus".[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13)

The [Cabanagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabanagem "Cabanagem") was the revolt in which blacks, Native Americans, and [mestizos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo "Mestizo") fought against the white political elite and took power in 1835. The Cabanagem reduced the population of the then state of [Grão-Pará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A1 "Pará") from about 100,000 to 60,000.[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14) The involvement of rebels from the Upper Amazon (Manaus today) in what was originally a movement based in [Belém](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel%C3%A9m "Belém") was crucial for the birth of the current state of Amazonas. During the brief period of revolution, the Cabanos of the Upper Amazon, bands of rebels, roamed throughout the region, occupying Manaus twice, and, in most settlements, their arrival was greeted by the non-white population spontaneously joining their ranks, leading to a greater number of adherents to the movement. With that there was an integration of people in the region thus forming the state.[&#8202;](#ref-back-15-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-15)

### Rubber boom

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Rubber boom")\]

Manaus was at the center of the Amazon region's [rubber boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubber_boom "Rubber boom") during the late 19th century. For a time, it was "one of the gaudiest cities of the world".[&#8202;](#ref-back-16-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-16) Historian Robin Furneaux wrote of this period, "No extravagance, however absurd, deterred" the rubber barons. "If one rubber baron bought a vast yacht, another would install a tame lion in his villa, and a third would water his horse on champagne."[&#8202;](#ref-back-17-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-17) The city built a grand opera house, with vast domes and gilded balconies, and using marble, glass, and crystal, from around Europe. The opera house cost ten million (public-funded) dollars. In one season, half the members of one visiting opera troupe died of [yellow fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_fever "Yellow fever").[&#8202;](#ref-back-18-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-18) The opera house, called the [Teatro Amazonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teatro_Amazonas "Teatro Amazonas"), was effectively closed for most of the 20th Century. However it was used in scenes of the [Werner Herzog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Herzog "Werner Herzog") film _[Fitzcarraldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitzcarraldo "Fitzcarraldo")_ (1982). After a gap of almost 90 years, it reopened to produce live opera in 1997 and is now attracting performers from all over the world.[&#8202;](#ref-back-opera-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-opera)

When the seeds of the rubber tree were smuggled out of the Amazon region to be cultivated on plantations in Southeast Asia,[&#8202;](#ref-back-20-0)[\[Note 1\]](#refnote-20) Brazil and Peru lost their monopoly on the product. The rubber boom ended abruptly, many people left its major cities, and Manaus fell into poverty. The rubber boom had made possible electrification of the city before it was installed in many European cities, but the end of the rubber boom made the generators too expensive to run. The city was not able to generate electricity again for years.[&#8202;](#ref-back-opera-1)[\[19\]](#refnote-opera)

### Free zone

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Free zone")\]

In the 1960s during the establishment of the military dictatorship in Brazil, the newly installed government concerned about the "demographic gap in Brazil", began to introduce numerous projects in the interior of the country, especially in the Amazon region, with the introduction of the Manaus free trade zone in 1967,[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-21) and with the opening of new roads within the region, the city had a wide period of investments in financial and economic capital, both national and international, attracted by the tax incentives granted by the free zone, in this period, Manaus had enormous demographic growth becoming one of the most populous cities in Brazil.[&#8202;](#ref-back-IBGE_(Brazil-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-IBGE_(Brazil)-22)

### Recent events

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Recent events")\]

Manaus was one of the host cities of the [2014 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup "2014 FIFA World Cup") and one of the seats of some Olympic football games.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NYT-Manaus-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-NYT-Manaus) It was the only host city in the Amazon rainforest and the most geographically isolated, being further north and west than any of the other host cities. A massive [prison riot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prison_riot "Prison riot") occurred [in January 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_2017_Brazil_prison_riots "January 2017 Brazil prison riots"), having begun in Manaus and later spreading to two additional cities in Brazil,[&#8202;](#ref-back-Riot_in_Natal,_Brazil-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-Riot_in_Natal,_Brazil) thus unleashing security problems within the country.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Folha_de_Sao_Paulo_(Brazil-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-Folha_de_Sao_Paulo_(Brazil)-25)[&#8202;](#ref-back-The_New_York_Times_(US-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-The_New_York_Times_(US)-26)

During the [COVID-19 pandemic in Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Brazil "COVID-19 pandemic in Brazil"), an estimated 76% of the population of Manaus was infected with coronavirus,[&#8202;](#ref-back-27-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-27) and the possibility of [herd immunity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herd_immunity "Herd immunity") was discussed.[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[27\]](#refnote-28)[&#8202;](#ref-back-29-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-29) However, a second outbreak infected people in Manaus, this time with the [Lineage B.1.1.248](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lineage_B.1.1.248 "Lineage B.1.1.248") variant starting in early January 2021.[&#8202;](#ref-back-virological-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-virological)[&#8202;](#ref-back-31-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-31)

## Geography

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Geography")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5c/Brazil_Amazonas_Manaus_location_map.svg/250px-Brazil_Amazonas_Manaus_location_map.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Brazil_Amazonas_Manaus_location_map.svg) Location in the state of Amazonas[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/18/Amazon_CIAT_%285%29.jpg/250px-Amazon_CIAT_%285%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_CIAT_(5).jpg) Aerial view of the [Amazon rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_rainforest "Amazon rainforest"), near Manaus

The largest city in northern Brazil, Manaus occupies an area of 11,401 square kilometres (4,402sqmi), with a density of 158.06 inhabitants per square kilometre (409.4/sqmi). It is the neighboring city of [Presidente Figueiredo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidente_Figueiredo "Presidente Figueiredo"), [Careiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Careiro "Careiro"), [Iranduba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranduba "Iranduba"), [Rio Preto da Eva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Preto_da_Eva "Rio Preto da Eva"), [Itacoatiara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itacoatiara,_Amazonas "Itacoatiara, Amazonas"), and [Novo Airão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novo_Air%C3%A3o "Novo Airão").

### Vegetation

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Vegetation")\]

Manaus is located in the middle of the [Amazon Rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_rainforest "Amazon rainforest"). The Amazon represents over half of the planet's remaining [rainforests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainforest "Rainforest") and comprises the largest and most species-rich tract of [tropical rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest "Tropical rainforest") in the world. Wet tropical forests are the most species-rich [biome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biome "Biome"), and tropical forests in the [Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americas "Americas") are consistently more species-rich than the wet forests in [Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Africa "Africa") and [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia "Asia").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Turner_2001-0)[\[31\]](#refnote-Turner_2001) As the largest tract of tropical rainforest in the Americas, the Amazonian rainforests have unparalleled [biodiversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiversity "Biodiversity"). More than one-third of all species in the world live in the Amazon rainforest.[&#8202;](#ref-back-33-0)[\[32\]](#refnote-33)

### Green areas

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Green areas")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4e/Curvas_do_Parque_Nacional_de_Anavilhanas.jpg/250px-Curvas_do_Parque_Nacional_de_Anavilhanas.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Curvas_do_Parque_Nacional_de_Anavilhanas.jpg) [Anavilhanas National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anavilhanas_National_Park "Anavilhanas National Park").[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d3/Novo_air%C3%A3o_2.jpg/250px-Novo_air%C3%A3o_2.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Novo_air%C3%A3o_2.jpg) Anavilhas National Park ground view.

Despite being located in the Amazon, Manaus is densely developed and has few green areas in the city. The largest green areas are:

- Mindu Park, located in the center-south of the city, the district Park 10. The Park of Mindú, established in 1989, is one of the largest and most visited parks in the city.
- Bilhares Park, established in 2005, located in the south-central region of Manaus, in the neighborhood of [Planalto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planalto_(Manaus)?action=edit&redlink=1 "Planalto (Manaus) (page does not exist)")\[ [pt](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planalto%20(Manaus) "pt:Planalto (Manaus)")\] ("plateau").
- Area of the green hill of Aleixo, created in the 1980s, located in the east of the city and is one of the largest urban green areas.
- [Sumaúma State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suma%C3%BAma_State_Park "Sumaúma State Park"), a state park located in the north of Manaus, in the New Town district. It is the smallest state park of the Brazilian Amazon Basin.
- [Castanheiras Pied Tamarin Wildlife Refuge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castanheiras_Pied_Tamarin_Wildlife_Refuge "Castanheiras Pied Tamarin Wildlife Refuge"), a 95-hectare (230-acre) refuge created in 1982 to protect a population of endangered [pied tamarins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pied_tamarin "Pied tamarin").
- [Adolfo Ducke Forest Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolfo_Ducke_Forest_Reserve "Adolfo Ducke Forest Reserve"), a biological reserve established in 1963, and covers an area of 100 square kilometres (10,000 hectares, 39 square miles). The Reserve is managed by INPA (Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas da Amazonia—National Institute for Amazon Research).
- Part of the [Anavilhanas National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anavilhanas_National_Park "Anavilhanas National Park"), a 350,018-hectare (864,910-acre) conservation unit that was originally an ecological station created in 1981.[&#8202;](#ref-back-34-0)[\[33\]](#refnote-34)
- About 75% of the [Rio Negro Left Bank Environmental Protection Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_Left_Bank_Environmental_Protection_Area "Rio Negro Left Bank Environmental Protection Area"), a 611,008-hectare (1,509,830-acre) sustainable use conservation area created in 1995.[&#8202;](#ref-back-35-0)[\[34\]](#refnote-35)
- The 11,930-hectare (29,500-acre) [Tupé Sustainable Development Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tup%C3%A9_Sustainable_Development_Reserve "Tupé Sustainable Development Reserve"), created in 2005, about 25 kilometres (16mi) west of the city.[&#8202;](#ref-back-36-0)[\[35\]](#refnote-36)
- The 86,601-hectare (214,000-acre) [Rio Negro State Park South Section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_State_Park_South_Section "Rio Negro State Park South Section"), created in 1995, about 40 kilometres (25mi) by boat to the northwest of the city.[&#8202;](#ref-back-37-0)[\[36\]](#refnote-37)

### Climate

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Climate")\]

Manaus has a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate "Tropical monsoon climate") ( _Am_) according to the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification "Köppen climate classification") system, just dry enough in its driest month to not be a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate "Tropical rainforest climate"), with the average annual compensated temperature of 27.4°C (81.3°F) and high [air humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_humidity "Air humidity"), with a rainfall index around 2,300mm (90.6in) annually. The seasons are relatively well-defined concerning rain: July to September is relatively dry, and December to May (summer and autumn) is very rainy. Summer and autumn are colder than winter and spring (June to November). Thunderstorms are frequent every day in the summer (December to February), but they can occur at any time of the year. There have been occasional occurrences of [hail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hail "Hail") in the city.[&#8202;](#ref-back-38-0)[\[37\]](#refnote-38)

Due to the city's proximity to the [equator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equator "Equator"), the heat is constant in the local climate. There are no cold days in winter (June to August); instead, winter is hotter than summer. Periodically, very intense polar [air masses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_masses "Air masses") occur in the [South-Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro-Sul "Centro-Sul") part of Brazil and in the southwest of the [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_basin "Amazon basin"), and have some effect on the city, as occurred in August 1955. But although they are rare, they influence the climate, causing the temperature to drop to 18°C (64.4°F) or below.[&#8202;](#ref-back-39-0)[\[38\]](#refnote-39) The proximity to the forest usually avoids extremes of heat and makes the city wet.[&#8202;](#ref-back-climate_3-0)[\[39\]](#refnote-climate_3)

According to the National Institute of Meteorology (INMET), the highest temperature registered in the city was 39°C (102.2°F) in 2015, and the lowest was 12°C (53.6°F) in 1989.

On November 26, 2009, a case of [acid rain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acid_rain "Acid rain") was recorded in Manaus. Air pollution, caused in large part by the accumulation of smoke from burning, associated with the [sulfur dioxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulfur_dioxide "Sulfur dioxide") emitted by cars, was the cause of this phenomenon. Although the incidence of acid rain is common in some Brazilian capitals where there is a great concentration of cars, in Manaus (and other cities of the [Amazonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_(Brazilian_state) "Amazonas (Brazilian state)")) the situation is aggravated by the prolonged period of drought with the smoke from forest fires.[&#8202;](#ref-back-chuva_ácida-0)[\[40\]](#refnote-chuva_ácida)

## Hydrology

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Hydrology")\]

The urban area covers all or part of four river basins, all tributaries of the Rio Negro. The São Raimundo and Educandos streams are completely contained in the city. The [Tarumã Açu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarum%C3%A3_A%C3%A7u_River "Tarumã Açu River") forms the western boundary of the city in its lower reaches, and is fed by several tributaries that originate in the Ducke Reserve and run through the north and west of the city. The [Puraquequara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puraquequara_River?action=edit&redlink=1 "Puraquequara River (page does not exist)") forms the east boundary of the urban area in its lower section.[&#8202;](#ref-back-52-0)[\[51\]](#refnote-52)

According to a 2013 genetic study, the ancestry of the inhabitants of Manaus is 45.9% European, 37.8% Native American, and 16.3% African.[&#8202;](#ref-back-54-0)[\[53\]](#refnote-54)

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/00/Igreja_de_S%C3%A3o_Sebasti%C3%A3o%2C_Manaus.jpg/250px-Igreja_de_S%C3%A3o_Sebasti%C3%A3o%2C_Manaus.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Igreja_de_S%C3%A3o_Sebasti%C3%A3o,_Manaus.jpg) [St. Sebastian Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Saint_Sebastian_(Manaus) "Church of Saint Sebastian (Manaus)")

The city has been influenced by [Catholicism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicism "Catholicism") since the time of European colonialism, and the majority of Manauenses are [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic "Catholic")—there are nevertheless dozens of different Protestant denominations in the city. [Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism "Judaism"), [Candomblé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candombl%C3%A9 "Candomblé"), [Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam "Islam"), and [spiritualism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiritualism_(movement) "Spiritualism (movement)"), among others, are also practised.[&#8202;](#ref-back-calvarymanaus.com-2)[\[11\]](#refnote-calvarymanaus.com) There is a community of [Amazonian Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonian_Jews "Amazonian Jews") in Manaus. The city's [Catedral Metropolitana Nossa Senhora da Conceição](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Cathedral_of_Our_Lady_of_the_Conception,_Manaus "Metropolitan Cathedral of Our Lady of the Conception, Manaus") is the seat of the [Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Manaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Manaus "Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Manaus").

The city has a very diverse presence of Protestant or Reformed faiths, such as the [Presbyterian Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterian_Church_of_Brazil "Presbyterian Church of Brazil"), [Calvary Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvary_Chapel "Calvary Chapel"), For Christ International Church of Grace of God, Pentecostal Church of God in Brazil, [Methodist Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodist_Church "Methodist Church"), the [Anglican Episcopal Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Episcopal_Church "Anglican Episcopal Church"), the [Baptist Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptist_Church "Baptist Church"), an [Assembly of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_of_God "Assembly of God") Church, the [Seventh-day Adventist Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seventh-day_Adventist_Church "Seventh-day Adventist Church"), the [Universal Church of the Kingdom of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Church_of_the_Kingdom_of_God "Universal Church of the Kingdom of God"), and the [Jehovah's Witnesses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jehovah's_Witnesses "Jehovah's Witnesses") among others. These churches are experiencing considerable growth, mainly in the outskirts of the city. [The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Church_of_Jesus_Christ_of_Latter-day_Saints "The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints") also has a large presence, with a [LDS temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manaus_Brazil_Temple "Manaus Brazil Temple") having been built in the city, the sixth in Brazil.[&#8202;](#ref-back-55-0)[\[54\]](#refnote-55)

### Metropolitan region

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Metropolitan region")\]

The Metropolitan Region of Manaus (RMM) is a [metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_area "Metropolitan area") that comprises eight [cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities "Cities") of the Amazonas state, but without conurbation.

### Regions

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: Regions")\]

Manaus is divided into seven regions: North, Southern, Central-South, East, West, Mid-West, and Rural area. The eastern region of the city is the most populated, with approximately 600,000 inhabitants (2007).[&#8202;](#ref-back-56-0)[\[55\]](#refnote-56) The northern region of the city has had the highest rate of population growth in recent years, and has the largest [neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighborhood "Neighborhood") of the city, the Nova Cidade neighborhood. The Center-South region has the highest [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income "Per capita income").[&#8202;](#ref-back-57-0)[\[56\]](#refnote-57) The Eastern Zone is known for having a large number of hills.

### Neighborhoods

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Neighborhoods")\]

Further information: [pt:Lista de bairros de Manaus](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lista%20de%20bairros%20de%20Manaus "pt:Lista de bairros de Manaus") and [Compensa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compensa "Compensa")

The first [neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighborhood "Neighborhood") (bairro) established in Manaus was Educandos. From there, other areas of the city began to be occupied since the arrival of migrants from other regions of Brazil.

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1f/Bairro_de_Educandos_from_the_Rio_Negro%2C_Manaus%2C_Amazonas%2C_Brazil_%28May_2026%29.jpg/250px-Bairro_de_Educandos_from_the_Rio_Negro%2C_Manaus%2C_Amazonas%2C_Brazil_%28May_2026%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Bairro_de_Educandos_from_the_Rio_Negro,_Manaus,_Amazonas,_Brazil_(May_2026).jpg) Bairro de Educandos from the Rio Negro

Manaus has the largest neighborhood in Latin America, the neighborhood of Cidade Nova, which has 264,449 inhabitants, but it is estimated that the population exceeds 300,000 inhabitants. Cidade Nova is larger than all the [cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City "City") inside the rest of Amazonas state.[&#8202;](#ref-back-58-0)[\[57\]](#refnote-58) With the permanence and the strengthening of [Free Economic Zone of Manaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Economic_Zone_of_Manaus "Free Economic Zone of Manaus"), the city began to receive investments and constant migration of people from many parts of the state and [northern Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Brazil "Northern Brazil").

The wealthiest neighborhood in Manaus is Adrianópolis, located in the Central-South Area of the city. Downtown Manaus is located in the Southern area of the city, next to [Rio Negro River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_(Amazon) "Rio Negro (Amazon)"). After years of development, the historical center has been neglected by the authorities and it has become an area mostly for commerce and poor housing. There is a plan to restore the city centre to its former glory by removing beggars and irregular sellers from sidewalks and by doing that provide more safety for tourists and locals who are trying to walk in the historical areas of the city. All these plans were prompted by the 2014 World Cup.

## Economy

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Economy")\]

See also: [Economy of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_Brazil "Economy of Brazil") and [Free Economic Zone of Manaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Economic_Zone_of_Manaus "Free Economic Zone of Manaus")

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e4/Avenida_Mario_Ypiranga_-_Manaus_-_Brasil.jpg/250px-Avenida_Mario_Ypiranga_-_Manaus_-_Brasil.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Avenida_Mario_Ypiranga_-_Manaus_-_Brasil.jpg) Mario Ypiranga Avenue

Manaus is the sixth-largest economy in Brazil. According to [IBGE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBGE "IBGE") in 2014, its GDP was [R$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_real "Brazilian real") 67,5 billion.[&#8202;](#ref-back-59-0)[\[58\]](#refnote-59) The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income "Per capita income") for the city was R$33,446.[&#8202;](#ref-back-60-0)[\[59\]](#refnote-60) Although the main industry of Manaus through much of the 20th century was [rubber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_rubber "Natural rubber"), its importance has declined. Given its location, fish, wild fruits like Açaí and Cupuaçu, and [Brazil nuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_nuts "Brazil nuts") initiate important trades, as do petroleum refining, soap manufacturing, and chemical industries. Over the last decades, a system of federal investments and tax incentives has turned the surrounding region into a major industrial center (the Free Economic Zone of Manaus).

The mobile phone companies [LG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LG "LG"), [Nokia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia "Nokia"), [Samsung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung "Samsung"), [Siemens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siemens "Siemens"), [Sagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagem "Sagem"), [Gradiente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gradiente "Gradiente"), and [BenQ-Siemens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BenQ-Siemens "BenQ-Siemens") operate mobile phone manufacturing plants in Manaus.[&#8202;](#ref-back-61-0)[\[60\]](#refnote-61)[&#8202;](#ref-back-62-0)[\[61\]](#refnote-62) Plastic lens manufacturer [Essilor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essilor "Essilor") also has a plant here. The Brazilian [sport utility vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport_utility_vehicle "Sport utility vehicle") manufacturer [Amazon Veiculos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Veiculos?action=edit&redlink=1 "Amazon Veiculos (page does not exist)") is headquartered in Manaus.[&#8202;](#ref-back-63-0)[\[62\]](#refnote-63) Two airlines, [MAP Linhas Aéreas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAP_Linhas_A%C3%A9reas "MAP Linhas Aéreas") and [Manaus Aerotáxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manaus_Aerot%C3%A1xi "Manaus Aerotáxi"), have headquarters on the grounds of [Eduardo Gomes International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manaus_International_Airport "Manaus International Airport") in Manaus.[&#8202;](#ref-back-64-0)[\[63\]](#refnote-64)[&#8202;](#ref-back-65-0)[\[64\]](#refnote-65)

### Free Trade Zone

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Free Trade Zone")\]

The initial idea of a Free Trade Port in Manaus came from Deputy Francisco Pereira da Silva and was subsequently formalized by Law No. 3.173 on June 6, 1957. The project was approved by the National Congress on October 23, 1951, under No. 1.310 and regulated by Decree No. 47.757 on February 2, 1960. It was then amended by rapporteur Maurício Jopper, an engineer, who by agreement with the original author, justified the creation of a Free Trade Zone instead of a Free Trade Port.

For the first ten years, the ZFM (Manaus Free Trade Zone) was located in a warehouse rented from Manaus Harbour, in the Port of Manaus, and relied on federal funds. It was perhaps due to this lack of its own resources that there was little credibility in the project. On February 28, 1967, President [Castelo Branco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humberto_de_Alencar_Castelo_Branco "Humberto de Alencar Castelo Branco") signed Decree-Law No. 288, which redefined the Manaus Free Trade Zone in more concrete terms. The new Decree-Law stipulated that the Manaus Free Trade Zone would have a radius of 10km (6.2mi) with an industrial center as well as an agricultural center and that these would be given the economic means to allow for regional development in order to lift the Amazon out of the economic isolation that it had fallen into at that time.

On August 28, 1967, the Manaus Free Trade Zone Authority, SUBFRAME, was created. SUBFRAME is an independent body with its own legal status and assets and has financial and administrative autonomy. Tax incentives and the subsequent complementary legislation created comparative advantages in the region with respect to other parts of the country and as a result the Manaus Free Trade Zone attracted new investment to the area. These incentives constituted tax exemptions administered federally by SUBFRAME and SUDAM.

## Transportation

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=23 "Edit section: Transportation")\]

### Airports

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=24 "Edit section: Airports")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/eb/Aeroporto-de-Manaus.jpg/250px-Aeroporto-de-Manaus.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Aeroporto-de-Manaus.jpg) [Manaus International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Gomes_International_Airport "Eduardo Gomes International Airport")

[Eduardo Gomes International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Gomes_International_Airport "Eduardo Gomes International Airport") is the airport serving Manaus. The airport has two passenger terminals, one for scheduled flights and the other for regional aviation. It also has three [cargo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cargo "Cargo") [terminals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_terminal "Airport terminal").

Eduardo Gomes International Airport is Brazil's third largest in freight movement,[&#8202;](#ref-back-69-0)[\[68\]](#refnote-69) handling the import and export demand from the Manaus [Industrial Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Economic_Zone_of_Manaus "Free Economic Zone of Manaus"). For this reason, Infraero invested in the construction of the third cargo terminal, opened on December 14, 2004. [TAM Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAM_Airlines "TAM Airlines") also inaugurated its own cargo terminal near the airport in 2008, which claims to be the largest cargo terminal in Brazil. The country's major dedicated freight route is between Manaus and [Viracopos International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viracopos_International_Airport "Viracopos International Airport"), which is operated by wide-body jets. Other freight routes include North America and Europe.

The passenger terminal had been fully refurbished and expanded in time for the [2014 FIFA Football World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup "2014 FIFA World Cup"), which held 4 games in Manaus. The airport currently operates daily international flights to [Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami "Miami") and [Orlando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida "Orlando, Florida"), United States, by [American Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Airlines "American Airlines") and [LATAM Airlines Brasil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAM_Airlines "TAM Airlines"); to [the city of Panama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_City "Panama City"), by [Copa Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_Airlines "Copa Airlines"); and to [Barcelona, Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona,_Venezuela "Barcelona, Venezuela"), by [Avior Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avior_Airlines "Avior Airlines"). The airport has direct flights to all major airports in Brazil, operated by the three major carriers: [Gol Transportes Aéreos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gol_Transportes_A%C3%A9reos "Gol Transportes Aéreos"), [TAM Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAM_Airlines "TAM Airlines"), and [Azul Brazilian Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azul_Brazilian_Airlines "Azul Brazilian Airlines"). The airport's [IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA "IATA") code is MAO.

[Manaus Air Force Base - ALA8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manaus_Air_Force_Base "Manaus Air Force Base"), one of the most important bases of the [Brazilian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Air_Force "Brazilian Air Force"), is located in Manaus at the former [Ponta Pelada Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponta_Pelada_Airport "Ponta Pelada Airport").

Apart from the Eduardo Gomes International Airport and Ponta Pelada Airport, Manaus is also served by [Flores Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flores_Airport_(Brazil) "Flores Airport (Brazil)"), used by small propeller aircraft and helicopters about 6 kilometres (4 miles) north of the city centre, simply known as the "Aeroclube" ( [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language "Portuguese language"): _Flying club_). On Sundays, it is used for parachuting and where flying classes can be hired. Due to the fact that it is surrounded by residential areas, and has a recent history of crashes, it is under constant pressure to be moved.

### Highways

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=25 "Edit section: Highways")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c6/Manaus_avenida.jpg/250px-Manaus_avenida.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Manaus_avenida.jpg) Gilberto Mestrinho road complex[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Ponte_Rio_Negro_-_Amazonas_-_Brasil.jpg/250px-Ponte_Rio_Negro_-_Amazonas_-_Brasil.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Ponte_Rio_Negro_-_Amazonas_-_Brasil.jpg) [Rio Negro Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_Bridge "Rio Negro Bridge")

There are two federal highways that intersect Manaus. There is a paved road heading North (BR-174) connecting Manaus to [Boa Vista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boa_Vista,_Roraima "Boa Vista, Roraima"), the capital of the State of [Roraima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roraima "Roraima") and to Venezuela. Strictly speaking, Manaus is connected by road to the rest of Brazil, as it is possible to drive continuously from Manaus into Venezuela, and then reenter Brazil through the [BR-364](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BR-364 "BR-364") in [Acre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre_(state) "Acre (state)") and its capital, [Rio Branco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Branco,_Acre "Rio Branco, Acre"), therefore passing through the countries of Colombia, Ecuador, Peru and Bolivia. As such a route is impractical for most motorists, the vast majority of transportation to and from Manaus is by boat or plane, except for journeys to [Roraima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roraima "Roraima"). _[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")_ noted that "there are still no roads to Manaus" from the rest of the country.[&#8202;](#ref-back-70-0)[\[69\]](#refnote-70)

The [BR-319](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BR-319 "BR-319") heads South connecting Manaus to [Porto Velho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_Velho "Porto Velho"), the state capital of [Rondônia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rond%C3%B4nia "Rondônia"). However, access to this highway requires a ferry crossing to [Careiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Careiro "Careiro"), across the [Rio Negro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_(Amazon) "Rio Negro (Amazon)") and [Amazon River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_River "Amazon River"), which takes about 40 minutes, and then is only paved for about another 100 kilometers (60mi) to Castanho. After that, the highway is not paved, and cannot be used. Various governments have promised to recover this land-link with the rest of the country, but environmental issues, high costs and complicated logistics have impeded any progress so far.

The two major state highways are the AM-010 and the AM-070. The AM-010 heads east, to [Itacoatiara, Amazonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itacoatiara,_Amazonas "Itacoatiara, Amazonas") at the banks of the Amazon River, which is the third largest city of the state. The AM-070 heads south, starting on the other side of the new [Rio Negro Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_Bridge "Rio Negro Bridge") at Manaus, and reaching [Manacapuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manacapuru "Manacapuru"), which lies at the banks of the [Solimoes River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solimoes_River "Solimoes River"), also known as the upper River Amazon, and which is the fourth largest city of the state. Both roads are paved and operate all year round.

### Port

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=26 "Edit section: Port")\]

Main article: [Port of Manaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Manaus "Port of Manaus")

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/Manaus_harbor.jpg/250px-Manaus_harbor.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Manaus_harbor.jpg) [Port of Manaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Manaus "Port of Manaus")

Ships dock at the main port in Manaus directly downtown on the banks of the Negro River. The terraced city is home to a network of bridged channels that divide it into several compartments. Several [mobile phone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_phone "Mobile phone") [companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Companies "Companies") have manufacturing plants in the port area, and other major [electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronics "Electronics") [manufacturers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacture "Manufacture") also have plants there. ### Taxis

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=27 "Edit section: Taxis")\]

Regular Manaus taxis are white and can be stopped anywhere. They are organized into separate cooperatives, each with their own contact phone numbers. All taxis are metered, which does not necessarily mean the meter will be used.

What is known as 'special taxis' are typically black vehicles and are considered a higher quality than the white taxis. They will charge a fixed rate for all journeys or daily hire. Most can only be booked locally; however, the reputable Brazil Airport Transfers[&#8202;](#ref-back-71-0)[\[70\]](#refnote-71) has recently started providing airport transfer and general transportation services in Manaus.

## Events and holidays

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=29 "Edit section: Events and holidays")\]

The [annual calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annual_calendar "Annual calendar") of [festivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festival "Festival") in Manaus starts in late February / early March. The Manaus [carnival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnival "Carnival") (carnaval) celebrations are a good start to upcoming events and include traditional processions and samba dancing at the Sambódromo in the Centro de Convenções (Convention Centre). May is a popular time to pay a visit to Manaus, since the city hosts both the Ponta Negra Music and the Amazonas de Opera festivals during this month. Staged at the Teatro Amazonas, the Opera Festival lasts around three weeks and usually runs into early June. The Floclorico do Amazonas (Amazonas Folklore Festival) is in June, and this has grown to become a major event, involving a huge array of [folk dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_dancing "Folk dancing") and [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music "Music"), culminating in the Procissao Fluvial de São Pedro (St. Peter River Procession), when hundreds of riverboats sail along the [Rio Negro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_(Amazon) "Rio Negro (Amazon)"), honouring the patron saint of fishermen.

October 24 was the day in 1848 that Manaus legally became a city. This [anniversary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anniversary "Anniversary") is always cause for a party, culminating in fireworks at the end of the day. In November is the week-long [Amazonas Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_Film_Festival "Amazonas Film Festival"), with [films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film "Film") and [documentaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documentary "Documentary") often emphasising [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology "Ecology"), [ethnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnology "Ethnology") and [human relationships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_relationship "Human relationship").[&#8202;](#ref-back-72-0)[\[71\]](#refnote-72)

- February—Amazonas Carnival—samba schools parade at the "sambódromo" in the Convention Center
- May—Ponta Negra's Music Festival
- May—Amazonas Opera Festival
- June—Amazonas Folklore Festival
- June 29—São Pedro Fluvial Procession
- July—Amazonas Jazz Festival
- September 5—Elevation of Amazonas to the category of Brazilian Province
- October 24—Anniversary of Manaus
- December 31—Ponta Negra's New Year's Eve Party

## Sights and attractions

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=30 "Edit section: Sights and attractions")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/47/Teatro_Amazonas%2C_Manaus_AM.jpg/250px-Teatro_Amazonas%2C_Manaus_AM.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Teatro_Amazonas,_Manaus_AM.jpg) [Amazon Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Theatre "Amazon Theatre"), in Manaus. More than 120 years old, it represents the city's heyday during the [rubber boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_rubber_boom "Amazon rubber boom").[&#8202;](#ref-back-73-0)[\[72\]](#refnote-73)[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Amazonas_Philharmonic.JPG/250px-Amazonas_Philharmonic.JPG?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Amazonas_Philharmonic.JPG)

[Amazonas Philharmonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_Philharmonic "Amazonas Philharmonic")

Because of Manaus' location within the [Amazon rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_rainforest "Amazon rainforest"), it attracts a substantial number of Brazilian and foreign tourists, who come to see wildlife on land and in the rivers. It is also home to one of the most endangered primates in Brazil, the [pied tamarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pied_tamarin "Pied tamarin").

Tour boats leave Manaus to see the Meeting of the Waters, where the black waters of the Negro River meet the brown waters of the Solimoes River, flowing side by side without mixing for about 9km (6mi). Visitors can also explore riverbanks and "igarapes", swim and [canoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canoe "Canoe") in placid lakes, simply walk in the lush [forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest "Forest") or stay at [hotels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel "Hotel") in the jungle.

About 18km (11mi) from downtown is Ponta Negra beach, a neighbourhood that has a beachfront and popular nightlife area.[&#8202;](#ref-back-74-0)[\[73\]](#refnote-74) A luxurious hotel is located at the west end of Ponta Negra; its [zoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoo "Zoo") and orchid greenhouse as well as preserved woods and beach are open for public visits.

The [Mercado Adolpho Lisboa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercado_Adolpho_Lisboa "Mercado Adolpho Lisboa"), founded in 1882, is the city's oldest marketplace, trading in fruit, vegetables, and especially fish. It is a copy of the Les Halles market of Paris.[&#8202;](#ref-back-75-0)[\[74\]](#refnote-75) Other interesting historical sites include the [customs building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customs_House,_Manaus "Customs House, Manaus"), of mixed styles and medieval inspiration; the Rio Negro Palace cultural center; and the [Justice Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palace_of_Justice,_Manaus "Palace of Justice, Manaus"), right next to the Amazonas Opera House.

Manaus has also many large parks with native forest preservation areas, such as the Bosque da Ciência and Parque do Mindú. The largest urban forest in the world is located within the [Federal University of Amazonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Amazonas "Federal University of Amazonas"), which was founded on January 17, 1909, and is the oldest federal university in Brazil.

Manaus also has several malls such as Manauara Shopping, Amazonas Shopping Center, Millennium Shopping, Shopping Ponta Negra, Studio 5 Festival Mall, Shopping Cidade Nova, Manaus Plaza Shopping, Shopping Sao José, and other small Shopping Areas. Most of these malls include large food courts and movie theaters.

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=32 "Edit section: Amazonas Opera House")\]

The Amazonas Opera House, inaugurated in 1896, has 700 seats and was constructed with bricks brought from [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe "Europe"), French [glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass "Glass"), and Italian [marble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble "Marble"). The theater is home to the [Amazonas Philharmonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_Philharmonic "Amazonas Philharmonic") orchestra which regularly rehearses and performs there along with choirs, jazz bands, dance performances, and more.[&#8202;](#ref-back-76-0)[\[75\]](#refnote-76)

### Meeting of Waters

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=35 "Edit section: Meeting of Waters")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d6/Mixing_of_Waters_-_panoramio.jpg/250px-Mixing_of_Waters_-_panoramio.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Mixing_of_Waters_-_panoramio.jpg) The [natural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural "Natural") [phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenomenon "Phenomenon") of the [confluence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confluence "Confluence") of the [Rio Negro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_(Amazon) "Rio Negro (Amazon)")'s water and the [Solimões River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solim%C3%B5es_River "Solimões River")'s water

Main article: [Meeting of Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meeting_of_Waters "Meeting of Waters")

The Meeting of Waters ( [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language "Portuguese language"): _Encontro das Àguas_) is a natural phenomenon caused by the [confluence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confluence "Confluence") of the Rio Negro's dark water and the Solimões River's muddy brown water that come together to form the Amazonas River. For 6km (3.7mi) or more, both rivers' [waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water "Water") run side by side without mixing.

The reason for this is not clear, although it is likely that the main factors are differences in the speed of the current, the volumes of water and the different densities of the two rivers. It is not thought that other differences between the two rivers (temperature and acidity) affect the mixing process significantly.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Amazon_Handbook-0)[\[78\]](#refnote-Amazon_Handbook) The Negro River flows approximately 2km/h (1.2mph) at 28°C (82°F), while the Solimões River flows 4 to 6km/h (2.5 to 3.7mph) at 22°C (72°F).[&#8202;](#ref-back-80-0)[\[79\]](#refnote-80)

### Beaches and waterfalls

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=37 "Edit section: Beaches and waterfalls")\]

For outings to beaches and parks situated near the city, it is often necessary to use [boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boat "Boat"). The beaches are formed right after the river water level starts dropping, which lasts from August to November. Starting in December, as the river rises, the [waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water "Water") invade the sand and the woods on the banks. The Paricatuba Waterfall, located on the right bank of the [Negro River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Negro_(Amazon) "Rio Negro (Amazon)"), along a small tributary, is formed by sedimentary rocks, surrounded by abundant vegetation. Access is by boat. The best time to visit is from August to February. Love Cascade located in the Guedes bayou, with cold and crystal-clear water, is accessible only by boat and, then, hiking through the forest.

Tupé Beach is approximately 34km (21mi) from Manaus. This [beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beach "Beach") is well frequented by bathers on holidays and weekends. It is accessible only by boat. Moon Beach is located on the left bank of the Negro River, 23km (14mi) from Manaus. It is accessed only by boat. The beach is shaped like a crescent moon and is surrounded by [rare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rare_species "Rare species") [vegetation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetation "Vegetation"). Lion Waterfall is located on km 34 of the AM-010 highway (Manaus-Itacoatiara).

### Football

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=39 "Edit section: Football")\]

The most successful club in Manaus is [Nacional Futebol Clube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nacional_Futebol_Clube "Nacional Futebol Clube"), founded on 13 January 1913. Formerly a participant of the [highest division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Brasileiro_S%C3%A9rie_A "Campeonato Brasileiro Série A") several times between 1970 and 1990, Nacional are 40-time [state champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Amazonense "Campeonato Amazonense"), which makes them the highest-ranked [Amazonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_(Brazilian_state) "Amazonas (Brazilian state)") football club in the [CBF ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBF_ranking "CBF ranking"), the national state championships record holder, and the state record holder for the most championship titles.

The city has quite a few other clubs with distinguished histories such as [Atlético Rio Negro Clube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Rio_Negro_Clube "Atlético Rio Negro Clube"), also founded in 1913, but in November, who have won the [state championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Amazonense "Campeonato Amazonense") 17 times. [National Fast Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Fast_Club "National Fast Club"), founded after a split from Nacional Clube, have won six [state championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Amazonense "Campeonato Amazonense"), in addition to being [Northern Region champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_Norte "Copa Norte") and [Northeastern Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_do_Nordeste "Copa do Nordeste") runners-up in 1970. [São Raimundo EC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Raimundo_Esporte_Clube_(AM) "São Raimundo Esporte Clube (AM)"), founded on November 18, 1918. They have won the [state championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Amazonense "Campeonato Amazonense") six times and the [North Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_Norte "Copa Norte") 3 times. [Sul América Esporte Clube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sul_Am%C3%A9rica_Esporte_Clube "Sul América Esporte Clube"), founded on 1 of May, 1932. They have won the [state championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Amazonense "Campeonato Amazonense") twice in 1992 and 1993. Finally, [Atlético Clipper Clube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Clipper_Clube "Atlético Clipper Clube") who have twice finished as runners-up in the [state championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Amazonense "Campeonato Amazonense") in 1996 and 2002.

[Manaus Futebol Clube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manaus_Futebol_Clube "Manaus Futebol Clube"), founded in 2013, competes in the [Campeonato Brasileiro Série C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Brasileiro_S%C3%A9rie_C "Campeonato Brasileiro Série C").

#### 2014 FIFA World Cup

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=40 "Edit section: 2014 FIFA World Cup")\]

Manaus was chosen in 2009 to be a host city for the [2014 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup "2014 FIFA World Cup"), after a competition to represent the North Region of Brazil with neighboring [Pará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A1 "Pará") state capital, [Belém](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel%C3%A9m,_Par%C3%A1 "Belém, Pará").

Manaus was restructured in order to host such a big event. A new airport was built, streets throughout the city were repaved and new and improved sidewalks were built. The communications infrastructure of the city was improved with [4G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4G "4G") networks installed by the biggest mobile phone carriers in Brazil.

The [Vivaldão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivald%C3%A3o "Vivaldão"), previously the largest stadium in Manaus, was inaugurated in 1970 by the Brazilian national team in their last game in the country before they headed to their victorious [1970 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_FIFA_World_Cup "1970 FIFA World Cup") in Mexico. It was demolished to be replaced by the 44,000-seater [Arena Amazônia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Amaz%C3%B4nia "Arena Amazônia") for the 2014 World Cup.[&#8202;](#ref-back-82-0)[\[81\]](#refnote-82)

The first 2014 World Cup match held in Manaus was [England vs Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_D#England_vs_Italy "2014 FIFA World Cup Group D") on June 14. The second match was [Cameroon vs Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_A#Cameroon_vs_Croatia "2014 FIFA World Cup Group A") on June 18, to be followed by [USA vs Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_G#United_States_vs_Portugal "2014 FIFA World Cup Group G") on June 22. The last was [Honduras vs Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_E#Honduras_vs_Switzerland "2014 FIFA World Cup Group E") on June 25. Manaus, known for its intense heat and humidity, was the site of the World Cup's first-ever official water break on June 22 in the match between Portugal and the United States.

### Brazilian jiu-jitsu

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=41 "Edit section: Brazilian jiu-jitsu")\]

Manaus is the origin of several world-champion [Brazilian jiu-jitsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu "Brazilian jiu-jitsu") black belts, [mixed martial artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_martial_artists "Mixed martial artists") and submission grapplers. Champions such as [Fredson Paixão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredson_Paix%C3%A3o "Fredson Paixão"), [Wallid Ismail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallid_Ismail "Wallid Ismail"), [Saulo Ribeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saulo_Ribeiro "Saulo Ribeiro"), Cristiane De Souza, [Alexandre Ribeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Ribeiro "Alexandre Ribeiro"), [Ronaldo Souza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronaldo_Souza "Ronaldo Souza"), [Diogo Reis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diogo_Reis "Diogo Reis"), [Micael Galvão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micael_Galv%C3%A3o "Micael Galvão"), Fabricio Andrey, and [Diego Lopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Lopes_(fighter) "Diego Lopes (fighter)") hail from Manaus. Brazilian jiu-jitsu is a major component of MMA ( [mixed martial arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_martial_arts "Mixed martial arts")). [José Aldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Aldo "José Aldo") (born September 9, 1986) is a black belt in Brazilian jiu-jitsu and a notable UFC fighter. Aldo defeated [Mike Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Brown_(fighter) "Mike Brown (fighter)") at [WEC 44](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WEC_44 "WEC 44") to win the title and has since successfully defended his WEC title against [Urijah Faber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urijah_Faber "Urijah Faber") & [Manvel Gamburyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manvel_Gamburyan "Manvel Gamburyan"). He later became the UFC Featherweight champion, with title defenses against such notable fighters as [Mark Hominick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Hominick "Mark Hominick") and [Kenny Florian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Florian "Kenny Florian").

## In popular culture

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Manaus&action=edit&section=46 "Edit section: In popular culture")\]

In the anime and manga _Dr. Stone,_ an explosion located in the coordinates 3° 7' S, 60° 1' W, roughly in the municipal cemetery of Manaus, caused the petrification of all humans on earth in the year 2019.

The city of Manaus and its surrounding Amazon region are also depicted in [Ann Patchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Patchett "Ann Patchett")'s novel _State of Wonder_, which explores scientific expeditions and the mysteries of the rainforest.
