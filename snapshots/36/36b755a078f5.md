2）[BASF法](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=BASF%E6%B3%95&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiJCQVNG5rOVIiwiemhpZGFfc291cmNlIjoiZW50aXR5IiwiY29udGVudF9pZCI6MTczOTE0MTE3LCJjb250ZW50X3R5cGUiOiJBcnRpY2xlIiwibWF0Y2hfb3JkZXIiOjEsInpkX3Rva2VuIjpudWxsfQ.zinljqF8OtqMojJF2IYUiMNcKauq7ptjfUdYkzEr9r8&zhida_source=entity)：由异丁烯、甲醛合成得到甲基丁烯醛和异戊烯醇；再进一步得到柠檬醛。3）[新和成法](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%96%B0%E5%92%8C%E6%88%90%E6%B3%95&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLmlrDlkozmiJDms5UiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.tADTrW-heXUGDI8BmCsq9gpZWpM4uODWAtHoQc63yKU&zhida_source=entity)：

由[丙酮](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%B8%99%E9%85%AE&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLkuJnpha4iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.3bWjk0lsV4nQ7lkai9TO1Q1xOp4UbmHeiULzeRNtap0&zhida_source=entity)和乙炔，通过多步反应生成脱氢芳樟醇，再通过催化重排得到柠檬醛。

**感官特征**：柠檬醛有强烈的柠檬香气。

**物理性质**

外观为液体，为反式和[顺式异构体](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%A1%BA%E5%BC%8F%E5%BC%82%E6%9E%84%E4%BD%93&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLpobrlvI_lvILmnoTkvZMiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.XCsISh0siDTleVPRaQdiice1_1E6ehyZDeqUDciW4c4&zhida_source=entity)之混合物，两者的比例约为7:3。反式体称为[香叶醛](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%A6%99%E5%8F%B6%E9%86%9B&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLpppnlj7bphpsiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.DqRcFt2y9yw8GaUyGf7O7k6KMVPV-vHRU_JOVMCartE&zhida_source=entity)，沸点110~112℃/1.6kPa；顺式体称为[橙花醛](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%A9%99%E8%8A%B1%E9%86%9B&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLmqZnoirHphpsiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.FNn8c6sltt1yhOE3ntN_adjxI2R1KTZ4R75P7LhZJtQ&zhida_source=entity)，沸点102~104℃/1.6kPa。异构体混合物的沸点为228~229℃。

柠檬醛可自天然来源的柠檬草油、山苍子油等单离获取。通常是先采用真空高效[分馏法](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%88%86%E9%A6%8F%E6%B3%95&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLliIbppo_ms5UiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.0CE6-ScuFcJE-ErfHAWwhIl-r7yl5pE7if7VMO-97Mk&zhida_source=entity)，获得较高含量的柠檬醛。为了获得高纯度的柠檬醛，还须用[亚硫酸钠](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%BA%9A%E7%A1%AB%E9%85%B8%E9%92%A0&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLkuprnoavphbjpkqAiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.oA8nFV5247oCkMVTtjeo4T6-fIFLicv_GBUn7Da-rs8&zhida_source=entity)处理生成[加成物](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%8A%A0%E6%88%90%E7%89%A9&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLliqDmiJDniakiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.c_VFFRgNU-lY8wEacb-21Exb0f823s_nYBjEC4gEksY&zhida_source=entity)，用碱液水解磺酸盐加成物，分解出的柠檬醛再经真空分馏，可获得含醛量98%以上的纯品。柠檬醛的合成方法很多，主要有[松节油](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%9D%BE%E8%8A%82%E6%B2%B9&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLmnb7oioLmsrkiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.tpiL3d_xWlegDX5AXK6xVLlyQehZF4QnewvCVrwUxHc&zhida_source=entity)系统的合成路线和[石油化工](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type= **应用**

柠檬醛可用于[日化香精](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%97%A5%E5%8C%96%E9%A6%99%E7%B2%BE&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLml6XljJbpppnnsr4iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.3wKB43NX57HK8_sBvKjEz0iZRlObSvhreNJIIIP_Xgc&zhida_source=entity)配方中，用量可在10%以内。但由于它在空气中、碱性介质中，以及光照下均不稳定，本身易变色，在香精中也易与某些香料化合物产生[化学反应](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%8C%96%E5%AD%A6%E5%8F%8D%E5%BA%94&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLljJblrablj43lupQiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.eUQ0XamB1U9l7FAOv4z8uboIqJF9daVvmzAcTBwn1cE&zhida_source=entity)生成深色反应物，因此在调香拟定配方时要慎重选用。此外由于它在单独使用时有过敏作用，故在调香配方中应与防过敏的香料一起使用，如25%的右旋苧烯、柑橘类混合萜烯或[α-蒎烯](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%CE%B1-%E8%92%8E%E7%83%AF&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLOsS3oko7ng68iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.FWzE0rrdc_CIdJ02C_6pp_24m269QQ3DfGiiucd0ia4&zhida_source=entity)等。柠檬醛在食用香精中很有用途，尤其是在柑橘类香精配方中几乎必不可少。常用于调配橘子、柠檬、酸柠檬、苹果、樱桃、圆柚、葡萄等食用香精。柠檬醛除了自身可作为香料外，它还是用于制造紫罗兰酮、[甲基紫罗兰酮](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=173914117&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%94%B2%E5%9F%BA%E7%B4%AB%E7%BD%97%E5%85%B0%E9%85%AE&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzODMsInEiOiLnlLLln7rntKvnvZflhbDpha4iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxNzM5MTQxMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlc
