2024 European Parliament election in Hungary

The **2024 European Parliament elections** in [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary "Hungary") were held on 9 June 2024 as part of the [2024 European Parliament election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_European_Parliament_election "2024 European Parliament election").[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3) This was the first European election to take place after [Brexit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brexit "Brexit") and on the same day as [local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hungarian_local_elections "2024 Hungarian local elections").[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5)

This was the first nationwide election contested by the [Tisza Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tisza_Party "Tisza Party") under [Péter Magyar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A9ter_Magyar "Péter Magyar"), who became the most popular opposition figure to then- [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Hungary "Prime Minister of Hungary") [Viktor Orbán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Orb%C3%A1n "Viktor Orbán") since the [Novák scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katalin_Nov%C3%A1k_presidential_pardon_scandal "Katalin Novák presidential pardon scandal") broke in February 2024. Though the [Fidesz–KDNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidesz%E2%80%93KDNP "Fidesz–KDNP") alliance of then [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Hungary "Prime Minister of Hungary") [Viktor Orbán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Orb%C3%A1n "Viktor Orbán") won the election, Tisza won the largest share of any non-Fidesz party in a nationwide election since 2010, while Fidesz won its lowest share in a European election since Hungary [joined the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_enlargement_of_the_European_Union "2004 enlargement of the European Union").

As of 2026, this is the most recent nationwide election won by Fidesz–KDNP, which would lose the [2026 Hungarian parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Hungarian_parliamentary_election "2026 Hungarian parliamentary election") to the Tisza Party.

## Background

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2024_European_Parliament_election_in_Hungary&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Background")\]

The [European Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Parliament "European Parliament") considers Hungary a " [hybrid regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_regime "Hybrid regime") of electoral autocracy" since 2022.[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6)

## Electoral system

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2024_European_Parliament_election_in_Hungary&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Electoral system")\]

Main article: [Electoral system of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_system_of_Hungary "Electoral system of Hungary")

Hungary uses [closed lists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_list "Closed list") and the [D'Hondt method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Hondt_method "D'Hondt method") to elect seats to the European Parliament, with a 5% threshold.
