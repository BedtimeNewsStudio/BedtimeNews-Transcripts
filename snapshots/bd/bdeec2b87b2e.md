## About

#### Exports

In 2024, [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) exported $21.5k of [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans), making it the 100th largest exporter of [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) (out of 137) in the world. During the same year, [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) were the 1,131st most exported product (out of 1,197) in [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus). In 2024, the main destinations of Russia's [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) exports were: [Belgium](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/bel) ($9.27k), [Kazakhstan](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/kaz) ($9.12k), [Austria](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/aut) ($3.07k), and [France](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/fra) ($19).

The fastest growing markets for [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) exports from [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) between 2023 and 2024 were: [Kazakhstan](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/kaz) ($4.84k), and [Austria](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/aut) ($1.81k).

#### Imports

In 2024, [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) imported $106M of [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans), becoming the 22nd largest importer of [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) (out of 153) in the world. During the same year, [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) were the 341st most imported product (out of 1,198) in [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus). In 2024, [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) imported [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) primarily from: [Estonia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/est) ($58.6M), [Ecuador](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/ecu) ($27.2M), [Togo](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/tgo) ($6.6M), [Cote d'Ivoire](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/civ) ($5.81M), and [Madagascar](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/mdg) ($3.33M).

The fastest growing origins for [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) imports in [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) between 2023 and 2024 were: [Estonia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/est) ($35.4M), [Ecuador](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/ecu) ($21.6M), and [Uganda](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/uga) ($2.53M).

#### Product Classification

[Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) are part of [Cocoa & cocoa preparations](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-and-cocoa-preparations) and include: [Cocoa Beans (Raw/Roasted, Whole/Broken)](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans-rawroasted-wholebroken).

## Latest Trends

### January 2022

#### Overview

In January 2022, [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) exported $70.2 and imported $12M of [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans), resulting in a negative trade balance of $12M. Between December 2021 and January 2022, the exports of [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) from [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) decreased by $367 (-83.9%), from $437 to $70.2. During the same period, imports decreased by $10.9M (-47.8%), from $22.9M to $12M.

#### Origins and Destinations

In January 2022, [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) imported [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) mostly from [Cote d'Ivoire](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/civ) ($9.53M), [Ecuador](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/ecu) ($1.03M), [Peru](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/per) ($683k), [Nigeria](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/nga) ($506k), and [Ghana](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/gha) ($191k).

#### Growth

In January 2022, the year-on-year exports of [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) from [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) decreased by 99.1%. This was explained primarily by a decrease in exports to Armenia (-$4.55k or -99.7%), Belarus (-$3.41k or -99.2%), and Kazakhstan (-$62.4 or -68.5%). During the same month, year-on-year imports of [Cocoa Beans](https://oec.world/en/profile/hs/cocoa-beans) to [Russia](https://oec.world/en/profile/country/rus) increased by 4.94%. This was explained primarily by an increase in imports from Peru (+$680k or 20.3k%), Nigeria (+$506k or 100%), and Cote d'Ivoire (+$355k or 3.87%).
