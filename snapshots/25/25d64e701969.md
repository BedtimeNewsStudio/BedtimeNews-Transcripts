# Kazakh TV Operator Stops Broadcasts Of Several Russian Stations

* By [RFE/RL's Kazakh Service](/author/rfe-rls-kazakh-service/kvv_qq "RFE/RL's Kazakh Service")

Kazakh TV Operator Stops Broadcasts Of Several Russian Stations

[![](/Content/responsive/img/Google-G-logo.svg) Prefer us on Google](https://www.google.com/preferences/source?q=rferl.org)

![Kazakhstan's television operator says the move is aimed at reducing the share of foreign news channels on the local market. (file photo)](https://gdb.rferl.org/0613c34a-54bd-417f-9bac-2c88b9b9a043_cx0_cy15_cw0_w250_r1_s.jpg)

Kazakhstan's television operator says the move is aimed at reducing the share of foreign news channels on the local market. (file photo)

Kazakh television operator TVCOM says it has stopped broadcasting several stations launched by Russian state-run Channel One in a move to reduce the share of foreign news channels on the local market. TVCOM said the move was also due to the fact that its subscribers are "more media literate, they get news from social networks." The Central Asian country has taken several moves to rein in Russian media. In November last year, it blocked the Sputnik24 Internet portal that broadcast Russian TV channels. In August, the Russian website "Tsargrad" was blocked for what the government called "signs of extremism." *To read the original story by RFE/RL's Kazakh Service, click **[here](https://www.azattyq.org/a/32759407.html)**.*
