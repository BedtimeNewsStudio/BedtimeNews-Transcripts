The **2017 National Women's Soccer League season** was the fifth season of the [National Women's Soccer League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Women's_Soccer_League "National Women's Soccer League"), the top division of women's soccer in the United States. Including the NWSL's two professional predecessors, [Women's Professional Soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Professional_Soccer "Women's Professional Soccer") (2009–2011) and the [Women's United Soccer Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_United_Soccer_Association "Women's United Soccer Association") (2001–2003), it was the eleventh overall season of FIFA- and USSF-sanctioned top-division women's soccer in the United States. The league is operated by the [United States Soccer Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Soccer_Federation "United States Soccer Federation") and receives major financial backing from that body. Further financial backing is provided by the [Canadian Soccer Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Soccer_Association "Canadian Soccer Association"); both national federations pay the league salaries of many of their respective national team members in an effort to nurture talent in those nations.

The reigning champions [Western New York Flash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_New_York_Flash "Western New York Flash") did not play under that name; the franchise was sold, relocated to [Cary, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cary,_North_Carolina "Cary, North Carolina") and rebranded as the [North Carolina Courage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_Courage "North Carolina Courage").[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1)

After schedule disruptions in the previous two years caused by the [2015 FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup "2015 FIFA Women's World Cup") and the [2016 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Summer_Olympics "2016 Summer Olympics"), NWSL teams in 2017 once again played 24 games, a schedule last played during the [2014 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_National_Women's_Soccer_League_season "2014 National Women's Soccer League season").[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) The Courage won the [NWSL Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWSL_Shield "NWSL Shield") (the regular season), topping the [Portland Thorns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Thorns_FC "Portland Thorns FC") by two points.

The [NWSL Playoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWSL_Playoffs "NWSL Playoffs") semifinals were held October 7–8, 2017, and were won by the Thorns and the Courage. The [NWSL Championship Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWSL_Playoffs "NWSL Playoffs") was held on October 14, 2017 at [Orlando City Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_City_Stadium "Orlando City Stadium") in [Orlando, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida "Orlando, Florida").[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3) The Thorns won 1–0, becoming [NWSL Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Women's_Soccer_League#Championship_and_Shield "National Women's Soccer League"), their second championship.

### Tiebreakers

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2017_National_Women%27s_Soccer_League_season&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Tiebreakers")\]

The initial determining factor for a team's position in the standings is most points earned, with three points earned for a win, one point for a draw, and zero points for a loss. If two or more teams tie in point total, when determining rank and playoff qualification and seeding, the NWSL uses the following tiebreaker rules,[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8) going down the list until all teams are ranked.

1. Head-to-head win–loss record between the teams (or points-per-game if more than two teams).
2. Greater goal difference across the entire season (against all teams, not just tied teams).
3. Greatest total number of goals scored (against all teams).
4. Apply #1–3 to games played on the road.
5. Apply #1–3 to games played at home.
6. If teams are still equal, ranking will be determined by a coin toss.

NOTE: If two clubs remain tied after another club with the same number of points advances during any step, the tie breaker reverts to step 1 of the two-club format.

## NWSL Playoffs

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2017_National_Women%27s_Soccer_League_season&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: NWSL Playoffs")\]

Further information: [NWSL Playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWSL_Playoffs "NWSL Playoffs")

The top four teams from the regular season competed for the NWSL Championship. In one semifinal, the [North Carolina Courage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_Courage "North Carolina Courage") defeated the [Chicago Red Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Red_Stars "Chicago Red Stars") 1–0 on a dramatic 89th-minute goal. In the other, [Portland Thorns FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Thorns_FC "Portland Thorns FC") defeated the [Orlando Pride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Pride "Orlando Pride") 4–1. One week later, in the final on October 14, Portland defeated North Carolina 1–0 to claim the NWSL Championship.
