**Sachin Gupta** (born March 17, 1982) is an American professional basketball executive who currently serves as Chief of Analytics at Chelsea Football Club, working alongside the likes of Santiago Alvarez Barbosa and Federico Bettuzzi.

## Early life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Gupta_(executive)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")\]

Gupta earned a degree in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_Engineering "Electrical Engineering") and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science "Computer science") from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology "Massachusetts Institute of Technology"), as well as an [MBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration "Master of Business Administration") degree from the [Stanford Graduate School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Graduate_School_of_Business "Stanford Graduate School of Business").

While working for ESPN in 2006, Gupta created the 'NBA Trade Machine' on the network's website, providing users with the opportunity to virtually assume the role of a general manager. The website incorporates current [player contracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highest-paid_NBA_players_by_season "Highest-paid NBA players by season") and integrates the rules of the [NBA Collective Bargaining Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Collective_Bargaining_Agreement "NBA Collective Bargaining Agreement"), accurately assessing whether a trade is possible based on [salary cap restrictions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_salary_cap "NBA salary cap") and other provisions in the [CBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Basketball_Association "Chinese Basketball Association").[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-2) The inspiration for the website came from Chris Ramsay, the son of [Jack Ramsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Ramsay "Jack Ramsay").

## Executive career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Gupta_(executive)&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Executive career")\]

Gupta started his career as a special advisor (2006–13) to GM [Daryl Morey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryl_Morey "Daryl Morey") with the [Houston Rockets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_Rockets "Houston Rockets"), switched to consultant (2013) and VP of basketball operations with the [Philadelphia 76ers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_76ers "Philadelphia 76ers") (2014–16) under [Sam Hinkie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Hinkie "Sam Hinkie"), and transitioned to assistant [general manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_manager "General manager") (2018–19) with the [Detroit Pistons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_Pistons "Detroit Pistons").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-5)

In 2018, [Daryl Morey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryl_Morey "Daryl Morey") said, "Sachin's fingerprints are all over this time, from his first six seasons with the Rockets to contributions he's made this past year."

In May 2019, the [Minnesota Timberwolves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Timberwolves "Minnesota Timberwolves") named Gupta as the new EVP of Basketball Operations under president [Gersson Rosas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gersson_Rosas "Gersson Rosas").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-6)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-7) Gupta provided specialized expertise on the [NBA Collective Bargaining Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Collective_Bargaining_Agreement "NBA Collective Bargaining Agreement") and team analytics.

General Manager [Scott Layden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Layden "Scott Layden") said at the time, “Sachin makes the team better because we know that we have in front of us, a guy who was really a great friend, but also we know he's the smartest guy in the room who can help us and steer us in the right direction.”

Rosas praised Sachin's basketball expertise and diverse professional background, noting that his role in Minnesota extended beyond statistical analysis to include regular scouting assignments, film sessions and collaboration with coaches and players. [\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-8)

On September 22, 2021, shortly after Rosas was relieved of his duties as President of Basketball Operations,[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-9) Gupta was promoted as the new interim President of Basketball Operations, while keeping his role as EVP of Basketball Operations.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-10)

In September 2024 it was announced that Gupta would leave his position at the Timberwolves to join soccer club [Chelsea F.C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_F.C. "Chelsea F.C.").[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Gupta_%28executive%29#cite_note-11)
