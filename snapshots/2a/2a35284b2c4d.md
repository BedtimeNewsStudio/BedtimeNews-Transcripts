**1\. Introduction**

University of Science and Technology of Hanoi (USTH) is a public university established in 2009 within the framework of the Intergovernmental Agreement between the two countries between Vietnam and France with the orientation to become an excellent university for training in science and technology.

USTH is under the Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology (VAST), the leading research academy in Vietnam. In addition, the USTH University also receives strong support from the French consortium of 40 French universities and institutions in training and research. The USTH university is proud to be a symbol of higher education and research cooperation between Vietnam and France.

**2\. Scope of Work**

USTH is looking for candidates to hold the position of Technician for the Equipment Management Department with specific duties as follows:

– Carry out measurements and analysis on the lab’s equipment system;

– Operate auxiliary equipment and monitor consumption of energy, electricity, water, gas, and consumables;

– Monitor and record equipment usage’s lab note, keep equipment records, assess equipment’s operating status, maintain and operate equipment assigned in charge;

– Prepare tools and equipment for students to practice;

– Inspect and maintain equipment and tools;

– Operate a safe and clean Labs;

– Monthly reporting on the status of assigned equipment;

– Perform other tasks as requested by the Head of Equipment Management Department and the Rector Board.

**3. Qualifications, Skill, and Experience Requirements**

– Graduated from Universities, Colleague or Vocational Schools in one of the following majors: Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Automation, Electro-mechanical Engineering, Electronic Physics, Engineering Physics, Materials Engineering, Biology, Biochemistry, Biological Engineering, and Chemical Engineering;

– Have working experience in the position of operating for machinery and equipment in the above specialties;

– Able to communicate in English;

– Agility, hard work, creativity, honesty and high sense of responsibility;

– Proficient in MS offices and other relative computer’s software.

**4\. Application**

The application package must be in English and contain:

– Curriculum vitae in English

–  Application letter in English

– Copy of diplomas and certificates (if any)

– Recommendation letters (if any)
