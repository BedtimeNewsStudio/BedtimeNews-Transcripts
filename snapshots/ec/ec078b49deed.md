# Goods Exports and Imports December 2024

## Goods Exports and Imports December 2024

## Exports level hits record €224 billion in 2024

Online ISSN: 2009-5155

CSO statistical release, 17 February 2025, 11am

### Key Findings

- The first estimates for 2024 show that exports were valued at a record €223.8 billion, and imports were valued at €133.6 billion.

- Unadjusted goods exports were €15.5 billion in December 2024, an increase of €1.7 billion on December 2023.

- Unadjusted goods imports fell by €309.3 million to €11.7 billion in December 2024 compared with December 2023.

- Seasonally adjusted goods exports were €17.4 billion in December 2024, a decline of €2.8 billion compared with the previous month.

- Seasonally adjusted goods imports were €11.0 billion in December 2024, down €517.7 million when compared with November 2024.

- Exports of goods to the United States were €72.6 billion in 2024, up €18.6 billion (+34%) on 2023, while imports of goods from the US were €22.5 billion, a decrease of 2% on 2023.

- Exports of Medical & Pharmaceutical Products rose by €22.4 billion (+29%) to €99.9 billion in 2024 compared with 2023, while imports grew by €3.0 billion (+24%) to €15.7 billion.

### Statistician's Comment

The Central Statistics Office (CSO) has today (17 February 2025) published Goods Exports and Imports December 2024.

Commenting on the release, Jane Burmanje, Statistician in the International Trade in Goods Division said: _“In 2024, Ireland’s exports of goods reached a record level of €224 billion, an increase of €28 billion compared with 2023. Imports of goods in 2024 were €134 billion, €6.7 billion lower than the 2023 level, but €30 billion higher than in 2021._

_In December 2024, Ireland’s unadjusted exports of goods increased by €1.7 billion to €15.5 billion compared with December 2023. The unadjusted imports for December 2024 were valued at €11.7 billion, a decrease of €309 million on December 2023._

_Seasonally adjusted imports were €11 billion in December 2024._ _When seasonally adjusted, exports of goods fell by €2.8 billion to €17.4 billion when compared with November 2024._

_Exports of goods to the United States were €72.6 billion in 2024, an increase of €18.6 billion (+34%) on 2023, while imports of goods from the US were €22.5 billion, a decrease of 2% on 2023._

_Both exports and imports of Medical & Pharmaceutical Products increased in 2024 compared with 2023. In 2024, exports of Medical & Pharmaceutical Products rose by €22.4 billion (+29%) to €99.9 billion, while imports grew by €3 billion (+24%) to €15.7 billion compared with 2023. Exports of Medical & Pharmaceutical Products accounted for 45% of good exports in 2024.”_

### Seasonally adjusted goods trade exports and imports decreased in December 2024

Seasonally adjusted goods exports fell by €2.8 billion (-14%) to €17.4 billion in December 2024 compared with November 2024, while seasonally adjusted imports of goods fell by €518 million (-5%) to €11.0 billion, according to preliminary figures.

This led to a decrease of €2.2 billion (-26%) in the seasonally adjusted trade surplus in goods to €6.5 billion in December 2024 compared with November 2024 _(See Table 2)_. Seasonal adjustment compares month-to-month data by removing fluctuations that may occur due to seasonal patterns in trade.

### Exports and Imports

The (unadjusted) value of goods exports increased by €1.7 billion (+13%) to €15.5 billion in December 2024 compared with December 2023 (€13.8 billion).

The value of goods exports for the year 2024 was up by €27.8 billion (+14%) to €223.8 billion, when compared with 2023 (€196.0 billion).

The (unadjusted) value of goods imports decreased by €309.3 million (-3%) to €11.7 billion in December 2024 compared with December 2023 (€12 billion).

The value of goods imports for 2024 decreased by €6.7 billion (-5%) to €133.6 billion, when compared with 2023.
