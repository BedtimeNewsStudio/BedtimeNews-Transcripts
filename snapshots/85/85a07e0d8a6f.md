# FIDIC Awards 2015 - Results

The following projects were awarded at the recent FIDIC Awards Gala Dinner celebrated in the Atlantis Asateer in Dubai, UAE on Monday 14 September 2015.
The award categories recognised were, "Outstanding Project of the Year", and "Award of Merit" in 2015.

Please see below the list of awarded projects in alphabetical order by country. Pictures of the Awards Gala are also available on the link below.

Congratulations to all companies awarded!

### Click [**here**](https://www.flickr.com/gp/135673489@N07/ufpQ60) to access FIDIC Awards Gala pictures.
