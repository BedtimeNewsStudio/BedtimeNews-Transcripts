Dec 10, 2025

U.S. Marines with Marine Fighter Attack Squadron (VMFA) 225, U.S. Marine Corps Forces, South, utilize a Short for Airfield Tactical Support loader to load a GBU-54 Laser Joint Direct Attack Munition onto a U.S. Marine Corps F-35B Lightning II assigned to VMFA-225 during a hot load training at Jose Aponte de la Torre Airport in Ceiba, Puerto Rico, Oct. 31, 2025. U.S. military forces are deployed to the Caribbean in support of the U.S. Southern Command mission, Department of War-directed operations, and the president’s priorities to disrupt illicit drug trafficking and protect the homeland.
