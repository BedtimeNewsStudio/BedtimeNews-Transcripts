July 13, 2023

SAG-AFTRA’s National Board convened this morning, July 13, and voted to issue a strike of the Producers–SAG-AFTRA TV/Theatrical Contracts of 2014, as amended by the 2017 and 2020 memoranda of agreement, effective July 14, at 12:01 a.m.

Please download, review and save the [TV/Theatrical/Streaming strike order](https://www.sagaftra.org/files/sa_documents/Strike%20Notice%20to%20Members.pdf), which can be found at the link below.

Picketing will begin at 9 a.m. PT/12 p.m. ET Friday.

Additional information, FAQs and picket locations can be found at [sagaftrastrike.org](https://www.sagaftrastrike.org/).

### About SAG-AFTRA

SAG-AFTRA represents approximately 160,000 actors, announcers, broadcast journalists, dancers, DJs, news writers, news editors, program hosts, puppeteers, recording artists, singers, stunt performers, voiceover artists and other entertainment and media professionals. SAG-AFTRA members are the faces and voices that entertain and inform America and the world. A proud affiliate of the AFL-CIO, SAG-AFTRA has national offices in Los Angeles and New York and local offices nationwide representing members working together to secure the strongest protections for entertainment and media artists into the 21st century and beyond. Visit SAG-AFTRA online at [SAGAFTRA.org.](https://www.sagaftra.org/)
