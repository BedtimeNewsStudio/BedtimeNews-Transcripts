# CCDC response following a pre-print describing a papermill involving crystal structures

Modified on Thu, 18 Dec, 2025 at 2:28 PM

Following the publication of a pre-print on Research Square ( [https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1)) in 2022, describing the activities of a prolific papermill in crystallography, the CCDC began investigations into potentially fraudulent data in the Cambridge Structural Database (CSD).

Our investigations found 992 structures linked to publications named in the pre-print. We added a note to all impacted structures in the WebCSD which reads “This structure is currently under review following a 2022 study of a prolific papermill  [https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1).”

In the course of investigations, all implicated publishers were contacted. Following contact with one journal, comments for 39 structures (4% of implicated structures) related to the papermill pre-print to say that following extensive review by the publishers and the CCDC there are currently no concerns on the data.

As publications have been retracted by journals, the CCDC has retracted entries to reflect this. Thanks to the efforts of the publishers, 686 entries (69%) implicated by the pre-print have been retracted through our platforms. As of the 2025 release, after several rounds of investigation, internal discussion and attempts made to contact journals, CCDC have come to a decision on how to approach the remaining 267 structures.

The CCDC aims to be a record of all published scientific literature. As these structures have not been retracted by their respective journals, we feel a duty to include these structures in the CSD in some form. We have thus decided that all published structures implicated in the papermill pre-print, where we have no integrity concerns about the data and we have not received information from journals that their investigations have concluded, will be added to the CSD with 3D coordinates removed. This will mean that these structures will not be able to be found when performing a 3D search of the CSD – but they will be able to be found through all other searches (e.g. 2D diagram search, chemical name search, formula search). This will also mean that 3D information, such as bond lengths, angles and torsions, will not become part of our knowledge bases – Mogul and Isostar.

We recognize that integrity investigations can take many years, and that investigations may still be ongoing. We have taken this decision, in part, due to the large number of implicated structures that have already been retracted. However, we also recognize that not every implicated publication that was assessed by a publisher was found to have misconduct or required retraction. This decision allows the structures to become part of the CSD and searchable by users while we await the final judgements of the journal.

There are a small number of structures implicated by the papermill pre-print where we do have concerns about the data. This may be due to similarity between that dataset and another structure in the CSD. In these cases, we have contacted the journal about our concerns. While we wait for responses, these structures will be added to the CSD with no 3D coordinates or 2D diagrams. This means that the structures also won’t be able to be found by a 2D diagram search – but will be findable in all other searches.

Should the associated paper be retracted in future, the structures will be retracted. Conversely, should we receive communication that the journals have completed their investigations and no action will be taken, we will reinstate the structure as a standard entry.

Last updated: 15/12/25

For detailed responses to our investigations over time, please see below.

### 2025

Update 29th April

As publications have been retracted by journals, the CCDC has retracted entries to reflect this. Thanks to the efforts of the publishers, 674 entries (68%) implicated by the pre-print have been retracted through our platforms. Investigations are continuing for the remaining 279 structures implicated by the pre-print and we are continuing to monitor and investigate new cases as these are identified. The CCDC has also invested in the automation of more of our data integrity checks and processes and we will continue to look at how we can broaden these and work with publishers to support their peer review processes.

### 2024

#### Update: 6th December

In our [March 2024 desktop release](https://www.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/discover/blog/1-3-million-and-beyond-the-2024-march-data-update/), a further 110 entries implicated by [a pre-print about papermills](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1) were retracted, taking the total number of retractions related to the papermill pre-print in the CSD to 471 and the total number of retractions in the CSD to 814.

Since then, prior to the June 2024 data update, thanks to the efforts of the publishers a further 123 entries implicated by the pre-print have been retracted through our web platforms (Access Structures and WebCSD).

In the December 2024 release, the total number of retracted structures in the CSD is now 1002. This includes a further 78 retractions relating to the papermill pre-print (bringing the total of related retractions to 672). With the main release, these retractions will now appear in the Desktop version of the CSD (upon the updating the software), as well as being visible on our web platforms.

This means that 68% of the entries implicated by a pre-print about papermills have been retracted and a further 4% of entries have additional comments to let users know that following extensive review by the publishers and the CCDC there are currently no concerns on the data.

Investigations are continuing for the remaining 281 structures implicated by the pre-print and we are continuing to monitor and investigate new cases as these are identified. We have also invested in automating more of our data integrity checks and processes and we will continue to look at how we can broaden these and work with publishers to support their peer review processes.

### 2023

#### Update: 1st November

In our last data release of 2023, scheduled for later this year, a further 152 entries implicated by [the pre-print](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1) will be retracted. These retractions are already visible to users accessing the CSD via our web platforms and takes the total number of retractions related to the papermill pre-print in the CSD to 361.

We have updated the comments for 39 structures related to the papermill pre-print to say that following extensive review by the publishers and the CCDC there are currently no concerns on the data. A further 36 structures related to the papermill pre-print have been updated where our investigations have identified the entry has almost identical reflection data with another entry in the CSD with a different structural formula.

Investigations are continuing for the remaining 556 structures implicated by the pre-print. Cases where reflection data has not yet been made available can be especially challenging to assess. We will continue to work with publishers, reviewers and depositors and are grateful for how the community has come together to tackle the issues so far.

Work to broaden the automated data integrity checks and processes, conversations with publishers on how we can support their peer review processes as well as collaborations with the community remain a key priority for the CCDC.

#### Update: 27th April

Our investigations continue on structures associated with a [pre-print on papermills in crystallography](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1). Previously, all structures were updated to contain an editorial comment that was visible in WebCSD. With this release this comment will now be visible on implicated structures in our desktop software.

Since being made aware of the pre-print, all implicated publishers have been contacted. We are aware of 125 publications that have been retracted as the result of investigations by the publishers. Therefore, 209 of the implicated structures have now been retracted from the CSD. The existing editorial comment on a further 39 structures has been updated to reflect where publishers have investigated and confirmed no issues have been identified (in agreement with CCDC integrity investigations).

We will continue to collaborate with publishers on the remaining structures following COPE guidelines and extend these new integrity checks out more widely in the CSD.

[More about the data update here](https://www.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/discover/blog/new-and-notable-structures-added-to-the-csd-additional-improvements-and-data-integrity-update/).

### 2022

#### Update: 15th June 2022

In the latest CSD data update we have suspended the addition of 277 structures linked to publications implicated in the pre-print that had yet to be included in the desktop data release. Alongside these datasets we have also delayed the release of a number of other datasets related to those implicated in the pre-print, while our investigations continue.

In addition, we have removed all structures linked to publications mentioned in the papermill pre-print from our data subsets to minimize the impact of these structures.

[More about the data update here.](https://www.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/Community/blog/june-2022-csd-update/)

#### Update: 5th May 2022

Our investigation following the recent pre-print on a prolific papermill in crystallography ( [https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1)) has found 992 structures linked to publications named there.

Of these, 715 are in the latest desktop release (2021.3  March 2022 CSD data update) and 277 are only available in our online versions of the CSD.

We have added a note to all impacted structures in the WebCSD which reads _“This structure is currently under review following a 2022 study of a prolific papermill_ _[https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1)_ _._ _” For example:_

Please contact [support@ccdc.cam.ac.uk](mailto:support@ccdc.cam.ac.uk) if you have any questions or concerns regarding this matter.

#### Update: 28th April 2022

Following the recent pre-print publication on Research Square ( [https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1537438/v1)) we have begun investigations into potentially fraudulent data in the Cambridge Structural Database (CSD).

At the time of writing, we have so far identified 620 structures in the CSD which are associated to publications named in the pre-print. Our database team and data integrity scientist are working with our contacts at the other databases and the journals involved to fully understand the issue.

We take this matter very seriously. The CSD aims to be an accurate reflection of the literature, and so we will follow developments closely with the journals involved, and retract data when appropriate.

We are also working to add a note to all affected structures which will be visible on WebCSD once complete.

Our investigation to understand the issue and the root cause continues, internally and with our partners. We will post updates here as we learn more.

We acknowledge and appreciate the work the author of the pre-print has undertaken, as well as others that have taken efforts to alert us when issues like this arise.

Please contact [support@ccdc.cam.ac.uk](mailto:support@ccdc.cam.ac.uk) if you have any questions or concerns regarding this matter.
