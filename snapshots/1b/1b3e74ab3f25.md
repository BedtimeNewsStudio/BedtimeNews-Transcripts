# Benzema: "No one can force me to sing the Marseillaise"

03/19/2013

Speaking to French radio channel 'RMC', Karim Benzema aired his views on the France national team and hit back at the people who have condemned him for not singing the national anthem before games. "I've never sung the Marseillaise, and it's not like singing it would make me score a hat-trick", he said.

The Real Madrid striker was nevertheless keen to press home how much he enjoys representing 'Les Bleus'. "I love the national side. It's a dream to play for France, but no one can force me to sing the Marseillaise. I don't see what the problem is. Zidane, for example, never sang it either".

Benzema, who hasn't scored an international goal since notching in a friendly against Estonia on 5th June 2012, also told listeners that he needs more support to get firing again for France.

"I give my all out on the pitch, but I need help from my teammates. I can't do it all on my own", the Real Madrid forward stated, looking ahead to the decisive 2014 World Cup qualifiers against Georgia and Spain.

"I can't just pick up the ball and dribble past eight players", Benzema noted. "People only talk about the chances I've missed, but no one ever mentions the goals I've set up for my teammates. It's as if that didn't count for anything", the France marksman added.
