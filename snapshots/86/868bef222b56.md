# Vietnamese Deputy Defense Minister Sexually Harasses South Korean Official

By Lee Ka-young

Published 2025.10.21. 06:55Updated 2025.10.21. 15:25

![On the 11th, Vice Defense Minister Lee Doo-hee shakes hands with Vietnam's Vice Defense Minister Hoang Xuan Chien and takes a commemorative photo at the Ministry of National Defense. /Yonhap](https://www.chosun.com/resizer/v2/JTFTVVDRNNK23CKZXUL5NEQDPU.jpg?auth=0c98ed4c08ed8fef1c717c14cf7cfca11aaa23b781d4f8e8b70c6ce02cfa8528&width=616)

On the 11th, Vice Defense Minister Lee Doo-hee shakes hands with Vietnam's Vice Defense Minister Hoang Xuan Chien and takes a commemorative photo at the Ministry of National Defense. /Yonhap

It was belatedly revealed that a Vietnamese deputy defense minister who visited South Korea last month sexually harassed a South Korean defense ministry official.

According to the military on the 21st, Hoang Xuan Chien, the Vietnamese deputy defense minister who visited South Korea on the occasion of the Seoul Defense Dialogue, SDD, was reported to have made inappropriate physical contact with a female South Korean official at a dinner attended by high-ranking military officials from both countries on the 11th of last month. The defense ministry, deeming the case serious, summoned a Vietnamese military attaché in South Korea eight days after the incident to protest.

The defense ministry condemned the deputy minister’s actions to the Vietnamese side and requested prevention of recurrence, and the Vietnamese side was reported to have expressed their intention to prevent recurrence.

A source from the defense ministry stated, “We have taken appropriate measures in accordance with principles,” adding, “Disclosure of detailed factual relations is limited in consideration of the victim’s wishes.”
