# All Tokyo 2020 new permanent venues completed on schedule

The Tokyo Organising Committee of the Olympic and Paralympic Games (Tokyo 2020) today announced the completion of all new permanent venues for the Olympic and Paralympic Games Tokyo 2020.

![All Tokyo 2020 new permanent venues completed on schedule](https://img.olympics.com/images/image/private/t_s_16_9_g_auto/t_s_w1460/f_auto/primary/ku1wkzflo0mfy5ckoj58)

06 March 2020 05:43 GMT-51 min read

[Add as preferred on Google](https://www.google.com/preferences/source?q=olympics.com)

The construction of the Tokyo Aquatics Centre has been completed on schedule by the end of February. Constructed by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government (TMG), the Tokyo Aquatics Centre is scheduled to be inaugurated on 22 March.

[![](https://img.olympics.com/images/image/private/t_s_1-1_g_auto/w_75/f_auto/primary/px4gjgfe8h6yaboyesf2)\\
\\

A total of 43 venues — eight new permanent venues, 25 existing facilities and 10 temporary venues — will be used at the Tokyo 2020 Games. With the completion of all new permanent venues, the overall preparation of all venues is progressing on schedule.
