# St. George classmates speak about suspect arrested for assassination of Charlie Kirk

[Skip to content](#content)

by: Joseph DeGolyer, [Sorina Trauntvein](https://www.abc4.com/author/sorina-trauntvein/ "Posts by Sorina Trauntvein"), [Cannon Secrist](https://www.abc4.com/author/cannon-secrist/ "Posts by Cannon Secrist")

Posted: Sep 12, 2025 / 01:55 PM MDT

Updated: Sep 12, 2025 / 04:41 PM MDT

SHARE

ST. GEORGE, Utah ( **[ABC4](http://abc4.com/)**) — The suspect arrested in connection with the assassination of Charlie Kirk was reportedly a 22-year-old college student in St. George: Tyler James Robinson.

Dixie Technical College has confirmed that the suspect arrested in connection with the deadly shooting of Charlie Kirk at Utah Valley University in Orem on Sept. 10th was a third-year student in their electrical apprenticeship program. Due to student privacy laws, they did not release any additional information.

According to a statement from Utah State University (USU) in Logan and the Utah Board of Higher Education (UBHE), he also completed a semester at USU. UBHE stated that Robinson also received credits from Utah Tech University in St. George through concurrent enrollment while in high school from 2019 to 2021.

Ethan Samfong, a student at Dixie Technical College, spoke with ABC4’s Joseph DeGolyer about attending the same school as Robinson.

“I was actually in class when I found out, and they were like going crazy and videos were everywhere. I’m a huge supporter of him, I watch him all the time… at first I thought it was fake because like, that’s just crazy that it’s this local,” Samfong shared.

Samfong told ABC4 that he recently learned that he lived near the suspected shooter.

“I lived next to the guy, so that was really scary to find out last night. And then to find out that he was a few doors away or a building away, it’s kind of frightening,” Samfong expressed.

Another student who didn’t want to appear on camera said he was in the same program as Robinson at Dixie Technical College. That student said Robinson was a “quiet, unassuming” young man.

“I had a couple friends that went to technical college with him — electrical school. But, they pretty much just said he sat in the corner by himself, didn’t say much,” that student’s friend said.

Kristin Schwiermann, **[a neighbor who lived just a few doors down](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/neighbors-suspect-manhunt-charlie-kirk-shooter/)** from Robinson’s family, confirmed that he was a Pine View High School graduate. She stated that she heard he had high ACT scores and earned a scholarship.

“It’s kind of scary, you know, because there’s been a lot of different things that happened to St George recently, like the Ruby Franke situation, now this,” Caden Neilson, who says he attended Pine View High School with Robinson, said.

![](https://www.abc4.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2025/09/Featured-Image-24.png?w=900)2021 Yearbook photo of Tyler Robinson. (KTVX)2021 Yearbook photo of Tyler Robinson. (KTVX)

“It’s just wild to think that there’s all these people around here and you don’t know what’s going on or what people are thinking and planning on doing. Like no one would have ever expected someone from Washington, Utah going to try to and assassinate Charlie Kirk and succeeding with it,” Neilson continued.

Neilson, alongside other neighbors in the area who know the family, expressed that their hearts are with them after making the difficult decision to turn Robinson in for his alleged crime.

“You just got to do what you believe is best and what you believe is right. And they believed that the best thing to do is turn in their son. And I can imagine how hard that would be…” Neilson expressed. “I feel for their family, and I think it’s a scary time that they’re going through. And I hope for the best for all of them and all their siblings and everything and hope they do well.”

The full statement from the Utah Board of Higher Education regarding Robinson’s arrest is below.

> In response to the arrest of the suspect in the killing of Charlie Kirk, we can confirm that Tyler James Robinson is a third-year student in the electrical apprenticeship program at Dixie Technical College. He briefly attended Utah State University (one semester in 2021) and received concurrent enrollment credit through Utah Tech University while in high school (2019-2021).
>
> Utah’s colleges and universities work to ensure that more than 225,000 students are gaining the skills, knowledge and experiences they need to succeed in the workforce and build stronger communities. Our campuses foster exploration, learning and the respectful exchange of diverse viewpoints. Attempts to silence or intimidate those voices have no place here. We will continue working to ensure our students, faculty and guests can safely express their perspectives.
>
> – Utah Board of Higher Education statement
