## SLS (Space Launch System) Rocket

NASA’s Artemis rocket is the SLS (Space Launch System), a super heavy‑lift rocket that uses solid rocket boosters derived from the space shuttle and liquid-fueled engines attached to a new central core stage. SLS provides the power needed to lift the Orion spacecraft off Earth and send it into deep space.

[Learn More](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/space-launch-system/)

## Orion Spacecraft

The Orion spacecraft is specifically designed to carry astronauts on deep space exploration missions farther than ever before. To keep the crew and spacecraft safe, Orion incorporates a comprehensive suite of advanced systems—including environmental control and life support, navigation and communications technologies, radiation protection, and the world’s largest heat shield—designed to withstand the extreme conditions of deep space and ensure astronauts return safely home.

[Learn More](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/orion-spacecraft/)

![The Orion spacecraft in flight with the Moon visible in the distance](https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/art001e000255%20FD5/art001e000255%20FD5~large.jpg?w=1920&h=1440&fit=clip&crop=faces%2Cfocalpoint)

## Exploration Ground Systems

Exploration Ground Systems, based at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida, develops and operates the systems and facilities needed to process, launch, and recover rockets and spacecraft for NASA’s Artemis missions.

[Learn More](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/exploration-ground-systems/)

![](https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/NHQ202209240005/NHQ202209240005~large.jpg?w=1920&h=1184&fit=clip&crop=faces%2Cfocalpoint)
