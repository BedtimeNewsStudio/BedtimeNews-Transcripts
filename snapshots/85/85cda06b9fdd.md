Kirk, a conservative activist and co-founder of [Turning Point USA](https://www.newsweek.com/topic/turning-point-usa), was fatally shot at Utah Valley University on Wednesday. He was 31.

## What To Know

Matt Robinson is a registered [Republican](https://www.newsweek.com/topic/republican), according to public records reviewed by _Newsweek_. Early reports indicated that he was a veteran of the Washington County Sheriff's Office, but the Washington County Attorney's office said that reporting was not accurate and that Robinson does not have ties to local law enforcement.

"The individual arrested is NOT related to Washington County law enforcement! There are multiple people with the same names," the attorney's office said in a statement posted to [Facebook](https://www.newsweek.com/topic/facebook).

Utah Governor Spencer Cox said that a family member of Tyler Robinson contacted a family friend on Thursday, who then informed the Washington County Sheriff's Office of information indicating that Tyler had either confessed to or implied involvement in the incident.

Cox said Tyler was not a student at Utah Valley University.

A family member of Tyler was interviewed by investigators, Cox said. The family member said Tyler had become more political in recent years and mentioned that Kirk was coming to Utah Valley University to another family member at a dinner.

The family member said Tyler said he did not like Kirk and the viewpoints he held, according to the governor.

Tyler Robinson allegedly fled the scene of the shooting on Wednesday, prompting officials to launch a multi-day search. The [FBI](https://www.newsweek.com/topic/fbi) had offered a reward of up to $100,000 for information leading to the identification and arrest of the suspect.

Kirk leaves behind a wife and two children. Trump said he has spoken to Kirk's wife, Erika Kirk.

"She's devastated, but in between the devastation, they want to keep Turning Point going," Trump told reporters on Thursday.

## What People Are Saying

**FBI Director Kash Patel, at a press conference on Friday:**"The FBI and our partners are proud to stand here today together to bring justice to the family of Charlie Kirk and honor his memory."

**Utah Governor Spencer Cox, at a press conference on Friday:**"It is also much bigger than an attack on an individual. It is an attack on all of us. It is an attack on the American experiment. It is an attack on our ideals. This cuts to the very foundation of who we are, of who we have been and who we could be in better times."

## What Happens Next

Cox said Tyler Robinson "will be charged soon and will be held accountable" and that the suspect was booked into the Utah County Jail. He is believed to have acted alone, and officials have no information that would lead to any additional arrests.

The investigation is ongoing.
