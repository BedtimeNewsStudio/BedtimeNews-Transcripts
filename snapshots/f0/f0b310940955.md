**Sachin Hindurao Vaze** (born 22 February 1972) is a former Indian police officer who served as an [Assistant Police Inspector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector#India "Inspector") in [Mumbai Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Police "Mumbai Police") (under the [Maharashtra Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharashtra_Police "Maharashtra Police")). He was dismissed from service by Mumbai Police Commissioner under Article 311 (2) (b) without a departmental enquiry. He is currently in the custody of the [National Investigation Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Investigation_Agency "National Investigation Agency") for his involvement in the [Antilia bomb scare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antilia_bomb_scare "Antilia bomb scare") and the murder of Mansukh Hiren.[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1) He was a member of [Uddhav Thackeray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uddhav_Thackeray "Uddhav Thackeray") [Shiv Sena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiv_Sena_(1966%E2%80%932022) "Shiv Sena (1966–2022)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) Faction

Vaze was an " [encounter specialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encounter_specialist "Encounter specialist")" within the [Mumbai Encounter Squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Encounter_Squad "Mumbai Encounter Squad"); he was involved with extra judicial deaths of as many as 63 alleged criminals, while never being injured in any encounter. He was suspended for 17 years after he was arrested in the custodial death of Khwaja Yunus, but was granted bail and later reinstated in June 2017. He led the [Raigad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raigad_district "Raigad district") police team in the arrest of [Republic TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_TV "Republic TV") editor-in-chief, [Arnab Goswami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnab_Goswami "Arnab Goswami"). In March 2021, he was arrested again for his role and involvement in the Antilla Bomb Scare. After his arrest, he was suspended once again from police service. He was dismissed from his police position on 11 May 2021.[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3)

## Biography

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Vaze&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Biography")\]

Vaze was born on 22 February 1972 in [Kolhapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolhapur "Kolhapur"), [Maharashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharashtra "Maharashtra"). He joined the [State Police Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Police_Services "State Police Services") in 1990. He was first posted to the [Naxal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naxalite "Naxalite")-affected area of [Gadchiroli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gadchiroli "Gadchiroli").[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)

### 'Encounters' squad

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Vaze&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: 'Encounters' squad")\]

In 1992, Vaze was transferred to [Thane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thane "Thane") City [Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police "Police"), where he quickly gained fame for detecting several significant cases. Rising in rank, he eventually headed the Special Squad of the Thane Police and began carrying out [encounters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_encounter "Police encounter") in the city, allegedly killing over 60 criminals in the Mumbai-Thane area.This squad, formed around 1997 to address growing extortion demands and open violence, soon became associated with [encounter killings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encounter_killing "Encounter killing").[&#8202;](#ref-back-UAA-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-UAA)

Subsequently, Vaze was selected to work in the [Mumbai Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Police "Mumbai Police") elite Crime Intelligence Unit (CIU),[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6) and along with his mentor [Pradeep Sharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pradeep_Sharma_(police_officer) "Pradeep Sharma (police officer)"), later was transferred to the Anti- [Extortion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extortion "Extortion") Cell (AE Cell), where his first-ever encounter was recorded.

Vaze's career spanned 30 years, during which he became known for "encounter killings" of 63 alleged criminals, including notorious gangsters like _Munna Nepali_.[&#8202;](#ref-back-F-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-F)[&#8202;](#ref-back-R1RRPR-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-R1RRPR) Vaze also dealt with cases of counterfeit currency and was instrumental in dealing with cases involving film industry personalities.

In 2008, Vaze joined [Shiv Sena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiv_Sena_(1966%E2%80%932022) "Shiv Sena (1966–2022)"), a political party in [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India "India").[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-:0) However he never renewed his membership so it later "expired".[&#8202;](#ref-back-10-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-10)

### Suspension and reinstatement

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Vaze&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Suspension and reinstatement")\]

On 3 March 2004, Vaze and 14 other policemen were suspended on murder charges in the custodial death of **Khwaja Yunus**, a suspect in the 2002 [Ghatkopar bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Mumbai_bus_bombing "2002 Mumbai bus bombing") case. Vaze subsequently resigned from the police in November 2007 after requests for his reinstatement were rejected by the [Maharashtra Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Maharashtra "Government of Maharashtra").[&#8202;](#ref-back-HIndustan_Times-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-HIndustan_Times)

On 6 June 2020, Vaze's suspension was revoked by the [Maharashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharashtra "Maharashtra") government led by [Uddhav Thackeray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uddhav_Thackeray "Uddhav Thackeray"), and he was reinstated by [Mumbai Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Police "Mumbai Police") amid a police staff shortage at the height of the [COVID-19 pandemic in Maharashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Maharashtra "COVID-19 pandemic in Maharashtra").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Hindu-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-Hindu)
Nationalist Congress Party President [Sharad Pawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharad_Pawar "Sharad Pawar") said that neither Thackeray nor the state home minister [Anil Deshmukh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anil_Deshmukh "Anil Deshmukh") were responsible for the reinstatement.[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13)

In a press conference, Maharashtra ex- [CM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_minister_of_Maharashtra "Chief minister of Maharashtra") [Devendra Fadnavis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devendra_Fadnavis "Devendra Fadnavis") claimed that when he was Chief Minister of [Maharashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharashtra "Maharashtra") in 2018, [Shiv Sena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiv_Sena_(1966%E2%80%932022) "Shiv Sena (1966–2022)") asked him to reinstate Vaze, which he disposed after consulting the state Advocate General.[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14)

### Technology crime

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Vaze&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Technology crime")\]

Vaze is also known for his technology and cyber skills. Frequently seen with modern electronics in his hands and pockets, Vaze is known to have solved many serious cybercrimes. He is also believed to have established cellphone interception units and [email](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Email "Email") tracking units of [Mumbai Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Police "Mumbai Police"). Vaze came into the limelight in 1997 when he detected international [credit card fraud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_card_fraud "Credit card fraud"), which was the first time that a gang of such swindlers was arrested in [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia "Asia"). Vaze continued this effort in [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai "Mumbai") in 2001.[&#8202;](#ref-back-D-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-D)[&#8202;](#ref-back-I2-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-I2) There was conjecture that Vaze was framed in the Khwaja Yunus murder case.[&#8202;](#ref-back-H11-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-H11) Vaze resigned in 2007.[&#8202;](#ref-back-UAAAA-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-UAAAA)

Vaze also released a mobile application called MobiCID. In short order, MobiCID gained the attention of various media sectors.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Tcknnkmt-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-Tcknnkmt)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Cknnkmt-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-Cknnkmt)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Znkmt-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-Znkmt)

## 2021 Antilia bomb scare case

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Vaze&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: 2021 Antilia bomb scare case")\]

Main article: [Antilia bomb scare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antilia_bomb_scare "Antilia bomb scare")

On 25 February 2021, a [Mahindra Scorpio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahindra_Scorpio "Mahindra Scorpio") SUV with fake license plates and around 20 explosive gelatin sticks was parked outside Mumbai businessman [Mukesh Ambani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukesh_Ambani "Mukesh Ambani")'s residence [Antilia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antilia_(building) "Antilia (building)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-22-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-22) Vaze was the first investigating officer in the bomb-scare case.[&#8202;](#ref-back-reconstruction-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-reconstruction) The presumed car owner, Mansukh Hiren, who had filed a police complaint about the SUV being stolen, was later found dead in a creek near [Thane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thane "Thane") on 5 March 2021.[&#8202;](#ref-back-24-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-24) Three days before his death, Hiren had written a letter accusing the police of harassment. The leader of opposition of Maharashtra, [Devendra Fadnavis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devendra_Fadnavis "Devendra Fadnavis") demanded the arrest of Vaze for Hiren's alleged murder.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:1-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-:1) Fadnavis claimed that Hiren's wife suspected that Vaze killed her husband.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:1-1)[\[25\]](#refnote-:1)[&#8202;](#ref-back-26-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-26)

Vaze applied for interim relief, but was rejected by the [Sessions court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sessions_Court "Sessions Court") in Thane. After over 12 hours of questioning, he was arrested by [National Investigation Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Investigation_Agency "National Investigation Agency") on 13 March for his involvement in placing the explosives-laden vehicle near [Carmichael Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmichael_Road "Carmichael Road") on 25 February.[&#8202;](#ref-back-27-0)[\[27\]](#refnote-27)[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-28) The NIA searched Vaze's office, seizing documents, electronic devices, and a [Mercedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz "Mercedes-Benz") vehicle being used by Vaze, which contained ₹500,000 cash, a cash-counting machine and the original license plates of the Scorpio found earlier near [Antilia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antilia_(building) "Antilia (building)").
Assistant [Sub-Inspector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-inspector "Sub-inspector") of Police Sunil Toke claimed that Vaze drove the seized Mercedes car into the Commissionerate of Police.[&#8202;](#ref-back-29-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-29) An Assistant Police Inspector Riyazuddin Kazi was investigated who had removed a Digital Video Recorder (DVR) of CCTVs from the housing society where Vaze lived in Thane.[&#8202;](#ref-back-30-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-30) As part of reconstructing the sequence of events, Vaze was made to walk near the spot where the SUV was found.[&#8202;](#ref-back-reconstruction-1)[\[23\]](#refnote-reconstruction)

In a 21 March 2021 statement, the Maharashtra anti-terrorism squad named Vaze as the prime suspect in Mansukh Hiran's murder case. ATS has arrested cricket bookie Naresh Ramniklal Ghor and police officer Vinayak Balasaheb Shinde, who worked with Vaze, for their involvement.[&#8202;](#ref-back-31-0)[\[31\]](#refnote-31)[&#8202;](#ref-back-32-0)[\[32\]](#refnote-32) Officer Shinde had allegedly asked Hiren to meet him at [Ghodbunder Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghodbunder_Road "Ghodbunder Road"). Shinde was a constable in the same crime branch unit where Vaze was posted, and was already serving a life sentence after being convicted in the fake encounter case of _Lakhan Bhaiyya,_ but was out on furlough. On 11 May 2021, he was officially dismissed from the Mumbai police.[&#8202;](#ref-back-33-0)[\[33\]](#refnote-33)

### Extortion allegation

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Vaze&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Extortion allegation")\]

Vaze's supervisor, [Param Bir Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Param_Bir_Singh "Param Bir Singh"), who had worked closely with him, was removed from his post of Mumbai Police Commissioner. Singh claimed, in a detailed letter to the Maharashtra Chief Minister, that Vaze was involved in a scheme to collect [₹](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_rupee_sign "Indian rupee sign") 100 [Crore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crore "Crore") from businesses in Mumbai per month as demanded by the Maharashtra home minister [Anil Deshmukh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anil_Deshmukh "Anil Deshmukh").[&#8202;](#ref-back-34-0)[\[34\]](#refnote-34)[&#8202;](#ref-back-35-0)[\[35\]](#refnote-35) Deshmukh's office denied the claim and stated that Param Bir Singh made false allegations against him to save himself from legal action.[&#8202;](#ref-back-36-0)[\[36\]](#refnote-36)[&#8202;](#ref-back-37-0)[\[37\]](#refnote-37)
Devendra Fadnavis claimed that Vaze and Param Bir Singh were just pawns in the hands of the current [Maha Vikas Aghadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maha_Vikas_Aghadi "Maha Vikas Aghadi") (a coalition of [NCP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalist_Congress_Party "Nationalist Congress Party"), [Shiv Sena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiv_Sena_(1966%E2%80%932022) "Shiv Sena (1966–2022)") and [Indian National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Congress "Indian National Congress")) state government.[&#8202;](#ref-back-38-0)[\[38\]](#refnote-38) Param Bir Singh asked the Supreme Court on 22 March for a [CBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bureau_of_Investigation "Central Bureau of Investigation") probe against Deshmukh, claiming interference by him in police investigations.[&#8202;](#ref-back-39-0)[\[39\]](#refnote-39)

Mumbai-based [newspaper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newspaper "Newspaper") Mid-day has learned from members of Indian Hotel and Restaurant Association (AHAR) that Vaze had started collecting money from December 2020. Vaze would call from his office inside the compound of the Commissioner of Police office to pay monthly amounts and if paid, he would ensure that Social Service Branch would not conduct any raids. It reported a source stating that "SSB carried out raids at hotels and bars and filed cases. The last SSB raid was in early February. After that, no raid was conducted as everybody agreed to pay the money to be able to operate."[&#8202;](#ref-back-40-0)[\[40\]](#refnote-40)

## Books, publications, movies

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sachin_Vaze&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Books, publications, movies")\]

While in service Vaze was instrumental in forming new rules, manuals, and directives for Maharashtra Police. After resigning from service Vaze participated in television debates, and published articles in newspapers and periodicals.[&#8202;](#ref-back-RsDCbxR-0)[\[42\]](#refnote-RsDCbxR) He has also been publishing articles on cyber crime issues. He has been a researcher, advisor and contributor to various leading newspapers, TV channels as well as online portals.[&#8202;](#ref-back-RsDCKvR-0)[\[43\]](#refnote-RsDCKvR) He was the creator of the first social networking portal in Marathi, laibhaari.com, a Marathi version of Facebook. The portal put on test run on 4 November 2010 and had a membership figure of more than 15,000 till it formally launched on 21 November 2010.[&#8202;](#ref-back-RmR1-0)[\[44\]](#refnote-RmR1)[&#8202;](#ref-back-R8RRnm1w-0)[\[45\]](#refnote-R8RRnm1w)

Vaze is the author of _Jinkun Harleli Ladhaai_ (Winning and losing battle) in Marathi, an account of the [26/11 Mumbai attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Mumbai_attacks "2008 Mumbai attacks").[&#8202;](#ref-back-RsDCvR-0)[\[46\]](#refnote-RsDCvR) He also wrote _Sheena Bora - The Murder That Shook India_, about the [Sheena Bora murder case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheena_Bora_murder_case "Sheena Bora murder case") in Mumbai.[&#8202;](#ref-back-RsDmCKvR-0)[\[47\]](#refnote-RsDmCKvR)

He has served as a source and advisor on books projects by [Hussain Zaidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hussain_Zaidi "Hussain Zaidi") ( _Headley & I_, _My Name is Abu Salem_, _[Byculla to Bangkok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byculla_to_Bangkok "Byculla to Bangkok")_), [Adrian Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Levy "Adrian Levy") and Shirish Thorat ( _The Scout: The Definitive Account of David Headley and the Mumbai Attacks_).[&#8202;](#ref-back-RskDCR2-0)[\[48\]](#refnote-RskDCR2)

Vaze was portrayed by [Pushkar Shrotri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushkar_Shrotri "Pushkar Shrotri") in the 2014 Indian [Marathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathi_language "Marathi language")-language crime-thriller film _[Rege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rege "Rege")_.
