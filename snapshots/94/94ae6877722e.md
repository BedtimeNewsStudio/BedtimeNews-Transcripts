# Celotex RS5000 PIR insulation

A [rainscreen](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen "Rainscreen") (sometimes referred to as a ‘drained and [ventilated](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Ventilated "Ventilated")’ or ‘pressure-equalised’ [façade](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fa%C3%A7ade "Façade")) is the outer [part](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Part "Part") of a double- [wall construction](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Wall_construction "Wall construction") that can be used to [form](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Form "Form") the [exterior walls](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Exterior_wall "Exterior wall") of [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building"). Typically, [rainscreens](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen "Rainscreen") are formed of relatively thin, [pre-fabricated](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Pre-fabricated "Pre-fabricated") [panels](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Panel "Panel") that prevent significant amounts of [water](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Water "Water") from penetrating into the [wall construction](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Wall_construction "Wall construction"). [Thermal insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Thermal_insulation "Thermal insulation"), [airtightness](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Airtightness "Airtightness") and [structural](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Structural "Structural") stability are provided by the second, inner [part](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Part "Part") of the [wall construction](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Wall_construction "Wall construction").

[Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") RS5000 is a type of [thermal insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Thermal_insulation "Thermal insulation") designed specifically for use in [rainscreen cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen_cladding "Rainscreen cladding") [systems](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems"). It [forms](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Form "Form") a [layer](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Layer "Layer") of [thermal insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Thermal_insulation "Thermal insulation") between the [rainscreen cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen_cladding "Rainscreen cladding") and the inner [part](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Part "Part") of the [wall construction](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Wall_construction "Wall construction"). It is formed from a rigid [polyisocyanurate](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyisocyanurate "Polyisocyanurate") foam [core](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Core "Core") (PIR) and has a low- [emissivity](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Emissivity "Emissivity") textured [aluminium](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Aluminium "Aluminium") foil facing.

[Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") have confirmed that they [supplied](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Supplied "Supplied") this [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") for the [Grenfell Tower](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_tower "Grenfell tower") [refurbishment](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Refurbishment "Refurbishment").

On 14 June 2017, a [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") broke out in [Grenfell Tower](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_tower "Grenfell tower"), a [block](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Block "Block") of [flats](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Flats "Flats") in North Kensington, [London](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/London "London"). The [fire spread](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_spread "Fire spread") up the outside of the [Tower](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Tower "Tower") unexpectedly quickly, and initially, suspicion focussed on the Reynobond PE [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") [rainscreen](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen "Rainscreen") [panels](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Panel "Panel"). However, on 23 June 2017, the Police confirmed that small- [scale](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Scale "Scale") [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") tests had been carried out on the Reynobond [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") and the [Celotex insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex_insulation "Celotex insulation") and both had failed.

Detective Superintendent Fiona McCormack said; "The [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") was more [flammable](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Flammable "Flammable") than the [cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Cladding "Cladding"). Tests show the [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") [samples](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Samples "Samples") combusted soon after the test started."

The [Building Regulations](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_regulations "Building regulations") require that [materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials") used in [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building") with [storeys](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Storey "Storey") more than 18 m above the [ground](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Ground "Ground") have [limited combustibility](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Limited_combustibility "Limited combustibility"). [Limited combustibility](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Limited_combustibility "Limited combustibility") can be demonstrated by [testing](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Testing "Testing") [materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials") in accordance with [BS 8414](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BS_8414 "BS 8414") and satisfying the [performance requirements](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Performance_requirements "Performance requirements") set out in [BR 135](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BR_135 "BR 135").

The [Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") website states specifically that [Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") RS5000; “Has been tested to BS 8414-2:2005, meets the requirements in [BR 135](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BR_135 "BR 135") and (sic) the first PIR [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") suitable for [rainscreen cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen_cladding "Rainscreen cladding") applications above 18 [metres](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Metre "Metre") in [height](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Height "Height")” Ref [https://www.celotex.co.uk/products/rs5000](https://www.celotex.co.uk/products/rs5000) Accessed 23 June 2017.

The [product](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Products "Products") is advertised as having a Class O [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") [performance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Performance "Performance") and is intended for use in warm [steel frame](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Steel_frame "Steel frame") [constructions](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Construction "Construction") (that is the [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") is on the outside of the [construction](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Construction "Construction")) for [ventilated](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Ventilated "Ventilated") facade applications ( [rainscreens](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen "Rainscreen")).

[BR 135](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BR_135 "BR 135"), ' [Fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") [performance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Performance "Performance") of external [thermal insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Thermal_insulation "Thermal insulation") for [walls](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Walls "Walls") of [multistorey](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Multistorey "Multistorey") [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building")', sets out principles, [design methodologies](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Design_methodology "Design methodology") and [fire spread](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_spread "Fire spread") [performance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Performance "Performance") characteristics for non- [loadbearing](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Loadbearing "Loadbearing") [cladding systems](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Cladding_system "Cladding system"). It was first published in 1988 in response to the increasing use of [thermal insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Thermal_insulation "Thermal insulation") in the [refurbishment](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Refurbishment "Refurbishment") of [multistorey](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Multistorey "Multistorey") [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building").

BS 8414-2:2005, ' [Fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") [performance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Performance "Performance") of [external cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/External_cladding "External cladding") [systems](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems")', describes the test method for non- [loadbearing](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Loadbearing "Loadbearing") [external cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/External_cladding "External cladding") [systems](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems").

' [Rainscreen Cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen_cladding "Rainscreen cladding") [Compliance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Compliance "Compliance") [Guide](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Guide "Guide") when [specifying](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Specifying "Specifying") [Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") RS5000 in [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building") above 18 [metres](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Metre "Metre")', explains the route taken to meet the [performance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Performance "Performance") criteria set out in [BR 135](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BR_135 "BR 135") through [testing](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Testing "Testing") to BS 8414-1:2002 or BS 8414-2:2005. [https://www.celotex.co.uk/assets/rainscreen-compliance\_specificationguide\_mar15.pdf](https://www.celotex.co.uk/assets/rainscreen-compliance_specificationguide_mar15.pdf)

This [process](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Process "Process") involves [testing](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Testing "Testing") the [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems") as a whole, not just the [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") [material](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials") itself. It states:

|  |
| --- |
| [Fire stopping](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_stopping "Fire stopping") was provided by [ventilated](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Ventilated "Ventilated") horizontal [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") breaks positioned at each [floor slab](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Floor_slab "Floor slab") edge and above the [hearth](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Hearth "Hearth") opening. Vertical non- [ventilated](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Ventilated "Ventilated") [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") breaks were provided at [the edges](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/The_Edge "The Edge") of both the main face and the [return](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Return "Return") wing and around the [hearth](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Hearth "Hearth") opening. [Ventilated](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Ventilated "Ventilated") [fire barriers](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_barrier "Fire barrier") comprised of stonewool [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") with Class O [aluminium](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Aluminium "Aluminium") foil facings and a continuous bonded [intumescent strip](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Intumescent_strip "Intumescent strip"). Non- [ventilated](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Ventilated "Ventilated") [fire barriers](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_barrier "Fire barrier") comprised of stonewool [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") with Class O [aluminium](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Aluminium "Aluminium") foil facings specifically intended to fully [fill](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fill "Fill") the void. |

It makes clear that the [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") [performance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Performance "Performance") and [classification](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Classification "Classification") reported only relate to the [components](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Component "Component") and [construction](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Construction "Construction") described. Any changes would need to be considered by individual [building designers](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_designers "Building designers").

It was also reported by the press that PIR [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") can emit [hydrogen](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Hydrogen "Hydrogen") cyanide in the [event](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Event "Event") of a [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire"), and that this could have contributed to some of the injuries seen in the victims of the [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire"). Richard [Hull](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Hull "Hull"), professor of chemistry and [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") science at the [University](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Universities "Universities") of Central Lancashire said; "...every [flat](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Flats "Flats") would have its own source of PIR foam, which would have produced enough [hydrogen](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Hydrogen "Hydrogen") cyanide to kill all the people in that [flat](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Flats "Flats"),"

The [health and safety](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Health_and_safety "Health and safety") [datasheet](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Datum "Datum") associated with the [product](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Products "Products") notes; 'The [products](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Products "Products") will burn if [exposed](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Exposed "Exposed") to a [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") of sufficient [heat](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Heat "Heat") and intensity. As with all organic [materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials"), toxic [gases](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Gases "Gases") will be released with [combustion](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Combustion "Combustion"). Do not incinerate [waste](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Waste "Waste"). Do not inhale fumes. [Fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") fighters should attack the [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") according to the [combustible materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Combustible_materials "Combustible materials") present, and use breathing apparatus.' Ref [https://www.celotex.co.uk/assets/health-and-safety-hands-apr15.pdf](https://www.celotex.co.uk/assets/health-and-safety-hands-apr15.pdf)

It was later reported by the BBC on 12 July 2017, that medical [discharge](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Discharge "Discharge") [papers](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Paper "Paper") revealed 12-year-old Luana Gomes had been treated for the effects of cyanide poisoning, and that her sister and mother had also been treated. Ref [http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-40568640](http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-40568640)

Following the findings of the Police tests, [Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") issued a statement on 23 June 2017 saying:

| --- |
| ... [Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") believes that the right thing to do is to stop the [supply](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Supplies "Supplies") of [Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") RS5000 for [rainscreen cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen_cladding "Rainscreen cladding") [systems](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems") in [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building") over 18m tall with immediate effect (including in respect of ongoing [projects](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Project "Project")), pending further clarity. "... Given the [developments](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Development "Development") of the past twenty four hours, we wish to discuss with the [authorities](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Authority "Authority") how we can restore [confidence](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Confidence "Confidence") in the [products](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Products "Products") that we [supply](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Supplies "Supplies") to the above 18m [market](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Market "Market"). |

On 26 June 2017, Sajid Javid announced the creation of the [Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_Tower_independent_expert_advisory_panel "Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel") to advise the [government](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Government "Government") on the [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire").

On 29 June 2017, Theresa May announced that Sir Martin Moore-Bick had been [appointed](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Appointed "Appointed") to [lead](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Lead "Lead") a [Public Inquiry](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Public_inquiries "Public inquiries") into the [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire"). For more [information](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Information "Information") see: [Grenfell Tower Inquiry](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_Tower_Inquiry "Grenfell Tower Inquiry").

On 6 July 2017, the [Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_Tower_independent_expert_advisory_panel "Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel") advised that further [testing](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Testing "Testing") should be carried out by [BRE](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BRE "BRE") to establish how different types of [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") behave in a [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") in combination with different [types of insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Types_of_insulation "Types of insulation"). This, they suggest will help [landlords](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Landlord "Landlord") decide what further [measures](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Measure "Measure") may be needed to make their [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building") [safe](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Safe "Safe").

The tests will look at 6 combinations of 3 different types of [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding"), with [polyethylene](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyethylene "Polyethylene"), [fire retardant](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_retardant "Fire retardant") [polyethylene](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyethylene "Polyethylene"), and [non-combustible](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Non-combustible "Non-combustible") [mineral](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Mineral "Mineral") [cores](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Core "Core"), combined with [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") of rigid [polyisocyanurate](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyisocyanurate "Polyisocyanurate") foam and [non-combustible](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Non-combustible "Non-combustible") [mineral wool](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Mineral_wool "Mineral wool"). The tests will be carried out in accordance with [BS 8414](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BS_8414 "BS 8414"), and involve [building](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building") [complete](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Complete "Complete") [cladding systems](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Cladding_system "Cladding system") 9 [metres](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Metre "Metre") tall and then subjecting them to a severe [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire"). The results will be made publically available, but [landlords](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Landlord "Landlord") should take [professional](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Professional "Professional") advice on the implications for their [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building").

Ref [https://www.gov.uk/government/news/expert-panel-recommends-further-tests-on-cladding-and-insulation](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/expert-panel-recommends-further-tests-on-cladding-and-insulation)

The first results of these tests were revealed on 28 July 2017, when DCMS announced that a [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems") comprising [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") with [polyethylene](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyethylene "Polyethylene") [filler](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fillers "Fillers") (Category 3) and foam [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation"), with [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") breaks and [cavity barriers](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Cavity_barrier "Cavity barrier") in [place](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Place "Place") did not satisfy the requirements of the [building regulations](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_regulations "Building regulations"). 82 [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building") are thought to have this combination of [materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials") in their [wall construction](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Wall_construction "Wall construction"). Ref [https://www.gov.uk/government/news/first-systems-test-reaffirms-actions-for-landlords](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/first-systems-test-reaffirms-actions-for-landlords)

Following these results, the [government](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Government "Government") issued further advice for [landlords](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Landlord "Landlord") of affected [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building"). Ref [https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/advice-for-building-owners-large-scale-wall-system-test](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/advice-for-building-owners-large-scale-wall-system-test)

[Communities](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Communities "Communities") Secretary The Rt Hon Sajid Javid MP also announced an [independent review of the building regulations and fire safety](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Independent_review_of_the_building_regulations_and_fire_safety "Independent review of the building regulations and fire safety"). See: [Independent review of the building regulations and fire safety](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Independent_review_of_the_building_regulations_and_fire_safety "Independent review of the building regulations and fire safety") for more [information](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Information "Information").

On 2 August 2017, the [government](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Government "Government") announced that the second series of tests had been [completed](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Completed "Completed"), [testing](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Testing "Testing") a [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems") consisting of [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") with a [polyethylene](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyethylene "Polyethylene") [filler](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fillers "Fillers") (category 3) with [stone](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Stone "Stone") wool [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation"). The [Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_Tower_independent_expert_advisory_panel "Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel") advised that the combination does not meet current [building regulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_regulations "Building regulations") [guidance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Guidance "Guidance"). There are 111 [buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building "Building") known to use this [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems"). Ref [https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-fire-safety-testing-programme-continues](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-fire-safety-testing-programme-continues)

On 8 August, results of the third series of tests was published, consisting of [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") with a [fire retardant](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_retardant "Fire retardant") [polyethylene](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyethylene "Polyethylene") [filler](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fillers "Fillers") (category 2 in screening tests) with PIR foam [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation"). Again, the [Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_Tower_independent_expert_advisory_panel "Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel") advised that the combination of [materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials") does not meet current [Building Regulations](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_regulations "Building regulations") [guidance](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Guidance "Guidance"). It was also reported that to further [build](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Build "Build") the evidence available, the [government](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Government "Government") has commissioned a seventh large [scale](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Scale "Scale") test on [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") with [fire retardant](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_retardant "Fire retardant") [polyethylene](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyethylene "Polyethylene") [filler](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fillers "Fillers") (category 2 in screening tests) with [phenolic foam insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Phenolic_foam_insulation "Phenolic foam insulation"). Ref [https://www.gov.uk/government/news/further-results-published-from-governments-fire-testing-programme](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/further-results-published-from-governments-fire-testing-programme)

On 11 August, the results of the fourth round of tests was published, relating to [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") with a [fire resistant](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_resistant "Fire resistant") [polyethylene](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyethylene "Polyethylene") [filler](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fillers "Fillers") (category 2 in screening tests) and [stone](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Stone "Stone") wool [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") (a [form](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Form "Form") of [mineral wool](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Mineral_wool "Mineral wool")). The combination of [materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials") passed the test and so can be [compliant](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Compliant "Compliant") with the [Building Regulations](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_regulations "Building regulations"). Following the tests, Advice for [building owners](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_owner "Building owner"): large- [scale](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Scale "Scale") [wall](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Walls "Walls") [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems") test 4 was published by the [Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_Tower_independent_expert_advisory_panel "Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel").

On 14 August, the results of the fifth test, assessing [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") with a [limited combustibility](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Limited_combustibility "Limited combustibility") [filler](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fillers "Fillers") (category 1 in screening tests) with PIR foam [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation") were published. The [Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_Tower_independent_expert_advisory_panel "Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel") reported that these results show the combination of [materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials") can comply with the [building regulations](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_regulations "Building regulations"). Ref [https://www.gov.uk/government/news/second-wall-cladding-system-passes-latest-government-fire-safety-test](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/second-wall-cladding-system-passes-latest-government-fire-safety-test)

On 21 August 2017, the results of the sixth large [scale](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Scale "Scale") tests were published, revealing that a [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems") consisting of [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") with a [fire retardant](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire_retardant "Fire retardant") [polyethylene](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Polyethylene "Polyethylene") [filler](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fillers "Fillers") (category 2 in screening tests) with [phenolic foam insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Phenolic_foam_insulation "Phenolic foam insulation") did not satisfy the [Building Regulations](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_regulations "Building regulations"). Ref [https://www.gov.uk/government/news/latest-large-scale-government-fire-safety-test-result-published](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/latest-large-scale-government-fire-safety-test-result-published)

On 25 August, the results of the final test were published, a [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems") consisting of [ACM cladding](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding "ACM cladding") with a [limited combustibility](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Limited_combustibility "Limited combustibility") [filler](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fillers "Fillers") (category 1 in screening tests) with [stone](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Stone "Stone") wool [insulation](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Insulation "Insulation"). The [Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_Tower_independent_expert_advisory_panel "Grenfell Tower independent expert advisory panel") reported that this combination of [materials](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Materials "Materials") can be [compliant](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Compliant "Compliant") with the [building regulations](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Building_regulations "Building regulations") when [installed](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Installed "Installed") and maintained properly. Ref [https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-completes-large-scale-fire-safety-testing-programme](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-completes-large-scale-fire-safety-testing-programme)

For more [information](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Information "Information") see: [ACM cladding testing by BRE](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM_cladding_testing_by_BRE "ACM cladding testing by BRE").

In February 2018, [BRE](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BRE "BRE") issued a statement revealing that the original tests they had carried out on the [cladding system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Cladding_system "Cladding system") in 2014 were carried out on a test [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems") that was not [constructed](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Constructed "Constructed") according to [Celotex’s](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") [design](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Design "Design") [specification](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Specifications "Specifications"). The statement said; "The [cladding system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Cladding_system "Cladding system") in that test included [Celotex](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Celotex "Celotex") RS5000 and [fibre cement](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fibre_cement "Fibre cement") [board](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Board "Board") [rainscreen](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Rainscreen "Rainscreen") (declared reaction to [fire](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Fire "Fire") [classification](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Classification "Classification") A2) – this was not an [Aluminium Composite Material](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Aluminium_Composite_Material "Aluminium Composite Material") ( [ACM](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/ACM "ACM")), nor was it the [cladding system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Cladding_system "Cladding system") on [Grenfell Tower](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Grenfell_tower "Grenfell tower")." [BRE](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BRE "BRE") made clear that they did not [design](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Design "Design"), [select](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/SELECT "SELECT") or install the test [system](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Systems "Systems"), and were not involved in the [sample](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Samples "Samples") [selection process](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Selection_process "Selection process"). Ref [http://bregroup.com/press-releases/celotex-statement/](http://bregroup.com/press-releases/celotex-statement/)

In June 2018, [Housing Secretary](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Housing_secretary "Housing secretary") James Brokenshire launched a [consultation on banning the use of combustible materials in the external walls of high-rise residential buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Consultation_on_banning_the_use_of_combustible_materials_in_the_external_walls_of_high-rise_residential_buildings "Consultation on banning the use of combustible materials in the external walls of high-rise residential buildings").

In August 2022 [BRE](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BRE "BRE") withdrew it [certification](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Certification "Certification") from all Kingspan related [products](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Products "Products").

In September 2023 [BRE](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/BRE "BRE") published an update on its decision to withdraw [certification](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Certification "Certification") from Kingspan in August of 2022. For further [information](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Information "Information") see the article; [LPCB certification and Kingspan](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/LPCB_certification_and_Kingspan "LPCB certification and Kingspan")
