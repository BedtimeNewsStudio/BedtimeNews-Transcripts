Apply

The gross output of an industry or a sector less its [intermediate inputs](https://www.bea.gov/help/glossary/intermediate-inputs); the contribution of an industry or sector to [gross domestic product (GDP)](https://www.bea.gov/help/glossary/gross-domestic-product-gdp). Value added by industry can also be measured as the sum of [compensation of employees](https://www.bea.gov/help/glossary/compensation-employees-paid), [taxes on production and imports](https://www.bea.gov/help/glossary/taxes-production-and-imports) less [subsidies](https://www.bea.gov/help/glossary/subsidies), and [gross operating surplus](https://www.bea.gov/help/glossary/gross-operating-surplus).
