# Publication of the Grenfell Tower Inquiry phase 2 report

The Inquiry published its final report on 4 September 2024. The Government will carefully consider its recommendations, to ensure that such a tragedy can never occur again.

From:
:   [Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government](/government/organisations/ministry-of-housing-communities-local-government), [Home Office](/government/organisations/home-office) and [Cabinet Office](/government/organisations/cabinet-office)

Published:
:   4 September 2024
