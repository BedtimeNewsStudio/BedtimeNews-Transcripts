# FIDIC Awards 2016 - Results

FIDIC is pleased to recognise the following projects which received an Award at the FIDIC Awards Gala Dinner celebrated in the "Beldi Country Club" of Marrakesh, Morocco on Monday 26 September 2016. The award categories recognised were, "Outstanding Project of the Year", and "Award of Merit". Please see below the list of awarded projects in alphabetical order by country. Congratulations to all companies awarded!
