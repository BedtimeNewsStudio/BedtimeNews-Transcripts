# OUR    LEADERSHIP

ADNOC is managed by its executive leadership team, with an independent board of directors.

The board has overall responsibility for the company, including approving and overseeing the implementation of our strategic objectives, governance framework and corporate culture.

Our experienced management team are responsible for driving excellence across our business and championing our company values - ensuring sustainable growth and shareholder value.
