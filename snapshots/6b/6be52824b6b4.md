# NBA Finals television ratings

NBA Finals television ratings

List of television ratings NBA Finals in the United States

This is a list of [television ratings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_ratings "Nielsen ratings") for [NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Finals "NBA Finals") in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States"), based on [Nielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_Media_Research "Nielsen Media Research") viewing data.[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1)

The highest rated and most watched NBA Finals series was the [1998 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_NBA_Finals "1998 NBA Finals") between the [Chicago Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997-98_Chicago_Bulls_season "1997-98 Chicago Bulls season") and [Utah Jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997%E2%80%9398_Utah_Jazz_season "1997–98 Utah Jazz season"), which averaged an 18.7 rating / 33 share and 29.04 million viewers on [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_on_NBC "NBA on NBC"). That series also featured the highest rated and most watched NBA Finals game, as the Sunday night averaged a 22.3 rating / 38 share and 35.89 million viewers. The [1987 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_NBA_Finals "1987 NBA Finals") between the [Los Angeles Lakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986%E2%80%9387_Los_Angeles_Lakers_season "1986–87 Los Angeles Lakers season") and [Boston Celtics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986%E2%80%9387_Boston_Celtics_season "1986–87 Boston Celtics season") was the highest rated and most watched NBA Finals series on [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_on_CBS "NBA on CBS"), averaging a 15.9 rating / 32 share and 24.12 million viewers. Game 7 of the [1988 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_NBA_Finals "1988 NBA Finals") registered the network's highest rated and most watched NBA game with a 21.2 rating / 37 share.

The [2015 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_NBA_Finals "2015 NBA Finals") between the [Golden State Warriors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014%E2%80%9315_Golden_State_Warriors_season "2014–15 Golden State Warriors season") and [Cleveland Cavaliers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014%E2%80%9315_Cleveland_Cavaliers_season "2014–15 Cleveland Cavaliers season") was the highest rated NBA Finals series on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_on_ABC "NBA on ABC") averaging an 11.6 rating / 21 share and 19.94 million viewers. The [2026 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NBA_Finals "2026 NBA Finals") between [New York Knicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_New_York_Knicks_season "2025–26 New York Knicks season") and [San Antonio Spurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_San_Antonio_Spurs_season "2025–26 San Antonio Spurs season") was the most watched NBA Final series on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_on_ABC "NBA on ABC") averaging a 9.4 rating / 31 share and 20.58 million viewers. Game 7 of the [2016 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_NBA_Finals "2016 NBA Finals") registered the network's highest rated and most watched NBA game with an average 15.8 rating / 29 share and 31.02 million viewers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) It was the first [basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball "Basketball") game to draw more than 30 million average viewers in 18 years, and only the seventh non- [NFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League "National Football League") sports telecast (excluding the [Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Games "Olympic Games")) to have done so since 1998.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Game7Rating-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-Game7Rating)

The [2019 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_NBA_Finals "2019 NBA Finals") between the [Golden State Warriors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Golden_State_Warriors_season "2018–19 Golden State Warriors season") and the [Toronto Raptors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Toronto_Raptors_season "2018–19 Toronto Raptors season") had a drop in American viewership however combined North American viewership. Analysts cited the presence of a Canadian team (Canadian viewership does not count towards U.S. Nielsen ratings, leading to only one U.S. home market being reflected in viewership), as a factor in the drop.[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5) At the same time, the presence of the Toronto Raptors in the NBA Finals boosted Canadian viewership to record levels.[&#8202;](#ref-back-ca_g1_ratings-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-ca_g1_ratings)

For the [2020 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_NBA_Finals "2020 NBA Finals"), the ratings dropped to a historic low, with one of the games drawing only 5.1 million viewers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-yahoo_20202-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-yahoo_20202) The average viewers figure over 6 games was 7.5 million, which is a 51% decline from the previous year.[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8) Some have claimed this was due to the players' political activism; others have claimed this was due to the finals being played out of season caused by the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic"), as other sports such as the [NHL playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_playoffs "NHL playoffs") and the [MLB playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLB_playoffs "MLB playoffs") also showed a significant decline in ratings.[&#8202;](#ref-back-yahoo_20202-1)[\[7\]](#refnote-yahoo_20202)[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9)
