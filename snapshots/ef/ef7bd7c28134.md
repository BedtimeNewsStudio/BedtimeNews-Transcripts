## The rise of Douyin e-commerce: from a content platform to an e-commerce challenger

Since the official launch of Taobao Live in 2016, the process of live broadcast e-commerce has been accelerating, and it has undergone rapid ups and downs and reshaping in the following seven years. In the face of the ups and downs of top anchors, the intensification of platform competition, and the successive introduction of policies and industry standards, emerging forces have quietly risen in the undercurrent. Among them, Douyin, as a late entrant, made efforts around 2018 and achieved a rapid leap in just a few years, and finally established a unique global interest e-commerce model. In this process, platforms, anchors, merchants, institutions and consumers have interacted with each other to shape the growth trajectory of Douyin e-commerce. Looking back on the past seven years, we have been able to sort out a clear context of innovation, competition and maturity from the whole picture of Douyin e-commerce.

In 2020, the GMV (total merchandise turnover) of Douyin e-commerce exceeded 500 billion yuan, an increase of more than three times compared with 2019. In 2023, the sales scale of Douyin e-commerce will increase fivefold again, with GMV reaching 2.6 trillion yuan, and it is expected to exceed 3 trillion yuan in 2024. Douyin e-commerce ranks fourth in GMV in 2023, and the GMV volume in 2023 is roughly 8 trillion for Alibaba, 3.7 trillion for Pinduoduo, and 3.6 trillion for JD.com.

## 1\. Early Exploration and the Emergence of Live E-commerce (2016-2018)

Before 2016, China's e-commerce environment had gone through the maturity of the graphic era, but consumers' potential shopping desires had not yet been fully stimulated by more efficient content forms. In March of that year, Taobao Live was launched, officially bringing the concept of "live broadcast + e-commerce" into the mainstream vision. Shortly thereafter, Jingdong also actively followed up, and platforms such as Mushroom Street and Weinian tested the water at the micro level, but they did not cause a sensation. It was this concentrated outbreak in 2016 that kicked off the domestic live broadcast e-commerce in the savage growth and the attention of the whole network.

During this period, the ecology of big anchors has begun to take shape. Wei Ya and Li Jiaqi appeared one after another, quickly attracting fans through ultra-long-term live broadcasts and in-depth shopping guide skills, creating a sales miracle of breaking 100 million yuan in a single game within a few months of broadcasting. On the other hand, Luo Yonghao also visited the Jingdong live broadcast room at the end of 2016 to promote his own mobile phone products. Taobao and JD.com, which have been deeply involved in the field of e-commerce retail for many years, chose to take the lead in testing the water, so that live broadcast e-commerce can move towards the era of "goods-oriented". However, at this time, Douyin is still in the rising stage, still focusing on short video content, and has not yet officially debuted in the field of e-commerce.

It wasn't until around 2018 that Douyin and Kuaishou rose rapidly - the former gathered users in the content explosion, and the latter gathered fans with sinking and human ties. In the embryonic period of live e-commerce, different platforms have their own focuses: Taobao and JD.com are more inclined to seize opportunities with their deep e-commerce genes; Douyin and Kuaishou start from the original content to find a breakthrough point for the e-commerce layout. At this time, Douyin e-commerce was still in the gestation stage, but it laid the groundwork for its later full-scale outbreak.

## 2\. Douyin Breaks Ground: Connecting Short Video Content with E-commerce Links (2018-2019)

In 2018, the number of Douyin users surged. Under the short video tuyere, Douyin has inherent advantages in content innovation and entertainment ecological construction. At this time, the e-commerce team is exploring in a low-key manner internally to build a channel for the platform to connect with merchants. In June of that year, the cooperation between Kuaishou and Youzan allowed the market to see the feasibility of "short video + e-commerce", which also gave Douyin more inspiration.

Different from traditional e-commerce platforms, Douyin's initial logic is not shelf thinking, but "content first". In the Douyin system, the product is not front-loaded, but the creator naturally grows grass through short videos, and then guides users to the e-commerce platform to buy in the form of external links. This model of "content first, purchase later" makes consumers inadvertently stimulated to shop in a pan-entertainment environment.

However, early merchants were not familiar with this new type of link. Platforms have to negotiate door-to-door to encourage merchants to settle in and try short videos to bring goods. Around 2019, merchants in Hangzhou took the lead in testing the waters, and in the future, a large number of sellers in Hangzhou's e-commerce circle took short video placement and Douyin site conversion as routine operations. At this stage, Douyin e-commerce has not yet fully accelerated, but it has initially shown the road of "breaking the game with content".

## 3\. Epidemic Catalysis and Live Link Acceleration (2020)

![罗永浩](https://static.foodtalks.cn/image/post/2723b6d576797fad25caf18930d76f5e.png)

罗永浩

At the beginning of 2020, the epidemic broke out, offline consumption plummeted, and all walks of life turned to live broadcast e-commerce to save themselves. At this time, Douyin seized the window period and greatly tilted resources to the live broadcast room. This year, live broadcast e-commerce has gone from concept to all-round surge, and Douyin, as a latecomer, is no longer confined to "second-class e-commerce" and indirect shopping guides, but quickly built its own e-commerce infrastructure.

At this critical juncture, Douyin e-commerce attracts heavyweight anchors in the form of negotiation, cooperation, and traffic tilt. On April 1, 2020, Luo Yonghao officially launched on Douyin, and his performance of bringing 110 million yuan in 3 hours of his debut attracted much attention. This cooperation has formed a benchmark effect for Douyin e-commerce, and also conveyed a strong signal to the outside world that Douyin is moving from a content platform to a mature e-commerce ecosystem.

At the same time, Douyin continues to improve the product pool and merchant ecology. In the past, merchants used to regard Douyin as a traffic acquisition tool, but with the rapid increase in sales in the live broadcast room (the daily sales of some vertical categories can reach 100 million yuan), coupled with the launch of Douyin stores, and the gradual improvement of product management and performance guarantees, the closed loop of Douyin e-commerce has gradually matured.

## 4\. The Changing Pattern of Top Anchors and the Rise of Merchants' Self-Broadcasting (2021-2022)

In 2021, the live broadcast e-commerce pattern will tend to be diversified, and "king-level" anchors such as Wei Ya, Li Jiaqi, Simba, and Luo Yonghao will achieve sky-high transactions. Between the platform and the head anchor, the chain of interests has become closer. However, at the end of the same year, Wei Ya was fined 1.314 billion yuan for tax evasion, which profoundly affected the entire industry, and she was not indifferent to Douyin.

This incident has changed the industry's attitude towards top anchors. In order to avoid over-reliance on certain anchors, Douyin, e-commerce agencies and merchants are starting to look for a more secure development route. Driven by Douyin e-commerce, "store self-broadcasting" and waist experts have risen, and a large number of small and medium-sized anchors, rookies in the industrial belt, and vertical lecturers have emerged. Compared with the earlier "anchor dominance", Douyin's e-commerce ecology presents a multi-level and distributed pattern. After 2022, the platform put forward the idea of cultivating 200,000 new anchors in the industrial belt, which is obviously a long-term steady state layout.

At the same time, celebrities, artists, and Internet entrepreneurs from all walks of life have entered Douyin live broadcast. These entrants with different backgrounds have virtually enriched the Douyin e-commerce field, and also made merchants realize that they don't have to blindly rely on the top V, and their own brands are also feasible. The data shows that merchant self-broadcasting accounts for more than 60% of GMV on Taobao Live and other platforms, and brand self-broadcasting cases such as Hongxing Erke and Tianfu Cola have also emerged on Douyin. Douyin e-commerce boosts diverse anchor groups with traffic, and gradually balances the relationship between supply and demand and discourse power.

## 5\. MCN Institutions Move Forward and Capital Layout (2022)

With the layering of the anchor ecosystem, MCN institutions have gradually surfaced as behind-the-scenes strategists. Behind the top anchors and Internet celebrities, MCN institutions mostly provide product selection, operation, supply chain docking and commercialization strategy support. The rapid development of Douyin e-commerce has promoted the explosion of the number of MCN institutions. In 2020, the total number of MCN institutions in China exceeded 20,000, with a compound annual growth rate of nearly 89%.

At the same time, MCN institutions have successively introduced capital from listed companies. At the end of 2022, "Yuanwang Technology" and "Oriental Selection", which focus on live broadcasting, have become landmark events in the capital market. Luo Yonghao's Make a Friend team also signed a five-year exclusive cooperation with Century Sage Scientific, a Hong Kong-listed company, to ensure technical support and resource implementation. Douyin e-commerce has shifted from a single anchor-driven to a multi-account matrix and multi-platform collaboration in the MCN ecosystem, and capital inflow and resource integration have made the industry more large-scale and systematic.

In order to reduce the risk of a single popular anchor, MCN institutions continue to fission by splitting more new anchors, expanding categories, and slicing communication. The Douyin e-commerce platform encourages MCN organizations to provide consumers with a better and more credible shopping experience through strict content grading and quality standards.

## 6\. Product Innovation from Content to Global Interest E-commerce (2021-2023)

![](https://static.foodtalks.cn/image/post/3ed89f0ce527d5dcf41e404835ef9992.png)

Screenshot of Dong Yuhui bringing goods in the live broadcast room of Oriental Selection

Image source: Douyin @ Oriental Selection

With the continuous iteration of Douyin e-commerce, the concept of global interest e-commerce was proposed at the first ecological conference of Douyin e-commerce in 2021. Kang Zeyu, president of Douyin e-commerce, once emphasized that its core lies in satisfying users' potential shopping interests, and through the integration of short videos, live broadcasts, shopping malls, and search, consumers can naturally reach high-quality products at good prices in the content.

On May 16, 2023, data from the 3rd Douyin E-commerce Ecological Conference showed that in the past year, the GMV of the platform has increased by more than 80%, the GMV of the mall has increased by 277% year-on-year, the GMV of e-commerce search has increased by 159% year-on-year, and the GMV of the shelf scene has reached 30%. In the past year, the average daily views of live broadcasts on the platform exceeded 2.9 billion, the average daily searches for e-commerce intentions exceeded 400 million, and more than 30 billion items were sold throughout the year. These figures show that Douyin e-commerce is no longer a small fight in the initial stage, but has become an important channel that users and merchants cannot ignore.

At the beginning of 2024, Douyin Supermarket will be launched, and the "next-day delivery" model will optimize the logistics experience. On March 26, 2024, the Douyin Mall App has been downloaded more than 400 million times on Android, marking Douyin's official creation of a global shopping scene. At the Douyin E-commerce Author Ceremony in September 2024, Douyin e-commerce GMV increased by 46% year-on-year, shelf scene GMV increased by 86% year-on-year, and new merchants settled in an 83% year-on-year increase.

## 7\. Consumer Rights and Industry Governance: Measuring Advances and Regressions in the New Stage (2020-2023)

In the initial stage of the savage growth of live broadcast e-commerce, counterfeit and shoddy products, order swiping, and data fraud incidents appeared from time to time. While consumers enjoy low prices and discounts, they also have to face problems such as product selection, quality and service. After 2020, multiple departments intensively issued industry norms, the China Advertising Association issued the Code of Conduct for Online Live Streaming Marketing, and the Ministry of Commerce publicly solicited opinions on the Specifications for the Management and Service of Live Streaming E-commerce Platforms. Douyin e-commerce will also launch consumer rights protection reports in 2021 and 2022 to intercept and clean up illegal goods and stores, assist law enforcement agencies in cracking down on illegal elements, and use 180 million yuan of protection funds for consumer rights protection.

The platform is paying more and more attention to compliance and experience, from copyright protection, technical prevention and control, brand cooperation to offline crackdown and public education, all of which reflect its efforts to guide the positive development of the industry. Despite ongoing industry controversy, platforms, merchants, streamers and consumers are working together to build a healthier shopping environment. The improvement of data sampling, negative evaluation, performance guarantee, and multi-dimensional standards enables users to have a more solid foundation of trust in content-based shopping.

In this process, Douyin e-commerce not only tries to create a preferred platform for users to discover high-quality products, but also constantly pursues a virtuous cycle of goods, prices and services. The strong rectification that began in 2020 has provided a safe foundation for the subsequent vigorous development, and also gave Douyin e-commerce the opportunity to show its skills on the mature track.

## 8\. Conclusion

From the birth of live broadcast e-commerce in 2016 to the gradual establishment of the global interest model of Douyin e-commerce in 2023, in the past seven years, the industry has experienced a complete cycle from scattered exploration to multi-platform competition. Douyin e-commerce reached consumers with content in the early stage, and continued to strengthen the supply chain and services in the later stage, with both the story of the rise and fall of the head anchor, as well as the signs of MCN and capital. After deeply participating in the industry standardization process, Douyin e-commerce is moving towards a diversified future.

Nowadays, when consumers across the network are already familiar with "shopping" products in short videos and live broadcast rooms, when millions of merchants regard self-broadcasting as the standard configuration of daily operations, when the cooperation between MCN institutions and brand merchants continues to deepen, and when quality and price, low price and trust achieve a dynamic balance, Douyin e-commerce has become an indispensable force in the live broadcast e-commerce ecology.
