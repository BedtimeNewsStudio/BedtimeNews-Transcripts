Defining market cap:

The market capitalization of a company is the market's way of valuing the entire company. It's determined by multiplying the current share price by the total outstanding shares.
The market cap tells us how big a company is and is extremely useful for comparing against other companies.

Since a stock's share price can swing wildly from day to day and week to week, we'd argue that market cap is not really the best way to determine what a company is worth.
Using [discounted cash flow analysis](https://www.financecharts.com/stocks/BIDU/dcf-calculator) is a more useful way to
value a company (and thus know what to pay for its shares), though as with any valuation method it has its own flaws.
