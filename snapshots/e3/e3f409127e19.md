# Sudan - Attacks on civilians in El Fasher (DG ECHO, UNOCHA, IRC, IOM, Yale School of Public Health, media) (ECHO Daily Flash of 29 October 2025)

Format
News and Press Release Source

- [ECHO](https://reliefweb.int/organization/echo)

Posted29 Oct 2025Originally published29 Oct 2025Origin[View original](https://erccportal.jrc.ec.europa.eu/ECHOProducts//EchoFlash#/echo-flash-items/29909)

- On 27 October, the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) captured the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) headquarters in El Fasher town, North Darfur, prompting the withdrawal of SAF from the city and marking the fall of the last state capital still outside RSF control among the five Darfur states.

- The city had been besieged by the RSF since mid-April 2024. Before its capture, an estimated 260,000 civilians were trapped inside El Fasher with limited access to food, water, and medical care. IOM reports that over 33,000 people managed to flee the town on 27 and 28 October towards the localities of Melit and Tawila.

- Following the city’s capture, reports and videos showed widespread attacks on civilians, including summary executions, house-to-house raids, sexual violence, and attacks on people attempting to flee. OHCHR and the Yale School of Public Health confirmed evidence of mass detentions and killings, with satellite imagery showing objects consistent with bodies in the streets.

- Access to El Fasher remains blocked, with no humanitarian presence left in the city. DG ECHO partners are scaling up their response in Tawila, which already hosts around 400,000 IDPs, with three additional reception sites being established in Tawila locality.
