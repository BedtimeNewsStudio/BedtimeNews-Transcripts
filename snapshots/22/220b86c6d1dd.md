#### Results

About the election

Short description of the context and results of the election.

[View field in the data dictionary](https://data.ipu.org/data-dictionary/about_the_election)

The Rastriya Swatantra Party, formed by Mr. Balen Shah in 2022, won a majority in the early elections to the 275-member House of Representatives. The party sharply increased its share of seats from 20 in 2022 to 182 in 2026. In contrast, the two largest forces in the 2022 legislature saw their number of seats decline sharply. The Nepali Congress (NC, led by Mr. Gagan Thapa) won 38 seats, down from 89. The Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Marxist-Leninist (UML) led by former Prime Minister KP Sharma Oli) won 25 seats, down from 78.

The newly elected members were sworn in on 26 March 2026. The following day, Mr. Shah – a former Mayor of Kathmandu and rapper – was sworn in as the new Prime Minister. At 36, he is the country’s youngest person to serve in the post. Two thirds of his 15-member Cabinet are below 40 years of age. On 2 April, the newly elected House of Representatives held its first session and elected Mr. Dol Prasad Aryal (Rastriya Swatantra Party) as its new Speaker.

The early elections in 2026 followed unprecedented youth-led protests against the Government’s ban on social media in September 2025. Young protestors adopted a song by Mr. Shah as one of their anthems during the protests. Following the resignation of Prime Minister KP Sharma Oli, on 12 September 2025, President Ram Chandra Poudel appointed Ms. Sushila Karki, a former Chief Justice, as the interim Prime Minister. The President also dissolved the House of Representatives, setting parliamentary elections for 5 March 2026. During election campaigning, the major parties promised to tackle corruption, improve governance and provide more jobs.
