# State Railway of Thailand (SRT)

First line opened 1897. Original lines were to 1 435 mm gauge, but converted between 1919 and 1930 to 1 000 mm gauge. State Railway established as an autonomous body in 1951. Restructuring in 2010 created new Traffic, Property and Rolling Stock Maintenance businesses.

SRT is the operator of a the suburban metre-gauge network being developed in Bangkok. On August 2 2021 the SRT Dark Red Line (Bang Sue**–** Rangsit, 21·6 km north, serving eight intermediate stations) and Light Red Line (Bang Sue**–** Taling Chan, 14·6 km west, serving two intermediate stations with two infill stations to follow) began trial operation, with full commercial operation due to start in November 2021.

The Dark Red line is projected to be extended further north to Thammasat University (10 km) and south via Phaya Thai to Hua Lamphong (11 km), then continuing southwest following the existing rail alignment to Maha Chai (39 km). The Light Red line is also to be extended, from Taling Chan west to Salaya (12 km), with a branch back to Sirajto the west of the city centre (6·5 km), and from Bang Sue south to Ratcha Withi in the the city centre then east following the Airport Link to Hua Mak (25 km).

SRT also operates the isolated standard-gauge airport rail link in Bangkok (28·4 km, 8 stations) opened on August 23 2010.
