## G4S China

G4S is part of Allied Universal®, a leading security and facility services company, provides proactive security services and cutting-edge smart technology to deliver tailored, integrated security solutions that allow clients to focus on their core business. Through a global workforce of approximately 800,000 people, we leverage best practices in communities all over the world.

G4S China started providing security services to local market since 2001, offering a range of security services and security systems. As one of the first international security company being awarded a security license in Mainland China, G4S Zhejiang Secure Solutions Company Limited provides manned security solutions across Mainland China through strategic partnerships with licensed security providers, combining the strengths of both local and international resources. The company is headquartered in Shanghai with branches in Beijing, Hangzhou, Shenzhen and Guangzhou.
