1968年，法国学者[勒皮雄](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%8B%92%E7%9A%AE%E9%9B%84&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLli5Lnmq7pm4QiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.nOHGXK-PVaKgcexConD7eWezdtS3s7uqRqcuJLlvMN8&zhida_source=entity)（Ie pichon）根据地形、地质、构造、地震和其他地球物理资料的分析和计算，将全球板块划分为美洲、太平洋、欧亚、非洲、印度-澳大利亚（简称印-澳板块）、[南极洲](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%8D%97%E6%9E%81%E6%B4%B2&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLljZfmnoHmtLIiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.OwgnOQX5VAzqKZ1uTFCD-kFrCt8t2iLdJRs8hxdVYEc&zhida_source=entity)等六大板块。

![Image 7](https://pica.zhimg.com/v2-c77d8ee7582339d401a99f78bfcda350_1440w.jpg)

上述的每个板块面积都大于1x107km2。除太平洋板块绝大部分是由洋壳组成外，其余5个板块均有洋壳岩石圈与[陆壳](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%99%86%E5%A3%B3&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLpmYblo7MiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.1mnWcVieL9fh5CGRQoc7_473JF-TJbj92sV-aFBbQmc&zhida_source=entity)岩石圈复合而成。如美洲板块是由美洲大陆和西大西洋（大西洋[洋脊](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%B4%8B%E8%84%8A&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLmtIvohIoiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.KS0uP-bsgv9RM3y10XwFJGTUHFJrqli3Eo-XkZQBagc&zhida_source=entity)以西部分）组成的，[非洲](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=2&q=%E9%9D%9E%E6%B4%B2&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLpnZ7mtLIiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MiwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.WbHPoHyzjbjoQ0_GEC-kUQ6SoTFXf4ZAFhAU8jExjsE&zhida_source=entity)板块是由[非洲大陆](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%9D%9E%E6%B4%B2%E5%A4%A7%E9%99%86&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLpnZ7mtLLlpKfpmYYiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.1RNIRz_e6ea4ZPuTkr-UHuMG8BpK1Qa02dMdqlUpYPQ&zhida_source=entity)和东大西洋组成的。因此，板块的范围并不与所在的大陆或大洋一致。

稍后，根据震中的集中分布带，学者们又从美洲和欧亚板块划分出若干次及板块。美洲板块被划分为南美、[北美](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%8C%97%E7%BE%8E&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLljJfnvo4iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.f2Wzk53LSefKVuCOaRmIbp9Y3pbGqsKHJz9LfOeuWzk&zhida_source=entity)、加勒比、科克斯、纳兹卡5个次级板块。从欧亚板块中，单独划分出阿拉伯、菲律宾2个次级板块，称为比较流行的全球板块划分方案。

![Image 8](https://pic2.zhimg.com/v2-c9c6dfde6482ba077d5bb6ff6d99c2d1_1440w.jpg)

在全球板块中，各板块相连方式多样，[边界类型](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%BE%B9%E7%95%8C%E7%B1%BB%E5%9E%8B&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLovrnnlYznsbvlnosiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.UFetRkOdgAECVGaT-sTS6KKL70Fi8fHSFVz3ZGJqeIk&zhida_source=entity)复杂。其中，太平洋、[纳兹卡](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=2&q=%E7%BA%B3%E5%85%B9%E5%8D%A1&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLnurPlhbnljaEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MiwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.zZ8fFo_xvmxn12TCUKFtoKIyHc69e1GRQUjXXb-iPRU&zhida_source=entity)、南极洲、非洲和印-澳5个板块均以洋中脊为界，而欧亚、[菲律宾](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=2&q=%E8%8F%B2%E5%BE%8B%E5%AE%BE&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLoj7Llvovlrr4iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MiwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.UL6Tb1iiuY23mm3_PwOGirgqIM5ZhpWkPffIXhohbyo&zhida_source=entity)、[太平洋](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=4&q=%E5%A4%AA%E5%B9%B3%E6%B4%8B&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLlpKrlubPmtIsiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6NCwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.sOvGkl-Aex5tKFaPJMoXUoRssbFh_kjiZqDj4mRI19Y&zhida_source=entity)3个板块则以海沟为界。

![Image 9](https://pica.zhimg.com/v2-266577c38697d0e93782917147b639ca_1440w.jpg)

在非洲板块内部，发育一个近南北向的[东非裂谷带](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%B8%9C%E9%9D%9E%E8%A3%82%E8%B0%B7%E5%B8%A6&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLkuJzpnZ7oo4LosLfluKYiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.n-SKglHAMDKoE5TSFPtUgfGSr69xcaeuFAEjPh8NOy8&zhida_source=entity)，是现代最大型的[大陆裂谷](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%A4%A7%E9%99%86%E8%A3%82%E8%B0%B7&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLlpKfpmYboo4LosLciLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.HGwHNqSeL4MCEHKCMefXbw6e74tNK6QOoaeCUlqUEYE&zhida_source=entity)。在非洲板块东北界，是一个北西走向、狭长状的红海，属于离散型边界。[印度洋](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%8D%B0%E5%BA%A6%E6%B4%8B&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLljbDluqbmtIsiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.TC0uEe7OKJ5_EYzi-k7Ig6BZM9qCtJFygtR7sc_e6QQ&zhida_source=entity)洋脊从印度洋中部向北延伸后，通过近东西向的亚丁湾后，以北西方向进入红海。红海以东地区正在向北运动。红海海底[热流值](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%83%AD%E6%B5%81%E5%80%BC&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLng63mtYHlgLwiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.MUM9XpC4rW-JU84WIyogo_HkmgJovXogpC-4NzsY6PU&zhida_source=entity)为3.66HFU，[亚丁湾](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=2&q=%E4%BA%9A%E4%B8%81%E6%B9%BE&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLkuprkuIHmub4iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MiwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.kVHK6ssJ3ceBp4nHaEYZbqmPpIvbWMKw3mrBw0sWoU4&zhida_source=entity)的热流值达3.95HFU，远超所有洋脊的平均热流值。在[红海](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=5&q=%E7%BA%A2%E6%B5%B7&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLnuqLmtbciLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6NSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.-H_2oF0UNzadqRDMMq_tsjqCGZSJJcu5L00SKZ-CzhE&zhida_source=entity)海底的三个深约2000m的凹地中，发现有高温度（达40℃以上）、高盐度（达240‰）及高金属含量（超过正常海水金属含量的1000~50000倍）的海水。可能是从[地幔](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%9C%B0%E5%B9%94&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLlnLDluZQiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.6SSmqQNMr9SMX_gPYEtuwZvFg1ZOw6FJSAuZwcypVF4&zhida_source=entity)中分泌出来的热水与海水的混合物。目前认为，红海是一个正在分裂扩张的地带，属于大洋形成的初始阶段，它正朝着形成大西洋性[洋盆](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=109181017&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%B4%8B%E7%9B%86&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2ODgsInEiOiLmtIvnm4YiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoxMDkxODEwMTcsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.jBdiovc1BEZRU4euwLa6yTrrwhvq7h12XWSvePETXMI&zhida_source=entity)的方向逐渐演化（Condie，1996）。
