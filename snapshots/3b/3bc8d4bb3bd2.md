# What is the Venice Access Fee - Venezia Unica

![Logo](https://cda.veneziaunica.it/venezia-unica-logo-negativo.svg)

## About the Access Fee

THE TRIAL PERIOD FOR THE ACCESS FEE FOR 2026 HAS ENDED. THEREFORE, AS OF 27 JULY 2026, THE ACCESS FEE DOES NOT APPLY, AND IT IS POSSIBLE TO VISIT VENICE WITHOUT MAKING ANY PAYMENT OR REQUESTING AN EXEMPTION FROM THE FEE.

The Access Fee will be payable again from 2027. The arrangements will be announced by 31 December 2026. The fee will be payable on the following dates: [cda.ve.it/en](https://cda.ve.it/en/)

For any further updates, please follow the City of Venice’s official online communication channels.
