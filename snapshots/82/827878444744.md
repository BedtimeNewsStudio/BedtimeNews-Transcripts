As of 2023, the [United States](https://www.britannica.com/place/United-States) was the world’s third most-populated country, with more than 335,000,000 people. The U.S. state with the largest population is [California](https://www.britannica.com/place/California-state), which has some 39,000,000 residents. Second is [Texas](https://www.britannica.com/place/Texas-state), with more than 30,000,000. Several states have less than 1,000,000 people, and [Wyoming](https://www.britannica.com/place/Wyoming-state) holds the title of the least-populated state, with fewer than 600,000.
