**Cédric Patrice Thierry Villani** (French:[\[sedʁikpatʁistjeʁivilani\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/French "Help:IPA/French"); born 5 October 1973) is a French [mathematician and politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mathematician-politicians "List of mathematician-politicians") working primarily on [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equation "Partial differential equation"), [Riemannian geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_geometry "Riemannian geometry") and [mathematical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physics "Mathematical physics"). He was awarded the [Fields Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fields_Medal "Fields Medal") in 2010, and he was the director of [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University "Sorbonne University")'s [Institut Henri Poincaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Henri_Poincar%C3%A9 "Institut Henri Poincaré") from 2009 to 2017. As of September 2025, he is a professor at the [École normale supérieure de Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89cole_normale_sup%C3%A9rieure_de_Rennes "École normale supérieure de Rennes").[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2)

Villani has given two lectures at the [Royal Institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institution "Royal Institution"), the first titled 'Birth of a Theorem'.[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3) The English translation of his book _Théorème vivant_ ( _Living Theorem_) has the same title.
In the book he describes the links between his research on [kinetic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_theory_of_gases "Kinetic theory of gases") and that of the mathematician [Carlo Cercignani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlo_Cercignani "Carlo Cercignani"). Villani, in fact, proved the so-called [Cercignani's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercignani_conjecture "Cercignani conjecture").\[ _[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_\] His second lecture at the Royal Institution is titled 'The Extraordinary Theorems of [John Nash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Forbes_Nash_Jr. "John Forbes Nash Jr.")'.[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)

Villani was elected as the deputy for [Essonne's 5th constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essonne's_5th_constituency "Essonne's 5th constituency") in the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_(France) "National Assembly (France)"), the lower house of the [French Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Parliament "French Parliament"), during the [2017 legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_French_legislative_election "2017 French legislative election").[&#8202;](#ref-back-2017-05-12_ABC-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-2017-05-12_ABC) He was elected as a member of [La République En Marche!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_R%C3%A9publique_En_Marche! "La République En Marche!") (LREM), but in May 2020 left the party to form a new party, [Ecology, Democracy, Solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology,_Democracy,_Solidarity "Ecology, Democracy, Solidarity") (EDS).[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6)[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7) Following the dissolution of EDS, Villani joined [Ecology Generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology_Generation "Ecology Generation"), and ran for re-election [in 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_legislative_election "2022 French legislative election") under the banner of the [NUPES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NUPES "NUPES"),[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8) ultimately [losing his seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_MPs_who_lost_their_seat_in_the_2022_French_legislative_election "List of MPs who lost their seat in the 2022 French legislative election") to LREM candidate [Paul Midy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Midy "Paul Midy") by 19 votes.[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9)

He was elected vice president of the French [Parliamentary Office for the Evaluation of Scientific and Technological Choices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Office_for_the_Evaluation_of_Scientific_and_Technological_Choices "Parliamentary Office for the Evaluation of Scientific and Technological Choices") in July 2017.

## Biography

After attending the [Lycée Louis-le-Grand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyc%C3%A9e_Louis-le-Grand "Lycée Louis-le-Grand"), Villani was admitted at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89cole_Normale_Sup%C3%A9rieure "École Normale Supérieure") in Paris and studied there from 1992 to 1996, after which he was appointed an [agrégé préparateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agr%C3%A9g%C3%A9_pr%C3%A9parateur "Agrégé préparateur") at the same school.[&#8202;](#ref-back-10-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-10) He received his doctorate at [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University "Paris Dauphine University") in 1998, under the supervision of [Pierre-Louis Lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre-Louis_Lions "Pierre-Louis Lions"), and became professor at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89cole_normale_sup%C3%A9rieure_de_Lyon "École normale supérieure de Lyon") in 2000. He is now professor at the [University of Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lyon "University of Lyon"). He was director of the [Institut Henri Poincaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Henri_Poincar%C3%A9 "Institut Henri Poincaré") in Paris from 2009 to 2017.[&#8202;](#ref-back-11-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-11)[&#8202;](#ref-back-fm-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-fm)

He has held various visiting positions at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech "Georgia Tech") (Fall 1999), the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley "University of California, Berkeley") (Spring 2004), and the [Institute for Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Advanced_Study "Institute for Advanced Study"), [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey "Princeton, New Jersey") (Spring 2009).[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13)

## Mathematical work

Villani has worked on the theory of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations "Partial differential equations") involved in [statistical mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_mechanics "Statistical mechanics"), specifically the [Boltzmann equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_equation "Boltzmann equation"), where, with Laurent Desvillettes, he was the first to prove how quickly convergence occurs for initial values not near equilibrium.[&#8202;](#ref-back-fm-1)[\[12\]](#refnote-fm) He has written with Giuseppe Toscani on this subject. With [Clément Mouhot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cl%C3%A9ment_Mouhot "Clément Mouhot"), he has worked on nonlinear [Landau damping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau_damping "Landau damping").[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14) He has worked on the theory of [optimal transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_transport "Optimal transport") and its applications to differential geometry, and with [John Lott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lott_(mathematician) "John Lott (mathematician)") has defined a notion of bounded [Ricci curvature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricci_curvature "Ricci curvature") for general measured length spaces.[&#8202;](#ref-back-15-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-15) He also served on the Mathematical Sciences jury for the [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize "Infosys Prize") in 2015 and 2016.

Villani received the Fields Medal for his work on [Landau damping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau_damping "Landau damping") and the [Boltzmann equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_equation "Boltzmann equation").[&#8202;](#ref-back-fm-2)[\[12\]](#refnote-fm) He described the development of his theorem in his autobiographical book _Théorème vivant_ (2012), published in [English translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language "English language") as _Birth of a Theorem: A Mathematical Adventure_ (2015).[&#8202;](#ref-back-16-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-16)[&#8202;](#ref-back-17-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-17) He gave a [TED talk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TED_(conference) "TED (conference)") at the 2016 conference in Vancouver.[&#8202;](#ref-back-TED-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-TED)

## Political career

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fd/R%C3%A9union_publique_de_La_R%C3%A9publique_En_Marche_%21_Japon%2C_avec_le_d%C3%A9put%C3%A9_C%C3%A9dric_Villani_le_jeudi_26_novembre_de_%C3%A0_Akasaka%2C_Tokyo.jpg/500px-R%C3%A9union_publique_de_La_R%C3%A9publique_En_Marche_%21_Japon%2C_avec_le_d%C3%A9put%C3%A9_C%C3%A9dric_Villani_le_jeudi_26_novembre_de_%C3%A0_Akasaka%2C_Tokyo.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:R%C3%A9union_publique_de_La_R%C3%A9publique_En_Marche_!_Japon,_avec_le_d%C3%A9put%C3%A9_C%C3%A9dric_Villani_le_jeudi_26_novembre_de_%C3%A0_Akasaka,_Tokyo.jpg) Cédric Villani at a public meeting of En Marche in Tokyo

In 2017, it was announced that Villani had been selected as a candidate for _[En Marche!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_R%C3%A9publique_En_Marche! "La République En Marche!")_ (LREM) in the [2017 French legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_French_legislative_election "2017 French legislative election"), for [Essonne's 5th constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essonne's_5th_constituency "Essonne's 5th constituency").[&#8202;](#ref-back-19-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-19)[&#8202;](#ref-back-20-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-20) In the first round of voting, Villani obtained 47% of the vote and was thus strongly placed for the second round[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-21) which he won with 69.36% of the vote.[&#8202;](#ref-back-22-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-22)

In 2019, Villani applied to be selected to lead the LREM candidate slate for [the 2020 Paris election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_municipal_elections "2020 French municipal elections"). By July 2019, he was one of three LREM candidates, all [deputies in the](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_deputies_of_the_15th_National_Assembly_of_France "List of deputies of the 15th National Assembly of France") [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_(France) "National Assembly (France)"), still seeking the position; the other two were [Benjamin Griveaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Griveaux "Benjamin Griveaux") (who had been the [government spokesperson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Government_spokespeople_of_France "List of Government spokespeople of France")) and [Hugues Renson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugues_Renson "Hugues Renson") (who had been the vice-president of the National Assembly). On 10 July, the nomination committee picked Griveaux.[&#8202;](#ref-back-23-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-23) On 4 September, Villani officially announced his candidacy for the municipal election.[&#8202;](#ref-back-24-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-24)

## Other activities

- France China Foundation, former Member of the Strategic Committee[&#8202;](#ref-back-25-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-25)

In 2018, the [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France") [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics "Economics") [magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magazine "Magazine") [Challenges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challenges_(magazine) "Challenges (magazine)") said that Villani has been approached by Europanova [thinktank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinktank "Thinktank"). He presided over the jury of Digital In-Pulse, a startup program dedicated to accompanying entrepreneurs and start-ups, managed by [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China "China") [corporate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate "Corporate") [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei "Huawei"). The magazine also says that Villani is still the President of the endowment funds of the French [Henri Poincaré Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Henri_Poincar%C3%A9 "Institut Henri Poincaré"), and [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei "Huawei") is among the top private donors.[&#8202;](#ref-back-26-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-26) Villani declined the journalist's request for comment, and the article indicates that the French [counter-intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-intelligence "Counter-intelligence") service presented him as "too naïve" regarding those opportunities.
