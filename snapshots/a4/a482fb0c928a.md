**Ng Chee Khern** [PPA(E)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingat_Pentadbiran_Awam "Pingat Pentadbiran Awam") [LOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Merit "Legion of Merit") (born 18 October 1965) is a Singaporean civil servant and former [major-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major-general "Major-general") who served as [Chief of Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Singapore_Air_Force#List_of_chiefs_of_Air_Force "Republic of Singapore Air Force") between 2006 and 2009. He previously served as Permanent Secretary (Smart Nation and Digital Government) under the [Prime Minister's Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister's_Office_(Singapore) "Prime Minister's Office (Singapore)"), and Chairman of the [Government Technology Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Technology_Agency "Government Technology Agency").[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1) He was appointed Permanent Secretary (Manpower) in April 2022.[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) He retired from the public service on 1 December 2025 after more than 40 years in public service.[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3)

## Education

Ng is an alumnus of [Victoria School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_School "Victoria School") and [Hwa Chong Junior College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwa_Chong_Junior_College "Hwa Chong Junior College"),[&#8202;](#ref-back-VS_Class_of_2010_-_Sec_4_Graduation-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-VS_Class_of_2010_-_Sec_4_Graduation)[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5) and was a recipient of the [President's Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President's_Scholar "President's Scholar") and [Singapore Armed Forces Overseas Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Armed_Forces_Overseas_Scholarship "Singapore Armed Forces Overseas Scholarship") in 1985.[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6)

He graduated from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford") with a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts "Bachelor of Arts") with second upper class honours degree and a [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts_(Oxbridge_and_Dublin) "Master of Arts (Oxbridge and Dublin)") degree in [philosophy, politics and economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy,_politics_and_economics "Philosophy, politics and economics") (PPE). He also completed a [Master of Public Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Public_Administration "Master of Public Administration") degree at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University "Harvard University").[&#8202;](#ref-back-New_Air_Force_Chief_Takes_Over-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-New_Air_Force_Chief_Takes_Over)

## Military career

Ng enlisted in the [Singapore Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Armed_Forces "Singapore Armed Forces") (SAF) in 1984 and served in the [Republic of Singapore Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Singapore_Air_Force "Republic of Singapore Air Force") (RSAF). He flew the [Northrop F-5E Tiger-II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_F-5 "Northrop F-5") and the [F-16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics_F-16_Fighting_Falcon "General Dynamics F-16 Fighting Falcon") Fighting Falcon.[&#8202;](#ref-back-AFM275-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-AFM275)

Throughout his military career, Ng held various appointments, including: Commanding Officer, [149 Squadron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/149_Squadron,_Republic_of_Singapore_Air_Force "149 Squadron, Republic of Singapore Air Force"); Commander, [Tengah Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tengah_Air_Base "Tengah Air Base"); Director, Joint Operations and Planning Directorate; Chief of Staff (Air Staff). He succeeded [Lim Kim Choon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lim_Kim_Choon "Lim Kim Choon") as the Chief of Air Force on 24 March 2006[&#8202;](#ref-back-New_Air_Force_Chief_Takes_Over-1)[\[7\]](#refnote-New_Air_Force_Chief_Takes_Over) and relinquished this appointment to his second brother, [Ng Chee Meng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ng_Chee_Meng "Ng Chee Meng"), on 10 December 2009.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Brother_succeeds_RSAF_chief-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-Brother_succeeds_RSAF_chief)

## Civil Service career

After leaving the Air Force, Ng was appointed [Director of the Security and Intelligence Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_and_Intelligence_Division#Directors "Security and Intelligence Division") on 1 September 2010.[&#8202;](#ref-back-CapitaMall_Trust-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-CapitaMall_Trust)[&#8202;](#ref-back-11-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-11) He left the post on 1 May 2014, and served as Permanent Secretary (Defence Development) at the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_(Singapore) "Ministry of Defence (Singapore)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12) He was also appointed Second Permanent Secretary at the [Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_(Singapore) "Ministry of Health (Singapore)") on 1 August 2014.[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13)

Ng is also a member of the boards of various governmental organisations, including: [Public Utilities Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Utilities_Board "Public Utilities Board") (PUB); CapitaMall Trust Management; Chartered Ammunition Industries; ST Aerospace Systems; Changi International Airshow & Events; MDSO Supervisory Board; Construction Industry Development Board; [Defence Science and Technology Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Science_and_Technology_Agency "Defence Science and Technology Agency"); [DSO National Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Science_Organisation "Defence Science Organisation"); [Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Aviation_Authority_of_Singapore "Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore").[&#8202;](#ref-back-CapitaMall_Trust-1)[\[10\]](#refnote-CapitaMall_Trust)

## Personal life

Ng has two younger brothers. [Ng Chee Meng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ng_Chee_Meng "Ng Chee Meng"), a politician and former [lieutenant-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant-general "Lieutenant-general") who served as [Chief of Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Singapore_Air_Force#List_of_chiefs_of_Air_Force "Republic of Singapore Air Force") between 2009 and 2013, [Chief of Defence Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_Force_(Singapore) "Chief of Defence Force (Singapore)") between 2013 and 2015, and [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Singapore "Parliament of Singapore") (MP) for [Pasir Ris–Punggol GRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasir_Ris-Punggol_Group_Representation_Constituency "Pasir Ris-Punggol Group Representation Constituency") between 2015 and 2020. [Ng Chee Peng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ng_Chee_Peng "Ng Chee Peng"), a former two-star [rear-admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear-admiral "Rear-admiral") who served as [Chief of Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Navy_(Singapore) "Chief of Navy (Singapore)") between 2011 and 2014.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Brother_succeeds_RSAF_chief-1)[\[9\]](#refnote-Brother_succeeds_RSAF_chief) Ng and his brothers are [Chinese Singaporean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Singaporean "Chinese Singaporean") of [Teochew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_people "Teochew people") descent.[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14)
