# Statistical Review: Summary of Key Association Data and Information

**United States**  
More than one third (35.2%) of the U.S. housing is in a community association (e.g. planned communities, condominium communities, and housing cooperatives), according to the National and State Statistical Review for Community Association Data, published by the Foundation for Community Association Research. In 1970, there were 10,000 community associations with approximately 2.1 million residents. According to the latest [Fact Book 2025](https://foundation.caionline.org/publications/factbook/), the Foundation estimates there are 373,000 community associations in the U.S. with 78.1 million residents.
