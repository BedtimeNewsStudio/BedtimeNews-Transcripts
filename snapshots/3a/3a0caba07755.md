| 研究目的： | 1、主要目的：<br>1) 评估速效救心丸临床实际应用治疗慢性稳定型冠心病心绞痛的即刻疗效，即心绞痛缓解时间；<br>2) 评估速效救心丸临床实际应用治疗慢性稳定型冠心病心绞痛对患者心绞痛发作次数、严重程度、持续时间以及硝酸甘油用量等的影响；<br>3) 以麝香保心丸、复方丹参滴丸作为对照评估速效救心丸治疗慢性稳定型冠心病心绞痛的经济性；<br>4) 评估速效救心丸实际临床应用的安全性。<br>2、次要目的：<br>1) 评估速效救心丸在临床实际应用治疗慢性稳定型冠心病心绞痛对患者心血管事件发生的影响；<br>2) 评估速效救心丸实际应用过程中不同人群和亚组人群的临床疗效特征（实际使用方法、剂量、用药时间等效果异质性差异）；<br>3) 建立速效救心丸临床实际应用医院登记的研究、质控、数据管理和统计分析平台，长期积累临床数据，不断挖掘速效救心丸的临床疗效优势，监测安全性数据，为速效救心丸的实际临床应用提供可靠数据支持；<br>4) 以复方丹参滴丸、麝香保心丸为对照，评价速效救心丸治疗慢性稳定型冠心病心绞痛的临床作用不同特点；<br>5) 通过社区监测及宣贯，进一步扩大速效救心丸的影响力，让医生和患者充分了解速效救心丸的合理使用方法；<br>6) 撰写并不断改进速效救心丸实际用药指南。 |
| Objectives of Study： | 1.Primary objectives: <br>1) To evaluate the immediate effect of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan in treatment of chronic stable angina pectoris, i.e., time of remission of angina pectoris;<br>2) To evaluate the impact of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan on frequency, severity, duration of angina pectoris and dosage of nitroglycerin in patients with chronic stable angina pectoris treated;<br>3) To evaluate the economy of Suxiao Jiuxin wan in treatment of chronic stable angina pectoris, in which Shexiang Baoxin Wan and Compound Danshen Dripping Pill are taken as a control;<br>4) To evaluate the safety of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan in clinical treatment. <br>2\. Secondary objectives:<br>1) To evaluate the effect of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan on cardiovascular events in patients with chronic stable angina pectrois in clinical treatment;<br>2) To evaluate the clinical efficacy characteristics of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan in different groups and subgroups of patients in clinical treatment (heterogeneous differences in actual usage, dosage, time of medication and so forth);<br>3) To establish a platform of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan in clinical treatment used for hospital registration research, quality control, data management and statistical analysis, have a long-term accumulation of clinical data, constantly tap the advantages of Suxiao Jiuxin wan, monitor safety data, so as to provide reliable data support for clinical treatment of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan;<br>4) To evaluate different clinical characteristics of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan in treatment of chronic stable coronary angina pectoris, in which Shexiang Baoxin Wan and Compound Danshen Dripping Pill are taken as a control;<br>5) To further expand the influence of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan through community monitoring and public promotion, so as to let doctors and patients fully understand its use;<br>6) To compile and constantly improve the guide for medication of Suxiao Jiuxin Wan. |
