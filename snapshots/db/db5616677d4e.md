On November 21, 2024 (the " **Petition Date**"), Northvolt AB and eight affiliated debtors (collectively, the " **Debtors**") filed [voluntary petitions](https://cases.stretto.com/Northvolt/court-docket/voluntary-petitions/) for relief under Chapter 11 of the United States Bankruptcy Code. The Debtors' bankruptcy cases are jointly administered under Case No. 24-90577 in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas (the " **Bankruptcy Court**") before the Honorable Alfredo R. Pérez.

For immediate information about the bankruptcy case, ask Stretto Conductor. Additional information about the bankruptcy cases may be obtained by calling the case information line at **855.314.8797** (Toll-Free) or **714.479.2596**(International). You may also submit an inquiry via email by clicking [HERE](mailto:NorthvoltInquiries@Stretto.com?subject=In%20re%3A%20Northvolt%20-%20Creditor%20Inquiry).
