## Press release No. 260 of 9 July 2019

![](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Press/Graphics/Traffic-Accidents/2019/traffic-accidents-persons-killed-year-2018-finally.png?__blob=normal&v=3)[![Vergroessern](https://www.destatis.de/SiteGlobals/Frontend/Images/icons/expand.svg?__blob=normal&v=5)](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Press/Graphics/Traffic-Accidents/2019/traffic-accidents-persons-killed-year-2018-finally.png?__blob=poster&v=3 "Trend in the number of persons killed in road traffic accidents (Larger version opens in new window)")

WIESBADEN – In 2018, 3,275 people were killed in road traffic accidents in Germany. The Federal Statistical Office (Destatis) also reports that this was an increase of 95, or +3%, on 2017. Another 396,000 people were injured in road traffic accidents, that was 5,706 more than in 2017 (+1.5%).

# Consent to using cookies and similar technologies

- With your **consent**, we will use the Matomo service to measure your user activity on our website and to improve our website’s functionalities.
- To this end, we store information on your end device, such as cookies, and we only access information on your end device that is required for the relevant purpose.
- The data collected are never shared with third parties and are stored for a maximum of 30 days in pseudonymised form.
- You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by changing your [„privacy settings“](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Service/Privacy-Policy/_node.html#settings "privacy settings").

Details

**Technological description:**

- This website uses the Matomo web analysis service to analyse and optimise the use of our website. The software runs only on web servers operated by the service provider "ITZ Bund" on behalf of the Federal Statistical Office. The legal basis for processing using Matomo is your explicit consent as per Article 6 paragraph 1 point a of GDPR in conjunction with Section 25 (1) of the Telecommunications Telemedia Data Protection Act (TTDSG).
- The script ‘matomo.js’ is executed and a cookie ‘matomoTracking’ is set. In the background, the system creates a fingerprint that consists of

  - two bytes of the IP address of the system used to access the website,
  - the website accessed,
  - the website from which the user accessed our website (referrer),
  - the subpages accessed from our website,
  - the length of time spent on the website,
  - and how often the website is accessed.
