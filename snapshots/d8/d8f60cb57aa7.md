# China, U.S. Test Intelligent-Drone Swarms in Race for Military AI Dominance

## Beijing and Washington prioritize autonomous vehicles and surveillance, study shows

By

[Alastair Gale](https://www.wsj.com/news/author/alastair-gale)

Aug. 19, 2023 10:32 am ET

* * *

Listen

(2 min)

![The U.S. held a joint exercise with the U.K. and Australia in April using AI-enabled drones.](https://images.wsj.net/im-838466?width=700&height=466)The U.S. held a joint exercise with the U.K. and Australia in April using AI-enabled drones.Defence Science and Technology Laboratory/UK Ministry of Defence

The U.S. and China are accelerating research on how to integrate artificial intelligence into their militaries as part of a global race to take advantage of the fast-developing technology.

Among the priorities for both sides: weapons that can find their way to a target without human help and AI tools to identify targets from satellite images.
