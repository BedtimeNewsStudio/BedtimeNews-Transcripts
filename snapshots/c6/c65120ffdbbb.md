### Fighting Dogs

#### Q & A for Fighting Dogs

[This link will open in a new windowClick here to view](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/files/QnA_for_fighting_dogs_-20260904_Eng.pdf)

**What are “fighting dogs”?**

Under Schedule 1 of the Dangerous Dogs Regulation (Cap. 167D), "fighting dogs" cover four statutory types: Pit Bull Terrier, Japanese Tosa, Dogo Argentino, and Fila Braziliero, as well as any crossbreed of these four types.

**The Control of Fighting Dogs under Cap. 167D**

1\. The importation of fighting dogs is prohibited.

![Pit Bull Terrier](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic1.jpg)

![Dogo Argentino](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic2.jpg)

![Japanese Tosa](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic3.jpg)

![Fila Braziliero](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic4.jpg)

2\. The breeding of fighting dogs is prohibited. Existing fighting dogs must be neutered by a registered veterinary surgeon and the keeper of an existing fighting dog must possess a valid certificate from the veterinary surgeon certifying that the dog has been neutered.

![A large dog on an operation table](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic5.jpg)

3\. When in public places, a fighting dog must be securely fitted with a muzzle to prevent it from biting any person. It must also be securely held on a leash no longer than 1.5m long by a person.

![A muzzle](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic8.jpg)

![leash not longer than 1.5 metres](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic7.jpg)

4. Fighting dogs must be identified at all times by wearing a specified collar. The specified collar must be at least 1.5cm wide marked on its obverse side with a bright yellow background and at least 4 red diagonal stripes. The stripes must be at least 1.5cm apart and each of them must be at least 1.5cm wide.

![Special dog collar](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic6.jpg)

### Known Dangerous Dogs

**What is a “Known Dangerous Dog”?**

Under Section 10 of the Dangerous Dogs Regulation (Cap. 167D), if a dog performs any of the following acts, whether in a public place or not, in the absence of provocation of any form, it may be classified by a magistrate’s orders as a known dangerous dog:

(i) Biting or attacking any person, resulting in the person’s

- death, or

- serious injury resulting in in-patient treatment in a hospital

(ii) Biting or attacking any domestic animal, resulting in the animal’s

- death, or

- serious injury resulting in:

    \- surgery under general anaesthesia at a veterinary clinic or hospital, or

    \- euthanasia by a veterinary surgeon

(iii) Repeatedly attacking people, or otherwise putting people in fear of being attacked

**The control of “known dangerous dogs” under Cap. 167D**

1.Known dangerous dogs must be neutered by a registered veterinary surgeon and the keeper of a known dangerous dog must possess a valid certificate from the veterinary surgeon certifying that the dog has been neutered.

![](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic5.jpg)

2.When in public places, a known dangerous dog must be securely fitted with a muzzle to prevent it from biting any person. It must also be securely held on a leash no longer than 1.5m long by a person.

![](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic8.jpg)![](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic7.jpg)

3\. A known dangerous dog must be identified at all times by wearing a specified collar. The specified collar must be at least 1.5cm wide marked on its obverse side with a bright yellow background and at least 4 red diagonal stripes. The stripes must be at least 1.5cm apart and each of them must be at least 1.5cm wide.

![](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/proper_care_of_pets/dogs/images/dog_1_5_1_pic6.jpg)

### Legislation

**Applicable Legislation:**

[Cap. 167D DANGEROUS DOGS REGULATION](http://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap167D!en)

Those contravening the Dangerous Dogs Regulation are liable to a maximum fine of $50,000 and imprisonment for 6 months.
