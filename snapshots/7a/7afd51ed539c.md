WASHINGTON (AP) — The Trump administration has built up a [fleet of warships near Venezuela](https://apnews.com/article/us-military-buildup-caribbean-venezuela-ships-troops-810f6181371f53536c723ea562c5277c), including the [largest U.S. aircraft carrier](https://apnews.com/article/aircraft-carrier-venezuela-trump-ford-strikes-drugs-241ea9e039d717b9f80f793922cb7cc9), as American forces keep blowing up small boats accused of smuggling narcotics for drug cartels.

The expanding U.S. military presence is fueling speculation that land attacks inside the South American country could be next. Even as tensions rise, President Donald Trump said Sunday that the U.S. “may be having some discussions” with Venezuelan President [Nicolás Maduro](https://apnews.com/hub/nicolas-maduro), who faces [charges of narcoterrorism in the U.S.](https://apnews.com/article/caribbean-indictments-armed-forces-united-states-miami-d82797206561db03851e47df125c243f)

Trump didn’t offer details but said “Venezuela would like to talk.”

Maduro has insisted that the real purpose of the U.S. military operations — including the largest presence of U.S. warships in Latin America in decades — is to force him from office.

The Trump administration says the military has killed at least 83 people in [21 known strikes](https://apnews.com/article/trump-petro-colombia-drugs-us-aid-c3955b2ce351737119920741178e0567) in the Caribbean Sea and eastern Pacific Ocean since early September. Trump has [justified the attacks](https://apnews.com/article/trump-maduro-drugs-venezuela-911-hegseth-3db3aafed492556bb9ca7de855c4849e) as a necessary escalation to stem the flow of drugs into the United States and asserted the U.S. is engaged in an [“armed conflict” with drug cartels](https://apnews.com/article/trump-cartels-armed-conflict-cb57804807e55a00ace60ad5f4d4f24d).

As the number of strikes has grown without a declaration of war from Congress, lawmakers have questioned the legal reasoning for them.

## Feb. 20

The Trump administration [formally designates](https://apnews.com/article/trump-cartels-foreign-terrorist-organizations-eb35567b69fc66f13f7f79fb90906a50) eight Latin American crime organizations as foreign terrorist organizations.

The label is normally reserved for groups like al-Qaida or the Islamic State that use violence for political ends — not for profit-focused crime rings.

## Aug. 19

The U.S. military [deploys three guided-missile destroyers](https://apnews.com/article/trump-venezuela-destroyers-maduro-drug-cartels-e33794ebc24d9031e536d132ce205b4c) to the waters off Venezuela as part of Trump’s effort to combat threats from Latin American drug cartels.

The naval force in the Caribbean grows within weeks to include three amphibious assault ships and other vessels, carrying about 6,000 sailors and Marines and a variety of aircraft.

The U.S. deploys F-35 fighter jets to Puerto Rico in September, while a Navy submarine carrying cruise missiles operates off South America.

## Sept. 2

The U.S. [carries out its first strike](https://apnews.com/article/venezuela-strike-rubio-trump-0f901b2a30ee20e314bcab1385ffb0c0) against what Trump says was a drug-carrying vessel that departed from [Venezuela](https://apnews.com/hub/venezuela) and was operated by Tren de Aragua.

Trump says 11 people were killed and posts a short video clip of a small vessel appearing to explode in flames.

## Sept. 10

[In a letter to the White House](https://apnews.com/article/trump-military-drugs-boat-venezuela-5d28da68e3ea1b5b1b14829af8aadd7b), Democratic senators say the Trump administration has provided “no legitimate legal justification” for the strike.

## Sept. 15

The U.S. military [carries out its second strike](https://apnews.com/article/trump-venezuela-drug-cartels-05c5b0de282178419d46a3e93fbd2521) against an alleged drug boat, killing three people.

Asked what proof the U.S. has that the vessel was carrying drugs, Trump told reporters that big bags of cocaine and fentanyl were spattered all over the ocean. However, images of what Trump described were not released by the military or the White House.

## Sept. 19

Trump says the U.S. military carried out [its third fatal strike](https://apnews.com/article/strike-drug-smuggling-vessel-275ab9837373a928aa3376e50d8d39b0) against an alleged drug-smuggling vessel. The president says the attack killed three people and that intelligence “confirmed the vessel was trafficking illicit narcotics.”

## Oct. 2

Trump [declares drug cartels to be unlawful combatants](https://apnews.com/article/trump-cartels-armed-conflict-cb57804807e55a00ace60ad5f4d4f24d) and says the U.S. is now in an “armed conflict” with them, according to a Trump administration memo obtained by The Associated Press.

The memo appears to represent an extraordinary [assertion of presidential war powers](https://apnews.com/article/trump-venezuela-boat-strikes-warships-6ffd9e6f4eb738048c816c6d8257a6ef) and draws criticism from some lawmakers, including Republican Sen. Rand Paul of Kentucky.

## Oct. 3

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth says he ordered [a fourth strike on a small boat](https://apnews.com/article/trump-hegseth-venezuela-drug-cartels-unlawful-combatants-1848b02febe08acacb82979d7da47dfb) he accuses of carrying drugs. He says four men were killed but offers no details on who they were or what group they belonged to.

## Oct. 15

Trump confirms [he has authorized the CIA](https://apnews.com/article/trump-cia-covert-operations-venezuela-ecb477ac7f07d5beaf48d44dee75c5e5) to conduct covert operations inside Venezuela and says he was weighing carrying out land operations in the country.

## Oct. 16

The Navy admiral who oversees military operations in the region [says he will retire](https://apnews.com/article/alvin-holsey-southern-command-boat-strikes-venezuela-7cf34c822b56945129baf7f0d2635f95) in December.

Adm. Alvin Holsey became leader of U.S. Southern Command only last November, overseeing an area that encompasses the Caribbean and waters off South America. Such postings typically last between three and four years.

## Oct. 16

Trump says the U.S. struck a sixth suspected drug-carrying vessel in the Caribbean, [killing two people and leaving two survivors](https://apnews.com/article/venezuela-drug-smuggling-pentagon-strike-12820c173da3de80c1a7a559c252c743) who were on the semi-submersible craft.

The president later says the survivors would be [sent to Ecuador and Colombia](https://apnews.com/article/pentagon-military-strikes-drugs-caribbean-ecuador-colombia-c1d4c025e1dfe21cc7ee67d7aa4e05bd), their home countries, “for detention and prosecution.” Repatriation avoided questions about what their legal status would have been in the U.S. justice system.

## Oct. 20

“Never before in my over 20 years on the committee can I recall seeing a combatant commander leave their post this early and amid such turmoil,” Smith said in a statement of Holsey’s impending departure. “I have also never seen such a staggering lack of transparency on behalf of an Administration and the Department to meaningfully inform Congress on the use of lethal military force.”

## Oct. 21

[Hegseth says the U.S. military](https://apnews.com/article/trump-cartels-drug-boat-strike-ffb1b720fa018336316238463924b78a) launched its eighth strike against an alleged drug-carrying vessel, killing two people in the eastern Pacific.

The attack marks an expansion of the military’s targeting area to the waters off South America where much of the cocaine from the world’s largest producers is smuggled.

## Oct. 22

Hegseth announces the ninth strike, another in the eastern Pacific, saying three men were killed.

## Oct. 24

Hegseth orders the U.S. military’s most advanced aircraft carrier, the USS Gerald R. Ford, to the region in a significant escalation of [military firepower](https://apnews.com/article/us-military-buildup-caribbean-venezuela-ships-troops-810f6181371f53536c723ea562c5277c).

## Oct. 24

Hegseth says the military [conducted the 10th strike](https://apnews.com/article/trump-cartels-hegseth-drugs-boat-strikes-6c3316b2852723e26c39dc701bba9d52) on a suspected drug-running boat, leaving six people dead. He says the vessel was operated by the [Tren de Aragua gang](https://apnews.com/article/venezuela-strike-caribbean-rubio-trump-tren-de-aragua-9e0dac7dee5a3fb14a16370508fc460d), which originated in a Venezuelan prison.

## Oct. 27

Hegseth says [three more strikes](https://apnews.com/article/drug-cartels-hegseth-pacific-8f9f65dd67c0bc55b6dd70b109df0216) were carried out in the eastern Pacific that killed 14 people and left one survivor.

Hegseth said Mexican authorities “assumed responsibility for coordinating the rescue” of the sole survivor, who is presumed dead after Mexico suspended its search after four days.

## Oct. 29

Hegseth says the U.S. military [carried out another strike](https://apnews.com/article/us-military-boat-strike-cartels-venezuela-hegseth-trump-b24b3845b3c54c1a15239e5debef0fd6) on a boat he said was carrying drugs in the eastern Pacific, killing all four people aboard in the 14th attack.

## Oct. 29

Sen. Mark Warner, the ranking Democrat on the Senate Intelligence Committee, says the Trump administration [briefed Republicans](https://apnews.com/article/us-military-boat-strike-cartels-venezuela-hegseth-trump-b24b3845b3c54c1a15239e5debef0fd6) — but not Democrats — on the boat strikes.

The Senate at the time was facing a potential vote on a war powers resolution that would prohibit strikes in or near Venezuela without congressional approval.

## Oct. 31

U.N. human rights chief Volker Türk [calls for an investigation](https://apnews.com/article/un-us-military-strikes-south-america-ed0ce092af5ad2c64b34ed51f8ab59cc) into the strikes, in what appeared to be the first such condemnation of its kind from a United Nations organization.

Ravina Shamdasani, a spokeswoman for Türk’s office, relayed his message at a briefing: “The U.S. must halt such attacks and take all measures necessary to prevent the extrajudicial killing of people aboard these boats.”

## Nov. 1

Hegseth posts another video as he announces [the 15th known strike](https://apnews.com/article/us-military-strike-drug-smugglers-caribbean-44e98087294f015dc77e17025ecbb5e8), saying the vessel in the Caribbean was operated by a U.S.-designated terrorist organization. He does not name the group and says three people were killed.

## Nov. 4

In the [16th known strike](https://apnews.com/article/boat-strikes-drug-cartels-hegseth-trump-deec973bc09899e6b586f121beb466f4), Hegseth posts on social media that two people were killed aboard a vessel in the eastern Pacific.

The same day, the [Ford aircraft carrier](https://apnews.com/article/aircraft-carriers-trump-venezuela-middle-east-a458b9c4f1a5690e19d7c532dd6b7fd7) leaves the Mediterranean Sea on its way to the Caribbean.

## Nov. 6

Hegseth announces the [17th known strike](https://apnews.com/article/trump-drug-cartels-hegseth-boat-strikes-fb83b0f02efd32d595a12aa4f6dc1864), which killed three people.

## Nov. 6

Senate Republicans [vote to reject legislation](https://apnews.com/article/trump-venezuela-strikes-congress-6c0061bf5a9c417e66f76fcc6ee57405) that would have limited Trump’s ability to order an attack on Venezuelan soil without congressional authorization.

Lawmakers from both parties had demanded [more information on the strikes](https://apnews.com/article/trump-cartels-armed-conflict-cb57804807e55a00ace60ad5f4d4f24d), but Republicans appeared more willing to give Trump leeway to continue his [buildup of naval forces](https://apnews.com/article/us-military-buildup-caribbean-venezuela-ships-troops-810f6181371f53536c723ea562c5277c).

## Nov. 9

The U.S. military [strikes two vessels](https://apnews.com/article/drug-boats-strikes-hegseth-trump-a31326b981ad622b53e918c11b2ecac5) in the eastern Pacific, killing six people, according to an announcement from Hegseth the following day.

## Nov. 10

The [20th known strike](https://apnews.com/article/boat-strike-drugs-venezuela-military-trump-3a13baec2e07a75af9cd9eafb3cc99e7) on a boat accused of transporting drugs kills four people in the Caribbean, according to a social media post from the U.S. military’s Southern Command, which oversees the region.

## Nov. 11

Venezuela’s government [launches what it says is a “massive” mobilization](https://apnews.com/article/venezuela-ford-aircraft-carrier-drugs-military-trump-a86ddc6f5f51e12c87cbd9c55978c911) of troops and volunteers for two days of exercises prompted by the U.S. military buildup.

Defense Minister Vladimir Padrino López asserts that Venezuela’s military is “stronger than ever in its unity, morale and equipment.”

## Nov. 15

[Three people are killed](https://apnews.com/article/aircraft-carrier-venezuela-trump-ford-strikes-drugs-241ea9e039d717b9f80f793922cb7cc9) after the U.S. military conducts its 21st strike on an alleged drug-smuggling boat in the eastern Pacific, according to a post from Southern Command a day later.

## Nov. 16

The Ford [arrives in the Caribbean](https://apnews.com/article/aircraft-carrier-venezuela-trump-ford-strikes-drugs-241ea9e039d717b9f80f793922cb7cc9), marking a major moment in the Trump administration’s show of force.

The aircraft carrier’s arrival brings the total number of troops in the region to around 12,000 on nearly a dozen Navy ships in what Hegseth has dubbed “Operation Southern Spear.”

## Nov. 16

Trump says the U.S. “ [may be having some discussions](https://apnews.com/article/aircraft-carrier-venezuela-trump-ford-strikes-drugs-241ea9e039d717b9f80f793922cb7cc9) ” with Maduro and that “Venezuela would like to talk,” without offering details.

“I’ll talk to anybody,” Trump said. “We’ll see what happens.”

Garcia Cano reported from Caracas, Venezuela.
