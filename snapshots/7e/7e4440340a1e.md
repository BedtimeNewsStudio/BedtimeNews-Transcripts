# UID Bali Campus | United In Diversity

## UID Bali Campus

United In Diversity (UID) Bali Campus is a pioneering learning hub located in the heart of Kura Kura Bali, designed to be the Center of Future Knowledge by fostering innovation, collaboration, and sustainable solutions for global challenges. Officially inaugurated by Indonesia's President Joko Widodo in 2022 during the G20 Summit in Bali, UID Bali Campus stands as a testament to Indonesia’s commitment to driving sustainable development and future-oriented learning.

The UID Bali Campus is more than just an educational institution—it is a vibrant ecosystem that brings together leaders, thinkers, innovators, and changemakers from diverse sectors to co-create solutions aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). With a strong focus on sustainability, innovation, and inclusive leadership, the campus hosts a range of programs, workshops, and forums that address pressing global issues, from climate change and social entrepreneurship to technology and governance.

The campus is not just one more university - Indonesia and the world already have many. The campus is a special kind of higher educational institution, united in diversity. Its outcome will include graduates who can think critically, are open to new insights, able to collaborate with multi-stakeholder, and willing to co-create and contribute to solving pressing challenges.

**UID Bali Campus Building**

A defining feature of UID Bali Campus is its architectural design, which embodies the principles of sustainability and harmony with nature. The campus is housed in a state-of-the-art, sustainable building that integrates eco-friendly materials, energy-efficient systems, and green spaces to create an environment conducive to learning and innovation. This commitment to sustainability is not only reflected in the infrastructure but also in the ethos of the programs conducted here, emphasizing environmental stewardship and sustainable growth.

The campus serves as a hub for various impactful programs and initiatives, including leadership development workshops, blended finance forums, and environmental conservation projects. It also acts as the central venue for high-profile events like the Tri Hita Karana Forum, which convenes global leaders to discuss sustainable development strategies, and the Global Blended Finance Alliance initiatives, aimed at mobilizing capital for sustainable projects.

UID Bali Campus is dedicated to nurturing the next generation of leaders and innovators who will drive positive change in Indonesia and beyond. By fostering a collaborative environment that bridges sectors and disciplines, the campus is positioned to become a beacon of future knowledge and a catalyst for transformative impact on a global scale.

**The Culture at UID Bali Campus**

The central notion of UID Bali Campus culture is harmony. This derives from the balinese tradition Tri Hita Karana: harmony within society, harmony with nature and spiritual harmony. The home of the campus, Kura Kura Island in Bali, is ideally situated for collaboration in taking on and solving local challenges.

The three principles of Tri Hita Karana are ancient but seem very relevant to current global emergencies. We aim to encourage individual and community actions that give a fairer, healthier, and happier future for all, and for generations to come.

**UID Bali Campus Mission**

The outcomes and contributions focus on creative approaches to these goals. Out primary outcome is talented learners, fellows, and graduates who are empowered to understand these goals and help to make changes for good. They learn to engage their minds, hands, and hearts. They learn ‘how’ to think and not ‘what’ to think.

A strength of UID Bali Campus is our ability to facilitate learning through reflective and contemplative methods based on critical thinking. UID Bali Campus builds on the highly successful programs of the umbrella organization, United In Diversity (UID). These include professional programs such as IDEAS (Innovative Dynamic Education and Action for Sustainability) with scholars from the MIT Sloan School of Management and the HDX programme which attracts top tier academics from around the world to teach long term urban solutions.

At UID Bali Campus we value a unique blend of ways of thinking, doing, and believing. Critical thinking and healthy scepticism are central to all learning. In addition, our community will employ four other C’s:

- Creativity - approaches that have never been taken before

- Complexity - thinking about emergent properties of complex systems

- Collaboration - working across boundaries and communities

- Change - bringing about positive transformations in society

**UID Bali Campus Features an Outstanding 111 Eco Facts**

Nestled at the heart of Kura Kura Bali, UID Bali Campus represents one of the key major elements and the development and its long-term strategic masterplan. It aims to provide an outstanding social and physical environment in which learning, research, and innovation will flourish.

Spread across 5Ha and with a sensitive consideration of culture, nature, and sustainability, the campus will offer a balanced mix of green open spaces, facilities, and social amenities.
