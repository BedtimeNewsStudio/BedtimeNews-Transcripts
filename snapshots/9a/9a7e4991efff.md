This is a **list of aviation shootdowns and accidents during the [Libyan crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_crisis_(2011%E2%80%93present) "Libyan crisis (2011–present)")**. It includes [helicopters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter "Helicopter"), [fixed-wing aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed-wing_aircraft "Fixed-wing aircraft") and [drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAV "UAV") (UAVs), and losses from the [First Libyan Civil War (2011)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_civil_war_(2011) "Libyan civil war (2011)"), the [Factional violence in Libya (2011–2014)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factional_violence_in_Libya_(2011%E2%80%932014) "Factional violence in Libya (2011–2014)"), the [Second Libyan Civil War (2014–2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_civil_war_(2014%E2%80%932020) "Libyan civil war (2014–2020)") and the current post-civil war fighting.
