![](https://d2prydcqrq5962.cloudfront.net/image/journal/article?img_id=1195218&t=1711283511955)

In 2023, the Sagrada Família welcomed a total of **4,707,367 visitors**, nearly as many as in 2019 when there were 4,717,881 visitors. This is also a progressive recovery from 2022, when 3,781,845 people visited the Basilica.

As construction is 100% privately funded, funds for the works depend on ticket sales and this progressive return to the normal levels of visitors in 2019 is a positive step towards completing Gaudí’s project.
