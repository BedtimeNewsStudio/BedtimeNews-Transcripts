## Who is Taylor Swift's tour manager, Robert Allen?

Robert Allen has been [Taylor Swift](https://www.sportskeeda.com/us/music/news-dave-portnoy-claims-taylor-swift-s-new-song-thank-aimee-kim-kardashian)'s tour manager throughout her career and continues to hold that position. Allen is also the younger sibling of Def Leppard's drummer, Rick Allen.

Rick Allen overcame a left-arm amputation and continued to play for his band. The drummer was ranked #7 on _Gigawise_ in the list of the _Greatest Drummers of All Time_. Through Robert Allen's contact, Swift also got to perform with Def Leppard on an episode of _CMT Crossroads._

Ad

Presently, Robert Allen is Swift's full-time manager and the head of 13 Management, making him one of the most influential people in the music industry operating from behind the scenes.

* * *

## All about Taylor Swift's management company, _13 Management_

![Opening Night of Taylor Swift | The Eras Tour (Photo by Kevin Winter/Getty Images for TAS Rights Management)](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2024/05/40e9a-17148016156817-1920.jpg)Opening Night of Taylor Swift \| The Eras Tour (Photo by Kevin Winter/Getty Images for TAS Rights Management)

_13 Management_ is [Taylor Swift's](https://www.sportskeeda.com/us/music/news-where-famous-songs-pet-shop-boys-neil-tennant-criticizes-taylor-swift-s-songs-the-tortured-poets-department-dominates-charts) management company, and she has been the firm's sole owner since its inception. Although little is known about the firm's internal business, Swift actively takes part in all the proceedings and delegates very little to the employees.

Ad

Although much of the firm is shrouded in secrecy, some members of the _13 Management's_ close circle were revealed in [Taylor Swift's](https://www.sportskeeda.com/us/music/news-him-know-went-on-matty-healy-s-aunt-debbie-dedes-reacts-taylor-swift-s-the-tortured-poets-department) documentary, _Miss Americana._

The company's top management comprises Swift's family: Andrea and Scott Swift and her brother, Austin Swift. They handle the big-money licensing deals for television and films.

Under Swift's family, Robert Allen, her tour manager, has the most power in 13 Management. He also works as [Swift](https://www.sportskeeda.com/us/movies/news-taylor-swift-s-latest-song-lyrics-seemingly-hint-presence-deadpool-wolverine)'s full-time manager and is one of the most powerful people in the music industry at present.

Ad

Tree Paine has been Swift's publicist since 2014. Before working for Taylor and _13 Management,_ Paine worked for Interscope Records for almost 19 years. She met Taylor in 2007 and was hired in 2014, right before the release of _1989._

Frank Bell holds the position of Director of Radio and Research and is also a shareholder at _13 Management._ He manages the radio outreach and creates marketing strategies for the company. When Frank Bell joined _13 Management_ in 2010, Robert Allen said:

Ad

> "Frank brings solid relationships and strong tactical radio expertise and insight to _13 Management_. As we ramp up for the _Taylor Swift Speak Now World Tour_ in 2011, Frank will be an invaluable resource to manage our radio outreach and develop our marketing research initiatives."

In September 2023, Douglas Baldridge took over as the general counsel at _13 Management_ after Jay Schaudies' retirement. Schaudies was a talent manager and lawyer at Swift's firm and had been with the company since its inception.

Ad

Baldridge is an experienced litigator who represented Swift in the infamous sexual harassment case she won in 2017. Apart from Baldridge, Swift works with famous music industry lawyer Donald Passman, who wrote the book _All You Need to Know About the Music Business._

Erica Worden works in _13 Management_ as Swift's personal assistant and road manager. Worden accompanies Swift in all her concerts and tours and has been a part of her inner circle since the very beginning.

Additionally, Swift's team at _13 Management_ also consists of Austin Fish and Jaidam Kirpich.
