# The world’s richest countries in 2024

## Our annual ranking compares economies in three different ways

Share

Jul 4th 2024\|2 min read

SORTING COUNTRIES into rich and poor can be difficult. Measures such as GDP are affected by population size (more people generally mean more output). But adjusting for population alone is not enough. Dollar income per person does not account for differences in prices between countries (a [Big Mac](https://www.economist.com/big-mac-index), for example, will set you back more in some places than in others, even after converting into dollars). Nor does it account for productivity (overall output per hour worked).
