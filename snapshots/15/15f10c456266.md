As Season Three’s launch approaches, _Call of Duty:_ _Warzone_ and the _Call of Duty_ franchise is celebrating two major milestones:

_Call of Duty: Warzone_ has surpassed 100 million players.

**The Numbers: What Do They Mean?**

Along with reaching 100 million Warzone players, check out the following infographic to show our findings across 28 billion matches, with a global player base of Operators infiltrating Verdansk and Rebirth Island from all seven continents.

![](https://imgs.callofduty.com/content/dam/atvi/callofduty/cod-touchui/blog/body/mw-wz/Warzone-One-Year-Infograph-Updated.jpg)

**_Call of Duty_ Surpasses 400 Million Premium Game Sales**

While celebrating the number of players who have dropped into _Warzone_, we also crossed another major milestone across the franchise: over 400 Million _Call of Duty_ premium games have been sold since the very first _Call of Duty®_ launched back in October 2003, to the franchise’s most recent release of _Call of Duty®: Black Ops Cold War_ in November 2020.

Thank you for creating countless memories, shared experiences with friends and for being a part of the greatest community in the world.
