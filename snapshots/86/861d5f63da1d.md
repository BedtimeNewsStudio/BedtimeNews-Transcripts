Title: UA 452.08 Dino Brugioni Collection 

Caption: Collection Photo # UA 452.08.01 "Photo Interpretation Unit, South Pacific, Esperito Santo, New Hebrides. Photo Interpreters at Work" 

Description: 25 photographs showing Photo Interpretation Center in the South Pacific during World War II. Interpretation Center locations on board ships at Esperito Santo, New Hebrides & Guadalcanal are shown. Naval Photography leadership are also shown: Charles H. Cox; Robert S. Quackenbush; Gooderham L. McCormick.
