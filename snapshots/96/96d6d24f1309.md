**Sindragosa**, formerly the prime consort of the [Blue Aspect](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dragon_Aspects "Dragon Aspects") [Malygos](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Malygos "Malygos") up until her death during the [War of the Ancients](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/War_of_the_Ancients "War of the Ancients"), was a mighty [frost wyrm](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Frost_wyrm "Frost wyrm") raised by the [Lich King](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lich_King "Lich King") himself to serve as the queen of the [Frostbrood](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Frostbrood "Frostbrood").[&#8202;](#ref-back-Where_Dragons_Fell-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-Where_Dragons_Fell) She was ultimately defeated by a group of [adventurers](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer "Adventurer") in [Icecrown Citadel](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Icecrown_Citadel_(instance) "Icecrown Citadel (instance)"), while her soul ultimately found peace alongside her consort within the [Veiled Ossuary](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Veiled_Ossuary "Veiled Ossuary").

## Biography

### War of the Scaleborn

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Sindragosa_the_Blue_HS.jpg/320px-Sindragosa_the_Blue_HS.jpg?e42e9b)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Sindragosa_the_Blue_HS.jpg)

[Enlarge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Sindragosa_the_Blue_HS.jpg "Enlarge")

Sindragosa in life, as seen in _[Hearthstone](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Hearthstone_(game) "Hearthstone (game)")_.

Long before the [Great Sundering](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Great_Sundering "Great Sundering"), Sindragosa was the beloved consort of [Malygos](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Malygos "Malygos"), [Aspect](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dragon_Aspects "Dragon Aspects") of the [blue dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blue_dragonflight "Blue dragonflight") and guardian of all [arcane](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arcane "Arcane") magic. She was one of the original [blue dragons](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blue_dragon "Blue dragon"), born as a [proto-dragon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Proto-dragon "Proto-dragon") and uplifted by [Order](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Order "Order") magic.[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7) Like the other dragons, Sindragosa fought the [Primalists](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Primalists "Primalists") for over a century in the [War of the Scaleborn](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/War_of_the_Scaleborn "War of the Scaleborn").

### War of the Ancients

During the [War of the Ancients](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/War_of_the_Ancients "War of the Ancients"), [Neltharion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathwing "Deathwing") the Earth Warder, Aspect of the [black flight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_dragonflight "Black dragonflight"), convinced his dragon brethren to empower a powerful artifact called the [Dragon Soul](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Demon_Soul "Demon Soul"), claiming that it would thwart the impending invasion of the [demonic](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Demon "Demon") [Burning Legion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Burning_Legion "Burning Legion"). Sindragosa seemed to suspect that anything was amiss with Neltharion. Yet, she failed to voice her concerns, so the Aspects willingly imparted a portion of their power into the artifact.[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8) However, Neltharion betrayed his dragon allies during the Legion's assault, provoking an apocalyptic battle in the skies over the [Well of Eternity](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Well_of_Eternity "Well of Eternity"). Malygos and Sindragosa joined the blue dragons in surrounding the Earth Warder and charged the black dragon. However, Neltharion used the might of the Dragon Soul to strike out, decimating nearly all of the blue dragonflight. He confronted Sindragosa,[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9) and the blast mortally wounded her and hurled her far across the land, deep into the [icy north](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Icecrown_Glacier "Icecrown Glacier").[&#8202;](#ref-back-UVG-1)[\[3\]](#refnote-UVG)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Cinematic_Art-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-Cinematic_Art)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Exploring_Azeroth_136-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-Exploring_Azeroth_136)

Blinded and near death, Sindragosa sought desperately to reach the [Dragonblight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dragonblight "Dragonblight") — the place where dragons instinctively travel to die. Weakened and no longer able to fly, Sindragosa plummeted to the cold earth in the peaks of [Icecrown](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Icecrown "Icecrown"). The blue dragon gathered what remained of her energy and called out to Malygos for aid. Her only answer was the howling arctic wind as she struggled on, but the distance was far too great; agonizingly, she realized that her spirit would not find rest within the Dragonblight. Her life continued to fade away as her sanity deteriorated. In the midst of her delirium, Sindragosa's final thoughts turned to bitterness and hatred: hatred against the Legion, hatred against Neltharion, hatred even against Malygos. But most of all, hatred against the world of mortals. And in her dying moments, Sindragosa vowed revenge.[&#8202;](#ref-back-UVG-2)[\[3\]](#refnote-UVG)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Cinematic_Art-1)[\[10\]](#refnote-Cinematic_Art)

### Wrath of the Lich King

[![Wrath of the Lich King](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/Wrath-Logo-Small.png?6705fd)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King "Wrath of the Lich King")**This section concerns content related to _[Wrath of the Lich King](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King "World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King")_.**

“Rise, Sindragosa! Rise and lead the Frostbrood into war!”

— The [Lich King](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lich_King "Lich King")[&#8202;](#ref-back-Where_Dragons_Fell-1)[\[6\]](#refnote-Where_Dragons_Fell)

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Frostwyrmcinematic.jpg/320px-Frostwyrmcinematic.jpg?5a874a)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Frostwyrmcinematic.jpg)

[Enlarge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Frostwyrmcinematic.jpg "Enlarge")

Sindragosa in the _[Wrath of the Lich King](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King "World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King")_ cinematic.

Thousands of years later, [Arthas Menethil](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Arthas_Menethil "Arthas Menethil"), the [Lich King](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lich_King "Lich King"), awoke from his long dormancy and journeyed to [Sindragosa's Fall](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sindragosa%27s_Fall "Sindragosa's Fall"), where he raised Sindragosa into undeath as a mighty skeletal [frost wyrm](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Frost_wyrm "Frost wyrm") and ordered her to lead the [Frostbrood](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Frostbrood "Frostbrood") into war.[&#8202;](#ref-back-UVG-3)[\[3\]](#refnote-UVG)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Where_Dragons_Fell-2)[\[6\]](#refnote-Where_Dragons_Fell)[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12) The Lich King's final desecration of her was to tear the spirits of Sindragosa's lost [whelps](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dragon_whelp "Dragon whelp") into undeath in front of her skeletal form, as a final affront to the great dragon.[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13) Arthas was known to use Sindragosa as a mount.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Frost_Infusion-1)[\[2\]](#refnote-Frost_Infusion)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Exploring_Azeroth_136-1)[\[11\]](#refnote-Exploring_Azeroth_136)

Sindragosa briefly appeared in the [Pit of Saron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Pit_of_Saron "Pit of Saron") after the defeat of [Scourgelord Tyrannus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Scourgelord_Tyrannus "Scourgelord Tyrannus"). While the leader of the army of liberated slaves ( [Martin Victus](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Martin_Victus "Martin Victus") for the [Alliance](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Alliance "Alliance"), [Gorkun Ironskull](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gorkun_Ironskull "Gorkun Ironskull") for the [Horde](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Horde "Horde")) commended the [adventurers](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer "Adventurer") for their bravery, Sindragosa rose up from the pit behind them and unleashed her fury, killing the leader and their army. The adventurers were pulled back to the entrance tunnel by [Jaina Proudmoore](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Jaina_Proudmoore "Jaina Proudmoore") or [Sylvanas Windrunner](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner "Sylvanas Windrunner") at the last second, saving them from a grisly death.

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Sindragosa_%28tactics%29.jpg/320px-Sindragosa_%28tactics%29.jpg?b17b66)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Sindragosa_(tactics).jpg)

[Enlarge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Sindragosa_(tactics).jpg "Enlarge")

Sindragosa in [Icecrown Citadel](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Icecrown_Citadel "Icecrown Citadel").

Sindragosa later retreated to her lair in the [Frostwing Halls](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Frostwing_Halls "Frostwing Halls") of [Icecrown Citadel](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Icecrown_Citadel_(instance) "Icecrown Citadel (instance)"), where she continued to strengthen her brood with the help of [Ymirheim](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ymirheim "Ymirheim")'s [vrykul](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Vrykul "Vrykul").[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14) After [Valithria Dreamwalker](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Valithria_Dreamwalker "Valithria Dreamwalker") was rescued by [adventurers](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer "Adventurer"), she gave the heroes verdant [life crystals](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Pulsing_Life_Crystal "Pulsing Life Crystal"), instructing them to battle the Frost Queen until she was weakened, and then use the crystals to coax out the essence that was dormant within her.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Respite-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-Respite) Similarly, [Darion Mograine](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darion_Mograine "Darion Mograine") instructed would-be wielders of [Shadowmourne](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shadowmourne "Shadowmourne") to grant [Shadow's Edge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shadow%27s_Edge "Shadow's Edge") its final infusion of power by subjecting themselves to four of Sindragosa's frozen breath attacks before ultimately destroying her.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Frost_Infusion-2)[\[2\]](#refnote-Frost_Infusion)

Sindragosa defended the Lich King in Icecrown Citadel until the [adventurers](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Adventurer "Adventurer") destroyed her, leaving only her skull behind.[&#8202;](#ref-back-UVG-4)[\[3\]](#refnote-UVG) But rather than leave her to suffer again in some form, the heroes took her [crystalline essence](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Crystalline_Essence_of_Sindragosa "Crystalline Essence of Sindragosa") to [Kalecgos](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kalecgos "Kalecgos") at [Wyrmrest Temple](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Wyrmrest_Temple "Wyrmrest Temple"), where she could truly be laid to rest.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Exploring_Azeroth_136-2)[\[11\]](#refnote-Exploring_Azeroth_136) Upon receiving the essence, Kalec stated _"So much suffering, so much needless destruction. These have not been kind times for my brothers and sisters. But thanks to your efforts, brave adventurers, this darkened yet still noble soul may find peace at last. Rest now, Sindragosa. You are home...."_[&#8202;](#ref-back-Respite-1)[\[15\]](#refnote-Respite)

### Dragonflight

[![Dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png?be4c0e)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight "Dragonflight")**This section concerns content related to _[Dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight "World of Warcraft: Dragonflight")_.**

_Main article: [Sindragosa (simulacrum)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sindragosa_(simulacrum) "Sindragosa (simulacrum)")_

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Malygos_and_Sindragosa_Sendoff.png/320px-Malygos_and_Sindragosa_Sendoff.png?8c7880)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Malygos_and_Sindragosa_Sendoff.png)

[Enlarge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Malygos_and_Sindragosa_Sendoff.png "Enlarge")

Malygos and Sindragosa sendoff.

When the [Dragon Isles](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dragon_Isles "Dragon Isles") reawakened in 40 ADP, [Kalecgos](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kalecgos "Kalecgos"), the new Aspect of the blue dragonflight, traveled to the [Azure Span](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Azure_Span "Azure Span") to reclaim the [Azure Oathstone](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Azure_Oathstone "Azure Oathstone") in hopes of restoring his aspectral powers. When he attempted to activate the oathstone, a simulacrum of Sindragosa, an arcane construct created from her knowledge and memories, appeared before him. Expecting [Malygos](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Malygos "Malygos"), the simulacrum surmised much time had passed since she was active. Kalec then explained what had happened and worked with her through his many endeavors.[&#8202;](#ref-back-16-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-16)

Eventually, Kalecgos and Sindragosa's simulacrum worked together to find more members of the [blue dragonflight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blue_dragonflight "Blue dragonflight") scattered across [Azeroth](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Azeroth "Azeroth"), so that they could free the souls of [Malygos](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Malygos "Malygos") and Sindragosa to finally rest in peace. Upon being released, the soul of Sindragosa connected with her simulacrum, making her see and feel everything that she went through alongside Kalecgos in recent times. Malygos apologized to Kalec for the burden he left him with, but in the end, both realized that Kalec was a good leader for the blue dragonflight, and that they were finally able to rest. The Spellweaver and his Azure Queen then departed to the afterlife, hand in hand.[&#8202;](#ref-back-17-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-17)

## Trivia

[![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Sindragosa_11.2_model.jpg/320px-Sindragosa_11.2_model.jpg?9a11ca)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Sindragosa_11.2_model.jpg)

[Enlarge](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Sindragosa_11.2_model.jpg "Enlarge")

Sindragosa in [patch 11.2.0](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_11.2.0 "Patch 11.2.0").

- In [patch 11.2.0](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_11.2.0 "Patch 11.2.0"), her model was changed to that of an armored [frost wyrm mount](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Undead_dragon_mounts "Undead dragon mounts"), similar to the ![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Ability_mount_redfrostwyrm_01.png/16px-Ability_mount_redfrostwyrm_01.png?7e205c)[\[Bloodbathed Frostbrood Vanquisher\]](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Reins_of_the_Bloodbathed_Frostbrood_Vanquisher) and ![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Ability_mount_redfrostwyrm_01.png/16px-Ability_mount_redfrostwyrm_01.png?7e205c)[\[Icebound Frostbrood Vanquisher\]](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Reins_of_the_Icebound_Frostbrood_Vanquisher). This affected every appearance Sindragosa makes in dragon form throughout the game. She regained her original model in [patch 11.2.5](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_11.2.5 "Patch 11.2.5").
- Sindragosa's name is likely a portmanteau of the words "sin" and "dragon", along with the traditional female blue dragon suffix "-gosa".
- Sindragosa was featured on the _Wrath of the Lich King_ [login screen](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Loading_screen "Loading screen") first introduced in [patch 3.0.2](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_3.0.2 "Patch 3.0.2"). Many players came to love and/or hate her ferocious roar.
- Sindragosa was one of the first two Icecrown Citadel bosses to be announced, the other being the Lich King himself.[&#8202;](#ref-back-19-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-19)
- Unused storyboards and animations for the _Wrath of the Lich King_ opening cinematic indicate that at some point, the cinematic was to depict the battle between the dragonflights over the Well of Eternity and Sindragosa's subsequent death.[&#8202;](#ref-back-20-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-20)
- Like [Lord Marrowgar](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lord_Marrowgar "Lord Marrowgar"), Sindragosa's model was changed in China due to Chinese law prohibiting the showing of bones in video games. Therefore, she instead uses a slightly modified [blue dragon](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blue_dragon "Blue dragon") model.
- ![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Spell_deathknight_breathofsindragosa.png/16px-Spell_deathknight_breathofsindragosa.png?60782a)[\[Breath of Sindragosa\]](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Breath_of_Sindragosa) is a level 100 [Frost](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Death_knight#Frost "Death knight") [death knight](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Death_knight "Death knight") [talent](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Talent "Talent").
- ![](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Achievement_boss_sindragosa.png/16px-Achievement_boss_sindragosa.png?20c034)[\[Sindragosa's Fury\]](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sindragosa%27s_Fury) is a golden [artifact trait](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Artifact_trait "Artifact trait") for the [Blades of the Fallen Prince](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blades_of_the_Fallen_Prince "Blades of the Fallen Prince"), summoning Sindragosa to breathe frost on enemies.
- In _[Heroes of the Storm](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_of_the_Storm "Heroes of the Storm")_, one of Arthas' Heroic abilities is "Summon Sindragosa", which causes Sindragosa to appear and fly over a portion of the battlefield, slowing and disabling enemies with her frost breath.
- Sindragosa is voiced by [Lani Minella](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lani_Minella "Lani Minella").
- Sindragosa's arcane simulacrum that she left behind on the Dragon Isles is a [high elf](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/High_elf "High elf") even though high elves didn't exist yet at the time she died.
