# Nadir, First Turkish company on the LBMA – Good Delivery List!

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6979e9bd877f27e6607176db/697c4e100fcca4efa1eac0a9_about-stats_logo.svg)
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/697a29b7edf8ec371df7e4ae/69976cd32f644b9b673e302c_LBMA-logo%5B1%5D.jpg)

Nadir Metal Refinery is the first and only Turkish company being accredited by the LBMA -London Bullion Market Association- for silver.

Nadir Metal Refinery has been accredited on the Good Delivery List for gold in 8th December 2011 and for silver in 20th June 2012.
