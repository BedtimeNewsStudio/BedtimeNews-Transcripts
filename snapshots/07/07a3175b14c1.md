**TMSR-LF1** ( [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters "Simplified Chinese characters"):液态燃料钍基熔盐实验堆lit.: "liquid fuel [thorium-based](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorium-based_nuclear_power "Thorium-based nuclear power") molten salt experimental reactor") is a 2 [MW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megawatt "Megawatt") t [molten salt reactor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molten_salt_reactor "Molten salt reactor") (MSR) [prototype](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prototype "Prototype") operating in northwest China.[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1)[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Liu_Yan_Zou_Sensitivity/Uncertainty-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-Liu_Yan_Zou_Sensitivity/Uncertainty)[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)[\[location note\]](#endnote_loc1)

The project is a collaboration of "nearly 100 domestic research institutions, universities and industrial companies", "dedicated to molten salt reactors and [thorium-uranium fuel cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorium_fuel_cycle "Thorium fuel cycle") studies".[&#8202;](#ref-back-CD20251101.4-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-CD20251101.4)

## History

Main article: [TMSR (Chinese reactor project)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TMSR_(Chinese_reactor_project) "TMSR (Chinese reactor project)")

TMSR-LF1 site [timelapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timelapse "Timelapse") (2018-2025) from [Sentinel-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentinel-2 "Sentinel-2") [satellite imagery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_imagery "Satellite imagery")

The "LF" (liquid fuel) design is directly based on the 1960s [Molten-Salt Reactor Experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molten-Salt_Reactor_Experiment "Molten-Salt Reactor Experiment").[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6) The site selected for the TMSR-LF1 is part of an [industrial park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_park "Industrial park")[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7) for the [chemical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_industry "Chemical industry") and [energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_industry "Energy industry") sectors, in a sparsely populated, arid region.[\[location note\]](#endnote_loc1) Site construction began in 2018.[&#8202;](#ref-back-WNA090822-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-WNA090822) At the groundbreaking, a [Taoist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoist "Taoist") ceremony was held; after images of it [went viral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Went_viral "Went viral") in China (an [atheist state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheist_state "Atheist state")), CAS disciplined staff members, and issued a public apology.[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9)

A reactor construction permit was issued by the Chinese [National Nuclear Safety Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Nuclear_Safety_Administration "National Nuclear Safety Administration") in January 2020.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NNSA2020-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-NNSA2020) Unforeseen delays were caused by the [COVID-19 pandemic in China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_China "COVID-19 pandemic in China").[&#8202;](#ref-back-11-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-11) Installation of equipment was finished in 2022.[&#8202;](#ref-back-CD20251101.4-1)[\[5\]](#refnote-CD20251101.4) Over 90 percent of the reactor's components are domestically produced, with 100 percent localization of key parts and a fully independent [supply chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supply_chain "Supply chain").[&#8202;](#ref-back-CD20251101.5-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-CD20251101.5)

Testing followed;[&#8202;](#ref-back-SCMP20210731-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-SCMP20210731)[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14) in August 2022, the Chinese [Ministry of Ecology and Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Ecology_and_Environment "Ministry of Ecology and Environment") informed SINAP that its [commissioning plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_commissioning "Project commissioning") for the LF1 had been approved.[&#8202;](#ref-back-WNA090822-1)[\[8\]](#refnote-WNA090822) A ten-year operating license was issued in June 2023.[&#8202;](#ref-back-nei-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-nei)[&#8202;](#ref-back-16-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-16) For the first 5–8 years, it is to be run in batch mode, before converting to continuous mode.[&#8202;](#ref-back-WNA090822-2)[\[8\]](#refnote-WNA090822)

[Criticality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticality_(status) "Criticality (status)") was first achieved on 11 October 2023.[&#8202;](#ref-back-SCMP20240726-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-SCMP20240726)[&#8202;](#ref-back-NNSA2023-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-NNSA2023)[&#8202;](#ref-back-GIF20250122-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-GIF20250122) On 17 June 2024, full power (2MWt) operation was achieved.[&#8202;](#ref-back-GIF20250122-1)[\[19\]](#refnote-GIF20250122) In September, it received a license to load thorium into the molten salt, the initial loading of which happened the following month,[&#8202;](#ref-back-CD20251101.4-2)[\[5\]](#refnote-CD20251101.4) and on 8 October, it operated at full power for 10 days with thorium in the molten salt; [Protactinium-233](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protactinium-233 "Protactinium-233") was detected, indicating successful [nuclear breeding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breeder_reactor "Breeder reactor").[&#8202;](#ref-back-GIF20250122-2)[\[19\]](#refnote-GIF20250122)[&#8202;](#ref-back-CD20251101.1-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-CD20251101.1)[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-21)

## Specifications

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f8/Conservativeness_Study_on_the_Seismic_Analysis_Method_for_Research_Reactor_Plant_Structure_Based_on_TMSR-LF1.fig2.png/500px-Conservativeness_Study_on_the_Seismic_Analysis_Method_for_Research_Reactor_Plant_Structure_Based_on_TMSR-LF1.fig2.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Conservativeness_Study_on_the_Seismic_Analysis_Method_for_Research_Reactor_Plant_Structure_Based_on_TMSR-LF1.fig2.png)

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/17/Conservativeness_Study_on_the_Seismic_Analysis_Method_for_Research_Reactor_Plant_Structure_Based_on_TMSR-LF1.fig1.png/500px-Conservativeness_Study_on_the_Seismic_Analysis_Method_for_Research_Reactor_Plant_Structure_Based_on_TMSR-LF1.fig1.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Conservativeness_Study_on_the_Seismic_Analysis_Method_for_Research_Reactor_Plant_Structure_Based_on_TMSR-LF1.fig1.png)

LF1 building [3D model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_model "3D model") and [floor plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floor_plan "Floor plan") published for [seismic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismic_analysis "Seismic analysis"); below-ground area (pink) holds the reactor.

The TMSR-LF1 is a [Generation IV reactor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generation_IV_reactor "Generation IV reactor") constructed with the following specifications:[&#8202;](#ref-back-22-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-22)[&#8202;](#ref-back-23-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-23)[&#8202;](#ref-back-ChenXia2019-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-ChenXia2019)

- Thermal power: 2 MW
- Fuel salt: [FLiBe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FLiBe "FLiBe") (>99.95% Li-7) with [fluorides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluoride "Fluoride") of [zirconium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zirconium_tetrafluoride "Zirconium tetrafluoride"), [uranium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uranium_tetrafluoride "Uranium tetrafluoride") ( [HALEU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HALEU "HALEU"): 19.75% U-235), and [thorium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorium_tetrafluoride "Thorium tetrafluoride")
  - inlet temperature: 630°C
  - outlet temperature: 650°C
  - volume: 1.68 m3
  - flow rate: ~50kg/s
- Coolant salt: FLiBe
  - inlet temperature: 560°C
  - outlet temperature: 580°C
  - flow rate: ~42kg/s
- Cover gas: [Argon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argon "Argon") (0.05 MPa)

  - volume: 1.6 m3
- Moderator: [nuclear graphite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_graphite "Nuclear graphite")
- Structural Material: UNS N10003 [superalloy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superalloy "Superalloy")
- Lifetime: 10 years
  - equivalent full power days: 300
  - maximum full power days per year: 60

The reactor is located underground, seated at the bottom of a 14m (46 foot) deep [dry well](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_well "Dry well"), which is capped at ground level, and above which rises a 20m (66 foot) tall roofed [atrium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atrium_(architecture) "Atrium (architecture)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-25-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-25)

## Location Notes

See also " [Maps](#Maps)" section of the lede's infobox. The most recently published official site plan map is Figure 2.1-2 in

**[^LF1 Location:](#ref_loc1)** the LF1 reactor is sited within an industrial park located in [Hongshagang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_township-level_divisions_of_Gansu#Minqin_County "List of township-level divisions of Gansu") (town), [Minqin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minqin_County "Minqin County") (county), [Wuwei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuwei,_Gansu "Wuwei, Gansu") (prefecture), [Gansu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gansu "Gansu") (province), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China "China"). The area is a [semi-desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alashan_Plateau_semi-desert "Alashan Plateau semi-desert") just south of the [Badain Jaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badain_Jaran_Desert "Badain Jaran Desert") section of the [Gobi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobi "Gobi"). As per official documentation, the TMSR-LF1 site is located at 38°57'31" N, 102°36'55" E. However, due to the [China GPS shift problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restrictions_on_geographic_data_in_China#GPS_shift_problem "Restrictions on geographic data in China"), the reactor location using Western GPS coordinates is approximately [38°57′37″N102°36′44″E﻿ / ﻿38.9602°N 102.6122°E﻿ / 38.9602; 102.6122](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=TMSR-LF1&params=38.9602_N_102.6122_E_) (about a third of a kilometer offset).
