# News Bulletin  December 28, 2021

**December 28, 2021**

---

No.326

## NEWS HIGHLIGHTS

### Basic Complete Tabulation on Population and Households of the 2020 Population Census of Japan was released

   On November 30, 2021, the Basic Complete Tabulation on Population and Households of the 2020 Population Census of Japan was released.  
    Based on the results of the complete count of questionnaires, the tabulation provides statistics on basic characteristics of population and households, statistics on housing conditions of households, statistics on foreigners, and statistics on households with aged household members, for the whole of Japan, prefectures and municipalities.

#### - Population

   The population of Japan as of October 1, 2020, was 126,146 thousand, a decline of 949 thousand (0.7%) from 2015, or 0.15% on an annual basis.  
    Broken down by sex, the male and female population numbered 61,350 thousand and 64,797 thousand, respectively, and the sex ratio was 94.7.  
    The population of Japanese citizens\* was 123,399 thousand (97.8% of the total population), a decline of 1,783 thousand (1.4%) from 2015, or 0.29% on an annual basis.  
    The population of foreign residents usually living in Japan\* was 2,747 thousand (2.2% of the total population), an increase of 835 thousand (43.6%) from 2015, or 7.51% on an annual basis.  
    Among the 47 prefectures, the population increased in 8 prefectures (Tokyo-to marked the highest increase rate [3.9%], followed by Okinawa-ken [2.4%] and Kanagawa-ken [1.2%]), while it decreased in 39 prefectures. Among 1,719 municipalities, the population decreased in 1,419 municipalities (82.5% of municipalities).

#### - Population by age group

   The population under 15 years old\* was 15,032 thousand (11.9% of the total population), those aged 15 to 64\* totaled 75,088 thousand (59.5%), and those aged 65 years old and over\* numbered 36,027 thousand (28.6%).  
    The percentage of the population under 15 years old\* was the lowest level in the world, and that of the population aged 65 years old and over\* was the highest level in the world.

#### - Foreigners

   Looking at the population of foreign residents usually living in Japan by nationality, ?China? was the largest with 667 thousand people (accounting for 27.8% of the total of the foreign residents), followed by ?Korea? with 375 thousand (15.6%) and ?Vietnam? with 321 thousand (13.4%).

#### - Households

  The number of households in Japan was 55,830 thousand as of October 1, 2020, an increase of 2,381 thousand from 2015.  
   The members per private household by prefecture decreased in all 47 prefectures, with Yamagata-ken having the largest number of persons at 2.61 and Tokyo-to having the smallest number at 1.92.

  For more details, please see: [http://www.stat.go.jp/english/data/kokusei/2020/summary.html](../../data/kokusei/2020/summary.html)

  \* These figures are based on the results with imputation. "Imputation" is the method of proportionally distributing by unknown values.
