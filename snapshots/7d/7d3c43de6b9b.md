# Brazil govt debt, public sector deficit end 2020 at record highs -central bank

Reuters

January 29, 2021

BRASILIA, Jan 29 (Reuters) - Brazil's national debt endedlast year at a record 89.3% of gross domestic product, and thepublic sector primary deficit ended 2020 at a record 9.5% ofGDP, the central bank said on Friday.

The public sector deficit excluding interest payments was51.8 billion reais ($9.5 billion) in December, the central banksaid, close to the 51.5 billion reais shortfall forecast in aReuters poll of economists and bringing the 2020 deficit to 703billion reais.($1 = 5.45 reais)(Reporting by Jamie McGeever; Editing by Hugh Lawson)
