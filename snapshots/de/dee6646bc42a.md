# IOC Members

© IOC/Quinton Meyer

The IOC members, natural persons, are representatives of the IOC in their respective countries, and not their country's delegate within the IOC. As stated in the [Olympic Charter](https://olympics.com/ioc/olympic-charter): "Members of the IOC represent and promote the interests of the IOC and of the [Olympic Movement](https://olympics.com/ioc/olympic-movement) in their countries and in the organisations of the Olympic Movement in which they serve".

They are listed here in order of seniority of their election.
