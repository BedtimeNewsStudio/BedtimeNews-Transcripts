## Format

\[ [edit](https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/index.php?title=World_Championship/2011&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Format")\]

- **Qualifiers** \- _April 2nd - June 6th, 2011_
  - Four Regional Qualifiers
    - The winners of each Qualifier will advance to the World Championship.
    - The runners-up and third place teams of the Europe and North America Qualifiers will advance to the World Championship.
- **Group Stage** \- _June 17th, 2011_
  - All eight teams are divided into two groups where they play in a Round Robin format.
    - All matches are played in a Bo1.
    - The top teams in each group advances to the Upper Bracket Semifinals of the Playoffs.
    - The bottom team in each group is eliminated.
    - The remaining teams advance to the Upper Bracket Quarterfinals of the Playoffs.
- **Playoffs** \- _June 18th - June 19th, 2011_
  - Six teams play in a double-elimination format over two days.
    - Two teams begin in the Upper Bracket Semifinals, four in the Upper Bracket Quarterfinals.
    - 5th Place Match is Bo1, all other rounds are Bo3.
    - Teams placing second in their Group start with a one win lead in their Upper Bracket Semifinal match.
    - Teams who have defeated an opponent previously in the Playoff Bracket start with a one win lead.
