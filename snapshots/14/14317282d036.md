(a) Cancellation of removal for certain permanent residentsThe [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) may cancel removal in the case of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) who is inadmissible or deportable from the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) if the [alien—](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b)

(1)

has been an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) [lawfully admitted for permanent residence](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2124716147-1201680062&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) for not less than 5 years,

(2)

has resided in the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) continuously for 7 years after having been admitted in any status, and

(3)

has not been convicted of any aggravated felony.

(b) Cancellation of removal and adjustment of status for certain nonpermanent residents

(1) In generalThe [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) may cancel removal of, and adjust to the status of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) [lawfully admitted for permanent residence](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2124716147-1201680062&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b), an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) who is inadmissible or deportable from the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) if the [alien—](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b)

(A)

has been physically present in the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) for a continuous period of not less than 10 years immediately preceding the date of such application;

(B)

has been a person of good moral character during such period;

(C)

has not been convicted of an offense under section [1182(a)(2)](/uscode/text/8/1182#a_2), [1227(a)(2)](/uscode/text/8/1227#a_2), or [1227(a)(3)](/uscode/text/8/1227#a_3) of this title, subject to paragraph (5); and

(D)

establishes that removal would result in exceptional and extremely unusual hardship to the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=)’s spouse, parent, or child, who is a citizen of the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) or an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) [lawfully admitted for permanent residence](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2124716147-1201680062&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b).

(2) Special rule for battered spouse or child

(A) AuthorityThe [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) may cancel removal of, and adjust to the status of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) [lawfully admitted for permanent residence](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2124716147-1201680062&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b), an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) who is inadmissible or deportable from the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) if the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) demonstrates that—

(i)

(I)

the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) has been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty by a spouse or parent who is or was a [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) citizen (or is the parent of a child of a [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) citizen and the child has been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty by such citizen parent);

(II)

the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) has been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty by a spouse or parent who is or was a lawful [permanent](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-668488878-1201680094&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) resident (or is the parent of a child of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) who is or was a lawful [permanent](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-668488878-1201680094&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) resident and the child has been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty by such [permanent](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-668488878-1201680094&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) resident parent); or

(III)

the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) has been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty by a [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) citizen or lawful [permanent](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-668488878-1201680094&term_occur=999&term_src=) resident whom the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) intended to marry, but whose marriage is not legitimate because of that [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) citizen’s or lawful [permanent](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-668488878-1201680094&term_occur=999&term_src=) resident’s bigamy;

(ii)

the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) has been physically present in the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) for a continuous period of not less than 3 years immediately preceding the date of such application, and the issuance of a charging document for removal proceedings shall not toll the 3-year period of continuous physical presence in the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=);

(iii)

the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) has been a person of good moral character during such period, subject to the provisions of subparagraph (C);

(iv)

the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) is not inadmissible under paragraph (2) or (3) of [section 1182(a) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1182#a), is not deportable under paragraphs (1)(G) or (2) through (4) of [section 1227(a) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1227#a), subject to paragraph (5), and has not been convicted of an aggravated felony; and

(v)

the removal would result in extreme hardship to the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=), the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=)’s child, or the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=)’s parent.

(B) Physical presence

Notwithstanding subsection (d)(2), for purposes of subparagraph (A)(ii) or for purposes of [section 1254(a)(3) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1254#a_3) (as in effect before the title III–A effective date in section 309 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996), an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) shall not be considered to have failed to maintain continuous physical presence by reason of an absence if the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) demonstrates a connection between the absence and the battering or extreme cruelty perpetrated against the [alien.](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) No absence or portion of an absence connected to the battering or extreme cruelty shall count toward the 90-day or 180-day limits established in subsection (d)(2). If any absence or aggregate absences exceed 180 days, the absences or portions of the absences will not be considered to break the period of continuous presence. Any such period of time excluded from the 180-day limit shall be excluded in computing the time during which the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) has been physically present for purposes of the 3-year requirement set forth in this subparagraph, subparagraph (A)(ii), and [section 1254(a)(3) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1254#a_3) (as in effect before the title III–A effective date in section 309 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996).

(C) Good moral character

Notwithstanding [section 1101(f) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1101#f), an act or [conviction](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2143927138-1201680132&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) that does not bar the [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) from granting relief under this paragraph by reason of subparagraph (A)(iv) shall not bar the [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) from finding the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) to be of good moral character under subparagraph (A)(iii) or [section 1254(a)(3) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1254#a_3) (as in effect before the title III–A effective date in section 309 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996), if the [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) finds that the act or [conviction](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2143927138-1201680132&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) was connected to the [alien’](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=)s having been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty and determines that a waiver is otherwise warranted.

(D) Credible evidence considered

In acting on applications under this paragraph, the [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) shall consider any credible evidence relevant to the application. The determination of what evidence is credible and the weight to be given that evidence shall be within the sole discretion of the [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=).

(3) Recordation of date

With respect to [aliens](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) who the [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) adjusts to the status of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) [lawfully admitted for permanent residence](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2124716147-1201680062&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) under paragraph (1) or (2), the [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) shall record the [alien’](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=)s lawful admission for [permanent](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-668488878-1201680094&term_occur=999&term_src=) residence as of the date of the [Attorney General’](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=)s cancellation of removal under paragraph (1) or (2).

(4) Children of battered aliens and parents of battered alien children

(A) In generalThe [Attorney General](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-133271130-1485256779&term_occur=999&term_src=) shall grant parole under [section 1182(d)(5) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1182#d_5) to any [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) who is a—

(i)

child of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) granted relief under section [1229b(b)(2)](/uscode/text/8/1229b#b_2) or [1254(a)(3)](/uscode/text/8/1254#a_3) of this title (as in effect before the title III–A effective date in section 309 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996); or

(ii)

parent of a child [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) granted relief under section [1229b(b)(2)](/uscode/text/8/1229b#b_2) or [1254(a)(3)](/uscode/text/8/1254#a_3) of this title (as in effect before the title III–A effective date in section 309 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996).

(B) Duration of parole

The grant of parole shall extend from the time of the grant of relief under subsection (b)(2) or [section 1254(a)(3) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1254#a_3) (as in effect before the title III–A effective date in section 309 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996) to the time the application for adjustment of status filed by [aliens](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) covered under this paragraph has been finally adjudicated. Applications for adjustment of status filed by [aliens](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) covered under this paragraph shall be treated as if the applicants were [VAWA self-petitioners.](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-915737567-1201680156&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) Failure by the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) granted relief under subsection (b)(2) or [section 1254(a)(3) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1254#a_3) (as in effect before the title III–A effective date in section 309 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996) to exercise due diligence in filing a visa petition on behalf of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) described in clause (i) or (ii) may result in revocation of parole.

(5) Application of domestic violence waiver authority

The authority provided under [section 1227(a)(7) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1227#a_7) may apply under paragraphs (1)(B), (1)(C), and (2)(A)(iv) in a cancellation of removal and adjustment of status proceeding.

(6) Relatives of trafficking victims

(A) In generalUpon written request by a law enforcement official, the Secretary of Homeland Security may parole under [section 1182(d)(5) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1182#d_5) any [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) who is a relative of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) granted continued presence under [section 7105(c)(3)(A) of title 22](/uscode/text/22/7105#c_3_A), if the relative—

(i) was, on the date on which law enforcement applied for such continued presence—

(I)

in the case of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) granted continued presence who is under 21 years of age, the spouse, child, parent, or [unmarried](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-1743703365-1201680102&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) sibling under 18 years of age, of the [alien;](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=title:8:chapter:12:subchapter:II:part:IV:section:1229b) or

(II)

in the case of an [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) granted continued presence who is 21 years of age or older, the spouse or child of the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=); or

(ii)

is a parent or sibling of the [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) who the requesting law enforcement official, in consultation with the Secretary of Homeland Security, as appropriate, determines to be in present danger of retaliation as a result of the [alien’](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=)s escape from the severe form of trafficking or cooperation with law enforcement, irrespective of age.

(B) Duration of parole

(i) In general

The Secretary may extend the parole granted under subparagraph (A) until the final adjudication of the application filed by the principal [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=) under [section 1101(a)(15)(T)(ii) of this title](/uscode/text/8/1101#a_15_T_ii).

(ii) Other limits on durationIf an application described in clause (i) is not filed, the parole granted under subparagraph (A) may extend until the later of—

(I)

the date on which the principal [alien](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-92903111-1485256781&term_occur=999&term_src=)’s authority to remain in the [United States](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8-USC-2032517217-1201680101&term_occur=999&term_src=) under [section 7105(c)(3)(A) of title 22](/uscode/text/22/7105#c_3_A) is terminated; or

(II)
