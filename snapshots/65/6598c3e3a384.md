# SHEIN：按需时尚生产模式

sheingroup.com A Pioneering Model for On-Demand Fashion Our on-demand model allows for a wider choice at affordable prices, making fashion accessible to more people around the world.
DEMAND-DRIVEN SMALL BATCH PRODUCTION SHEIN has the ability to measure demand signals and pass those signals directly to supplier partners, allowing for small-batch, less wasteful production ON-DEMAND PRODUCTION SHEIN listens and responds to customers’ needs so emerging trends can quickly become SHEIN products CUSTOMER FEEDBACK & ANALYSIS Customer demand signals guide whether designs are retired or replenished, and inform new designs, minimizing excess inventory DIGITALIZED SUPPLY CHAIN SHEIN’s supplier partners use our proprietary technology, which provides real-time insights into demand measurement, production planning, and supply chain visibility Audience Engagement Analysis of customer insights to better understand what customers want, when they want it Digitally-Empowered Supply Chain Proprietary technology improves efficiency and minimizes waste, lowering operational costs for manufacturers and lowering prices for customers Small Batch Manufacturing = Less Waste New designs are created in small batches of 100-200 pieces to measure demand, reducing inventory waste to single digit percentages—far below the industry average
