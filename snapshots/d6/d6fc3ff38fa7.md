## Putting an end to offshore tax evasion

With [**174 members**](#members), the Global Forum on Transparency and Exchange of Information for Tax Purposes is the leading [**multilateral body**](https://www.oecd.org/en/networks/global-forum-tax-transparency/who-we-are.html) mandated to ensure that jurisdictions around the world adhere to and effectively implement the standard on transparency and exchange of information on request (EOIR) and the standards for automatic exchange of information, covering financial accounts under the Common Reporting Standard (CRS) and crypto-asset transactions under the new Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF).

These objectives are achieved through robust **monitoring and peer review processes**. The Global Forum also runs an extensive **capacity-building programme** to support its members to implement the standards and help tax authorities make the best use of cross-border information sharing channels.

### Transparency and exchange of information on request

The EOIR standard requires the transparency of banking and accounting records as well as ownership of entities and legal arrangements. It provides a framework for obtaining information on request between tax authorities. The Global Forum monitors and peer reviews the global implementation of the standard since 2010.

### Automatic exchange of information

The standards for the automatic exchange of information (AEOI) cover financial accounts, under the Common Reporting Standard (CRS), and crypto-asset transactions under the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF). They allow for the automatic annual exchange of information on offshore financial accounts and crypto-asset transactions. The Global Forum continuously peer reviews and monitors their implementation, including the associated confidentiality and data safeguards requirements.

[CRS compliance ratings](https://www.oecd.org/en/networks/global-forum-tax-transparency/resources/automatic-exchange-of-information-ratings.html)

[Peer review of the automatic exchange of financial account information (2025)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/peer-review-of-the-automatic-exchange-of-financial-account-information-2025-update_bbf150e4-en.html)
