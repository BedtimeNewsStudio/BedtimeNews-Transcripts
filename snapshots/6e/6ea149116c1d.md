## I. Cars

## 1. Drivers Must Hold the Following Two Qualifications, Conditions, and Licenses
(1) 18 years of age.
(2) Attained (or renewed) a Taiwan driver's license or a reciprocal driver's
license and an international driver's license.

## 2. Notes Before Driving
(1) It is government policy to purchase mandatory automobile liability insurance.
(2) The majority of highways are tolled;
cars equipped with eTag barcodes are
recommended to facilitate toll collection.

(3) When driving with children
under 4 years old and 18 kg
(excluding taxis), a portable
baby crib, child-suitable
seat, or rear-facing child car
seat must be fitted in the
back seat according to age
and weight.

(4) Driving after taking drugs or
consuming alcohol is strictly
prohibited

## 3. Notes on Driving

(1) Drivers and passengers must wear a seatbelt.

Children under the age of 12 must be seated in the back.

(2) Cars use the right-hand lane of the road, and signs and markings must be followed accordingly.

Drive on the right

(3) Speed Regulations:
The speed limit is 80-110kph on highways and 60-90 kph on expressways. You must drive according to the speed limit. Speed markings on normal roads must be obeyed. Drive at 50kph if there are no speed markings on the road, 40kph in a low-speed vehicle lane, and 30kph on roads or alleys without lane markings in the city.

(4) Dangerous driving manners, including zigzagging, severely exceeding the speed limit, or arbitrarily approaching or suddenly changing lanes to force other cars to give way, are strictly prohibited.

(5) Drivers must not use a hand-held mobile phone or smoke while driving.

(6) White stripes markings on the road are for cars traveling in the same direction; yellow stripe markings are to separate cars traveling in opposite directions.

(7) Drivers must always allow pedestrians to cross on a pedestrian crossing regardless of whether a traffic officer is directing the traffic or a sign suggesting to do so.

(8) Turning right on a red light is prohibited unless there is a sign for turning right at the signalized intersection.

---

(9) When traveling through an intersection, cars approaching a red flashing light should give way to
cars approaching a yellow flashing light.

(10) Traveling a roundabout:
Cars should travel in an
anti-clockwise direction at a
roundabout.

(11) Some highways are toled, including National Freeway 1, National
Freeway 3, National freeway 3A,
and National Freeway 5. Please
note that there are no signs at the
entrance to a highway to specify "收費 (toll fees)."

## 4. Notes on Car Parking

(1) Red lines on the side of a road
mean no temporary parking
allowed while yellow lines mean
no temporary parking over 3 minutes.

No temporary parking
No parking

(2) Roadside parking bays in the city
are mostly metered. When a ticket
is issued, you must pay at a convenience store within the date given
on the ticket.

(3) No parking or temporary parking is permitted within 10 meters of an
intersection or bus stop; or 5 meters from fire hydrants and fire
station entrances.

(4) Temporary parking in a yellow grid or lane channel marking is strictly
prohibited.

---

(5) Children under 6 years must not be left alone in a car.

(6) Parking spaces dedicated to the disabled or women with children
must not be used without a valid certificate. A certificate must be
clearly visible through the windscreen when parking.

## 5. Other notes on Car

(1) When involved in a road traffic accident, the police must be
notified immediately (call 110) and the driver must remain at the
scene. Otherwise, he/she may be found criminally liable. If there
is an injured person, an ambulance can be called at the same
time (call 119).

II. Motorcycles

## 1. Motorcyclists Must Have the Following Two Qualifications, Conditions, and Licenses
(1) 18 years of age.
(2) Attained (or renewed) a Taiwanese driver's license or a reciprocal driver's
license and an international driver's license.

## 2. Notes Before Riding

(1) It is government policy to purchase mandatory motorcycle liability insurance.

(2) Traveling on a motorcycle after taking drugs or consuming alcohol is strictly prohibited. The offender will face criminal liability.

(3) Only 1 passenger is permitted to be carried on a motorcycle. No passenger is permitted on a small motorcycle.

## 3. Notes on Riding a Motorcycle

(1) Helmets are mandatory for both the motorcyclist and passenger.
(2) Motorcycles use the right-hand lane of the road, and signs and markings must be followed accordingly.
(3) Riding a motorcycle dangerously, including zigzag riding, severely
exceeding the speed limit, or arbitrarily approaching or suddenly
changing lanes to force other motorcycles to give way is strictly prohibited.

(4) A motorcyclist must not use a hand-held mobile phone or smoke while riding.

(5) White stripe markings on the road are for cars (motorcycles) traveling in the same direction; yellow stripe markings are to separate
cars (motorcycles) traveling in opposite directions.

(6) Motorcycles are prohibited on highways or expressways; however,
motorcycles over 250cc are permitted on expressways.

---

(7) Except for those over 250cc, motorcycles should stay on the outer 2 lanes on a 3-lane single direction road. Where there is a "No Motorcycle"禁行機車 (sign or marking), the motorcyclist must follow

(8) Motorcyclists must follow the two-stage left turn rule when turning at intersections.

(9) Motorcyclists must always allow a pedestrian to cross on a pedestrian crossing regardless of whether a traffic officer is directing the traffic or a sign suggesting to do so.

(10) Turning right on a red light is prohibited unless there is a sign for turning right at the signalized intersection.

(11) When traveling through an intersection, motorcycles approaching a red flashing light should give way to other motorcycles approaching a yellow flashing light.

(12) Traveling on a roundabout: Motorcycles should travel in an anti-clockwise direction at a roundabout.

## 4. Notes on Motorcycle Parking

(1) Red lines on the side of a road mean no temporary parking while yellow lines mean no temporary parking over 3 minutes.

---

(2) Some roadside parking in the city is metered. When a ticket is issued,
you must pay at a convenience store within the date given on the
ticket.

(3) No parking or temporary parking is permitted within 10 meters of an
intersection or a bus stop; or 5 meters from fire hydrants and fire
station entrances.

(4) Temporary parking in a yellow grid or lane channel marking is strictly
prohibited.

(5) Motorcycles over 250cc must be parked in a car parking bay. Other
motorcycles must park in dedicated spaces.

(6) Parking in space dedicated to the disabled is prohibited without a certificate.

## 5. Other notes on Motorcycle

(1) When involved in a road traffic accident, the police must be notified
immediately (call 110), and the motorcyclist must remain at the
scene. Otherwise, he/she may be found criminally liable. If there is an
injured person, an ambulance can be called at the same time (call
119).

## III. Bicycles

## 1. Notes Before Riding a Bicycle

(1) An electric bicycle must have a certification label either pasted or hung on the bicycle.
合格標章電動自行車

(2) A cyclist must be 18 years or over to carry a passenger (limited to children of 1-6 years old). When carrying a child on a bicycle, both the bicycle and seat must have a certification label.

(3) No passengers are permitted on an electric bicycle.

(4) Riding a bicycle after consuming alcohol is strictly prohibited, the offender will face criminal liability.

## 2. Notes on Riding a Bicycle

(1) A bicycle must travel on the right side of the road in the slow lane or on the side of the road.
Except for some sidewalks where cyclists are permitted, using other sidewalks, fast lanes, highways, and expressways are strictly prohibited.

(2) Riding an electric bicycle requires a helmet. Helmets are recommended for those riding other types of bicycles and their passengers.

(3) A cyclist must not use a hand-held mobile phone.

(4) White stripe markings on the road are for cars (bicycles) traveling in the same direction; yellow stripe markings are to separate cars (bicycles) traveling in opposite directions.

(5) Travel speed must not exceed 25kph.

(6) Cyclists must always allow a pedestrian to cross on a pedestrian crossing regardless of whether a traffic officer is directing the traffic or a sign suggesting to do so.

(7) Turning right on a red light is prohibited unless there is a sign for turning right at the signalized intersection.

## 3. Notes on Parking a Bicycle

Bicycles may be parked in motorcycle parking spaces on the road that are not metered. Parking in a bay dedicated to the disabled is prohibited.

When involved in a road traffic accident, the police must be notified immediately (call 110) and the cyclist must remain at the scene. Otherwise, he/she may be found criminally liable. If there is an injured person, an ambulance can be called at the same time (call 119).

## IV. Pedestrians

## 1. Notes on Pedestrians

(1) Pedestrians, including those who walk, those in a wheelchair, or medical mobility scooter, should use a sidewalk or pedestrian arcade. When on the road without a sidewalk or pedestrian arcade pedestrians must walk on the side while being mindful of other vehicles and pedestrians' movement.

(2) Scooters, inline skates, electric scooters, SEGWAYs, recreational scooters, or other similar personal vehicles are prohibited on the road.

(3) When crossing at an intersection, you must be mindful of the traffic direction and turning motorcycles.

(4) Do not arbitrarily cross the road; pedestrian crossings must be used for crossing where there is one.
