Adamson University

**Adamson University** (Filipino: _Pamantasang Adamson_; **AdU** or **ADU**) is a private [Catholic university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_university "Catholic university") operated by the [Congregation of the Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congregation_of_the_Mission "Congregation of the Mission") located in [Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila "Manila"), Philippines. The university has academic programs in graduate school, law, the [liberal arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_arts_education "Liberal arts education"), [sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sciences "Sciences"), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering "Engineering"), [nursing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nursing "Nursing"), [pharmacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacy "Pharmacy"), [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture "Architecture"), [business administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_administration "Business administration"), and education, as well as secondary, elementary, and [preschool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preschool "Preschool"). It is one of the universities in the country to receive an autonomous status by the [Commission on Higher Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Higher_Education_(Philippines) "Commission on Higher Education (Philippines)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-autogenerated4-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-autogenerated4)

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/8/86/Adamson_text_logo.png/250px-Adamson_text_logo.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Adamson_text_logo.png) Logotype of Adamson University

Adamson is a member of the [University Athletic Association of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Athletic_Association_of_the_Philippines "University Athletic Association of the Philippines"). In [its 80th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAAP_Season_80 "UAAP Season 80"), the university won first place in the cheerdancing competition.[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Adamson_University&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

George Lucas Adamson, a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks "Greeks") chemist, founded the **Adamson School of Industrial Chemistry** on June 20, 1932 as a single-classroom school for [industrial chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_chemistry "Industrial chemistry"). His cousin, Alexander Athos Adamson, came to the country in 1932 to assist him in administering the then-newly founded school, where he served as its vice president, treasurer, and registrar. Alexander's brother, George Athos Adamson, arrived two years after to serve as a professor and [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_(education) "Dean (education)") of both the school and the College of Engineering. On February 19, 1936, the school was renamed as the **Adamson School of Industrial Chemistry and Engineering**. In 1939, George Lucas' wife, Evdoxia Savaides Adamson, began working at the school, serving as a professor and eventually dean of both Colleges of Education and Liberal Arts and Sciences. She was soon followed by George Athos' wife, [Sofia Adamson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Adamson "Sofia Adamson"), who taught in the College of Education and briefly served as the Junior Normal College's Director. The school became university on February 5, 1941 upon the approval of the [Department of Public Instruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Education_(Philippines) "Department of Education (Philippines)").

The Adamsons worked at the university after the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War "Second World War"), except for George Athos and Sofia, who left the country after the war. George Lucas Adamson served as the university's president for 35 years. During his tenure, the university became a probationary member of the [University Athletics Association of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Athletics_Association_of_the_Philippines "University Athletics Association of the Philippines") in 1952; it was granted full membership in 1971. On December 4, 1964, the university was turned over to the [Congregation of the Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congregation_of_the_Mission "Congregation of the Mission") and was incorporated into the Adamson-Ozanam Education Institutions, Inc. Its patron saint is [Saint Vincent de Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Vincent_de_Paul "Saint Vincent de Paul").[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5)

Spanish priest Leandro I. Montañana became the university's second president, holding the office until 1985. He was succeeded by Fr. Rolando S. Dela Goza, C.M., the first Filipino president of the university. He held the office until 1994, where he was succeeded by Fr. Jimmy Belita, C.M. who served as president until 2003. Fr. Gregorio L. Bañaga, Jr., C.M. became the university's fifth president, a position he held until 2015.

The university celebrated its [diamond jubilee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_jubilee "Diamond jubilee") in 2007. The [National Historical Commission of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Historical_Commission_of_the_Philippines "National Historical Commission of the Philippines") designated the university as a historical site. A memorial was installed in front of the St. Vincent Building, the oldest building on campus, commemorating this heritage.

Adamson University was granted autonomous status by the [Commission on Higher Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Higher_Education_(Philippines) "Commission on Higher Education (Philippines)") in 2010, giving greater powers to the administration of the university independent to the commission.

On November 16, 2012, the university inaugurated a museum dedicated to its founders, inside the St. Vincent Building. The inauguration was part of the celebration of its 80th anniversary. In 2013, the university received an ISO 9001:2008 Management Systems certification from [TÜV Rheinland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%9CV_Rheinland "TÜV Rheinland").
On 2015, Fr. Marcelo V. Manimtim, C.M. became the sixth president of the university, succeeding Bañaga.

On December 13, 2023, Fr. Manimtim stepped down as the president of the University, and Fr. [Daniel Franklin E. Pilario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Franklin_Pilario "Daniel Franklin Pilario"), C.M., Ph.D., S.Th.D., became the 7th President and 3rd alumnus President of Adamson University.

### Manila main campus

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Adamson_University&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Manila main campus")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/9/9e/Adamson_University_top_view_%28San_Marcelino%2C_Ermita%2C_Manila%3B_12-23-2023%29.jpg/250px-Adamson_University_top_view_%28San_Marcelino%2C_Ermita%2C_Manila%3B_12-23-2023%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Adamson_University_top_view_(San_Marcelino,_Ermita,_Manila;_12-23-2023).jpg) Main campus[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6f/Adamson_University_FRC_Building.jpg/250px-Adamson_University_FRC_Building.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Adamson_University_FRC_Building.jpg) Francis Regis Clet Building[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/19/Saint_Vincent_Hall.jpg/250px-Saint_Vincent_Hall.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Saint_Vincent_Hall.jpg) Saint Vincent Hall

Adamson University is located in San Marcelino Street, [Ermita, Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ermita,_Manila "Ermita, Manila"), in an area known colloquially as the " [University Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Belt "University Belt")". The [Technological University of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technological_University_of_the_Philippines "Technological University of the Philippines"), [Santa Isabel College Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Isabel_College_Manila "Santa Isabel College Manila"), [Emilio Aguinaldo College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilio_Aguinaldo_College "Emilio Aguinaldo College"), and [Philippine Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Normal_University "Philippine Normal University") are its nearest neighbors. Adamson University has eight buildings occupying 10.7 hectares of land.[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6)

The school was located in three different campuses: [Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Cruz,_Manila "Santa Cruz, Manila") (1932–1933), [San Miguel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Miguel,_Manila "San Miguel, Manila") (1933–1939), and [Intramuros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intramuros "Intramuros") (1939-1941) before finally settling on its present location in 1946. The university expanded its campus in San Marcelino by acquiring the [Meralco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meralco "Meralco") building in 1968 and the [St. Theresa's College-Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Theresa's_College_of_Quezon_City "Saint Theresa's College of Quezon City") site just across the street in 1977.

### Saint Vincent Seminary campus

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Adamson_University&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Saint Vincent Seminary campus")\]

St. Vincent School of Theology is the Graduate School of Theology of Adamson University (AdU). It offers degrees such as Doctor of Philosophy in Theology (Ph.D. in theology), Master of Arts in Theology (MA Theology), Masters in Pastoral Ministry (MPM) and Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy (AB Philosophy). The diploma non-degree courses are Philosophy for Theological Studies (PTS), Introductory Theological Formation (ITF) and Christian Theology for the Laity (Hapág-Layko Program). The campus is located inside the Sanctuario de San Vicente de Paul in Tandang Sora, Quezon City.[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7)
