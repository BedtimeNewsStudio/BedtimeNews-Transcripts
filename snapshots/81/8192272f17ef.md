# 82% of US households have an Amazon Prime membership

Amazon.com Inc. has 105 million members in its Prime loyalty program, according to new estimates from [Consumer Intelligence Research Partners (CIRP).](https://www.cirpllc.com/)

This is 4 million more U.S. consumers than CIRP’s membership estimate in January. Prime members are roughly 57% of Amazon shoppers, according to CIRP.

Amazon does not disclose how many Prime members it has by country; however, [in 2018 it said it had more than 100 million globally.](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2018/04/18/amazon-finally-discloses-how-may-prime-members-it-has/) CIRP bases its estimates on survey from 500 U.S. consumers who made a purchase on Amazon April-June 2019.
