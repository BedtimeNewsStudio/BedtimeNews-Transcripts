# How much water does an Olympic sized swimming pool hold

How much water does an Olympic sized swimming pool hold?
2,500,000 litres or 2.5 megalitres of water.
Olympic size pools measure: 50 metres long, 25 metres wide, and a minimum of 2 metres deep. (25m x 50m x 2m = 2,500m3; 1L = 0.001m3 so 2,500 x 1000 = 2,500,000L) 660,430 gallons of water. 50 meters = 164.041995 feet (a) 25 meters = 82.0209975 feet (b) 2 meters = 6.5616798 feet (c) Volume = (a) * (b) * (c) = 88,286.66721 cubic feet 88,286.66721 cubic feet = 660,430.1339 U.S. gallons Source: http://wiki.answers.com/Q/How_much_water_does_an_Olympic_sized_swimming_pool_hold?
