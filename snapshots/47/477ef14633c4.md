# United States

EU trade relations with the United States. Facts, figures and latest developments.

Country or region
:   * United States

Trade topics
:   * Negotiations and agreements
    * Trade policy

The European Union and the United States have the world's largest bilateral trade and investment relationship, and enjoy the most integrated economic relationship in the world. Taking goods, services and investment into account, the EU and the US are each other’s largest trading partners by far.

Millions of jobs in the United States are related to EU-US trade and investment. The European Union is a reliable source of critical supplies to the United States, including medicinal ingredients and pharmaceutical products, advanced machinery and equipment, and aerospace parts and components. At the same time, the European Union is the largest buyer of the United States’ natural gas and oil, which is an important element for ensuring transatlantic energy security and to allow a shared strong response to Russia’s military aggression against Ukraine.

## Trade picture

* The transatlantic trade relationship is the most important commercial relationship in the world.
* EU-US trade in goods and services reaches an impressive €1.7 trillion. This means that every day, €4.6 billion worth of goods and services cross the Atlantic between the EU and the US. (Latest available data)
* EU-US goods and services trade is balanced: the difference between EU exports to the US and US exports to the EU stands at €20.8 billion; the equivalent of just 1.3% of the total trade between the EU and the US. (Latest available data)
* Total bilateral trade in goods between the EU and the US reached €910.6 billion in 2025. The EU exported €554.9 billion of goods to the US market, while importing €355.7 billion; this resulted in a goods trade surplus of €199.2 billion for the EU.
* Total bilateral trade in services between the EU and the US was worth €865.2 billion in 2024. The EU exported €343.4 billion of services to the US, while importing €521.8 billion from the US; this resulted in a services trade deficit of €178.4 billion for the EU.
* The EU and the US are also major investment partners. EU and US firms have €4.8 trillion worth of investment in each other’s markets (2024 data).
* US exports of goods and services to the EU support 2.6 million jobs in the US, and EU firms’ investments in the US employ 2.4 million people. To learn more, visit the EU's [Business Footprint in the United States Project](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/eu-footprint-us/home).

## The EU and the United States

### Deal on tariffs and trade

On 27 July 2025, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and US President Donald J. Trump agreed a deal on tariffs and trade, confirmed in the [Joint Statement on a US-EU framework on an agreement on reciprocal, fair and balanced trade](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/news/joint-statement-united-states-european-union-framework-agreement-reciprocal-fair-and-balanced-trade-2025-08-21_en) of 21 August 2025. The Joint Statement is the basis for the EU's engagement with the United States, including as regards the tariff treatment applicable to each other’s exports.

As part of the implementation of the EU-US Joint Statement, the EU adopted two regulations reducing tariffs on goods imported from the United States. As from 1 July 2026, the EU eliminated all duties on imports of US industrial goods and improved market access for certain non-sensitive agri-food products, including lobster, from the United States.

### The EU-US Trade and Technology Council

The European Union and the United States [launched the EU-US Trade and Technology Council](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/IP_21_2990) (TTC) at their summit in Brussels on 15 June 2021. The latest ministerial meeting was held in Leuven on 4 and 5 April 2024. The [TTC](https://circabc.europa.eu/ui/group/09242a36-a438-40fd-a7af-fe32e36cbd0e/library/edf43fce-6fdc-4a08-aba3-99bb7b35ca1d/details) served as a forum for the EU and the US to coordinate approaches to key global trade, economic and technology issues, and to deepen transatlantic trade and economic relations based on shared democratic values.

You can find more information on events related to the Trade and Technology Council on the Commission’s dedicated [Futurium](https://futurium.ec.europa.eu/en/EU-US-TTC) website.

### Negotiations

Despite the US being the EU’s largest trade and investment partner, there is no dedicated free trade agreement between the EU and the US. The Transatlantic Trade and Investment Partnership (TTIP) negotiations were launched in 2013, but ended in 2016 without conclusion. They were formally [closed](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/media/39180/st06052-en19.pdf) in 2019.
