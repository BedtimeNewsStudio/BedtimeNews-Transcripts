**Polad Israyil oghlu Hashimov** ( [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language "Azerbaijani language"): _Polad İsrayıl oğlu Həşimov_; 2 January 1975 – 14 July 2020) was an [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijanis "Azerbaijanis") military officer, [National Hero of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Hero_of_Azerbaijan "National Hero of Azerbaijan") and a [major general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_general "Major general") who served as the Deputy Commander and the Chief of Staff of the [3rdArmy Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_Army_Corps_(Azerbaijan) "3rd Army Corps (Azerbaijan)") of the [Azerbaijani Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_Armed_Forces "Azerbaijani Armed Forces").

Hashimov was a popular military figure who took part in the [First Nagorno-Karabakh War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Nagorno-Karabakh_War "First Nagorno-Karabakh War") and the [Four-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Nagorno-Karabakh_conflict "2016 Nagorno-Karabakh conflict"). He was awarded numerous honours during his military career, including jubilee and other medals. He commanded the Azerbaijani forces during the [border clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_2020_Armenian%E2%80%93Azerbaijani_clashes "July 2020 Armenian–Azerbaijani clashes") with [Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenia "Armenia") in July 2020 and was killed on the morning of 14 July while fighting on the front-line in the [Tovuz District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tovuz_District "Tovuz District") of Azerbaijan. His death sparked [violent protests throughout the country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Azerbaijani_protests "2020 Azerbaijani protests") calling for war with Armenia, a prelude to the [2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Nagorno-Karabakh_war "2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war"), which began in September of the same year. He was awarded the title of National Hero of Azerbaijan posthumously in December 2020.

Hashimov is the first and only general of the Azerbaijani Armed Forces to be killed during battle. Before him, [Ismat Gayibov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismat_Gayibov "Ismat Gayibov"), the first [Prosecutor General of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Prosecutors_General_of_Azerbaijan "List of Prosecutors General of Azerbaijan"), and [Mahammad Asadov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahammad_Asadov "Mahammad Asadov"), a major general in the [Azerbaijani Ministry of Internal Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Internal_Affairs_(Azerbaijan) "Ministry of Internal Affairs (Azerbaijan)"), were killed in November 1991 when the [Mil Mi-8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-8 "Mil Mi-8") helicopter they were in was [shot down by Armenian forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Azerbaijani_Mil_Mi-8_shootdown "1991 Azerbaijani Mil Mi-8 shootdown").

## Early life and education

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life and education")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/81/The_road_to_Seki_%282425469619%29.jpg/250px-The_road_to_Seki_%282425469619%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_road_to_Seki_(2425469619).jpg) Hashimov was born in a [working class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_class "Working class") family in [Vandam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C9%99ndam "Vəndam"), [Soviet Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_Soviet_Socialist_Republic "Azerbaijan Soviet Socialist Republic"), though he moved to the industrial city of [Sumgayit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumgait "Sumgait") with his family at a very young age.

Polad Israyil oglu Hashimov was born on 2January 1975, in [Vandam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C9%99ndam "Vəndam"), [Gutgashen District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qabala "Qabala") (modern-day [Gabala District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qabala_District "Qabala District")) of the [Azerbaijani SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_Soviet_Socialist_Republic "Azerbaijan Soviet Socialist Republic"), then part of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union "Soviet Union").[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) His father, Israyil, was a [metallurgist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgy "Metallurgy") and worked at an aluminum plant in [Sumgayit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumgait "Sumgait"), first as an engineer, then as a shift supervisor and manager.[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3) He died in 2005 after a lengthy illness.[&#8202;](#ref-back-suleymanova-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-suleymanova) His mother, Samaya, worked at the same plant. Hashimov's grandfather,[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5) Mikayil, a [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II") veteran,[&#8202;](#ref-back-suleymanova-1)[\[4\]](#refnote-suleymanova) named him "Polad"— [steel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steel "Steel") in [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language "Azerbaijani language").[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6) In July 1976, when Hashimov was one and a half years old, his family moved to Sumgayit and settled in the city's 13th micro-district.[&#8202;](#ref-back-dashsalahli-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-dashsalahli)

Hashimov was admitted to secondary school No. 28 in Sumgayit in 1982, and then to Secondary School No. 33 after its construction in 1986. In 1988, another educational institution, Secondary School No. 34, named after [Mikayil Mushfig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikayil_Mushfig "Mikayil Mushfig"), was built in the area and Hashimov continued his education there graduating in 1992. Hashimov excelled in mathematics during his secondary education.[&#8202;](#ref-back-dashsalahli-1)[\[7\]](#refnote-dashsalahli)

Despite intending to be a [lawyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawyer "Lawyer"), Hashimov decided instead to pursue a military career in 1992 during the height of the [First Nagorno-Karabakh War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Nagorno-Karabakh_War "First Nagorno-Karabakh War"). With his father's consent, he submitted his documents and was accepted to the [Baku Higher Combined Arms Command School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku_Higher_Combined_Arms_Command_School "Baku Higher Combined Arms Command School") in August of that year.[&#8202;](#ref-back-suleymanova-2)[\[4\]](#refnote-suleymanova) He graduated in 1995 with a degree in [motorised infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorized_infantry "Motorized infantry"). In 1994, he was sent by the [Azerbaijani Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_(Azerbaijan) "Ministry of Defence (Azerbaijan)") to a three-month training course at the Tuzla Infantry School in [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul "Istanbul"), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey "Turkey"). In July 2014, he entered the [Azerbaijan Higher Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_Higher_Military_Academy "Azerbaijan Higher Military Academy") named after [Heydar Aliyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heydar_Aliyev "Heydar Aliyev"), and in 2016, received a master's degree in military leadership.[&#8202;](#ref-back-nationallibrary-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-nationallibrary)

## Military career

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Military career")\]

### First Nagorno-Karabakh War

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: First Nagorno-Karabakh War")\]

In November 1992, during the First Nagorno-Karabakh War, Hashimov, who was training with the 702nd Motorised Rifle Brigade, fought in the successful defence of the villages of [Marzili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C9%99rzili "Mərzili") and [Novruzlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novruzlu,_Agdam "Novruzlu, Agdam") in the [Aghdam District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agdam_District "Agdam District") of [Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan "Azerbaijan"). He participated in [Operation Murovdagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Murovdagh "Operation Murovdagh") in February 1994.[&#8202;](#ref-back-nationallibrary-1)[\[8\]](#refnote-nationallibrary)[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9) He was involved in clashes with Armenian forces in the Guzgu, Koroghlu, and Omar military posts in the [Murov range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murovdagh "Murovdagh").[&#8202;](#ref-back-nationallibrary-2)[\[8\]](#refnote-nationallibrary)

After the signing of the [Bishkek Protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishkek_Protocol "Bishkek Protocol") ending the First Nagorno-Karabakh War, Hashimov served in military units stationed on the front-line of the ongoing [Nagorno-Karabakh conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagorno-Karabakh_conflict "Nagorno-Karabakh conflict") between [Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenia "Armenia") and the self-declared [Republic of Artsakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Artsakh "Republic of Artsakh"). For many years he was the commander of the military units stationed in [Barda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barda,_Azerbaijan "Barda, Azerbaijan"), [Dashkasan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashkasan "Dashkasan"), [Haji Zeynalabdin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hac%C4%B1_Zeynalabdin "Hacı Zeynalabdin"), Murov and [Shamkir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9E%C9%99mkir "Şəmkir").[&#8202;](#ref-back-suleymanova-3)[\[4\]](#refnote-suleymanova)[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbckimdir-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-bbckimdir) From 1995 to 2017, Hashimov held various positions, from motorised rifle squadron commander to military unit commander.[&#8202;](#ref-back-nationallibrary-3)[\[8\]](#refnote-nationallibrary) He was promoted to [major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_(rank) "Major (rank)") in 2003, and to [lieutenant colonel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_colonel "Lieutenant colonel") in 2009.[&#8202;](#ref-back-major-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-major)

### Four-Day War

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Four-Day War")\]

On 1April 2016, heavy fighting broke out between the Azerbaijani and Armenian forces along the [Nagorno-Karabakh Line of Contact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagorno-Karabakh_Line_of_Contact "Nagorno-Karabakh Line of Contact"), known as the [Four-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Nagorno-Karabakh_conflict "2016 Nagorno-Karabakh conflict").[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12) During the clashes, Hashimov served as Deputy Commander and Chief of Staff of the [1st Army Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Army_Corps_(Azerbaijan) "1st Army Corps (Azerbaijan)"). He commanded the Azerbaijani forces stationed in [Tartar District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartar_District "Tartar District"), and during night operations, his forces advanced towards [Talish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tal%C4%B1%C5%9F,_Tartar "Talış, Tartar"), capturing several strategic locations.[&#8202;](#ref-back-belabela-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-belabela) He was wounded during the clashes. One of the military posts Hashimov seized near Talış was later renamed "Polad" in his honour.[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbckimdir-1)[\[10\]](#refnote-bbckimdir) According to Turkish military expert Abdullah Ağar, the Azerbaijani military command ordered Hashimov to withdraw from the territories he had seized during the hostilities. After a ceasefire was reached on the front-line, Hashimov resigned immediately, but the [president of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Azerbaijan "President of Azerbaijan"), [Ilham Aliyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilham_Aliyev "Ilham Aliyev"), intervened and did not allow him to resign.[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14)

In 2017, Hashimov was appointed Deputy Commander and Chief of Staff of the [3rd Army Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_Army_Corps_(Azerbaijan) "3rd Army Corps (Azerbaijan)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-nationallibrary-4)[\[8\]](#refnote-nationallibrary) On 24June 2019, President Aliyev promoted Hashimov to [major general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_general "Major general"), the highest military rank in the Azerbaijani Army.[&#8202;](#ref-back-majorgeneral-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-majorgeneral) Hashimov became the first Gabalani general since [Ismayil bek Kutkashensky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismayil_bek_Kutkashensky "Ismayil bek Kutkashensky"), a 19th-century Azerbaijani military officer in the [Imperial Russian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Russian_Army "Imperial Russian Army").[&#8202;](#ref-back-farewell-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-farewell)

### Tovuz clashes

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Tovuz clashes")\]

On 12July 2020,[&#8202;](#ref-back-azatutyun-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-azatutyun) [skirmishes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_2020_Armenian%E2%80%93Azerbaijani_clashes "July 2020 Armenian–Azerbaijani clashes") erupted on the [border between Armenia and Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenia%E2%80%93Azerbaijan_border "Armenia–Azerbaijan border"),[&#8202;](#ref-back-diplomatie_150720-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-diplomatie_150720) mainly in the [Tavush Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tavush_Province "Tavush Province") of Armenia and the [Tovuz District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tovuz_District "Tovuz District") of Azerbaijan.[&#8202;](#ref-back-19-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-19)

Hashimov commanded the Azerbaijani forces during the clashes. In the first hours of the hostilities, three Azerbaijani servicemen were killed and four more were wounded.[&#8202;](#ref-back-20-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-20)[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-21) These clashes continued the next day using artillery and [drone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicle "Unmanned aerial vehicle") attacks, without infantry.[&#8202;](#ref-back-turan-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-turan) Azerbaijani authorities claimed their forces had "neutralised" more than 20 Armenian servicemen, and destroyed several Armenian firing positions, battalion headquarters, and other military targets.[&#8202;](#ref-back-23-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-23) Overall, at least 17 military personnel and one civilian were confirmed killed during the skirmishes.[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbctovuz-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-bbctovuz)

## Death

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Death")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cf/Polad_H%C9%99%C5%9Fimovun_m%C9%99zar%C4%B1.jpg/250px-Polad_H%C9%99%C5%9Fimovun_m%C9%99zar%C4%B1.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Polad_H%C9%99%C5%9Fimovun_m%C9%99zar%C4%B1.jpg) Hashimov's grave in the [Second Alley of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alley_of_Honor "Alley of Honor") in [Baku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku "Baku").

Further skirmishes occurred at night and continued into the morning of 14July. In the morning, seven Azerbaijani servicemen, six of them officers, were killed.[&#8202;](#ref-back-25-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-25) On the same day, the deputy defence minister of Azerbaijan, [Karim Valiyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Valiyev "Karim Valiyev"), announced that Hashimov and [Colonel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonel "Colonel") [Ilgar Mirzayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilgar_Mirzayev "Ilgar Mirzayev") were among the fallen Azerbaijani officers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-26-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-26)

Hashimov is the first and only general of the Azerbaijani Armed Forces to be killed during military operations. Before him, [Ismat Gayibov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismat_Gayibov "Ismat Gayibov"), the first [Prosecutor General of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Prosecutors_General_of_Azerbaijan "List of Prosecutors General of Azerbaijan"), and [Mahammad Asadov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahammad_Asadov "Mahammad Asadov"), major general in the [Azerbaijani Ministry of Internal Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Internal_Affairs_(Azerbaijan) "Ministry of Internal Affairs (Azerbaijan)"), were killed in November 1991 when the [Mil Mi-8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-8 "Mil Mi-8") helicopter they were in was [shot down by Armenian forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Azerbaijani_Mil_Mi-8_shootdown "1991 Azerbaijani Mil Mi-8 shootdown").[&#8202;](#ref-back-27-0)[\[27\]](#refnote-27)

### Funeral

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Funeral")\]

At noon on 14July, Hashimov's body was taken to Vandam and then to Sumgayit, where he lived.[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-28) He was buried alongside Colonel Mirzayev, on 15July in the [Second Alley of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alley_of_Honor "Alley of Honor") in [Baku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku "Baku").[&#8202;](#ref-back-29-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-29) Azerbaijan's minister of defence, [Zakir Hasanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zakir_Hasanov "Zakir Hasanov"), [chief of general staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_of_Azerbaijani_Armed_Forces "General Staff of Azerbaijani Armed Forces"), [Najmaddin Sadigov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najmaddin_Sadigov "Najmaddin Sadigov"), and, the [mayor of Baku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Baku#City_mayors "History of Baku"), [Eldar Azizov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldar_Azizov "Eldar Azizov"), attended the funeral.[&#8202;](#ref-back-30-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-30)[&#8202;](#ref-back-31-0)[\[31\]](#refnote-31) On the same day, President Ilham Aliyev had a telephone conversation with Hashimov's mother, expressing his deep condolences to her.[&#8202;](#ref-back-32-0)[\[32\]](#refnote-32)

### Domestic reactions

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Domestic reactions")\]

Hashimov's death caused outrage in Azerbaijani society.[&#8202;](#ref-back-33-0)[\[33\]](#refnote-33) Many speculated that his exact location was given to the Armenian side by a [sleeper agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mole_(espionage) "Mole (espionage)"),[&#8202;](#ref-back-34-0)[\[34\]](#refnote-34) the primary suspect being [Najmeddin Sadikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najmeddin_Sadikov "Najmeddin Sadikov"), Azerbaijan's chief of staff and the deputy minister of defence.[&#8202;](#ref-back-sadikov-0)[\[35\]](#refnote-sadikov)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Baxar.az-0)[\[36\]](#refnote-Baxar.az) This sparked [violent protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_2020_Azerbaijani_protests "July 2020 Azerbaijani protests") throughout the country.

When Hashimov's body was taken to his house in Sumgayit,[&#8202;](#ref-back-37-0)[\[37\]](#refnote-37) a crowd of hundreds of people from different parts of the city gathered there chanting pro-army slogans.[&#8202;](#ref-back-38-0)[\[38\]](#refnote-38) The demonstrations spread to others parts of Baku,[&#8202;](#ref-back-39-0)[\[39\]](#refnote-39) with about 50,000 demonstrators taking part.[&#8202;](#ref-back-pavlovska-0)[\[40\]](#refnote-pavlovska) They first marched to [Freedom Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azadliq_Square,_Baku "Azadliq Square, Baku"), then to the [Martyrs' Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martyrs'_Lane "Martyrs' Lane") but were not allowed to enter it,[&#8202;](#ref-back-kavkazuzel-0)[\[41\]](#refnote-kavkazuzel) or the [National Assembly of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_(Azerbaijan) "National Assembly (Azerbaijan)").[&#8202;](#ref-back-rfecriminalcase-0)[\[42\]](#refnote-rfecriminalcase) The Baku Police prevented vehicles from entering the area to ensure the crowd's safety.[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbcinterior-0)[\[43\]](#refnote-bbcinterior) The demonstrators, waving the [Azerbaijani flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Azerbaijan "Flag of Azerbaijan"), called for the end of the COVID-19 pandemic related [quarantine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarantine "Quarantine"), mobilisation and a war against Armenia to retake the disputed Nagorno-Karabakh region.[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbcnews-0)[\[44\]](#refnote-bbcnews)[&#8202;](#ref-back-ocmedia-0)[\[45\]](#refnote-ocmedia) There were smaller rallies in support of the military in other cities around Azerbaijan, usually connected to the burials of fallen soldiers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-eurasianet-0)[\[46\]](#refnote-eurasianet)

After the initial demonstrations, a smaller group reached the front of the National Assembly on the night of 14 and 15July. Apart from support for the army and calls for war,[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbcinterior-1)[\[43\]](#refnote-bbcinterior) the demonstrators demanded the resignation of the chief of general staff of the Azerbaijani Armed Forces and the deputy minister of defence, Najmeddin Sadikov.[&#8202;](#ref-back-rfecriminalcase-1)[\[42\]](#refnote-rfecriminalcase)[&#8202;](#ref-back-sadikov-1)[\[35\]](#refnote-sadikov)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Baxar.az-1)[\[36\]](#refnote-Baxar.az) At around 01:00, the street lights in front of the parliament building were turned off. However, the demonstrators protested this by turning on their phone lights and whistling. Although a representative from the Azerbaijani Ministry of Defence arrived to address the crowd, the demonstrators did not allow him to speak and demanded that President Ilham Aliyev come and speak.[&#8202;](#ref-back-rfecriminalcase-2)[\[42\]](#refnote-rfecriminalcase) At around 04:00, riots broke out, during which police cars were overturned.[&#8202;](#ref-back-jamnews-0)[\[47\]](#refnote-jamnews) Several demonstrators breached the National Assembly, protesting the government's inaction.[&#8202;](#ref-back-IntelliNews-0)[\[48\]](#refnote-IntelliNews) They smashed windows and chandeliers inside, but security forces later evicted them. A [BBC Azerbaijani Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_News "BBC News") correspondent at the scene reported that although there were guards inside, they did not interfere with the crowd.[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbcinterior-2)[\[43\]](#refnote-bbcinterior) According to a report by the Azerbaijani Parliament, the demonstrators damaged a number of items in the building worth 22,150 [AZN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_manat "Azerbaijani manat") ($13,000 US) in total.[&#8202;](#ref-back-49-0)[\[49\]](#refnote-49)[&#8202;](#ref-back-50-0)[\[50\]](#refnote-50) The Azerbaijani Ministry of Internal Affairs accused the demonstrators of deliberately disobeying the demands of police officers and attacking them with stones and other objects. As a result, seven police officers were injured, two State Traffic Police Department service cars were overturned and rendered useless, fourteen more service cars and the road infrastructure in the area were seriously damaged.[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbcinterior-3)[\[43\]](#refnote-bbcinterior) Following this, security forces used water cannons, tear gas and batons to disperse those gathered in front of the parliament building.[&#8202;](#ref-back-turanprotests-0)[\[51\]](#refnote-turanprotests) Other demonstrators tried to convince the police to join them.[&#8202;](#ref-back-eurasianet-1)[\[46\]](#refnote-eurasianet) The demonstrators moved towards Martyrs' Lane and continued protesting there. They began to disperse in the early morning near [Baku Boulevard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku_Boulevard "Baku Boulevard") and Freedom Square.[&#8202;](#ref-back-rfecriminalcase-3)[\[42\]](#refnote-rfecriminalcase) According to [_Turan_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turan_Information_Agency "Turan Information Agency"), an independent Azerbaijani news agency, several hundred protesters then marched, escorted by police, to the [Nariman Narimanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nariman_Narimanov "Nariman Narimanov") Monument and the building of the [Azerbaijani Ministry of Taxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Taxes_(Azerbaijan) "Ministry of Taxes (Azerbaijan)"), before dispersing.[&#8202;](#ref-back-turanprotests-1)[\[51\]](#refnote-turanprotests)
In late July, further protests erupted in Hashimov's native Vandam, when local officials removed the Azerbaijani flag hanging from the door of Hashimov's house. Ilham Baghirov, the representative of the Gabala District Executive Power in Vandam, stated that the flag was removed temporarily and was to be hung on a flag pole in front of the house.[&#8202;](#ref-back-52-0)[\[52\]](#refnote-52)

The preliminary investigation over the arrests was completed on 11September. 37 people were prosecuted, officially charged with intentionally causing minor bodily harm, intentionally destroying or damaging another's property, violating public order, and resisting or using force against a government official.[&#8202;](#ref-back-53-0)[\[53\]](#refnote-53) Despite this, according to other Azerbaijani sources, as many as 120 people were detained after the protests, including members of the [NIDA Civic Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nida_Civic_Movement "Nida Civic Movement") and journalists.[&#8202;](#ref-back-54-0)[\[54\]](#refnote-54)[&#8202;](#ref-back-voanida-0)[\[55\]](#refnote-voanida)

The protests were a key factor in the escalation of tensions that led to the [Second Nagorno-Karabakh War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Nagorno-Karabakh_war "2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war") later in September of the same year.[&#8202;](#ref-back-56-0)[\[56\]](#refnote-56)[&#8202;](#ref-back-57-0)[\[57\]](#refnote-57)[&#8202;](#ref-back-58-0)[\[58\]](#refnote-58)

Later, in January 2022, Hashimov's mother, Samaya Hashimova, alleged that [Karim Valiyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Valiyev "Karim Valiyev"), who was appointed [Chief of the General Staff of the Azerbaijani Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_of_Azerbaijani_Armed_Forces "General Staff of Azerbaijani Armed Forces") in July 2021, had her son killed in order to get the position instead of him. She also blamed Defence Minister Zakir Hasanov.[&#8202;](#ref-back-59-0)[\[59\]](#refnote-59)

## Personal life

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Personal life")\]

Hashimov had three siblings, Arzu,[&#8202;](#ref-back-60-0)[\[60\]](#refnote-60) Kamala,[&#8202;](#ref-back-61-0)[\[61\]](#refnote-61) and Ilham.[&#8202;](#ref-back-62-0)[\[62\]](#refnote-62) He married Ophelia Salmanova on 5September 2003.[&#8202;](#ref-back-suleymanova-4)[\[4\]](#refnote-suleymanova) The couple had three children, Davoud, Teimour, and Aybaniz.[&#8202;](#ref-back-63-0)[\[63\]](#refnote-63)

A popular military figure within the Azerbaijani society, Hashimov was well respected by both soldiers and officers. He was presented with an apartment for his services during the Four-Day War. Although Hashimov lived in a rented apartment, he donated it to the family of a fallen Azerbaijani serviceman.[&#8202;](#ref-back-bbckimdir-2)[\[10\]](#refnote-bbckimdir)[&#8202;](#ref-back-belabela-1)[\[13\]](#refnote-belabela)

## Legacy

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Polad_Hashimov&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Legacy")\]

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8d/Sumqay%C4%B1t%2C_28_n%C3%B6mr%C9%99li_m%C9%99kt%C9%99b%2C_Polad_H%C9%99%C5%9Fimov_Muzeyi_-_1.jpg/250px-Sumqay%C4%B1t%2C_28_n%C3%B6mr%C9%99li_m%C9%99kt%C9%99b%2C_Polad_H%C9%99%C5%9Fimov_Muzeyi_-_1.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sumqay%C4%B1t,_28_n%C3%B6mr%C9%99li_m%C9%99kt%C9%99b,_Polad_H%C9%99%C5%9Fimov_Muzeyi_-_1.jpg) Polad Hashimov Memorial Museum in [Sumgayit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumgayit "Sumgayit")

After Hashimov's death, "Polad" was the most popular name given to newborn boys, mainly in the Gabala District.[&#8202;](#ref-back-64-0)[\[64\]](#refnote-64)

In August 2020, two books dedicated to Hashimov were published. _Our Steel General_ ( [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language "Azerbaijani language"): _Polad Generalımız_) was published by Ganun Nashriyyati.[&#8202;](#ref-back-65-0)[\[65\]](#refnote-65) The second, written by Hadaf Nashrlari and XAN Nashriyyati, _General Polad Hashimov in Memories_ ( [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language "Azerbaijani language"): _General Polad Həşimov xatirələrdə_), contains the recollections of Hashimov's mother, wife, daughter, as well as his colleagues.[&#8202;](#ref-back-66-0)[\[66\]](#refnote-66)

After his death, the Azerbaijani singer [Chingiz Mustafayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chingiz_Mustafayev_(singer) "Chingiz Mustafayev (singer)"), who represented Azerbaijan in the [2019 Eurovision Song Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_2019 "Eurovision Song Contest 2019"), released a song titled _I'm Like My Homeland_ ( [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language "Azerbaijani language"): _Vətənim Kimiyəm_), dedicated to Hashimov and the other fallen soldiers of the border clashes.[&#8202;](#ref-back-67-0)[\[67\]](#refnote-67) On 22August 2020, Azerbaijani military singer [Shamistan Alizamanli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamistan_Alizamanli "Shamistan Alizamanli") released a song titled _My Pasha, Hey!_ ( [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language "Azerbaijani language"): _Paşam, hey!_) dedicated to Hashimov.[&#8202;](#ref-back-68-0)[\[68\]](#refnote-68) On 24August, a documentary titled _7 Moments of Polad_ ( [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language "Azerbaijani language"): _Poladın 7 anı_) dedicated to the life of Hashimov and directed by Ruslan Hussein was presented on [Ictimai TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0ctimai_Television "İctimai Television").[&#8202;](#ref-back-69-0)[\[69\]](#refnote-69)

In August 2020, a street was named after Hashimov in his native Vandam, in Gabala District,[&#8202;](#ref-back-70-0)[\[70\]](#refnote-70) and in January 2021, in [Guba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quba "Quba"),[&#8202;](#ref-back-71-0)[\[71\]](#refnote-71) and in [Keban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keban "Keban"), [Elazığ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elaz%C4%B1%C4%9F "Elazığ"), Turkey.[&#8202;](#ref-back-72-0)[\[72\]](#refnote-72) A park bearing his name was opened in the [Osmangazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmangazi "Osmangazi") District of [Bursa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bursa "Bursa"), Turkey, on 28October.[&#8202;](#ref-back-73-0)[\[73\]](#refnote-73) On May 7, 2021, the street in Baku, originally named after [Pavel Nakhimov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Nakhimov "Pavel Nakhimov"), was named after Polad Hashimov.[&#8202;](#ref-back-74-0)[\[74\]](#refnote-74)

Hashimov was declared a [National Hero of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Hero_of_Azerbaijan "National Hero of Azerbaijan") in December 2020, along with Ilgar Mirzayev and [Ibad Huseynov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibad_Huseynov "Ibad Huseynov"), by the decree of President Aliyev.[&#8202;](#ref-back-nationalhero-0)[\[75\]](#refnote-nationalhero)[&#8202;](#ref-back-76-0)[\[76\]](#refnote-76)
