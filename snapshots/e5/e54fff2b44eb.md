**Vladimir Petrovich Frolov** ( [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language "Russian language"): Владимир Петрович Фролов; 6 February 1967 – 10 March 2022) was a Russian [major general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_general "Major general") who died during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine "Russian invasion of Ukraine").

## Biography

Vladimir Frolov was born into the family of a [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II") veteran.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-gov.spb.ru-2) He joined the [Soviet Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Armed_Forces "Soviet Armed Forces") in 1984, enrolling as a cadet of the [Sverdlovsk Higher Military-Political Tank-Artillery School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sverdlovsk_Higher_Military-Political_Tank-Artillery_School?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sverdlovsk Higher Military-Political Tank-Artillery School")\[ [ru](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A1%D0%B2%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B4%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B5%20%D0%B2%D1%8B%D1%81%D1%88%D0%B5%D0%B5%20%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%BD%D0%BE-%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B8%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B5%20%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BA%D0%BE-%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%BB%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%B9%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B5%20%D1%83%D1%87%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%89%D0%B5 "ru:Свердловское высшее военно-политическое танко-артиллерийское училище")\]. He later graduated from the [Combined Arms Academy of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_Arms_Academy_of_the_Armed_Forces_of_the_Russian_Federation "Combined Arms Academy of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation") and the [Military Academy of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Academy_of_the_General_Staff_of_the_Armed_Forces_of_Russia "Military Academy of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of Russia").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-GS-3)

Frolov took part in the [Russian intervention in the Syrian civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_intervention_in_the_Syrian_civil_war "Russian intervention in the Syrian civil war"), and was promoted to the rank of major general by [Presidential Decree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decree_of_the_President_of_Russia "Decree of the President of Russia") No. 595 of [Vladimir Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin "Vladimir Putin") on 12 December 2019.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-GS-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-5)

The [8th Combined Arms Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Guards_Combined_Arms_Army_(Russia) "8th Guards Combined Arms Army (Russia)"), whose deputy commander was Frolov, was actively engaged in the [siege of Mariupol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Mariupol "Siege of Mariupol").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-6)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-7) According to the version of Speaker of the [People's Council of the Donetsk People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Council_of_the_Donetsk_People's_Republic "People's Council of the Donetsk People's Republic") [Artem Zhoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artem_Zhoga "Artem Zhoga"), Frolov was killed by a sniper on 10 March 2022 during fighting at the [Illich Steel and Iron Works](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illich_Steel_and_Iron_Works "Illich Steel and Iron Works"). He was buried in the ruins by Ukrainian troops. After the area was captured by Russian forces, Ukrainian prisoners of war notified Russian troops of Frolov's burial location.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-GS-3)

His death was announced by Russian officials on 16 April 2022.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-8) The same day, his burial was held with military honours at [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg "Saint Petersburg")'s [Serafimovskoe Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serafimovskoe_Cemetery "Serafimovskoe Cemetery") after a funeral service in [Novocherkassk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novocherkassk "Novocherkassk")'s [Ascension Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novocherkassk_Cathedral "Novocherkassk Cathedral").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-9) The ceremony was attended by the Governor of Saint Petersburg, [Alexander Beglov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Beglov "Alexander Beglov").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-gov.spb.ru-2)

## Awards

Frolov was posthumously awarded the title of [Hero of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_the_Russian_Federation "Hero of the Russian Federation") by [decree of the President of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decree_of_the_President_of_Russia "Decree of the President of Russia") dated 25 December 2023.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-GS-3) The gold star of the award was presented to Frolov's family by Alexander Beglov on 22 February 2024. Over his career Frolov had also been awarded the [Order of Military Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Military_Merit_(Russia) "Order of Military Merit (Russia)"), the [Order "For Merit to the Fatherland"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_%22For_Merit_to_the_Fatherland%22 "Order \"For Merit to the Fatherland\"") second class with swords, and the Soviet [Medal "For Battle Merit"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_%22For_Battle_Merit%22 "Medal \"For Battle Merit\""). He was also posthumously awarded the title of [Hero of the Donetsk People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_the_Donetsk_People's_Republic "Hero of the Donetsk People's Republic").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-GS-3)

## Memory

In September 2024, a bust of Frolov was installed in Mariupol; a municipal secondary school No. 4 was also named in his honor.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Frolov_(general)#cite_note-10)
