## Technology Redefines Global Investing

Founded in 2014, Tiger Brokers is a leading global online broker.

We leverage next-generation technology to enable clients to trade a wide range of securities across multiple global markets and currencies. Through our flagship mobile trading app, Tiger Trade, clients can trade equities in The U.S., Hong Kong, Singapore and Australia as well as futures, options, and funds.

Tiger Brokers now serves more than 10 million users and 2.7 million account holders1 worldwide via Tiger Trade. We hold 791 licenses and qualifications across Singapore, Hong Kong, the U.S., Australia and New Zealand.

In March 2019, Tiger Brokers was listed on NASDAQ with the ticker TIGR. Today our services have expanded beyond brokerage to include employee stock ownership plan (ESOP) management, investment banking, wealth management and investor education.

Tiger Brokers has earned international recognition for its innovative trading platform and commitment to enhancing the investing experience. We were named CNBC's World's Top Fintech Companies in both 2025 and 2026, and ranked #1 in the WealthTech category on Fortune's inaugural "Asia Fintech Innovators" list.

Tiger Brokers continues to grow rapidly, focusing on expanding our innovative investment platform and creating new links between global financial markets.
