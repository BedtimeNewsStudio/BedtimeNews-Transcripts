Taiwan's coal imports in 2024 fell to a record low of 57.44 million tonnes, down 2.13% from 2023, according to preliminary customs data.

The total import value was $9.38 billion, down 21.03% year on year (YoY). The average import price was calculated at $163.25/t, dropping 19.3% YoY, data showed.

![](https://cos.sxcoal.com/news/2025_image_1877548552606683136.png)

The December volume reached 4.45 million tonnes, 2.35% higher than the year-ago level of 4.35 million tonnes and rising 6.73% from 4.17 million tonnes a month ago, data showed.

The total import value was $600 million in December, down 22.13% YoY but up 4.47% MoM. The average import price was calculated at $134.85/t, dropping 23.92% YoY and 2.12% MoM, data showed.

Australia remained the largest source of coal imports to Taiwan in December, with shipments totaling 2.07 million tonnes (-8.08% YoY, +9.68% MoM), followed by Indonesia at 1.71 million tonnes (+43.66% YoY, +45.75% MoM) and Russia at 222,600 tonnes (-66.96% YoY, -58.78% MoM).

By type, the bulk of its coal imports was bituminous coal in December, hitting 3.46 million tonnes, rising 3.09% YoY and 1.19% MoM; imports of other coal reached 1 million tonnes, increasing 0.96% YoY and 31.73% MoM.

In December, Taiwan imported 10,600 tonnes of coke, surging 184.5% YoY and up 3% MoM, with the import value at $3.02 million, soaring 160% YoY and 20.57% MoM.

Taiwan also imported 1.79 million tonnes of LNG in December, down 4% YoY and 0.97% MoM, with the value falling 8.9% YoY but rising 1.77% MoM to $1.08 billion. Cumulative LNG imports over January-December amounted to 21.51 million tonnes, up 5.13% YoY, with the import value down 7.62% to $11.92 billion.
