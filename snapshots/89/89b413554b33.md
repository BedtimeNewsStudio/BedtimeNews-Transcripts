### Weather Alert – U.S. Embassy San Salvador, El Salvador (June 8, 2026)

**Location:** El Salvador

**Event:** The Civil Protection Agency in El Salvador has updated its alert at the national level to orange alert due to the influence of Tropical Storm Cristina, located to the south of the Central American coastline.  There is high probability of urban flooding, landslides, overflowing of rivers, and falling trees and rocks across the entire country of El Salvador through June 11.

**Actions to Take:**

- Track local updates from El Salvador’s Civil Protection and Ministry of Environment: [https://www.proteccioncivil.gob.sv](https://www.proteccioncivil.gob.sv/ "Original URL: https://www.proteccioncivil.gob.sv/. Click or tap if you trust this link.")  and [https://www.ambiente.gob.sv](https://www.ambiente.gob.sv/ "Original URL: https://www.ambiente.gob.sv/. Click or tap if you trust this link.") X page: [@PROCIVILSV](https://x.com/PROCIVILSV) and [@MedioAmbienteSV](https://x.com/MedioAmbienteSV).
- Monitor the local news for updates.
- Monitor the National Weather Service website https://www.weather.gov/ and National Hurricane Center website: [https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/)
- Seek secure shelter during a flood.
- Do not attempt to drive across water standing on a road no matter how shallow it seems.
- Prepare an emergency supply kit ( [ready.gov](http://ready.gov/ "Original URL: https://www.ready.gov/. Click or tap if you trust this link.")), which should include food and supplies needed in case of power outages or road closures.
