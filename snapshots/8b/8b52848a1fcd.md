The **Moei River** ( [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language "Thai language"): แม่น้ำเมย, [RTGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_General_System_of_Transcription "Royal Thai General System of Transcription"):_Maenam Moei_, IPA:[\[mɛ̂ːnáːmmɤːj\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Thai "Help:IPA/Thai")), also known as the **Thaungyin Rive** r ( [Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language "Burmese language"): သောင်ရင်းမြစ်; [S'gaw Karen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S'gaw_Karen_language "S'gaw Karen language"): သူမွဲကျိ) Eastern Pwo Karen Phlone:ယှုံမွဲခၠေါဟ် a tributary of the [Salween](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salween "Salween") River. Unlike most rivers in Thailand, the Moei River flows north in a northwest direction. It originates in [Phop Phra District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphoe_Phop_Phra "Amphoe Phop Phra"), [Tak Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tak_Province "Tak Province"), flowing then from south to north across [Mae Sot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphoe_Mae_Sot "Amphoe Mae Sot"), [Mae Ramat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphoe_Mae_Ramat "Amphoe Mae Ramat"), and [Tha Song Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphoe_Tha_Song_Yang "Amphoe Tha Song Yang") Districts, finally entering the Salween River within the limits of [Sop Moei District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sop_Moei_District "Sop Moei District") of [Mae Hong Son Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mae_Hong_Son_Province "Mae Hong Son Province"). The river is 327 kilometres (203mi) long.

The [Yuam River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuam_River "Yuam River") joins its left bank only 7 kilometres (4mi) before its confluence with the [Salween](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salween "Salween"). Many fish species inhabit its waters, including the [giant river catfish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_river_catfish "Giant river catfish").[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1)

## International border

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Moei_River&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: International border")\]

The Moei River forms a portion of the [border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar%E2%80%93Thailand_border "Myanmar–Thailand border") between [Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand "Thailand") and [Myanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar "Myanmar").

The river is the scene of clashes between the [Tatmadaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatmadaw "Tatmadaw") and Karen militias.[&#8202;](#ref-back-2-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-2) Often [Karen people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_people "Karen people") cross the river either in order to enter Thailand as refugees or to go back to Burma.[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3)

Flow of refugees increased as fighting in Burma intensified in 2024.[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4)[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5)[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6)
