# Orlando Tourism Statistics

Published Sep 8, 2026

Orlando is best known for its famous theme parks and warm, subtropical climate, which attracts millions of visitors each year.

## How many tourists visit Orlando each year?

Annual visitor numbers for Orlando typically **exceed 74 million**, making it one of the most visited cities in the United States.

- **75.8 million** visitors in **2019**, the highest recorded in recent years.
- **35.3 million** in **2020** due to the pandemic.
- **59.3 million** in **2021**.
- **74 million** visitorsin **2022** and **2023** respectively.
- **75.3 million** visitors in **2024**.

![Chart of Orlando annual visitor numbers by year](https://camperchamp.com/_astro/orlando-annual-visitors-a7c6070f_1B4gdP.webp)

## Who visited Orlando in 2024?

- **75.3 million** peoplevisited Orlando in **2024**. **68.8 million** were **domestic visitors** ( **91.4%** of total visitors) and **6.5 million** were **international visitors** ( **8.6%** of total visitors).

![Chart of Orlando 2024 visitors split between domestic and international](https://camperchamp.com/_astro/orlando-visitor-origin-4a3ac455_Z28rpgL.webp)

Orlando visitors in 2024 by origin: domestic and international.

## Where do Orlando visitors come from?

International tourists made up around **8.6%** of total arrivals in 2024. Most visitors are from **Canada** with **1.29 million visitors**.

- This was followed by the **United Kingdom with 907,900 visitors** and **Brazil with 697,200 visitors**.
- **Mexico** and **Colombia** made up the top 5 with **438,000 and 343,000 visitors respectively**.

![Chart of top source countries for international visitors to Orlando](https://camperchamp.com/_astro/orlando-top-source-country-of-tourists-46c03c76_Z26H9SN.webp)

Top source countries for international visitors to Orlando.

## How much did tourists spend in Orlando?

Visitors in Orlando spent approximately **$58.5 billion** during their visit in **2023.**

- Visitors spent **$48 billion** in Orlando in 2019.
- This dropped sharply to **$23.2 billion** in 2020, due to the Covid-19 pandemic.
- Spending rebounded in 2021 to **$40.3 billion**, followed by **$55.5 billion** in 2022.
- Tourists spending increased by **5.4%**, reaching **$58.5 billion** in **2023**.

![Chart of annual visitor spending in Orlando by year](https://camperchamp.com/_astro/orlando-annual-visitor-spending-ae9ec562_U7Hpm.webp)

Annual visitor spending in Orlando by year.

## Who spent the most on Orlando in 2023?

- Domestic visitor spending made up **91%** of total spending ( **$53.1 billion**) in **2023**.
- International visitor spending accounted for the remaining **9%** ( **$5.4 billion**).

![Chart of Orlando 2023 visitor spending split between domestic and international](https://camperchamp.com/_astro/orlando-visitor-spending-by-origin-37a0ed58_1P4iM0.webp)

## Orlando Tourist Attractions

The theme parks in Orlando are the main attractions, with **tens of millions** of visitors every year.

- Over **50 million annual** tourists visit **[Walt Disney World Resort](https://camperchamp.com/usa/walt-disney-world-tourism-statistics/):**
  - **Magic Kingdom**: Over **17 million** visitorsin **2022**.
  - **EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom**: **10-13 million** visitors each **annually**.
- **Universal Orlando Resort**:
  - **Universal Studios & Islands of Adventure**: **Around 10 million** visitors per attraction **per year**.
  - **Volcano Bay**: **Around 2 million** visitors **per year**.
- **SeaWorld Orlando**: **4.6 million** visitorsin **2019**.

## How long do tourists spend in Orlando?

- To see all of Orlando’s attractions (especially the big ones like Disney World and Universal Studios) visitors need at least **5 days** to experience the city.
- Overseas visitors stayed **8.6 nights** and domestic visitors stayed **3.1 nights** on average in 2023.

## How many tourists visited Orlando in 2024?

- Orlando received **75.33 million visitors**, reaching almost at the pre-pandemic levels and marking a **1.8% increase** over the previous year.
- Of this total, **68.8 million** were domestic travelers, while the remaining **6.5 million** were international visitors.

## How many tourists visited Orlando in 2023?

- **74 million** tourists visited Orlando in **2023**. **67.9 million** were **domestic** visitors (a **2% decrease** from the previous year) and **6.1 million** were **international** visitors (a **25% increase** from the previous year). The total visitor numbers are unchanged compared to 2022.

### How much did tourists spend in Orlando in 2023?

- Tourists spent **$58.5 billion** in Orlando in **2023**, representing a **5.4%** increase from **2022**( **$55.5 billion**) and a **22%** increase from **2019** ( **$48 billion**).

## How many tourists visited Orlando in 2022?

- **74 million** tourists visited Orlandoin **2022**, a **24.8% increase** from **2021**( **59.3 million visitors**).
- Of these, **69.1 million** were **domestic visitors** (93% of the total), while **4.9 million international visitors** accounted for the remaining 7%.

### How much did tourists spend on Orlando in 2022?

- Tourists in Orlando spent **$55.5 billion** in **2022**, a **37.7%** increase from **2021** ( **$40.30 billion**) and about **15.6%** higher than **2019**.

## How many tourists visited Orlando in 2021?

- **59.3 million** tourists visited Orlandoin **2021**, a **68% increase** from the previous year.
- Of the 59.3 million tourists who visited Orlando in 2021, **57.2 million were domestic** and **2.1 million were international visitors**.

### How much did tourists spend in Orlando in 2021?

- Tourists spent **$40.3 billion** in Orlando in **2021**, a **74% increase** from 2020 with an average spending of **$679 per visitor**.
- Overnight visitors contributed **$36.8 billion** and day visitors **$3.5 billion**. The top spending category was lodging.

## Orlando Tourism Statistics in 2020

- **35.3 million** visitorsin **2020**, a **53% drop from 2019**.
- **33.6 million domestic visitors** and only **1.7 million international visitors** in **2020**.
- Tourist spending reached **$23.2 billion** in **2020**, with **$20.9 billion** contributed by overnight visitors and **$2.3 billion** by day visitors.
- **$657** average spending per visitor.

## Orlando Inbound Tourism Statistics in 2019

- **75.8 million** tourists visited Orlandoin 2019. **69.3 million** were **domestic visitors** and **6.5 million** were **international visitors**.
- Tourist spending reached **$48 billion** in **2019**. **$45.4 billion** were spent by overnight visitors and **$2.6 billion** by day visitors.
- **$633** average spending per visitor.

## Orlando Inbound Tourism Statistics in 2023 and 2019

- **75.8 million** to **74 million** tourists between **2019** and **2023**, a **2.4%** decrease.
- Total tourist spending increased from **$48 billion** to **$58.5 billion**, a 22% increase despite a drop in tourist numbers.
- The average visitor spending increased by **24.9%**, from **$633** to **$790.5**.
