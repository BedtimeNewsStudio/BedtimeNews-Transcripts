| FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE<br>THURSDAY, JULY 16, 1998 | AT<br>(202) 514-2008<br>TDD (202) 514-1888 |
| **STATEMENT OF JOEL I. KLEIN, ASSISTANT ATTORNEY GENERAL FOR ANTITRUST, ON THE ABANDONMENT OF THE LOCKHEED-NORTHROP TRANSACTION**<br>"We welcome this decision to abandon the transaction. This<br>means that the United States government and the American people<br>will continue to receive the highest possible quality of military<br>products and services in the defense of this nation, at the lowest<br>possible cost to the American Taxpayer." <br>"We greatly appreciate the assistance of the Department of<br>Defense in investigating and litigating this matter. Justice and<br>defense were united in the view that the proposed merger could not<br>go forward. Both agencies are committed to ensuring that America's<br>fighting men and women have the best equipment that anyone can<br>develop, at prices that the U.S. Military can afford."<br>###<br>98-334 |  |
