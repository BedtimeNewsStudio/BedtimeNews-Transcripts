**Nusantara Capital City has officially been 
designated the new capital of the Republic of** 
**Indonesia.**

“Global City for All.”**

**Indonesia. Sustainably designed to international 
standards, it will be a contemporary metropolis.** 
**Nusantara is designed to be a liveable, 
innovative, and inclusive city.**

**In addition to serving as a future economic 
driver for Indonesia, Nusantara is a national 
identity symbol that embodies the nation’s 
diversity.**

**Nusantara has already altered the course** 
**of Indonesia’s development in its early** 
**construction phase.  Its exciting evolution** 
**justifies the comprehensive documentation of** 
**its development until 2045.**

# MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF INDONESIA

**I am pleased that the Nusantara Capital Authority (NCA) 
has issued its initial report. This publication presents a 
comprehensive picture of the NCA as the primary driving 
force behind the development of our new country’s 
capital, Nusantara Capital City, or Nusantara.**

**We are all aware that the development of Nusantara is a 
national priority. Building a new capital city entails more 
than simply relocating government headquarters and 
building infrastructure. The development of Nusantara 
is part of Indonesia’s grand strategy, as stated in the** 
**2045 Golden Indonesia (Indonesia Emas 2045) Vision, 
which serves as a road map to a more advanced, just, 
and prosperous Indonesia within the framework of the** 
**Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia.**

**The 2045 Golden Indonesia (Indonesia Emas 2045) Vision 
aims to position Indonesia as a developed nation by the 
time it commemorates its centennial of independence in** 
**2045. Our overarching goal is to achieve high standards 
of human excellence, technological prowess, equitable 
and beneficial well-being of all citizens, resilient national 
security, and authoritative and robust governance.**

---

**Nusantara highlights the shift in national development** 
**priorities to become more Indonesia-centric and,** 
**simultaneously, accelerates Indonesia’s economic** 
**transformation. As the governmental entity tasked with** 
**overseeing the development of this forthcoming capital** 
**city, the Nusantara Capital Authority is endeavoring** 
**to accelerate the preparation, construction, transfer,** 
**and administration of the Nusantara regional special** 
**government.**

**In light of this, I extend my appreciation to the NCA 
for its collaborative, communicative, and coordinating 
endeavors that contributed to the development of** 
**Nusantara. Rapid progress was evident at the Nusantara 
construction site each time I visited. Private investors 
have undertaken construction alongside governmental 
and non-governmental organization initiatives for 
reforestation and local community empowerment, in 
addition to the government’s physical infrastructure 
development. By 2024, I am optimistic that the Central** 
**Government Core Area of Nusantara will be operational.**

**This publication will hopefully inspire all of us to 
collaborate with the Nusantara Capital Authority in 
order to transform this new capital city into a global 
metropolis, while also increasing public awareness of** 
**Nusantara.**

# INTRODUCTION BY THE CHAIRMAN OF NUSANTARA CAPITAL AUTHORITY

PRESIDENT

**A new chapter in history is currently being 
written. At long last, a capital city has been 
constructed by its citizens. Proposing the 
establishment of a new national capital in** 
**1957 was Sukarno, the inaugural President 
of the Republic of Indonesia. It was 
deliberated later, during the term of the 
next President of the Republic of Indonesia.** 
**Conversely, during the administration of** 
**Joko Widodo, the 7th President of the** 
**Republic of Indonesia, the plan to relocate 
the national capital was entirely executed.** 
**President Joko Widodo formally declared 
the relocation of the republic’s capital from** 
**Jakarta to East Kalimantan on 16 August** 
**2019 during his State of the Nation Address 
at the MPR RI Annual General Session.**

**The President stated at the 2019 MPR** 
**Annual Session that the nation’s capital** 
**is not only a symbol of national identity,** 
**but also a reflection of the country’s** 
**progress. Nusantara was created to achieve** 
**economic equality and justice in Indonesia.** 
**It represents a significant step forward in** 
**national development toward the Golden** 
**Indonesia Vision for 2045. Simultaneously, it** 
**demonstrates to the world how Indonesia is** 
**implementing new strategies to achieve its** 
**long-term goals.**

**Nusantara’s development goes beyond simply 
constructing physical facilities for the central 
government and moving the State Civil Apparatus (ASN) 
from Jakarta to East Kalimantan. Nusantara represents 
a new urban civilization in Indonesia, embodying five 
characteristics: green, smart, inclusive, resilient, and 
sustainable. Green means utilizing green and low-carbon 
areas; smart means utilizing innovative technology; 
inclusive means an inclusive work and community 
environment; resilient means a strong society that wants 
to move forward; and sustainable means a sustainable 
city ecosystem.**

**The first stage of Nusantara development, which 
takes place between 2022 and 2024, is the first of five 
stages that will continue until 2045. In August 2022, 
construction began with land consolidation and clearing 
for the core building area and basic infrastructure.**

**The development of Nusantara Phase I has been** 
**accelerated since 2023, with particular emphasis on the** 
**construction of key infrastructure and road access to** 
**Nusantara, the Presidential Palace, President’s Office,** 
**Ministry Offices, National Axis, Ceremony Square,** 
**and offices for various government institutions. The** 
**development progress of Nusantara Phase I has been** 
**recorded at 71.57 percent as of January 2024.**

**Moreover, both domestic and foreign investors exhibit 
a significant level of interest in the development of** 
**Nusantara. The NCA received no less than 350 letters of 
intent (LOI) from both domestic and foreign investors.** 
**About 23 domestic investors demonstrated their 
commitment by taking part in all four cornerstone 
laying ceremonies (ground-breaking) of the Nusantara 
construction project, which took place in September** 
**2023, November 2023, December 2023, and January** 
**2024.**

**More importantly, Nusantara also places a high priority 
on human resource development. In 2022 and 2023, 
the NCA collaborated with other government agencies, 
state-owned enterprises, universities, and community 
organizations to empower local communities. The NCA 
works on a variety of projects, including mentor ship 
and skill development for people within the community.** 
**This is consistent with Nusantara’s goal of creating an 
inclusive city that encourages equitable participation 
from all parties. According to the Sustainable** 
**Development Goals (SDGs), no one left behind. We 
would like to express our heartfelt gratitude to everyone 
who has helped lay the groundwork for Nusantara’s 
growth as the first step toward a Golden Indonesia in** 
**2045 (Indonesia Emas 2045).**

**Bambang Susantono**
**Chairman**
**Nusantara Capital Authority**

###### Establishment of Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Committee

Sri Lanka Government and the Indonesian Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on the cooperation in the development of agricultural products from Sri Lanka to Indonesia. The MoU aims to enhance the competitiveness of Sri Lankan agricultural products in the Indonesian market, promote sustainable agriculture practices, and improve the quality and efficiency of agricultural products. The signing ceremony took place at the Government House in Colombo, Sri Lanka, on December 15, 2018.

**Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) prioritises sustainable 
development and business activities according to three environmental, 
social and governance criteria. The ESG Committee for the Nusantara** 
**Capital Authority aims to ensure that the management of the Nusantara 
area is carried out in accordance with ESG standards. The committee 
consists of environmental, social, governance and management 
experts. Nusantara is the first city in Indonesia to have a dedicated ESG 
committee.**

**in Kalimantan is to facilitate a significant**

**Nusantara, which resides in the Kutai**

kingdom exerted its authority in Sulawesi and** 
**Java.**

for the establishment of Nusantara due to 
its elevated position, convenient access, and 
close proximity to Balikpapan and Samarinda,

###### The Urgency of Moving the National Capital

**To what degree of urgency should the capital of the 
nation be transferred from Jakarta to Nusantara? Java** 
**Island is presently characterized by a dense population, 
especially in DKI Jakarta. Numerous factors necessitated 
the relocation of the nation’s capital, as President Joko** 
**Widodo once explained. As per the directive of the Head 
of State, an immediate relocation of the nation’s capital 
is imperative due to the significant strain that DKI** 
**Jakarta and Java Island are currently experiencing due to 
factors such as heavy traffic congestion, environmental 
pollution, and dense populations.**

**Indonesia’s economic growth has been centered on** 
**Java for decades. Java is home to 57% of the nation’s** 
**population, according to population statistics (Bappenas,** 
**2019); in 2020, the Central Statistics Agency (BPS)** 
**estimated that the proportion had increased to 59%. Java** 
**has made a substantial contribution to the nation’s gross** 
**domestic product thus far. The eastern part of Indonesia,** 
**Kalimantan, contributes between 2.8 and 8.5 percent** 
**of the nation’s gross domestic product. Therefore,** 
**development in East Kalimantan’s Nusantara exemplifies** 
**the government’s effort to promote inclusive economic** 
**growth by increasing the magnet for new economic** 
**expansion and diverting attention away from the island** 
**of Java. Going forward, Nusantara will emerge as the** 
**economic epicenter of both Kalimantan and Indonesia** 
**as a whole.**

###### National Capital (IKN) Law

**After undergoing deliberations in the government, the** 
**House of Representatives (DPR), and public examination,** 
**Law No. 3 of 2022 regarding the National Capital (IKN) 
was ultimately passed by the Indonesian DPR on 18** 
**February 2022. As per Presidential Decree No. 63 of 2022 
and Law No. 3 of 2022 regarding Specifications of the** 
**Master Plan for the Nusantara Capital City, Nusantara’s 
development will occur in five phases between 2022 and** 
**2045.**

**As a turning point in the great transformation of the** 
**Indonesian nation, the IKN Law explains that the existence 
of Nusantara is also a form of mainstreaming the symbols 
of national identity, green economy, green energy, smart 
transportation, and efficient and effective governance.**

**In his remarks on the Beranda Nusantara Program Towards** 
**the New National Capital, which was broadcasted on Radio** 
**Republik Indonesia on Wednesday, February 23, 2022,** 
**President Joko Widodo stated, “We will build Nusantara** 
**in a manner that authentically embodies the greatness** 
**of the Indonesian nation, reflects the national identity,** 
**ensures social, economic, and environmental sustainability,** 
**establishes a forest city, smart city, modern and sustainable** 
**city, and meets international standards.”**

**Nusantara is built on 8 main principles, which include 
designing in accordance with natural conditions; the 
concept of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika that signifies unity in 
diversity; being connected, active, and accessible; ensuring 
safety and affordability; promoting convenience and 
efficiency through technology; supporting low carbon 
emissions; adopting a circular and resilient approach; and 
providing economic opportunities for all. These principles 
embody the government’s aspiration to establish a 
community that is both environmentally sustainable and 
socially inclusive.**

**Furthermore, the Nusantara Capital Authority (NCA) 
institution is authorized to carry out development 
activities, encompassing the preparation, construction, 
transfer, and administration of the Nusantara special 
government.**

**The commitment to the development of Nusantara**
**is founded upon principles of sustainability. This was** 
**stated by Bambang Susantono on March 10, 2022,** 
**subsequent to his inauguration as Chairman of the** 
**Financial Services Authority (OJK) at the State Palace in** 
**Jakarta. “Building a city is not just about constructing**

**buildings. More importantly, it’s about fostering social 
cohesion and interactions among citizens, ensuring the 
city becomes both livable and lovable - a world-class 
city for all.” Therefore, the development of Nusantara 
represents Indonesia’s momentum in introducing a 
sustainable infrastructure development initiative by 
making Nusantara a zero-carbon emission city.**

**Since the initial stages of Nusantara’s development, 
the Ministry of National Development Planning/**
**Bappenas has mandated that a mere 25 percent of the 
current land area shall be utilized for construction and 
development. As an additional measure to address the 
issue of climate change, Indonesia intends to preserve 
the remaining part of the area as a protected green 
space. Furthermore, 10 percent of the land is allocated 
for food security purposes. In essence, the Nusantara 
development initiative serves as a means to restore the 
forested regions of Kalimantan.**

**In addition, Nusantara’s commitment to creating a forest 
city by restoring forest ecosystems and revitalizing the
beauty of Kalimantan’s tropical forests is exemplified by** 
**65 percent of the Nusantara project being established as 
protected areas. President Joko Widodo has consistently 
advocated for a stance that prioritizes sustainable 
development. This means the Nusantara region will 
possess the capacity to assimilate precipitation into the 
soil, thereby preventing flooding.**

**The concept of a sponge city has also been included in** 
**Law No. 3 of 2022, which concerns the National Capital.** 
**The sponge concept is designed to both replenish 
water availability in the region and restore the natural 
water cycle caused by the development of Nusantara.** 
**Hence, from a sustainability perspective, a sponge city 
integrates sustainable development principles, urban 
planning, infrastructure, and architecture to create a 
circular water system.**

**The Nusantara development concept is also managed** 
**by ecological and economic concerns. Nusantara is** 
**specifically designed to ensure it does not surpass the** 
**maximum capacity of its geographical region. Hence,** 
**the Nusantara region upholds the tenets of efficient** 
**construction by employing information technology** 
**that facilitates the elimination of waste, such as** 
**the implementation of an integrated urban water** 
**management system that integrates water utilities and** 
**urban water usage and waste in a comprehensive water** 
**recycling program.**

**As the development of Nusantara as a smart city takes** 
**place, the importance of building solid foundations for** 
**a smart city cannot be overly emphasized. The primary** 
**instrument for enhancing the effectiveness of municipal** 
**services, resource administration, and public safety has** 
**to be information technology-driven systems. The quality** 
**of life for the residents of Nusantara will be improved** 
**through the integration of technology into all facets of** 
**municipal services.**

# ABOUT NUSANTARA CAPITAL AUTHORITY

**The establishment of the Nusantara Capital Authority 
(NCA) was mandated by Law No. 3 of 2022 concerning** 
**National Capital, also known as the IKN Law, which 
was amended by Law No. 21 of 2023. The NCA is 
tasked with overseeing the development of Nusantara 
from the stage of development preparation to the 
implementation of the Nusantara Special Regional** 
**Government (Pemerintah Daerah Khusus).**

**Since the independence of Indonesia, the existence 
of the NCA has been a phenomenon in government 
administration. From an administrative standpoint, 
the Chairman of the NCA is directly responsible to the** 
**President of the Republic of Indonesia. While the NCA 
has responsibilities and authority at the governor’s 
level, its structural standing is equivalent to that of a 
ministerial institution.**

**According to Presidential Regulation Number 62 of** 
**2022, the NCA has been assigned to the planning, 
development, and relocation of Nusantara, as well as 
the management of the Special Regional Government 
for Nusantara and the development of Nusantara and** 
**Partner Regions. To this end, the NCA performs 28 
functions that link the NCA with different stakeholders, 
such as the central government, regional governments, 
ministries, police and army agencies, and international 
organizations.**

**The NCA possesses both general and special authority 
in accordance with Government Regulation No. 27 of** 
**2023. According to Article 2, Paragraph (1), the general 
authority of the NCA extends to all matters pertaining to 
the government, with the exception of those concerning 
absolute or central government affairs, comprising 
foreign policy, defense and security, judicial matters, 
national monetary and fiscal affairs, and religion.**

**Meanwhile, Article 3 Paragraph (1) explains the special 
authority of the NCA in greater detail in the attachment 
to the Presidential Decree, which consists of 68 pages 
and is divided into 31 areas of special authority, including:**

**• Providing investment permits, ease of doing business, 
and special facilities to parties who support financing 
in connection with activities for the preparation, 
development, and relocation of the National Capital, 
as well as the development of Nusantara Capital and** 
**Partner Regions.**

**• The regulations governing spatial planning, land, 
environment, and disaster management under the** 
**National Capital Law.**

**• Arrangement, guidance, implementation, and 
supervision of spatial planning in the national 
strategic area of Nusantara Capital in accordance 
with its authority.**

**• Coordinating the implementation of cross-sector and 
cross-stakeholder spatial planning in the national 
strategic area of Nusantara Capital City in accordance 
with its authority.**

**In accordance with Presidential Decree No. 62 of 2022, 
the NCA established a high-level organizational structure 
comprising 7 area deputies and a chairman, vice 
chairman, secretary, and legal and compliance unit. A 
minimum of two deputies are selected from sectors of** 
**East Kalimantan society in accordance with Presidential** 
**Decree No. 62 of 2022 regarding the NCA. Therefore, on** 
**13 October 2022, Myrna Asnawati Safitri was sworn in as** 
**Deputy for Environment and Natural Resources, followed 
by Alimuddin on February 13, 2023, as Deputy for Social,** 
**Cultural, and Community Empowerment.**

**The NCA leader after a coordination meeting at the Balikpapan the NCA office, East Kalimantan.**

**The provision of services to high-level (pratama) officials, 
functional staff (ASN), and non-ASN experts persisted 
until the conclusion of 2023. By the end of 2023, the NCA 
had approximately 300 personnel on staff.**

###### Strengthening the Authority of the NCA

**In accordance with the imperative to expedite the 
progress of Nusantara, subsequent to an assessment, 
the legislature and the government reached a consensus 
to enhance the authority of the NCA by amending 
multiple provisions within the articles of Law No. 3 of** 
**2022 pertaining to Nusantara. This amendment resulted 
in the enactment of Law No. 21 of 2023, which was 
formally published on 31 October 2023.**

**The ratification of the revised IKN Law, which becomes** 
**Law No. 21 of 2023, is crucial for the NCA to execute the**

**preparation, development, transfer, and administration 
of regional government for Nusantara or 4P in an 
efficient manner so that Nusantara can become a 
sustainable city and future economic engine of the 
nation.**

**Hence, by engaging stakeholders and the public at large, 
the government will achieve the transformation of the** 
**Indonesian nation in accordance with the 2045 Golden** 
**Indonesia (Indonesia Emas 2045) Vision through the 
advancement of Nusantara.**

**Moving forward, it is anticipated that the development 
of Nusantara will be incorporated into the National Long-**
**Term Development Plan (RPJPN) 2025–2045. Therefore,** 
**Nusantara’s status is incorporated as a sustainable 
national priority from a formal legal standpoint.**

###### Sustainable Forest City

**The NCA’s commitment to sustainable development 
is remarkable, especially in its ability to inspire 
international confidence and facilitate positive 
development as the first city in Indonesia to fully 
implement sustainability measures.**

**In accordance with the Nusantara Law and the Nusantara** 
**Development Master Plan, only 25 percent of Nusantara’s** 
**252,660 hectares would undergo development. There will 
be reforestation zones covering up to 65 percent of the 
land, and the remaining 10 percent will be allocated for food 
production.**

**The development and reforestation process in the** 
**Nusantara region takes into account the current land’s 
geographical location. This is due to the existing region at** 
**Nusantara, which consists of industrial forest plantations, 
oil palm plantations, and mining. As a result, the NCA’s 
activities in phase I are focused on ensuring that all parties 
adhere to the spatial planning guidelines. This is outlined 
in the Chairman of the NCA’s Circular Letter on the** 
**Arrangement of Mining and Plantation Licensing in the** 
**Nusantara region.**

**In light of this, the NCA has formed a Task Force for 
the prevention and handling of illegal mining activities 
in the Nusantara region. The Task Force consists**

**of law enforcement officials, the Indonesian National** 
**Armed Forces (TNI), central ministries, and the regional 
government. The Task Force will primarily focus on 
regulating mining activities in the Nusantara region, 
specifically the 61 active mining business licenses covering 
an area of 60,684 hectares with an opening area of 17,930 
hectares and an opening area of 3,795 hectares from illegal 
mining.**

**A further crucial measure is to implement River Watershed** 
**(DAS) rehabilitation. Prior to the establishment of Nusantara,** 
**the secondary forest area constituted merely 10 percent,** 
**with a deforestation rate of 1,000 hectares per year. Thus,** 
**the objective of DAS Rehabilitation is to achieve Nusantara’s** 
**vision of becoming a forest city, with 75 percent of its** 
**land allocated to green open spaces. Meanwhile, the** 
**rehabilitated land covers an area of 2,300 hectares.**

**Reforestation efforts were initiated at Nusantara through 
the development of the Mentawir Nursery. From its 
intended 15 million seedlings, the Mentawir Nursery has 
produced 2 million since its opening in 2021. Woody plants 
such as belangeran, ironwood, meranti, balsa, gaharu, 
nyatoh, nyamplung, tengkawang, and jabon are among 
the 39 varieties of seeds that exist. Aesthetic plants such 
as tanjung, flamboyan, tabebuya, and red shoots are also 
present, along with non-timber forest products such as 
sugar palm, cempedak, durian, jengkol, petai, mango, 
mangosteen, avocado, soursop, and others.**

**A forest city is defined as an integrated landscape** 
**dominated by forests or green open spaces that provides**
**forest ecosystem services to support life alongside,** 
**and in harmony with, nature. The Nusantara Miniature** 
**Tropical Rainforest was constructed in Nusantara’s Central** 
**Government Core Area as part of an effort to establish a** 
**tropical rainforest ecosystem. The Nusantara Miniature** 
**Tropical Rainforest, which was officially inaugurated by the** 
**President on 20 December 2023, on an area of 98 hectares,**

**is intended to perform forest and environmental purposes 
while also serving as a showcase for the community of the 
tropical rainforest succession processes. The plants at the** 
**Nusantara Miniature Tropical Rainforest are sourced from 
lowland tropical rainforests.**

**Another program for developing a sustainable city is** 
**the Nusantara Green Pesantren. This program involves** 
**multiple parties, including the NCA, Katadata, Benih** 
**Baik, Jejakin, Donatur Sabuk Hijau Nusantara, and** 
**Islamic Boarding Schools. This initiative aims to promote**

###### Harnessing the Sun: The First Step toward’s Nusantara’s Clean Energy Solutions

**As many as 21,600 solar panels have been installed on 
the 100 hectares of land in Nusantara. The installation 
work began in April 2023 as part of the first phase of the 
planned 50 Megawatt (MW) Solar Power Plant (PLTS) 
project, which will power the lights of the city.**

**This step is part of Nusantara’s commitment to 
developing IKN as a green and ecofriendly area while 
utilizing new and renewable energy, commonly referred 
to as green energy. The project executor is Nusantara** 
**Sembcorp Solar Energy (NSSE), a joint venture between** 
**Nusantara Renewable, a subsidiary of State Electricity** 
**Company (PLN) sub-holding company Nusantara Power,**

**and Sembawang Corp/Sembcorp (a Singaporean-based 
energy company).**

**NSSE, the construction of the 10 MW solar power plant, 
out of a total capacity of 50 MW, has been completed, 
with the Commercial Operation Date (COD) set for** 
**February or March 2024. The remaining 40 MW of PLTS is 
scheduled for COD in mid-2024, with a completion date 
in November 2024. "The first twenty thousand panels are 
for the initial 10 MW." Semcorp will install the rest. A total 
of 21,600 solar panels have been installed on the IKN’s 
solar-powered power plant. Syarief Andrian, Director**

# Shaping the Future: Empowering Indigenous Communities through Development

**Karyadi, chairman of the East Kalimantan Dayak** 
**Community (PDKT), is happy with the positive impact** 
**Nusantara has brought to the indigenous people over 
the last couple of years—a more vibrant economy.**

**The surrounding indigenous people have made three 
major requests: infrastructure improvements, clean 
water, and electricity. Karyadi is more than convinced 
that meeting the three requests through the existence 
of Nusantara will bring prosperity to the indigenous 
people.**

**“Infrastructures, such as roads, are critical to reducing** 
**transportation and logistics costs. The normally** 
**high prices of staple foods are correlated with high** 
**transportation costs. Fish from West Kutai Regency and** 
**Berau Regency have more chance to rotten if distributed** 
**on roads in poor condition,” said a concerned resident of** 
**Maridan Village, Sepaku.**

**Water is an urgent need for the Nusantara surrounding 
residents of Sepaku District, Penajam Paser Utara, who 
have struggled for years to access clean water. People 
are forced to purchase a gallon of water for drinking. “We 
hope that once its construction is completed, the Sepaku** 
**Semoi dam can supply clean water to the surrounding 
residents,” he said.**

**The residents of East Kalimantan already have an 
adequate supply of electricity, especially the residents of** 
**Sepaku, who now enjoy a consistent power supply.**

**According to Karyadi, public road infrastructure has 
improved since the Nusantara project commenced.** 
**“Around 99 percent of the roads there are already 
asphalted in excellent condition,” said the Barito-born 
chairman.**

**Smooth roads connecting the Nusantara buffer zone,** 
**Penajam Paser Utara, and the Kutai Kartanegara region 
to Balikpapan indicate an improving local economy.** 
**“Before 2019, it would take 4 hours to get to Sepaku from** 
**Balikpapan, but now it only takes 1.5–2 hours,” Karyadi 
said.**

the human resources (HR) capacity of the surrounding 
residents. Many young people with bachelor’s degrees 
live in Sepaku, but some prefer the private sector.** 
**“It’s concerning given they are more than qualified 
to contribute to Nusantara rather than just being 
onlookers,” he explained.**

**According to this Sepalu indigenous figure, engaging 
young people from East Kalimantan in Nusantara 
development will help narrow an otherwise larger 
social gap across the entire region. The NCA has also 
conducted numerous meetings with indigenous people 
to discuss their potential role in Nusantara's economic, 
socio-cultural, educational, environmental, and tourism 
development.**

**seeks to actively participate in addressing the**

**evidenced by the conversion of at least 65% of**

**is a pioneering city in Indonesia that has** 
**implemented a comprehensive strategy to attain** 
**net zero status.**

global effort to conserve biodiversity, primarily 
by safeguarding the natural environment where 
plants and animals thrive in Kalimantan. The**

###### EIGHT MAIN PRINCIPLES OF NUSANTARA DEVELOPMENT

###### Designing with Nature in Mind

**An important principle in designing the** 
**National Capital City is to take into account 
the local natural conditions. By acquiring 
a comprehensive knowledge of the local 
ecosystem, topography, and geographical 
features, the construction of the capital city 
can be seamlessly incorporated into the 
surrounding environment, ensuring the 
preservation of the existing ecosystem.**

###### Connected, Active, and Accessible

###### Bhinneka Tunggal Ika

**This principle emphasizes the significance 
of cultural, social, and ethnic diversity in the 
growth and progress of the National Capital** 
**City. By acknowledging and comprehending 
this range of differences, the Capital City can 
authentically embody the essence of unity 
in diversity, as exemplified in the national 
motto, “Bhinneka Tunggal Ika.”**

**The main emphasis of this principle is on 
strong connectivity and easy accessibility.** 
**Facilitating the establishment of 
infrastructure that fosters connections 
between different regions and ensures 
convenient accessibility for people is crucial.** 
**Efficient transport systems will bolster daily 
operations and foster economic expansion.**

###### Comfort and Efficiency through Technology

###### Safe and Affordable

**A safe and affordable environment is essential 
to the development of the National Capital.** 
**Security measures should be incorporated 
into the design, and housing and cost-ofliving policies should ensure that the Capital** 
**City is accessible to all levels of society 
without compromising quality of life.**

**The use of modern technology is vital for 
achieving convenience and efficiency in 
the National Capital. The incorporation 
of technological innovations into urban 
management, public services, and 
information systems will ensure efficient 
operations and convenience for citizens.**

###### Low Carbon Emission

**The installation of renewable energy capacity 
will entirely meet Nusantara’s energy 
needs. Increased energy efficiency in public 
buildings by 60 percent by 2045, with the 
goal of achieving Net Zero Emissions in IKN 
by 2045.**

###### Economic Opportunities for All

**Creating equitable economic opportunities 
for all levels of society is a basic principle.** 
**Infrastructure that promotes business 
growth and employment will transform the** 
**National Capital into an inclusive economic 
driver, ensuring that everyone benefits from 
development.**

###### Circular and Resilient

**Food production needs will be fulfilled using 
only ten percent of the land area within the** 
**Nusantara government region. In addition, 
a recycling rate of 60 percent is projected 
to be achieved by 2045, while complete 
management of wastewater through a 
treatment system is targeted to be achieved 
by 2035.**

# LOGO OF NUSANTARA : THE HAYAT TREE

**The discovery of the cosmological conception of the Tree 
of Life in Indonesia not only represents a vision, beliefs, 
and perspectives but is also a collective consciousness 
of the people of the archipelago that has for so long 
touched upon their human attitudes. This then became 
a core foundation in the designing of the Nusantara Tree 
of Life with an ethos of Indonesia’s varied proficiencies 
as well as the findings regarding the value of the 
archipelago’s ecological intelligence.**

**This logogram has aspects of nature, which are trees or 
wood, water, air, and earth, that are contained in its every 
morphological intention. Designed to stand vertically 
upright to signal its strength to become a home for** 
**Garuda Pancasila as well as a new home for all, a new 
civilization, and a new history of Indonesia.**

###### RATIONAL

**The logogram itself is made up of the universal,** 
**sequential Tree of Life cosmology throughout all corners** 
**of Indonesia. On the lower portion of the logo, there are** 
**the five tenets of the Pancasila which act as the roots.** 
**On the middle portion, there are maritime lanes as** 
**the branches. Whereas, the upper portion of the logo** 
**represents the power of civilization that bloomed with a** 
**reminding symbol of the independence of a sovereign** 
**Indonesia.**

**• The Sea, Maritime Strength, and Wawasan Nusantara.** 
**The Nusantara Tree of Life logogram represents the 
spirit of decentralization with the Nusantara Outlook 
that considers the sea not as a divider, rather as a 
connecting tissue which can act as a reminder of the** 
**Indonesian identity.**

**•**

**Respect for Indonesia’s Ecological Wealth. With the 
use of the Nusantara Tree of Life logogram as the 
face of the Nusantara Capital of Indonesia, what 
must always be remembered is the fundamental 
understanding that Indonesia’s rich ecology is the 
source of all life and inspiration for all the cultures 
within it. As such, understanding the importance of 
protection of nature by the humans of Indonesia is 
crucial, especially as they are our coexisting partner 
in life.**

**• Nusantara as a Marker of Time, Civilization, and a New**

and sophistications. All the national efforts and 
advancements of the world must be involved to 
create conditions that are harmonious in a future
