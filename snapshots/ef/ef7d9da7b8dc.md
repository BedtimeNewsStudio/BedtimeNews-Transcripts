**Sheikh Rehana Siddiq** (born 13 September 1955) is a Bangladeshi [Awami League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awami_League "Awami League") politician.[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3) She is the younger sister of the former Prime Minister [Sheikh Hasina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Hasina "Sheikh Hasina") and the daughter of the first [President of Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Bangladesh "President of Bangladesh") [Sheikh Mujibur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman "Sheikh Mujibur Rahman").[&#8202;](#ref-back-4-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-4) She is also the mother of [Tulip Siddiq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulip_Siddiq "Tulip Siddiq"), a British [Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_(UK) "Labour Party (UK)") politician[&#8202;](#ref-back-5-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-5) and former [Economic Secretary to the Treasury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_Secretary_to_the_Treasury "Economic Secretary to the Treasury").[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6) She is currently facing multiple corruption cases in [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh").[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-:0)[&#8202;](#ref-back-:1-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-:1)

## Early life

Rehana was 4th child of [Sheikh Mujibur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman "Sheikh Mujibur Rahman"), the first president of [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh "Bangladesh"), and [Sheikh Fazilatunnesa Mujib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Fazilatunnesa_Mujib "Sheikh Fazilatunnesa Mujib"), a housewife. She was placed under house arrest in [Dhanmondi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhanmondi_Thana "Dhanmondi Thana") along with her family by the [Pakistan Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Army "Pakistan Army") during [Bangladesh Liberation War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Liberation_War "Bangladesh Liberation War").[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9) She passed [matriculation examination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matriculation_examination "Matriculation examination") from [Shaheen School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAF_Shaheen_College_Dhaka "BAF Shaheen College Dhaka").[&#8202;](#ref-back-10-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-10)[&#8202;](#ref-back-11-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-11) She was in [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany "West Germany")[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12)[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13) with her elder sister [Sheikh Hasina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Hasina "Sheikh Hasina") when her family was [assassinated in a military coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman "Assassination of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman") by the [Bangladesh Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Army "Bangladesh Army") on 15 August 1975.[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14)[&#8202;](#ref-back-15-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-15) She was 20 years old at that time.[&#8202;](#ref-back-16-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-16)

## Career

After the assassination of [Sheikh Mujibur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman "Sheikh Mujibur Rahman"), Rehana and her elder sister [Sheikh Hasina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Hasina "Sheikh Hasina") took political refuge in India first and later to the United Kingdom. She had played an instrumental role in raising international awareness on the brutal and unlawful assassination of her entire family. After relentless campaign in different European countries, in the year 1979 in Sweden, Sheikh Rehana held the first international conference to call for an impartial trial on Bangabandhu Murder. The conference was attended by international jurists, European civil society members and her political colleagues in exile.[&#8202;](#ref-back-17-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-17) During her exile in the UK in 1980s, Sheikh Rehana assisted [Bangladesh Awami League (BAL)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Awami_League "Bangladesh Awami League") to reach out to European Civil Society and UN Organizations on the plight of Bangladeshi people under undemocratic and brutal military dictatorship it had witnessed from 1975 to 1991.[&#8202;](#ref-back-18-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-18)

Between the year 2007-08, while Sheikh Hasina was imprisoned under the emergency rule of [Fakhruddin Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fakhruddin_Ahmed "Fakhruddin Ahmed")’s military backed caretaker government, Sheikh Rehana on Hasina's behalf secretly organised Awami League and other political parties to bring back democratic elected government in Dhaka.[&#8202;](#ref-back-19-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-19)[&#8202;](#ref-back-20-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-20) Sheikh Rehana was made councilor of [BAL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Awami_League "Bangladesh Awami League") Dhaka South unit in 2016.[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-21)

Alam Group of Industries").[&#8202;](#ref-back-25-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-25)[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-1)[\[7\]](#refnote-:0)[&#8202;](#ref-back-26-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-26) Law adviser to [the Interim Government of Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yunus_ministry "Yunus ministry") [Asif Nazrul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asif_Nazrul "Asif Nazrul") stated on December 9, 2024 that "Younger sister Sheikh Rehana was Sheikh Hasina's cashier" in context of money laundering and corruption.[&#8202;](#ref-back-27-0)[\[27\]](#refnote-27)[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-28)

In December 2025, she was sentenced to 7 years in jail for the Purbachal scam cases.[&#8202;](#ref-back-29-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-29)[&#8202;](#ref-back-30-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-30)

## Residence and security

She was allotted a government house/plot in [Dhanmondi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhanmondi "Dhanmondi") for a "Token" 100 taka in 2001 by the government of Bangladesh.[&#8202;](#ref-back-31-0)[\[31\]](#refnote-31) Bangladesh government has a policy and law to allot government plots to prominent personalities, and as such this allotment was made to her considering her position as the country's father of the nation's daughter and the resulting security threat to her and her family members. Government has allotted such lands to many prominent political, social and cultural personalities in the past and still continues to do so. The first allocation was cancelled when Bangladesh Nationalist Party came to power. Later the decision to award the allotment was upheld in the High Court of Bangladesh following a Writ Petition filed by Sheikh Rehana.[&#8202;](#ref-back-32-0)[\[32\]](#refnote-32) But a change of heart saw her return the house to the government despite obtaining High Court order in her favour.[&#8202;](#ref-back-33-0)[\[33\]](#refnote-33)

In 2014 she was allocated a house in [Gulshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulshan_Thana "Gulshan Thana") after [Awami League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Awami_League "Bangladesh Awami League") came to power for a token price of 1001 taka following the High Court order which upheld the legality of her prior allotment.[&#8202;](#ref-back-34-0)[\[34\]](#refnote-34)[&#8202;](#ref-back-35-0)[\[35\]](#refnote-35) Given the violent history of brutal assassination of Sheikh Mujib family in 1975, and later attempts to assassinate Sheikh Hasina, which was 19 times in total,[&#8202;](#ref-back-36-0)[\[36\]](#refnote-36) and very high security risk towards the immediate Mujib family members, in 2015 she and her children were given lifelong protection by the government of Bangladesh through the [Special Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Security_Force "Special Security Force").[&#8202;](#ref-back-37-0)[\[37\]](#refnote-37)[&#8202;](#ref-back-38-0)[\[38\]](#refnote-38) Practice of extending such security protection is not rare for persons with high security risk to their lives.[&#8202;](#ref-back-39-0)[\[39\]](#refnote-39) The government also announced free utility for life for her and her family.[&#8202;](#ref-back-40-0)[\[40\]](#refnote-40) After the fall of the Awami League government, her residence in Gulshan was vandalized.[&#8202;](#ref-back-41-0)[\[41\]](#refnote-41) However, the [interim government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yunus_interim_government "Yunus interim government") decided to cancel this law.[&#8202;](#ref-back-42-0)[\[42\]](#refnote-42)[&#8202;](#ref-back-43-0)[\[43\]](#refnote-43) On 29 August 2024, the interim government decided to cancel this law.[&#8202;](#ref-back-44-0)[\[44\]](#refnote-44) Then on September 9, 2024, an ordinance was issued repealing the law.[&#8202;](#ref-back-45-0)[\[45\]](#refnote-45)[&#8202;](#ref-back-46-0)[\[46\]](#refnote-46)

In April 2025, the [Anti-Corruption Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Corruption_Commission_(Bangladesh) "Anti-Corruption Commission (Bangladesh)") seized Rehana's properties along with her family members after filing a successful petition with Dhaka Metropolitan Senior Special Judge Md Zakir Hossain.[&#8202;](#ref-back-47-0)[\[47\]](#refnote-47)

## Personal life

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7f/Sheikh_Hasina_greets_Tulip_Siddiq%27s_daughter_UK_2016-05-15.jpg/250px-Sheikh_Hasina_greets_Tulip_Siddiq%27s_daughter_UK_2016-05-15.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sheikh_Hasina_greets_Tulip_Siddiq's_daughter_UK_2016-05-15.jpg) Rehana, her daughter [Tulip Siddiq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulip_Siddiq "Tulip Siddiq") and son [Radwan Mujib Siddiq Bobby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radwan_Mujib_Siddiq_Bobby "Radwan Mujib Siddiq Bobby") with sister, the ousted Prime Minister of Bangladesh [Sheikh Hasina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Hasina "Sheikh Hasina") and nephew [Sajeeb Wazed Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sajeeb_Wazed_Joy "Sajeeb Wazed Joy") in 2016 in London.

Rehana is married to [Shafique Ahmed Siddique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shafique_Ahmed_Siddique "Shafique Ahmed Siddique"), retired professor of Department of Accounting & Information Systems, Faculty of Business Studies (FBS), [University of Dhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dhaka "University of Dhaka").[&#8202;](#ref-back-48-0)[\[48\]](#refnote-48) Her son, [Radwan Mujib Siddiq Bobby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radwan_Mujib_Siddiq_Bobby "Radwan Mujib Siddiq Bobby"), is a councilor of Bangladesh Awami League.[&#8202;](#ref-back-49-0)[\[49\]](#refnote-49) Her daughter, [Tulip Siddiq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulip_Siddiq "Tulip Siddiq"), is a Member of Parliament for the [Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_(UK) "Labour Party (UK)") in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom") [House of Commons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Commons_of_the_United_Kingdom "House of Commons of the United Kingdom") for the [Hampstead and Kilburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampstead_and_Kilburn_(UK_Parliament_constituency) "Hampstead and Kilburn (UK Parliament constituency)") constituency, her other daughter is Azmina Siddiq.[&#8202;](#ref-back-50-0)[\[50\]](#refnote-50)

Rehana often accompanied Sheikh Hasina on her official trips including the [state funeral of Elizabeth II, Queen of the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_guests_at_the_state_funeral_of_Elizabeth_II "List of guests at the state funeral of Elizabeth II") in 2022 and [Coronation of Charles III and Camilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronation_of_Charles_III_and_Camilla "Coronation of Charles III and Camilla") in 2023.[&#8202;](#ref-back-51-0)[\[51\]](#refnote-51) She also accompanied Hasina after she [resigned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resignation_of_Sheikh_Hasina "Resignation of Sheikh Hasina") as Prime Minister and fled Bangladesh to India during the [Non-cooperation movement (2024)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-cooperation_movement_(2024) "Non-cooperation movement (2024)") on 5 August.[&#8202;](#ref-back-52-0)[\[52\]](#refnote-52)
