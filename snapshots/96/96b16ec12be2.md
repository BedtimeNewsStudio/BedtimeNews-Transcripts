**Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited** ( [d/b/a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D/b/a "D/b/a") **Jio**) is an Indian [telecommunications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunications "Telecommunications") company, a [subsidiary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidiary "Subsidiary") of [Jio Platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jio_Platforms "Jio Platforms"). The company is headquartered in [Navi Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navi_Mumbai "Navi Mumbai"). It operates a national [LTE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LTE_(telecommunication) "LTE (telecommunication)") network with coverage across all 22 [telecom circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecom_circle "Telecom circle"). Jio offers [4G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4G "4G"), [4G+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LTE_Advanced "LTE Advanced") and [5G NR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5G_NR "5G NR") services all over India. Its [6G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6G_(network) "6G (network)") service is in the works.[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7)[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8)[&#8202;](#ref-back-9-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-9)[&#8202;](#ref-back-INDIANEXPRESS-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-INDIANEXPRESS)

Jio was soft launched on 27 December 2015 with a beta for partners and employees,[&#8202;](#ref-back-India_Today_2016-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-India_Today_2016)[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12) and became publicly available on 5 September 2016. It is the [largest mobile network operator in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_telecom_companies_in_India#Wireless_operators "List of telecom companies in India") and the [third largest mobile network operator in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mobile_network_operators#Terrestrial "List of mobile network operators") with over 52.4 [crore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crore "Crore") (524 million) subscribers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Q4-FY-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-Q4-FY)

## History

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/60/The_Prime_Minister%2C_Shri_Narendra_Modi_visits_the_exhibition_at_the_6th_edition_of_India_Mobile_Congress%2C_in_New_Delhi_on_October_01%2C_2022_%281%29.jpg/500px-The_Prime_Minister%2C_Shri_Narendra_Modi_visits_the_exhibition_at_the_6th_edition_of_India_Mobile_Congress%2C_in_New_Delhi_on_October_01%2C_2022_%281%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_Prime_Minister,_Shri_Narendra_Modi_visits_the_exhibition_at_the_6th_edition_of_India_Mobile_Congress,_in_New_Delhi_on_October_01,_2022_(1).jpg) Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narendra_Modi "Narendra Modi") visits the stall of Jio with [Ashwini Vaishnaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashwini_Vaishnaw "Ashwini Vaishnaw"), Minister of Railways and Communications along with [Mukesh Ambani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukesh_Ambani "Mukesh Ambani") and Akash Ambani on October 1, 2022.

Further information: [Jio effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jio_effect "Jio effect")

The company was registered in [Ambawadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambawadi "Ambawadi"), Ahmedabad, Gujarat, on 15 February 2007 as Infotel Broadband Services Limited (IBSL). In June 2010, [Reliance Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reliance_Industries "Reliance Industries") (RIL) bought a 95% stake in IBSL for ₹4,800 crore (US$500million). Although unlisted, IBSL was the only company that won broadband spectrum in all 22 circles in India in the 4G auction that took place earlier that year.[&#8202;](#ref-back-15-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-15) Later continuing as RIL's telecom subsidiary, Infotel Broadband Services Limited was renamed as Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited (RJIL) in January 2013.[&#8202;](#ref-back-16-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-16)

In June 2015, Jio announced that it would start its operations throughout the country by the end of 2015.[&#8202;](#ref-back-The_Economic_Times-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-The_Economic_Times) However, four months later in October, the company postponed the launch to the first quarter of the financial year 2016–2017.[&#8202;](#ref-back-auto-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-auto)

Later, in July 2015, a [PIL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_interest_litigation_in_India "Public interest litigation in India") filed in the [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_India "Supreme Court of India") by an NGO called the [Centre for Public Interest Litigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Public_Interest_Litigation "Centre for Public Interest Litigation"), through [Prashant Bhushan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prashant_Bhushan "Prashant Bhushan"), challenged the grant of a pan-India license to Jio by the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India "Government of India"). The PIL also alleged that the firm was being allowed to provide voice telephony along with its [4G data service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4G "4G"), by paying an additional fee of just ₹165.8 crore (US$17million) which was arbitrary and unreasonable, and contributed to a loss of ₹2,284.2 crore (US$240million) to the exchequer.[&#8202;](#ref-back-19-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-19)[&#8202;](#ref-back-20-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-20) The Indian [Department of Telecommunications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Telecommunications "Department of Telecommunications") (DoT), however, explained that the rules for 3G and BWA spectrum didn't restrict BWA winners from providing voice telephony. As a result, the PIL was revoked, and the accusations were dismissed.[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-21)

The 4G services were launched internally on 27 December 2015.[&#8202;](#ref-back-22-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-22) The company commercially launched its 4G services on 5 September 2016,[&#8202;](#ref-back-India_Today_2016-1)[\[11\]](#refnote-India_Today_2016) offering free data and voice services until 31 December,[&#8202;](#ref-back-23-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-23) which was later extended until 31 March 2017.[&#8202;](#ref-back-24-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-24)[&#8202;](#ref-back-25-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-25) Within the first month, Jio announced that it had acquired 1.6 crore (16 million) subscribers[&#8202;](#ref-back-26-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-26) and has crossed 5 crore (50 million) subscriber mark in 83 days since its launch,[&#8202;](#ref-back-27-0)[\[27\]](#refnote-27) subsequently crossing 100 million subscribers on 22 February 2017.[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-28)By October 2017[\[update\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jio&action=edit), it had about 13 crore (130 million) subscribers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-veconomist-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-veconomist)

On 5 October 2022, it has launched 5G services to [Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi "Delhi"), [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai "Mumbai"), [Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata "Kolkata") and [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai "Chennai").[&#8202;](#ref-back-indiatoday.in-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-indiatoday.in) As of March 2023, Jio 5G service was available in 365 cities across India.[&#8202;](#ref-back-31-0)[\[31\]](#refnote-31) As of April 2023, 5G service was available across 2,500+ cities in India.[&#8202;](#ref-back-32-0)[\[32\]](#refnote-32) In August 2023, it was announced that Jio had completed its rollout of 5G services nationwide, ahead of schedule.[&#8202;](#ref-back-33-0)[\[33\]](#refnote-33)

### Issue with incumbents

In September 2016, the [Telecom Regulatory Authority of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecom_Regulatory_Authority_of_India "Telecom Regulatory Authority of India") (TRAI) summoned Jio and the country's existing telecom operators like [Airtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airtel_India "Airtel India"), [Vodafone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vodafone_India "Vodafone India"), and [Idea Cellular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idea_Cellular "Idea Cellular") to meet and discuss an issue regarding the interconnection between the operators. This was a result of Jio complaining to TRAI and [Department of Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Telecom "Department of Telecom") (DoT) about other operators not honoring their commercial agreements to let Jio use their network resources. The company further added that the operators are trying to sabotage its entry into the telecom scene. However, DoT dismissed the request and directed TRAI to help settle the dispute amicably. Moreover, the [Cellular Operators Association of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellular_Operators_Association_of_India "Cellular Operators Association of India") (COAI) requested TRAI to include all the operators in the discussion instead of the three.[&#8202;](#ref-back-113-0)[\[113\]](#refnote-113)

The incumbent operators had previously approached the country's [PMO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister's_Office_(India) "Prime Minister's Office (India)") to reiterate their stance that they " _are in no way obliged or in any position to entertain Jio's requests for interconnection points as they do not have either the network or the financial resources to terminate the latter's humongous volumes of potentially asymmetric voice traffic._" Responding to this, Mukesh Ambani, owner of Jio, said, " _All operators have publicly said last week that they will provide this (interconnect and MNP). So, we are waiting. These are all great companies. They have their own reputations to protect. I am confident they won't violate the law._" Commenting about number portability, he added, "The number belongs to the consumer. No operator can cause trouble if they want to change operators."[&#8202;](#ref-back-114-0)[\[114\]](#refnote-114) However, on 12 September 2016, Idea Cellular agreed to allow Jio to use 196 of its interconnection access points.[&#8202;](#ref-back-115-0)[\[115\]](#refnote-115)

### Subscriber data breach

On 10 July 2017, Jio's customer data was allegedly leaked on the website magicapk.com.[&#8202;](#ref-back-116-0)[\[116\]](#refnote-116) The website was suspended shortly after the news of the breach broke out.[&#8202;](#ref-back-117-0)[\[117\]](#refnote-117)

### Farmers' boycott

During the [2020–2021 Indian Farmers' Protest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%932021_Indian_farmers'_protest "2020–2021 Indian farmers' protest"), farmers across multiple states in northern part of India boycotted and vandalized Jio's towers owing to allegations of Reliance Industries's support to controversial farm laws of India.[&#8202;](#ref-back-118-0)[\[118\]](#refnote-118) Jio blamed its competitors Airtel and Vodafone for spreading rumours of "Reliance being an undue beneficiary in the farm bills", an allegation both the companies denied.[&#8202;](#ref-back-119-0)[\[119\]](#refnote-119) The company saw a loss of around 25 lakh (2.5 million) subscribers in Punjab and Haryana between November and December 2020.[&#8202;](#ref-back-120-0)[\[120\]](#refnote-120)

### Bird deaths

See also: [5G misinformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5G_misinformation "5G misinformation")

In January 2021, several social media posts started circulating linking bird deaths to Jio's 5G network trials. The claims were found to be fake as 5G trials were not yet permitted. The cause of the death of the birds is determined as due to an outbreak of [bird flu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avian_influenza "Avian influenza").[&#8202;](#ref-back-121-0)[\[121\]](#refnote-121)
