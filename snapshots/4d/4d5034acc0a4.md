# Update of METI's licensing policies and procedures in relation to the Republic of Korea

In order to ensure the appropriate implementation of export control and regulation, the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) applies updated licensing policies and procedures on the export and transfer of controlled items and their relevant technologies to the Republic of Korea. In this website, METI consolidates information related to this update for reference, and keeps the information up-to-date.

## Security Export Control in Japan

The Security Export Control in Japan is implemented for the purpose of enabling proper development of foreign trade and maintaining peace and safety in Japan as well as in the international community by exercising the minimum necessary control based on the FEFTA (Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Act) under international export control regimes.
