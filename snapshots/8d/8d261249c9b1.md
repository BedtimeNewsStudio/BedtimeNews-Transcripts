# Reshuffling the BoM - Component Cost Dynamics Amid Memory Price Spikes

- _The continuous memory price hikes further reshuffled the smartphone BOM cost structure._
- _DRAM has surpassed the SoC, becoming the most costly single component in a smartphone._
- _2nm SoC will further push the smartphone BOM cost rise in the future._
- _The gross margin of flagship models can hardly match the gross margin of previous flagship models in the short term._

**Seoul, Beijing, Berlin, Buenos Aires, Fort Collins, Hong Kong, London, New Delhi, Taipei, Tokyo – July 28, 2026**

According to Counterpoint Research’s [**Memory Price Tracker**](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/reports/memory-price-tracker-mar-2026), smartphone memory prices increased by more than 80% QoQ in Q2 2026. This sharp increase has created a sustained structural impact on smartphone BOM costs. Based on Counterpoint’s [**BoM & Teardown Service**](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/reports/reshuffling-the-bom-component-cost-dynamics-amid-memory-price-spikes) model estimates, the BOM cost structure of 2026 smartphones has shifted further compared with the same-tier models launched in 2025.

- **Low-end:** In Q2 2026, the component cost of the models in similar configurations increased 70% YoY, and almost all of this was driven by memory. Smartphone OEMs have significantly cut orders for entry-level products and adjusted their portfolios to defend against the short-term risk of profit losses.
- **Mid-end:** The BoM cost increased by 52% YoY in Q2 2026.  Memory’s cost took 40% of the overall BoM cost. In this segment, the superior configurations in processors and camera systems are decreasing.
- **Premium:** In Q2, the segment’s BOM cost has also increased nearly 50% YoY. DRAM surpassed the SoC, became the most expensive single component in a premium smartphone. The rise in starting retail price spread to the premium segment in Q2.

[**Senior Analyst Shenghao Bai**](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/opinion-leader/76) commented, “For mid-to-low-end smartphones, although OEMs have optimized their product portfolios to mitigate cost pressures, they will continue to face risks of margin compression and potential losses for certain models. Meanwhile, the continued increase in flagship smartphone BOM costs is also slowing the pace of technology adoption in areas such as displays and imaging systems.”

For models equipped with higher-capacity NAND storage, the increase in BOM costs is expected to be more significant. The [1TB iPhone 18 Pro Max](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/infographic-iphone-17promax-and-iphone-18promax-e-bom-cost-comparison) is estimated to see a nearly $300 BOM cost increase compared to the equivalent iPhone 17 Pro Max, which was launched last year. Smartphone OEMs are likely to adopt differentiated pricing strategies across different storage tiers to balance rising component costs and consumer demand. Meanwhile, some OEMs are expected to adopt more cost-optimized NAND solutions for high-capacity variants to control BOM increases and limit the extent of retail price hikes.

Shenghao Bai added, “The adoption of 2nm flagship SoCs in future generations will further drive up BOM costs for next-generation flagship smartphones. Even with retail price increases, we expect overall gross margins to remain slightly below those of equivalent flagship models launched in 2025.”
