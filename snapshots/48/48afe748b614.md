# Division for Planetary Sciences

Division for Planetary Sciences

The **Division for Planetary Sciences** (DPS) is a division within the [American Astronomical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Astronomical_Society "American Astronomical Society") (AAS) devoted to [Solar System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System "Solar System") research.
[&#8202;](#ref-back-aas-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-aas) It was founded in 1968. The first organizing committee members were: [Edward Anders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Anders "Edward Anders"), [Lewis Branscomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_M._Branscomb "Lewis M. Branscomb"), [Joseph W. Chamberlain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_W._Chamberlain "Joseph W. Chamberlain"), [Richard M. Goody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_M._Goody "Richard M. Goody"), [John S. Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Scoville_Hall?action=edit&redlink=1 "John Scoville Hall"), [Arvidas Kliore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvidas_Kliore?action=edit&redlink=1 "Arvidas Kliore"), [Michael B. McElroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_McElroy_(scientist) "Michael McElroy (scientist)"), [Tobias Owen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobias_Owen,_Sr.?action=edit&redlink=1 "Tobias Owen, Sr."), [Gordon Pettengill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Pettengill "Gordon Pettengill"), [Carl Sagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Sagan "Carl Sagan"), and [Harlan James Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harlan_James_Smith "Harlan James Smith"). As of 2009, it is the largest special-interest division within the AAS.[&#8202;](#ref-back-aas-1)[\[1\]](#refnote-aas) As of Oct 2010, membership totaled approximately 1415 planetary scientists and astronomers, including about 20% residing outside the U.S.[&#8202;](#ref-back-dps_membership-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-dps_membership)

DPS sponsors six prizes. The [Kuiper Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuiper_Prize "Kuiper Prize") honors outstanding contributions to the field of planetary science. The [Urey Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urey_Prize "Urey Prize") recognizes outstanding achievement in planetary research by a young scientist. The [Masursky Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masursky_Award "Masursky Award") acknowledges outstanding service to planetary science and exploration. The [Carl Sagan Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Sagan_Medal "Carl Sagan Medal") recognizes and honors outstanding communication by an active planetary scientist to the general public. The [Jonathan Eberhart Planetary Sciences Journalism Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Eberhart_Planetary_Sciences_Journalism_Award "Jonathan Eberhart Planetary Sciences Journalism Award") is a prize that recognizes and stimulates distinguished popular writing on planetary sciences. The Claudia J. Alexander Prize recognizes outstanding achievement in planetary research by a mid-career scientist.

DPS has held meetings annually since 1970.[&#8202;](#ref-back-dps_meetings-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-dps_meetings)

The official journal of the DPS is _[Icarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icarus_(journal) "Icarus (journal)")_.
