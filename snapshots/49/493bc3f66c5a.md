# PM Modi in Israel highlights: India backs Gaza peace plan, declares 'terrorism anywhere threatens peace everywhere'

## Ahead of Mr. Modi's address, PM Netanyahu hailed his Indian counterpart calling him a great leader on the world stage

Addressing the Knesset, Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](https://www.thehindu.com/topic/Narendra_Modi/) condemned “barbaric terrorist attack” by Hamas on October 7, 2023 and said “we feel your pain, we share your grief”.

“India stands with Israel firmly with full conviction in this moment,” PM Modi tells Israeli Parliament.

**Also Read | [PM Modi’s Israel visit faces geopolitical headwinds](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/modis-israel-visit-faces-geopolitical-headwinds/article70670559.ece)**

Terrorism anywhere threatens peace everywhere, PM Modi asserted in an address at the Israeli Parliament, even as he threw New Delhi’s weight behind the Gaza peace initiative, saying it holds the promise of a “just and durable peace” in the region.

Ahead of Mr. Modi’s address, PM Netanyahu hailed his Indian counterpart calling him a great leader on the world stage.

  PM Modi conferred with Israel’s ‘Speaker of the Knesset Medal’

  Quietly slipped off to Israel after my challenge to scrap U.S. deal: Rahul's dig at PM Modi

  Congress leader Rahul Gandhi took a swipe at Prime Minister Narendra Modi, saying it has been more than 24 hours since he challenged the PM to scrap the India-U.S. interim trade deal, and once again, he (Mr. Modi)has “quietly slipped off to Israel”.

  In a post in Hindi on X, Mr. Gandhi said, “Narendra ‘Surrender’ Modi, it’s been more than 24 hours since I challenged you to cancel the US Trade Deal -- and once again, you’ve quietly slipped off to Israel.”
