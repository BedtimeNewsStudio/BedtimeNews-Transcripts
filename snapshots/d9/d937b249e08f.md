## About Longbridge

Longbridge Securities was founded in March 2019 and is headquartered in Singapore. The company holds 22 financial licenses and qualifications across the United States, Hong Kong, Singapore and other regions, having secured over USD150 million in strategic investments from major financial and investment institutions. Its subsidiary, Long Bridge HK Limited (CE No.: BPX066), holds Type 1, 2, 4, and 9 licenses issued by the Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

Longbridge's core management team consists of experienced financial executives from the banking industry and technical experts from leading global internet companies, possessing extensive global financial market experience and outstanding fintech R&D capabilities.

Longbridge was founded on a shared belief: technological advancement can significantly lower the barriers and costs of global investing, enabling more people to benefit from worldwide investment opportunities. Driven by technological innovation, our founding team is committed to leveraging cutting-edge large language model capabilities to connect the entire "explore → inspire → trade" chain of global assets, delivering comprehensive global investment experiences for investors at all levels.

Longbridge has developed LongbridgeAI, an investment-focused AI Agent based on large language models, making institutional-grade research and investment capabilities accessible to all investors. LongbridgeAI helps users clearly interpret market trends, identify potential opportunities, and provides data-supported decision recommendations—empowering everyone to make smarter investment choices and eliminating information gaps between retail investors and professional institutions.

We're building a convenient global trading network that allows investors to access U.S. and other major global markets through a single account at minimal cost, reducing barriers to global trading.
