# CSSA Scheme

The CSSA Scheme provides a safety net for those who cannot support themselves financially to meet their basic needs.

## Eligibility Criteria

An applicant must satisfy the following conditions:

1. [Residence Requirement](/en/pubsvc/socsecu/comprehens/cssa/cssa_ec/cssa_ec_rr/index.html)
2. [Financial Tests](/en/pubsvc/socsecu/comprehens/cssa/cssa_ec/cssa_ec_ft/index.html)
3. [Additional Criteria for Able-bodied Adults](/en/pubsvc/socsecu/comprehens/cssa/cssa_ec/cssa_ec_ac/index.html)

## Types of Payment

1. [Standard Rates](/en/pubsvc/socsecu/comprehens/cssa/cssa_paytype/cssa_standrate/index.html)
2. [Supplements](/en/pubsvc/socsecu/comprehens/cssa/cssa_paytype/cssa_supplement/index.html)
3. [Special Grants](/en/pubsvc/socsecu/comprehens/cssa/cssa_paytype/cssa_specgrant/index.html)

apply

## How to Apply

An applicant can make an application directly with the respective social security field unit for his/her place of residence by phone, by fax, by e-mail, by post, by online form or in person. An application may also be made through a referral to the Social Welfare Department by another government department or a non-governmental organization. Upon receipt of an application, staff of the Department will conduct investigation and verification of the applicant's circumstances and information provided through office interview and home visit.

## Service Charge

No service charge is required
