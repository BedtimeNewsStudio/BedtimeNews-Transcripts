### 4 General right to bail of accused persons and others.E+W

(1)A person to whom this section applies shall be granted bail except as provided in Schedule 1 to this Act.

(2)This section applies to a person who is accused of an offence when—

(a)he appears or is brought before a magistrates’ court or the Crown Court in the course of or in connection with proceedings for the offence, or

(b)he applies to a court for bail \[ [F1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/63/section/4#commentary-c8003381 "View the commentary text for this item") or for a variation of the conditions of bail\] in connection with the proceedings.

This subsection does not apply as respects proceedings on or after a person’s conviction of the offence [F2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/63/section/4#commentary-key-470e27471137831993f8ccad2cc2b8f4 "View the commentary text for this item")....
