# Papal Basilicas

The four Papal Basilicas in Rome are St Peter’s in the Vatican, St John Lateran’s, Saint Mary Major’s and St Paul’s Outside the Walls. These are the ‘main’ churches which will have Holy Doors opened by the Pope, doors which will remain open throughout the Jubilee year.
