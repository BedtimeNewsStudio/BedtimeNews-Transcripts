From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Indian military recruitment program

**Agnipath Scheme**[&#8202;](#ref-back-1-0)[\[1\]](#refnote-1) is a [tour of duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tour_of_duty "Tour of duty") style scheme approved by the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India "Government of India") on 14 June 2022 and implemented in the country a few months later in September 2022, for recruitment of soldiers below the rank of commissioned officers into the three services of the [armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Armed_Forces "Indian Armed Forces").[&#8202;](#ref-back-IE_explained-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-IE_explained) All recruits will be hired only for a four year period. Personnel recruited under this system are to be called _Agniveers_ (transl.Fire-Warriors), which will be a new military rank.[&#8202;](#ref-back-3-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-3)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Sushant_Singh-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-Sushant_Singh)

Although the scheme bypasses the traditional service benefits (such as pensions, rations, etc) allocated to personnel recruited in the armed forces,[&#8202;](#ref-back-DNA_what_is-0)[\[5\]](#refnote-DNA_what_is) the government provides up to ₹50 lakh (US$52,000) [rupees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_rupee "Indian rupee") in insurance coverage, and they are entitled to receive a lump sum cash payment, a skill certificate and support for finding future employment upon discharge from service.[&#8202;](#ref-back-6-0)[\[6\]](#refnote-6) Spokespersons from [Indian National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Congress "Indian National Congress"), [Communist Party of India (Marxist)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_India_(Marxist) "Communist Party of India (Marxist)") and [Communist Party of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_India "Communist Party of India"), from the opposition camp, have issued statements criticising and expressing concerns about the consequences of the new scheme. They have asked the scheme to be put on hold and that the scheme be discussed in the [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_India "Parliament of India").[&#8202;](#ref-back-7-0)[\[7\]](#refnote-7)

Recently, during [Operation Sindoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Sindoor "Operation Sindoor"), 3,000 Agniveers played a crucial role in defending the Indian western border from drone attacks orchestrated by Pakistan in the recent [2025 India-Pakistan conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_India%E2%80%93Pakistan_conflict "2025 India–Pakistan conflict").[&#8202;](#ref-back-8-0)[\[8\]](#refnote-8)

## Background

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Background")\]

Prior to the introduction of the scheme, soldiers were recruited into the armed forces on a 15+ year tenure with lifelong pension.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Hindu_Editorial-0)[\[9\]](#refnote-Hindu_Editorial) From 2019, no recruitment in the armed forces was done for three years. The Indian Government cited [COVID-19 pandemic in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_India "COVID-19 pandemic in India") for this. Meanwhile 50,000 to 60,000 soldiers continued to retire annually, leading to a personnel shortage that had begun to affect the operational capabilities of the armed forces.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NIE_two_years-0)[\[10\]](#refnote-NIE_two_years)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Hooda-0)[\[11\]](#refnote-Hooda)

Recruitment in the Indian Army under Modi Govt.[&#8202;](#ref-back-12-0)[\[12\]](#refnote-12)

In 2020 a ' [Tour of duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tour_of_duty "Tour of duty")' scheme was proposed for voluntary recruitment into the forces for civilians to enable them to join for three years of short service.[&#8202;](#ref-back-13-0)[\[13\]](#refnote-13) The proposed scheme was on a trial basis and was planned to start with a test group of 100 officers and 1000 soldiers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-14-0)[\[14\]](#refnote-14)

The scheme has been taken to be named after _Agnipath_, a Hindi poem by [Harivansh Rai Bachchan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harivansh_Rai_Bachchan "Harivansh Rai Bachchan") and a popular [1990 film of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agneepath_(1990_film) "Agneepath (1990 film)") based on the poem.[&#8202;](#ref-back-15-0)[\[15\]](#refnote-15)

## Overview

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Overview")\]

The Agnipath scheme was approved by the Indian Government in June 2022 to be implemented from September 2022. The announcement was done on 14 June 2022.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Announces-0)[\[16\]](#refnote-Announces) The scheme is for both male and female aspirants of age group 17.5 to 21 years. In the midst of widespread protests against the scheme, the Central Government raised the upper limit from 21 to 23, but only for recruitment in the year 2022.[&#8202;](#ref-back-17-0)[\[17\]](#refnote-17)[&#8202;](#ref-back-18-0)[\[18\]](#refnote-18) The recruitment through this scheme is to be twice a year for the Indian Army, the Indian Navy and the Indian Air Force. The posts available are below the officer cadre.[&#8202;](#ref-back-ET_what_is-0)[\[19\]](#refnote-ET_what_is)[&#8202;](#ref-back-IE_explained-1)[\[2\]](#refnote-IE_explained)[&#8202;](#ref-back-NDTV_veteran_suggestions-0)[\[20\]](#refnote-NDTV_veteran_suggestions)

The recruits named _Agniveers_ serve for a tenure of four years that include training for six months followed by 3.5 years deployment.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NDTV_veteran_suggestions-1)[\[20\]](#refnote-NDTV_veteran_suggestions) After retirement from the service, they will have the opportunity to apply to continue in the armed forces. Upto 25 percent of the total strength of the retiring batch will be selected for the permanent cadre.[&#8202;](#ref-back-21-0)[\[21\]](#refnote-21) Personnel who retire after 4 years of service will not be eligible for pension, but will receive a lump sum amount of approximately [₹](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_rupee "Indian rupee")11.71 [lakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakh "Lakh") (US$12,000) at the end of tenure.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Panag-0)[\[22\]](#refnote-Panag)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Hindu_Editorial-1)[\[9\]](#refnote-Hindu_Editorial)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Hindu_veterans-0)[\[23\]](#refnote-Hindu_veterans) The Indian government plans to recruit 45,000 to 50,000 new personnel every year through this scheme.[&#8202;](#ref-back-BBC_protests-0)[\[24\]](#refnote-BBC_protests) In September 2022, 46,000 youngsters were recruited through the scheme.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Hindu_Editorial-2)[\[9\]](#refnote-Hindu_Editorial)

## Reservation and welfare benefits

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Reservation and welfare benefits")\]

On 17 July 2022, [Jammu and Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_(union_territory) "Jammu and Kashmir (union territory)")'s Lieutenant Governor [Manoj Sinha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manoj_Sinha "Manoj Sinha") announced a 10% reservation in [Jammu and Kashmir Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_Police "Jammu and Kashmir Police") service for those who retire after serving in the military under the Agnipath scheme.[&#8202;](#ref-back-25-0)[\[25\]](#refnote-25)

On 12 July 2024, [Central Industrial Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Industrial_Security_Force "Central Industrial Security Force"), [Border Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Security_Force "Border Security Force") and [Railway Protection Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railway_Protection_Force "Railway Protection Force") announced 10% reservation and age relaxation for former Agniveer soldiers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-26-0)[\[26\]](#refnote-26) The reservation is also applicable within [Central Armed Police Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Armed_Police_Forces "Central Armed Police Forces") and [Assam Rifles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam_Rifles "Assam Rifles").[&#8202;](#ref-back-27-0)[\[27\]](#refnote-27)

On 17 July 2024, [Government of Haryana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Haryana "Government of Haryana") announced 10% reservation in the police force of [Haryana Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haryana_Police "Haryana Police"), mining guards and [forest guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_ranger "Park ranger"). The state government also announced incentives for [private sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_sector "Private sector") for hiring agniveers. Age relaxation for government jobs in Group B and C categories was also announced along with a 5% reservation in Group C jobs. The state government has also interest benefits for businesses set up by retired military under the Agnipath scheme. The chief minister also promised priority in government departments, boards and corporation jobs.[&#8202;](#ref-back-28-0)[\[28\]](#refnote-28)[&#8202;](#ref-back-29-0)[\[29\]](#refnote-29)

On 26 July 2024, [Odisha Chief Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_chief_ministers_of_Odisha "List of chief ministers of Odisha") [Mohan Charan Majhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohan_Charan_Majhi "Mohan Charan Majhi") announced 10% reservation and a 5-year age relaxation for Agniveers in the state's uniformed services.[&#8202;](#ref-back-30-0)[\[30\]](#refnote-30)

On 4 November 2026, the Union Cabinet of India has extended two year additional engagement period for all the first batch Agniveer recruits, to join the armed forces as regular soldiers, subject to their willingness and fulfillment of selection criteria. [&#8202;](#ref-back-31-0)[\[31\]](#refnote-31)

## Criticism

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Criticism")\]

The scheme will not include long tenures, pensions, or other benefits that existed in the old system.[&#8202;](#ref-back-DNA_what_is-1)[\[5\]](#refnote-DNA_what_is) Individuals aspiring to join Armed Forces were disappointed with the rules of the new scheme. The main causes of concern were the short length of service, no pension provisions for those released early, and the 17.5 to 21-year age restriction. Many of the current aspirants were rendered ineligible to serve in the Indian armed forces under these new rules.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NDTV_protests-0)[\[32\]](#refnote-NDTV_protests)

Before the introduction of the new scheme for recruitment, [Indian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Modi_ministry "Second Modi ministry") did not produce any [white papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_paper "White paper"). The scheme was neither debated in the parliament nor in the [Parliamentary Standing Committee on Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_Committee_on_Defence_(India) "Standing Committee on Defence (India)"). No information about the scheme was given to the public prior to its announcement.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Sushant_Singh-1)[\[4\]](#refnote-Sushant_Singh)

### By political parties and activists

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: By political parties and activists")\]

_[The Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hindu "The Hindu")_ in its editorial, asked the scheme to be put on hold and stated[&#8202;](#ref-back-Hindu_Editorial-3)[\[9\]](#refnote-Hindu_Editorial) —

> "If a soldier's job is now turned into contractual employment, it might not motivate aspirants who hope for [job security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_security "Job security"), pension, and honour. The argument that defence should not be treated as an employment guarantee scheme is rhetorically impressive, but the fact is that from the manufacturing of arms to the employment of soldiers, the sector plays a critical role in the economy. For a country like India, where unemployment remains a major policy challenge, the concerns cannot be dismissed easily. The Government also faces the very credible charge that this is nothing more than a cost-cutting plan."

- Opposition parties in India have asked the scheme to be put on hold and that the scheme be discussed in the Parliament.[&#8202;](#ref-back-33-0)[\[33\]](#refnote-33)
- The [Congress party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_party "Congress party"), criticizing the new recruitment plan 'Agnipath' in the army as unjust towards the youth, stated that if it comes to power, it will scrap this scheme and reinstate the old recruitment process. This was stated by the party's president, [Mallikarjun Kharge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mallikarjun_Kharge "Mallikarjun Kharge").[&#8202;](#ref-back-34-0)[\[34\]](#refnote-34)
- The [Communist Party of India (Marxist)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_India_(Marxist) "Communist Party of India (Marxist)") CPI(M) stated that it strongly disapproved of "the 'Agnipath' scheme that does disservice to India's national interests. Professional armed forces cannot be raised by recruiting 'soldiers on contract' for a period of four years. This scheme, to save pension money, severely compromises the quality and efficiency of our professional armed forces."[&#8202;](#ref-back-left-0)[\[35\]](#refnote-left)
- The [Indian National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Congress "Indian National Congress") called the scheme neither in the interest of the country nor its security, and has asked for a rollback of the scheme.[&#8202;](#ref-back-36-0)[\[36\]](#refnote-36)
- [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Congress "Indian National Congress") leader and former [Finance Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Finance_(India) "Minister of Finance (India)") [P. Chidambaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._Chidambaram "P. Chidambaram") took a dig at _Agnipath_ scheme and said, "If you wish to be trained as a driver, washerman, or barber, become an _Agniveer_ If you wish to be trained as a _chowkidar_, become an _Agniveer_ If you wish to learn to fry _pakoras_, become an _Agniveer_ If you wish to become a soldier, do not apply."[&#8202;](#ref-back-37-0)[\[37\]](#refnote-37)
- Congress MP [Rahul Gandhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahul_Gandhi "Rahul Gandhi") said, "When India faces threats on two fronts, the uncalled for _Agnipath_ Scheme reduces the operational effectiveness of our armed forces. The BJP government must stop compromising the dignity, traditions, valour, and discipline of our forces."[&#8202;](#ref-back-38-0)[\[38\]](#refnote-38) On 27 June, he said "They used to talk of ' [One Rank, One Pension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Rank,_One_Pension "One Rank, One Pension"), now they have come up with 'no rank, no pension'." He further said that the [Chinese army (People's Liberation Army)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Liberation_Army "People's Liberation Army") is 'sitting on our land' and asserted that the army should be strengthened. "When there is a war results of this will be evident...they are weakening the army, it will harm the country and they call themselves nationalists. Just like the [farm laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Indian_agriculture_acts "2020 Indian agriculture acts") were repealed, the government will have to roll back the Agnipath scheme also."[&#8202;](#ref-back-39-0)[\[39\]](#refnote-39)
- Congress leader and [Territorial Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territorial_Army_(India) "Territorial Army (India)") [Captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_(armed_forces) "Captain (armed forces)") [Sachin Pilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachin_Pilot "Sachin Pilot") appealed to the central government not to show "stubbornness" and immediately withdraw the new army recruitment scheme 'Agnipath' for wider deliberation to avoid recurrence of the [agriculture law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Indian_agriculture_acts "2020 Indian agriculture acts") episode. ### Data on employment of veterans

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Data on employment of veterans")\]

Data from [Director General Resettlement, Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India "Government of India") \- the nodal body responsible for rehabilitating retired military personnel, shows that states, Central public sector units, including defence [PSUs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_sector_undertakings_in_India "Public sector undertakings in India"), and [Central Armed Police Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Armed_Police_Forces "Central Armed Police Forces") (CAPF) have failed to recruit against the vacancies reserved for veterans.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-0)[\[62\]](#refnote-:0)

According to a Department of Personnel & Training (DoPT) provision, 10 per cent of vacancies in Central government jobs in Group C and 20 per cent in Group D are reserved for veterans.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-1)[\[62\]](#refnote-:0)

For public sector banks, Central public sector units, and CAPF, the reservation is 14.5 and 24.5 per cent, respectively. But, as of June last year,\[ _[clarification needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Please_clarify "Wikipedia:Please clarify")_\] ex-servicemen constituted only 1.15% of the Group C strength and 0.3% of the Group D strength in 94 of the 170 CPSUs.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-2)[\[62\]](#refnote-:0)

The picture is more dismal if Central ministries are taken into consideration. Between 32 Central ministries, only 1.60 per cent of the 22,168 positions reserved for veterans have been filled. For instance, the Indian Railways, one of the world's largest employers, could only fill 1.4 per cent (16,264 out of the 1.15 million) positions reserved for retired personnel of the armed forces. For the ten defence PSUs, where the Centre has announced a 10 per cent reservation for the Agnipath scheme, veterans constituted only 3.45 per cent and 2.71 per cent of Group C and Group D posts, respectively. Even the paramilitary forces haven't shown much interest in hiring veterans.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-3)[\[62\]](#refnote-:0)

As of June 2021, only 0.62 per cent of positions reserved for veterans have been filled by the five wings of the paramilitary forces - [Border Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Security_Force "Border Security Force") (BSF), [Central Reserve Police Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Reserve_Police_Force "Central Reserve Police Force") (CRPF), [Indo-Tibetan Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Tibetan_Border_Police "Indo-Tibetan Border Police") (ITBP), [Sashastra Seema Bal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sashastra_Seema_Bal "Sashastra Seema Bal") (SSB), and [Central Industrial Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Industrial_Security_Force "Central Industrial Security Force") (CISF).[&#8202;](#ref-back-:0-4)[\[62\]](#refnote-:0)

## Protests

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Protests")\]

On 14 June 2022, soon after the announcement of the scheme, violent protests erupted in several states in India where the army aspirants angry with the new scheme called for its rollback and damaged public property including buses and trains.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NDTV_protests-1)[\[32\]](#refnote-NDTV_protests) This scheme evoked mixed reactions from army veterans mostly negative.[&#8202;](#ref-back-63-0)[\[63\]](#refnote-63)[&#8202;](#ref-back-64-0)[\[64\]](#refnote-64)

On 15 June 2022, the news of the protest was first reported from the state of Bihar where National highways and railway tracks were blocked by the protesters.[&#8202;](#ref-back-65-0)[\[65\]](#refnote-65)

On 16 June 2022, violence was reported at Chhapra, Jehanabad, Munger and Nawada in Bihar.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Highways_blocked-0)[\[66\]](#refnote-Highways_blocked) Army aspirants burnt down trains and buses. They set fire to train bogies in Kaimur and Chhapra districts, blocking train traffic in Siwan, Ara, Jehanabad, Nawada, Saharsha, Chhapra, and road traffic in some places.[&#8202;](#ref-back-67-0)[\[67\]](#refnote-67) Later the protests along with violent incidents were reported in Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Haryana and other states.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Highways_blocked-1)[\[66\]](#refnote-Highways_blocked) More than 200 trains were affected by the protests, 35 trains have been cancelled and 13 trains were terminated short of their destination.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NDTV_protests-2)[\[32\]](#refnote-NDTV_protests)

On 17 June, protesters attacked the house of the Deputy Chief Minister of Bihar, [Renu Devi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renu_Devi "Renu Devi").[&#8202;](#ref-back-68-0)[\[68\]](#refnote-68)[&#8202;](#ref-back-69-0)[\[69\]](#refnote-69) The mobs of young protesters went on a rampage and set fire to trains and other property after which [Bihar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bihar "Bihar") government ordered internet shutdowns in 18 districts. More than 325 people were arrested in Bihar and 250 in Uttar Pradesh.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Protests_18_June-0)[\[70\]](#refnote-Protests_18_June) 12 trains were set on fire, and the movement of 300 trains were affected. 214 trains were cancelled, 11 trains were diverted and 90 were terminated short of their destination.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NDTV_12_trains-1)[\[44\]](#refnote-NDTV_12_trains) At least 1 person was killed in the protests.[&#8202;](#ref-back-auto-0)[\[71\]](#refnote-auto)

A 19 year old protester was shot dead in Telangana.[&#8202;](#ref-back-72-0)[\[72\]](#refnote-72) Internet was shut down in 12 districts of Bihar.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Navy_Chief-0)[\[73\]](#refnote-Navy_Chief) In the BJP-ruled Haryana massive protest occurred in which the angry protesters threw stones at vehicles in Ballabhgarh, squatted on railway tracks to stop train movement in Jind and burnt tyres in Rohtak. In Uttar Pradesh hundreds of angry young protesters carried bamboo sticks and stones and stormed railway premises in several cities, blocked highways and targeted private vehicles.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Protests_18_June-1)[\[70\]](#refnote-Protests_18_June)

On 18 June, protesters in Bihar called for a strike against the scheme. They clashed with the police and set fire to several vehicles since morning in Bihar.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Protests_18_June-2)[\[70\]](#refnote-Protests_18_June) Train services were stopped in Bihar till 8 pm on 18 June. They will be stopped again from 4 am onwards on 19 June. More than 350 trains were cancelled across India as the violence continued in several states.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Train_18_June-0)[\[74\]](#refnote-Train_18_June)

[Chief Minister of Rajasthan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_Rajasthan "Chief Minister of Rajasthan") [Ashok Gehlot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashok_Gehlot "Ashok Gehlot") leading the State Council of Ministers passed a unanimous resolution calling for the withdrawal of the scheme.[&#8202;](#ref-back-75-0)[\[75\]](#refnote-75) In [Kerala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala "Kerala") a large protest march was held in [Thiruvananthapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram "Thiruvananthapuram") and [Kozhikode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kozhikode "Kozhikode"), against the scheme.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Train_18_June-1)[\[74\]](#refnote-Train_18_June) The protesters said their strikes would continue till the Centre rolls back its decision and ensured justice.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Protests_18_June-3)[\[70\]](#refnote-Protests_18_June)

Among the states, the worst incidents of violence occurred in Bihar, where the protesters set trains on fire, vandalised railway stations, and attacked BJP leaders' offices, homes, and cars. Two BJP MPs and eight BJP legislators (including two Deputy Chief Ministers) were given the third-highest level 'Y' Category security by the central government after approval by [Narendra Modi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narendra_Modi "Narendra Modi").[&#8202;](#ref-back-BJP_office-0)[\[76\]](#refnote-BJP_office)

Student organization like [Students' Federation of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Students'_Federation_of_India "Students' Federation of India") (SFI) organised took massive rallies and protests in several parts of country against the alleged anti youth scheme. There were protests in Delhi, UP, Haryana, Rajasthan and other parts of the country. SFI along with [Democratic Youth Federation of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Youth_Federation_of_India "Democratic Youth Federation of India") have called for a protests at Jantar mantar on 19 June.[&#8202;](#ref-back-77-0)[\[77\]](#refnote-77)[&#8202;](#ref-back-78-0)[\[78\]](#refnote-78)

He also said that five trains, 60 coaches and 11 engines have been burnt, railway is preparing a full report for the damage caused to property.[&#8202;](#ref-back-79-0)[\[79\]](#refnote-79) In Karnataka, Police [lathi-charged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_charge "Baton charge") _Agnipath_ protesters in [Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka "Karnataka")'s [Dharwad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharwad "Dharwad"). Anti-'Agnipath' protests were also reported from other parts of Karnataka, such as [Gokak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gokak "Gokak") and [Belgaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgaum "Belgaum") districts.[&#8202;](#ref-back-80-0)[\[80\]](#refnote-80) Patna [District magistrate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_magistrate "District magistrate") said 23 FIRs were registered and 147 arrests were made. [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Congress "Indian National Congress") leader [Kanhaiya Kumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanhaiya_Kumar "Kanhaiya Kumar") called the new military recruitment scheme, "a scam" but added that the protests against it should be peaceful.[&#8202;](#ref-back-82-0)[\[82\]](#refnote-82)

### _Bharat Bandh_ (All India Strike)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Bharat Bandh (All India Strike)")\]

On 20 June, a country wide strike named _Bharat Bandh_ was called by the protesting organisations demanding a roll back of the scheme. The call was made on social media without naming any organisation.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Tribune_20_Jun-2)[\[43\]](#refnote-Tribune_20_Jun) More than 600 trains were cancelled due to the strike. The [Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi "Delhi")- [Gurgaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurgaon "Gurgaon") Expressway saw heavy traffic jams following a call for Bharat Bandh against the recruitment scheme. In [Faridabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faridabad "Faridabad") and [Noida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noida "Noida"), prohibitory orders had been imposed that prohibit the assembly of four or more persons. Massive jams was seen on the expressway near the [Sarhaul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarhaul "Sarhaul") border as [Delhi Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Police "Delhi Police") checked vehicles. Internet services were shut down in more than 20 districts of Bihar[&#8202;](#ref-back-83-0)[\[83\]](#refnote-83)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Facts_20_June-0)[\[84\]](#refnote-Facts_20_June)

In [Jharkhand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jharkhand "Jharkhand"), all schools were closed and heavy security was deployment in state on calls for a bandh.[&#8202;](#ref-back-85-0)[\[85\]](#refnote-85) In [Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka "Karnataka"), holiday was announced for 75 educational institutions on the wake [Prime Minister Narendra Modi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narendra_Modi "Narendra Modi")'s visit to the state. Some attributed it to security concerns due to the ongoing protests and calls for a bandh.[&#8202;](#ref-back-86-0)[\[86\]](#refnote-86)[&#8202;](#ref-back-87-0)[\[87\]](#refnote-87) [Section 144](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Section_144 "Section 144") was imposed in some parts of the state of [Rajasthan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan "Rajasthan"), [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab,_India "Punjab, India"), [Uttar Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttar_Pradesh "Uttar Pradesh") and [Haryana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haryana "Haryana").[&#8202;](#ref-back-88-0)[\[88\]](#refnote-88)[&#8202;](#ref-back-89-0)[\[89\]](#refnote-89) In [Jammu and Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_(union_territory) "Jammu and Kashmir (union territory)"), [Aam Aadmi Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aam_Aadmi_Party "Aam Aadmi Party") leader [Mehraj Malik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehraj_Malik "Mehraj Malik") put under house arrest.[&#8202;](#ref-back-90-0)[\[90\]](#refnote-90) [Government of Bihar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Bihar "Government of Bihar") suspended Internet in 20 districts amid protests. Meanwhile, it enhanced the securities of [BJP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharatiya_Janata_Party "Bharatiya Janata Party") offices in 11 districts. ## Plea in the Supreme Court against the scheme

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Plea in the Supreme Court against the scheme")\]

3 pleas were filed in the [Supreme Court of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_India "Supreme Court of India") in June by Advocates [M. L. Sharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manohar_Lal_Sharma "Manohar Lal Sharma"), Vishal Tiwari and Harsh Ajay Singh against the Agnipath scheme.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Plea_21_June-0)[\[111\]](#refnote-Plea_21_June) Sharma alleged that the government has quashed the century-old selection process for the Armed forces which is contrary to the constitutional provisions and without having parliamentary approval.

A plea, filed in the apex court by Advocate M. L. Sharma, has sought to quash the 14 June notification/press note claiming it to be illegal and unconstitutional.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:1-0)[\[112\]](#refnote-:1)[&#8202;](#ref-back-113-0)[\[113\]](#refnote-113) M. L. Sharma alleged that on 14 June 2022, contrary to the constitutional provisions and without having approval in parliament and without any gazette notification, the Centre tinkered with the century-old Army selection process and imposed the scheme for recruitment in all three Armed forces divisions.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:1-1)[\[112\]](#refnote-:1) The plea also referred to the protest all over the country against the scheme.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:1-2)[\[112\]](#refnote-:1) The plea has also sought directions to the Centre and Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Haryana, and Rajasthan governments to submit a status report on the violent protests.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:1-3)[\[112\]](#refnote-:1) It has sought a direction to set up an expert committee under the chairmanship of a retired apex court judge to examine the scheme and its impact on national security and Army.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:1-4)[\[112\]](#refnote-:1)[&#8202;](#ref-back-114-0)[\[114\]](#refnote-114)

On 6 July, a fresh plea was filed in the Supreme Court by ex-servicemen Ravindra Singh Shekhawat. He has sought quashing of notification issued by the Ministry of Defence, contending it as illegal, unconstitutional, and ultra vires to the rights guaranteed under the Constitution. He said, "The recently launched scheme has received skepticism nationwide from the armed forces aspirants and has left several questions unanswered. Some of the concerns are related to the aspirants who were in the middle/final stages of their recruitment process.This abrupt change in the recruitment process has evoked several unforeseeable circumstances for the aspirants and has put their futures at stake/in the dark. The scheme fails to accommodate the individuals who have been preparing for the armed forces for the last many years and have not been able to participate in the recruitment process due to lack of vacancies due to [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19 "COVID-19")," the plea said. Plea also sought direction to initiate a ' [Pilot project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilot_project "Pilot project")', to study the cause and effect of the scheme, and the impact of the newly introduced training process on combat effectiveness, operational readiness, and defence preparedness.[&#8202;](#ref-back-115-0)[\[115\]](#refnote-115)

On the same day, a petition was also moved before the [Delhi High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_High_Court "Delhi High Court") by various candidates shortlisted as [Airmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airman "Airman") in [Indian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Air_Force "Indian Air Force") seeking issuance of enrolment list and completion of previous recruitment as per a 2019 notification, without being affected by Centre's new Agnipath recruitment scheme for armed forces. A division bench consisting of Justice Suresh Kumar Kait and Justice Saurabh Banarjee deferred the petition for two weeks in view of the pendency of a similar matter before the Supreme Court. Filed through [Advocate Prashant Bhushan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prashant_Bhushan "Prashant Bhushan"), the plea has been moved by 20 candidates awaiting their enrolment as Airmen in Indian Air Force in Group "X" trades (except education instructor trade) and Group "Y" trades (except automobile technician), Indian Air Force (Security), Indian Air Force (Police) and musician trades.[&#8202;](#ref-back-116-0)[\[116\]](#refnote-116)

## First Agniveer batch (2023)

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: First Agniveer batch (2023)")\]

One of the oldest and the largest training centers of Indian Army, the Nashik Road-based Artillery Centre is one of the venues approved by army for training the youths recruited as Agniveers. The first batch consisting of 5000 Agniveers will undergo 31-week rigorous training at the Artiliery Centre, of which first 10 weeks will be for basic military training, whereas the balance 21 weeks will be for advanced training programmes. The Agniveers will be divided in to four disciplines- drivers, gunners, radio operators and technical assistants, on the basis of their aptitude and skill.[&#8202;](#ref-back-117-0)[\[117\]](#refnote-117)

The state of the art Agniveer Training Facility located at [Jammu and Kashmir Light Infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_Light_Infantry "Jammu and Kashmir Light Infantry")(JAKLI) Regiment Center, Dhansal of [Udhampur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udhampur_district "Udhampur district") was visited by General Officer Commanding-in- Chief (GOC-in-C) Northern Command, to review the preparedness and arrangement for the first batch.[&#8202;](#ref-back-118-0)[\[118\]](#refnote-118)

[Indian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Armed_Forces "Indian Armed Forces") will recruit 46,000 Agniveers in 2023 including 40,000 in the [Indian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Army "Indian Army").[&#8202;](#ref-back-119-0)[\[119\]](#refnote-119) The remaining 3,000 Agniveers each will be inducted into Indian Navy and Indian Air Force.[&#8202;](#ref-back-120-0)[\[120\]](#refnote-120) About 19,000 personnel below officer rank (PBOR) recruited as Agniveers of the Indian Army started training at nearly 40 centres across India from January 2023, while second batch 21,000 Agniveers will start training in March 2023.[&#8202;](#ref-back-121-0)[\[121\]](#refnote-121) 25% of Agniveers will be retained by the Indian Army at the end of a four-year tour of duty (ToD) to serve out their full tenure of 17 more years, while the remaining will be relieved.[&#8202;](#ref-back-122-0)[\[122\]](#refnote-122)

As of May 2024, two batches of 40,000 have completed training under the Army and 20,000 more are being trained since November 2023. Further, three batches of 7,385 Agniveer Navy and two batches of 4,955 Agniveer Vayu (Air Force) have completed training.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:4-0)[\[123\]](#refnote-:4)[&#8202;](#ref-back-:5-0)[\[124\]](#refnote-:5)

On 1 June 2024, another batch of 2,614 Agniveer Vayu (Air Force) trainees completed their training from Airmen Training School.[&#8202;](#ref-back-125-0)[\[125\]](#refnote-125) In June 2024, third batch of 2,500 Agniveers (914 from [Madras Regimental Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_Regiment#Madras_Regimental_Centre_and_Records "Madras Regiment"), 560 from [Maratha Light Infantry Regimental Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maratha_Light_Infantry#Regimental_Centre "Maratha Light Infantry"), 624 from [Punjab Regimental Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Regiment_(India)#Regimental_Centre "Punjab Regiment (India)"), 402 from [Ladakh Scouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladakh_Scouts "Ladakh Scouts")) completed their 31-week training and joined the Army.[&#8202;](#ref-back-126-0)[\[126\]](#refnote-126)[&#8202;](#ref-back-127-0)[\[127\]](#refnote-127)[&#8202;](#ref-back-128-0)[\[128\]](#refnote-128)[&#8202;](#ref-back-129-0)[\[129\]](#refnote-129)

In July 2024, reports suggested that around one lakh Agniveers are recruited in the army and about 70,000 were deployed. A vacancy of 50,000 Agniveers were reported for 2024-25 and recruitment procedure is on.[&#8202;](#ref-back-130-0)[\[130\]](#refnote-130)

By August 2024, three batches of around 2,500 Agniveer Navy sailors graduated from INS [Chilika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilika_Lake "Chilika Lake") training facility. The fourth batch of 1,429 sailors graduated from the same on 10 August 2024.[&#8202;](#ref-back-131-0)[\[131\]](#refnote-131)

## Amendment in recruitment procedure

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Agnipath_Scheme&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Amendment in recruitment procedure")\]

Army recruitment procedure of Agniveers is set to change. Army has decided to conduct online Common Entrance Exam (CEE) before the physical recruitment. Approximately 200 centers across the country have been identified for the conduct of first online CEE. Online applications for registration will be available for one month from mid February 2023 for the exam scheduled in April 2023. The amended recruitment process aims at cognitive aspect and reduction of large crowds during recruitment rallies resulting in better management and easier conduct.[&#8202;](#ref-back-139-0)[\[139\]](#refnote-139)

In May 2024, reports emerged about a survey that was conducted by Indian Army. It was reported that some changes in the scheme may be proposed to the government that will be formed after [2024 Indian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Indian_general_election "2024 Indian general election"). The survey, consisting of 10 questions, has been sent to the 'stakeholders' concerned. By the end of May, each group's responses to the particular questions will be compiled and evaluated further. According to the report, recruiters will also be required to reply to inquiries regarding the scheme's overall effect on Army recruiting following its implementation. The training staff at regimental centres, unit and sub-unit commanders of the Agniveers were also surveyed. The recruiters shall be asked about the impact of the scheme after int implementation. Along with observations on the Agniveers' training and general educational standards, the Army's training personnel at the regiment centres is expected to offer details on the Agniveers' physical standards. It is also expected of the staff to respond to different aspects of training and the influence of competition for permanent absorption in the Army on the overall mindset of the Agniveers.[&#8202;](#ref-back-:4-1)[\[123\]](#refnote-:4)[&#8202;](#ref-back-:5-1)[\[124\]](#refnote-:5)
