# 香港立法会：2024年公共收入保障（烟草税）令法律意见

立法會 Legislative Council LC Paper No. LS21/2024 Paper for the House Committee Meeting on 22 March 2024 Legal Service Division Report on Dutiable Commodities (Amendment) Bill 2024 I. SUMMARY 1. The Bill The Bill seeks to amend Part II of Schedule 1 to the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance (Cap. 109) to increase the rates of duty on various types of tobacco by about 31.92% to give effect to the proposal in the 2024-2025 Budget Speech delivered by the Financial Secretary on 28 February 2024 with effect from 11:00 am on 28 February 2024. 2. Public Consultation According to the Legislative Council Brief, the proposal has been formulated after taking into account views received from Members and other parties concerned during the Budget consultation process. 3. Consultation with LegCo Panel The Panel on Health Services has not been consulted specifically on the Bill. 4. Conclusion No difficulties have been identified in relation to the legal and drafting aspects of the Bill. Members may consider whether a Bills Committee should be formed to study the policy aspects of the Bill. - 2 - II. REPORT The date of First Reading of the Bill is 20 March 2024. Members may refer to the Legislative Council (“LegCo”) Brief (File Ref.: HHB CR 1/4041/24) issued by the Health Bureau on 4 March 2024 for further details. Object of the Bill 2. The Bill seeks to amend the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance (Cap. 109) to increase the rates of duty on various types of tobacco to give effect to the proposal in the 2024-2025 Budget Speech delivered by the Financial Secretary on 28 February 2024 (“Budget Speech”) with effect from 11:00 am on 28 February 2024. Provisions of the Bill 3. The Bill proposes to amend Part II of Schedule 1 to Cap. 109 to increase the rates of duty on various types of tobacco by about 31.92% as follows: Tobacco products Original rates (HK$) Proposed rates (HK$) (a) for each 1 000 cigarettes 2,506 3,306 (b) cigars 3,228/kg 4,258/kg (c) Chinese prepared tobacco 615/kg 811/kg (d) all other manufactured tobacco except tobacco intended for the manufacture of cigarettes 3,036/kg 4,005/kg 4. Tobacco duty was last increased in 2023. As stated in paragraph 13 of the LegCo Brief, the proposed increase is to sustain the effectiveness of tobacco duty as a tobacco control measure. According to paragraph 5 of the LegCo Brief, as of November 2023, tobacco duty accounted for about 64.52% of the retail price of cigarettes in Hong Kong, which is far below the proportion of 75% recommended by the World Health Organization. It was proposed in paragraph 193 of the Budget Speech that the duty on cigarettes be increased by 80 cents per stick (i.e. an increase of HK$16 per pack)1 and the duties on other tobacco products be increased at the same rate with immediate effect. According to the Administration, this would bring the proportion of tobacco duty to retail price of cigarettes to 70.58%,2 under the phased approach to increasing tobacco duty that takes into consideration the Government’s commitment to deter smoking and the public receptiveness. 1 According to the table under paragraph 14 of the LegCo Brief, the average retail price of a pack of cigarettes would increase from HK$77.68 to HK$93.68. 2 As stated in the table under paragraph 14 of the LegCo Brief. - 3 - Public Revenue Protection (Duty on Tobacco) Order 2024 (L.N. 25 of 2024) 5. On the day of the Budget Speech (i.e. 28 February 2024), the Chief Executive, after consultation with the Executive Council, made the Public Revenue Protection (Duty on Tobacco) Order 2024 (L.N. 25 of 2024) (“Order”) under section 2 of the Public Revenue Protection Ordinance (Cap. 120), which is a temporary measure to give immediate effect to the proposed increase of tobacco duty, i.e. from 11:00 am on 28 February 2024. The Schedule to the Order contains a proposed bill to amend Cap. 109, which is identical to the Bill. Members may refer to our report on the Order (LC Paper No. LS12/2024) for further details of the Order. Under section 5(2) of Cap. 120, the Order shall expire and cease to be in force upon the Bill becoming law or upon expiration of four months from the day on which the Order came into force, whichever first happens. Commencement 6. The Bill, if passed, would be deemed to have come into operation at 11:00 am on 28 February 2024. This would bring the commencement of the enacted Ordinance retrospectively to the commencement of the Order. Public Consultation 7. According to paragraph 24 of the LegCo Brief, the above legislative proposal has been formulated after taking into account views received from Members and other parties concerned during the Budget consultation process. Consultation with LegCo Panel 8. As advised by the Clerk to the Panel on Health Services, the Panel has not been consulted specifically on the Bill. Nevertheless, the Panel was briefed on the latest development of the Administration’s tobacco control measures and the public consultation on tobacco control strategies in July 2023. While the Panel generally supported the Administration’s tobacco control policies, individual members were concerned that an increase in tobacco duty might exploit the grassroots’ right to choose, lead to more illicit tobacco activities and the sale of low-quality cigarettes in Hong Kong. There was also a view that tobacco control measures should not be too extreme, and the Administration should examine how to balance the interests of various parties. - 4 - Conclusion 9. No difficulties have been identified in relation to the legal and drafting aspects of the Bill. Members may consider whether a Bills Committee should be formed to study the policy aspects of the Bill. Prepared by Yvonne WONG Assistant Legal Adviser Legislative Council Secretariat 20 March 2024
