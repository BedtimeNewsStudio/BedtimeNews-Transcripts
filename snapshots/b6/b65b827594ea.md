# 2025 Trends & Forecasts

Published: February 4, 2025

##### Key Takeaways:

- [Iconic Tower, Cairo, 393.8 meters, was the tallest completion of 2024 and became the first supertall building on the African continent.](#tallest2024)
- [China continues to dominate with 97 out of 138 completions in 2024.](#countries)
- [Buildings in Egypt and Türkiye joined the World’s 100 Tallest for the first time.](#new100tallest)
- [The total number of buildings 200 meters and higher (2,597) has doubled since 2017.](#double200m)
- [A record number of stalled projects portends a lower number of completions in 2025.](#predictions)

# After 2023 set a new tall building record with 184 completions, 2024 saw a steep 25 percent drop-off, with 138 buildings of 200 meters or greater in height completing.

Globally, the world has completed a total of 2,597 two-hundred-meter-plus (200m+) buildings and 258 supertall (300m+) buildings through 2024. The number of 200m+ buildings worldwide has now doubled since 2017, and another 396 are currently under construction.

_NOTE: This report reflects completion statistics as of the end of 2024. Data will be continuously updated as CTBUH confirms more building completions throughout 2025. The last building to be updated on this page was on November 5, 2026._

The tallest building to complete in 2024 was [Iconic Tower](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/iconic-tower/34420), in Cairo, at 393.8 meters. This is the first time that Cairo has completed the tallest building of the year, and it nearly triples Cairo’s previous tallest building height record (last set in 1994 by the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/ministry-of-foreign-affairs/4226) building, 143 meters).

# The 138 completions in 2024, while significantly lower than the prior year, still mark the 11th year in a row with over 100 such completions.

#### Predictions for 2025

[Back to top](#top)

As CTBUH surveys the tall building landscape for 2025, there are a record number of 200m+ projects on hold globally (113). Of these, 86 are in China, where curtailed financing has led to the high-profile difficulties for real estate developers in the past two years. As many of these projects have made substantial upward progress, they will likely complete eventually, but the outlook for 2025 thus far is characteristic of 2024—a decrease in overall completions from the peak in 2023.

There are 396 buildings over 200 meters that are currently under construction or topped-out, and 83 of those are supertalls. One megatall (600 meters or taller) building, [Jeddah Tower](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/jeddah-tower/2), 1,000 meters, restarted construction late in 2024, and is approximately one-third complete. Given the growing number of stalled tall building projects, CTBUH conservatively estimates at least 135 buildings of 200 meters and higher to complete in 2025; of these, we expect between 12 and 20 to be supertalls.
