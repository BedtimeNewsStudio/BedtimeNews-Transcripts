## Organization Chart

The Organization of Turkic States (OTS) is an intergovernmental organization dedicated to fostering comprehensive cooperation among Turkic States. The Organization was established with the siging of Nakhchivan Agreement in 2009.

The member states of the OTS are **Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Türkiye, and Uzbekistan**. The organization has also granted observer status to **Hungary (2018), Turkmenistan (2021), the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (2022), and the Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO) (2023).**

**Principal organs of Organization of Turkic States**

» Council of Heads of State

» Council of Foreign Ministers

» Council of Elders


» Senior Officials Committee

» The Secretariat


**Operation**

Organization's main decisive body is the **Council of Heads of States**, which is chaired by the country assuming the Chairmanship-in-office (rotation according to the alphabetical order). **The current chair is Azerbaijan.** The activities of the Organization are coordinated and supported by its **Secretariat** located in Istanbul.

**The Council of Elders** serves as the permanent consultative body within the Organization of Turkic States, embodying a revered tradition of seeking counsel and wisdom from esteemed ancestors—an integral practice within Turkic cultures. At the 8th Summit in Istanbul, there was a collective effort to enhance the Council's effectiveness and maximize its potential. The regulations governing the Council were consequently renewed by Heads of State, reflecting a commitment to leveraging the vast experience of its elder members. Mr. Binali Yıldırım, the Elder of Türkiye was appointed as the Chairman of this esteemed body.

The **Organization of Turkic States** also functions as an umbrella organization for existing cooperation mechanisms such as the International Organization of Turkic Culture (TURKSOY), the Parliamentary Assembly of the Turkic States (TURKPA), International Turkic Academy, Turkic Culture and Heritage Foundation,  Turkic Chamber of Commerce and Industry and the Turkic Investment Fund.
