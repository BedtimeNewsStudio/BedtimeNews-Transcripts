# Salaries in Germany in 2023: The StepStone Salary Report

February 22, 2023 [Salary insights press releases](https://www.thestepstonegroup.com/english/newsroom/press-releases/category/salary-insights/)

![](https://www.thestepstonegroup.com/wp-content/uploads/people-in-discussion-in-a-breakout-session-or-coffee-break-conference-meeting_press-releases_photo_the-stepstone-group-1156x650.jpg)

- Doctors earn the most, hotel and hospitality industry employees the least
- The gross median salary in Germany is €43,800
- StepStone analyzes more than 560,000 current salaries to create greater pay transparency in the job market
- The salary report, salary planner, and salary ranges in job ads on StepStone.de provide guidance for jobseekers

**DÜSSELDORF, February 22, 2023** **—** Doctors are still Germany’s highest earners, while employees in the hotel and hospitality industry are at the bottom end of the salary scale. The 2023 Salary Report by StepStone, the leading online recruitment platform, also revealed that the gross median salary of German employees is currently €43,800. Median means that the number of employees earning above or below this figure is exactly the same.

There are clear regional salary differences, including an East-West gap of 15%. Hamburg and Baden-Württemberg top the table, while Saxony-Anhalt languishes at the bottom. The median salary for men is around €46,000 and for women around €40,000, which equates to a gender pay gap of 13.1%. The adjusted pay gap between the sexes is 6.8%. For the report, StepStone analyzed more than 560,000 salaries from one of the largest salary databases in Germany. The datasets are representative in terms of age, gender, educational attainment, and geographical distribution across Germany’s federal states.

Although salary is one of the most important criteria in an applicant’s job search\[1\], it is still a taboo subject in Germany. “Salary transparency is a crucial factor when it comes to equal pay,” says Dr. Tobias Zimmermann, salary expert at StepStone. “Especially when the labor market is tight, openness about salaries is an important strategic lever in the competition for the best talent.”

In addition to the salary report, StepStone uses its data and machine-learning expertise to provide applicants with data-driven salary forecasts and to include salary ranges in its job listings. The aim is to use digital solutions to make the application process quicker, fairer, and simpler. “As a tech company, we analyze thousands of salaries every month and use this information to help jobseekers and employers find their best possible match. Our data shows that we are meeting this demand: Salary information is one of the biggest drivers of interaction with the job ads we publish,” explains Zimmermann. Job listings that include this information attract up to 10% more applications on StepStone.

## The key findings of the salary report at a glance:

### Professional groups: Doctors earn the most

- Doctors top the salary table. With a gross median salary of €93,800, they earn more than double the average.
- They are followed by management consultants (€54,000), engineers (€52,600), and IT professionals (€52,000).
- Employees in the hotel and hospitality industries earn least in a comparison of professional groups (€35,700).

### Sectors: Banking sector pays the highest salaries

- Employees in the banking sector (€57,600) and the aerospace industry (€56,200) are particularly high earners. The pharmaceuticals industry comes in at third place with a gross median salary of €54,800.
- At the bottom are the manual trades (€37,500), forestry and agriculture, fishing and horticulture (€36,100), and hospitality (€34,200).
- Salary increases with company size: While employers with up to 50 employees pay a gross median salary of €38,200, employees at large companies (> 5,000 employees) earn 41% more at €53,700.

### Gender pay gap: Men earn around 13% more

- At an average of €46,008, men earn 13.1% more than women (€40,000).
- But if we exclude the impact of factors such as age, sector, profession, education, professional experience, company size, federal state, city, and staff responsibility on the pay of men and women, the adjusted gender pay gap is 6.8%.

### Region: Salaries in Hamburg and Baden-Württemberg are around 10% above the average; the gap between East and West is 15%

- Western federal states lead the ranking: Hamburg has the highest average salary at €48,100. Baden-Württemberg is second with €48,000, followed by Hessen at €47,800. Thuringia (€36,600), Mecklenburg–Vorpommern (€36,200), and Saxony-Anhalt (€36,100) bring up the rear.
- Overall, the East-West gap is 15% (excluding Berlin), with an average salary of €38,700 in eastern Germany and €45,500 in the west.
- Stuttgart leads the city ranking with €54,100, followed by Frankfurt am Main (€54,000) and Karlsruhe (€49,600).

### Education: Academic qualifications and staff responsibility give a significant boost in salary

- Those with university or college degrees tend to earn higher gross salaries: Graduates earn €58,600, around 41% more than their colleagues without a degree (€41,500).
- Managers earn almost 20% more: At €50,100, the salary of employees with responsibility for other staff is significantly higher than that of employees without staff responsibility (€41,100).
- Professional experience has a positive impact on salary: While people at the beginning of their professional careers earn an average of €35,000, this rises to €49,300 after more than 11 years on the job.

Salary figures have been rounded up in the press release for better readability.

## About the 2023 StepStone Salary Report

The 2023 StepStone Salary Report draws on data from one of Germany’s largest salary information databases with data segmented by city and region, professional group, sector, professional experience, and much more. The collection and evaluation methodology used for this year’s report is different to that of the 2022 report. The salary information is now even more precise. Changes include a new weighting that ensures data is representative in terms of age, gender, university degree, and geographical distribution across the federal states. The information and salaries contained in the 2023 salary report are therefore not directly comparable with those in the 2022 report.

## About the database and methodology

The salary report is based on an analysis of 561,761 salary datasets collected between January 2021 and November 2022. Of these, 69% relate to men and 31% to women. The proportion of employees with staff responsibility is 32%. All salary data is stated in euros and refers to the median, unless otherwise specified. All salary figures refer to gross annual salary including bonuses, commission etc. They are based on salary information collected from full-time employees. The information is representative of the working population at national and regional level based on the criteria of age, gender, and educational attainment.

The report is based on the salary data in the StepStone database. Unlike previous salary reports, the information on the Gehalt.de portal is no longer collected directly, but via the StepStone salary planner. The data is collected via the StepStone salary planner on StepStone.de and scientifically analyzed. All data has been checked by our compensation consultants and weighted.

## What is the median salary? And how does it differ from the average salary?

The average is calculated by adding up all values and then dividing by the number of data records. The average value can be influenced by extremely high or low values. A comparison using the median therefore helps to better classify the average value. The median is the value that lies exactly in the middle of all the values. This means that the number of salaries that are lower and higher than the median salary is exactly the same.

## Gender pay gap

The stated gender pay gap describes the unadjusted pay gap, which mixes in many other parameters relevant to pay, such as professional experience and educational background. The unadjusted value compares all male salaries against all female salaries. It represents the difference in annual salary between men and women expressed as a percentage of the male annual salary.

However, many of the differences between men’s and women’s salary are due to other factors (such as age, sector, profession, education, professional experience, company size, federal state and city, and staff responsibility). Once these factors are taken into account, we get the adjusted gender pay gap. This can be understood as the salary difference between two people who are identical in every respect apart from gender.
