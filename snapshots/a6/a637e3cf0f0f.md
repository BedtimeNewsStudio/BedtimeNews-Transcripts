### History

- -

- Nov. 2005

- Launch of National Computing & Information Agency in Daejeon city


- Nov. 2007

- Launch of National Computing & Information Agency in Gwangju city


- Mar. 2016

- Designated as an Executive Agency (Facility Management Type)


- May 2016

- Established Daegu Center Promotion Team


- Jul. 2017

- Change of Name

National Computing & Information Agency(NCIA)

  --\> National Information Resources Service(NIRS)


- Apr. 2019

- Selected as the Best-Performing Executive Agency


- Apr. 2019

- Backup Center Construction Commenced


- Apr. 2022

- Cloud-Dedicated Daegu Center Construction Completed

(Commencement of Construction on September 2019)


- Jun. 2023

- Launch of NIRS Daegu Center


- Jun. 2026

- Launch of NIRS Backup Center (Disaster Recovery System Division)


- Oct. 2026

- Revocation of Executive Agency Status
