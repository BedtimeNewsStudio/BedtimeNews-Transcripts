You might know and love [Prime](https://www.aboutamazon.com/what-we-do/prime) for its fast, free delivery, access to exclusive deals and discounts year-round and during events like [Prime Day](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-prime-day-2026-date), and extensive streaming options with Prime Video—but those benefits barely scratch the surface of all the value that [comes with Prime](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/lesser-known-amazon-prime-benefits).

Prime provides the best value because it bundles savings, convenience, and entertainment, all in a single membership, offering a variety of benefits and perks for members.

What delivery benefits does Prime include?

Fast, free delivery. In the U.S., more than 300 million items are available with free Prime shipping, and tens of millions of the most popular items are available with free [Same-Day or One-Day Delivery](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/amazon-prime/amazon-same-day-delivery). Amazon delivered at our [fastest speeds ever](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-prime-same-day-next-day-delivery-2025) for Prime members globally in 2025 and delivered more than 13 billion items the same or next day. Prime members worldwide also saved nearly $105 billion on fast, free delivery. In the U.S., that means Prime members saved on average over $550 on their deliveries last year—nearly four times the cost of an annual membership fee.

Ultra-fast delivery. Prime members also enjoy added convenience with discounts on 1-hour and 2-hour delivery on fresh groceries and everyday essentials from Whole Foods Market and Amazon Fresh, [1-hour and 3-hour delivery](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-fast-delivery-orders) on over 90,000 items available on Amazon.com in select cities and towns, and ultra-fast delivery in about [30 minutes or less](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-now-30-minute-delivery) on everyday essentials and more with Amazon Now.

What entertainment benefits comes with Prime?

![Amazon Prime benefits](https://assets.aboutamazon.com/dims4/default/dcd475d/2147483647/strip/true/crop/1600x900+0+0/resize/767x431!/quality/90/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fassets.aboutamazon.com%2F82%2F07%2Ff279a766464384e26413ad4f724b%2Fevery-benefit-with-prime-inline-streaming.jpg)

Prime Video. [Prime Video](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/what-you-need-to-know-about-prime-video) is a first-stop entertainment destination offering customers a vast collection of premium programming in one app available across thousands of devices. Prime members can customize their viewing experience and find their favorite movies, series, and live sports—including [Amazon MGM Studios](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/tag/mgm)-produced series and movies; licensed fan favorites; and programming from [Prime Video subscriptions](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/what-are-prime-video-channels). Customers can also go behind the scenes of their favorite movies and series with exclusive [X-Ray](https://www.amazon.com/salp/xray) access. Learn more on our [Prime Video page](http://www.amazon.com/primevideo).

Amazon Music. Prime members get access to over [100 million songs](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/what-is-amazon-music) in shuffle mode, All-Access Playlists, plus the largest catalog of top ad-free podcasts, included with their membership.

Amazon Music Unlimited. As a Prime member, you can upgrade to Amazon Music Unlimited for unlimited, on-demand access to over 100 million songs, as well as millions of songs in HD, Ultra HD, and spatial audio for $10.99 per month for a monthly subscription, or $109 per year for an annual subscription. Non-Prime customers pay $11.99 per month.

Cloud Gaming with Luna. [Amazon Luna](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/what-is-amazon-luna) provides access to an evolving library of more than 50 games, including GameNight's collection of more than 25 cloud-playable, family-friendly games. Also enjoy a rotating collection of [PC games](https://amzn.to/4j3vZMA) for download, and a monthly channel subscription on Twitch.tv.

Amazon Photos. Prime members get unlimited [full-resolution photo storage](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/amazon-prime/how-to-use-amazon-photos-storage) along with 5 GB of video storage. You can easily share your photos with friends and family, and display images on devices like a Fire TV, Amazon Echo Show, and Amazon Fire tablet.

Amazon Kids+. Prime members save more on [Amazon Kids+](https://amzn.to/40jyWkz), at $4.99 per month, where your kids get to choose from thousands of kid-friendly books, movies, and educational games.

Kindle Books. Prime members also enjoy a broad selection of popular eBooks, audiobooks, comics, and manga at no extra cost.

Amazon First Reads, Amazon Original Stories, and Audible Premium Plus. Prime members have the option to choose one free Kindle book and a bonus short read monthly before it publishes through Amazon First Reads; read or listen to Amazon Original Stories from best-selling authors and new voices designed to be enjoyed in one sitting; and get two credits toward any audiobook with an [Audible](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/everything-to-know-about-audible) Premium Plus trial.

What shopping benefits do Prime members get?

Prime member exclusive deal events. During big deal events like [Prime Day](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-prime-day) and Prime Big Deal Days, you need to be a Prime member to enjoy the big savings.

Prime exclusive deals. Every day, there are thousands of exclusive-to-Prime-members sales and deals on items across the Amazon store.

Prime Visa. Prime members can apply for [Prime Visa](https://amzn.to/4hjUgwj), which earns unlimited 5% back at Amazon, Whole Foods Market, and Chase Travel purchases with an eligible Prime membership. The card also offers unlimited 2% back at gas stations, restaurants and on transit, and commuting, plus 1% on all other purchases—all with no annual card fee. Rewards can be redeemed as soon as the next day for Amazon purchases or cash back, gift cards, and travel. [Learn more](https://www.amazon.com/primevisa).

Prime Store Card. Prime members looking for great rewards at Amazon can apply for [Prime Store Card](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B008A0GNA8), which earns unlimited 5% back on Amazon purchases and at Whole Foods Market (In-Store Code required) with an eligible Prime membership—all with no annual card fee. Cardmembers can also choose from exclusive financing offers, like 0% APR financing on select Amazon purchases. Rewards can be redeemed for cash back or on Amazon purchases. [Learn more](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B008A0GNA8).

Prime Card Bonus. Eligible Prime members with a Prime credit card can also earn 10% back or more on a rotating selection of products and categories at Amazon.com. [Learn more](https://www.amazon.com/b?node=121188110011).

What everyday savings come with Prime?

Grocery savings and convenience. Prime members enjoy free Same-Day Delivery on Amazon.com on orders over $25 in most areas in eligible locations—on top of a wide selection of high-quality and low-priced grocery products and exclusive savings through Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods Market.

Fuel savings. Prime members can now save $0.10 per gallon at more than 7,500 bp, Amoco, and participating ampm and Thorntons fuel stations across the U.S. Based on average U.S. driver statistics when purchasing at qualifying locations\*, this means nearly $70 of savings per year—that’s nearly half the cost of an annual Prime membership.

$0 Delivery Fee with Uber Eats. Every Prime membership comes with $0 delivery on restaurant orders over $25 powered by [Uber Eats](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/prime-uber-eats-free-delivery)—with access to hundreds of thousands of restaurants directly on Amazon.com and the Amazon Shopping app. Members also enjoy exclusive Prime member deals and a seamless ordering experience with no extra apps, accounts, or steps required to get started.

5 free direct-message care consultations. As an introductory offer, eligible U.S. Prime members who use [Health AI](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-health-ai-agent-one-medical) will receive up to five free direct-message care consultations with a One Medical provider for more than 30 common conditions. These cover cold and flu, allergies and acid reflux, pink eye and UTIs, erectile dysfunction and anti-aging skin care, hair loss, and more. That’s up to $145 in value that eligible U.S. Prime members can enjoy at no additional cost when using Health AI.

Ready to enjoy all the great benefits of a Prime membership? It’s easy to get started.

24/7 Access to virtual care. Prime members can receive [One Medical membership](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/one-medical-review) for just $9 per month or $99 per year—a $100 discount from the regular membership. It covers access to One Medical's convenient technology and on-demand virtual care services at no extra cost for common health concerns like skin issues, cold and flu, and more.

Prescription savings. In addition to 24/7 access to licensed pharmacists and manufacturer-sponsored savings coupons applied automatically to all eligible orders at checkout, members can save up to 80% on prescriptions when paying without insurance at nearly 60,000 pharmacies, including [Amazon Pharmacy](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-pharmacy-kiosks-one-medical).

Affordable prescription program. Prime members who spend more than $5 a month out of pocket on common generic medications can enjoy the $5 monthly [RxPass subscription program](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/rxpass-amazon-pharmacy-medication-subscription-delivery), which gives access to more than 60 commonly prescribed generic medications, regardless of how many prescriptions they need, all delivered free to their door.

How much does a Prime membership cost?

![an image of a person enjoying the benefits of Amazon Prime](https://assets.aboutamazon.com/dims4/default/661a2ef/2147483647/strip/true/crop/2000x1125+0+0/resize/767x431!/quality/90/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fassets.aboutamazon.com%2Fbe%2F4a%2F23b069514be6a52db9d76b3db1a2%2Fprimebenefitssavemoney-hero.jpg)

In the U.S., anyone can [join Prime](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-to-sign-up-for-a-prime-membership) for $14.99 per month, or $139 per year if you pay annually. Amazon also offers additional, discounted membership options providing the exact same, valuable benefits of a regular Prime membership—including fast, free delivery on more than 300 million items, exclusive deals, and savings for eligible members—at only 50% of the cost. With [Amazon Family](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-does-amazon-family-work), members can share a broad range of benefits with one adult, as well as Prime Video and additional digital content like Kindle eBooks, audiobooks, and games, with one adult and up to four children in their household.

Young adults (ages 18-24) and higher education students can try [a discounted Prime membership](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-prime-for-young-adults) with a six-month $0 trial, then pay a discounted rate of $7.49 per month, or $69 per year. Qualifying government assistance recipients and income-verified customers [can try Prime Access](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-prime-access-discounted-membership) for 30 days before paying $6.99 per month. For more information about Prime, including discounted memberships, visit our [Prime](https://www.aboutamazon.com/what-we-do/prime).
