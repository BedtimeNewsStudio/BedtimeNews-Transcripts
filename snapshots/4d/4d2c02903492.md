在这一轮行情中，[银行股](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%93%B6%E8%A1%8C%E8%82%A1&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLpk7booYzogqEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.NpaRr10q0K4e7z3LNAc_X34FsQDVy5X1BXmpJ7PBxg4&zhida_source=entity)等优质资产板块以及以人工智能、芯片半导体、机器人、创新药、军工等新质生产力为代表的新经济板块表现优异。[科创50指数](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%A7%91%E5%88%9B50%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLnp5HliJs1MOaMh-aVsCIsInpoaWRhX3NvdXJjZSI6ImVudGl0eSIsImNvbnRlbnRfaWQiOjI2MjM4MDAyMywiY29udGVudF90eXBlIjoiQXJ0aWNsZSIsIm1hdGNoX29yZGVyIjoxLCJ6ZF90b2tlbiI6bnVsbH0.GiQUg9pT3uR2sbwOFRylf7l1mfknXUNVjC-wLzMBwhc&zhida_source=entity)表现亮眼，年初至今上涨37.99%。

谈及与2014—2015年牛市相比的异同，魏凤春表示，本轮“AI+半导体”驱动行情在多方面存在本质差异。技术上，AI与半导体技术壁垒更高，研发难度远超[移动互联网技术](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%A7%BB%E5%8A%A8%E4%BA%92%E8%81%94%E7%BD%91%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLnp7vliqjkupLogZTnvZHmioDmnK8iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.rEpqNw454dv3df1jkfRd2dk9WP4wrw7Py518P8YkiUU&zhida_source=entity)；政策方面，当前对AI伦理、数据[安全监管](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%AE%89%E5%85%A8%E7%9B%91%E7%AE%A1&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLlronlhajnm5HnrqEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.h6p4xHYlMP5HuRRAVKHlH3_jWCxo0f0sDJYYSv0Undc&zhida_source=entity)更严，而“互联网+”时期政策侧重行业发展。资金上，本轮外资、[量化资金](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%87%8F%E5%8C%96%E8%B5%84%E9%87%91&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLph4_ljJbotYTph5EiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.nQ4t4EzvCkQRB7V8YGXmmo4FColyVZTAqsZI3qze0iQ&zhida_source=entity)参与度高，而“互联网+”牛市杠杆资金占比大。另外，AI+半导体领域面临全球巨头竞争，缺乏足够内生动力，行业竞争格局更复杂。

[Wind](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=Wind&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiJXaW5kIiwiemhpZGFfc291cmNlIjoiZW50aXR5IiwiY29udGVudF9pZCI6MjYyMzgwMDIzLCJjb250ZW50X3R5cGUiOiJBcnRpY2xlIiwibWF0Y2hfb3JkZXIiOjEsInpkX3Rva2VuIjpudWxsfQ.EnsN3mt_p8ti7HrLXo1tMeXpBMK5pnzqZJ5q9ouyzGE&zhida_source=entity)数据显示，以8月28日的收盘价计算，寒武纪的市盈率（TTM）约为595倍，相比之下，贵州茅台的市盈率仅为20.2倍。

茅台代表着确定性和稳定性，过去十年营收和利润保持双位数增长，毛利率稳定，是价值投资的典范。而寒武纪代表着不确定性和高成长性，需要资本承受更大风险以获取更高回报。

这场王座更迭背后是估值逻辑的重大变化，是从消费能力和品牌溢价，向技术壁垒和市场空间的切换。这也是中国经济转型升级的影像。

“当下投资者是在按照未来主导产业的逻辑进行布局，科技是高质量发展的核心已经成为共识。”魏凤春表示。

一位[公募基金](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%85%AC%E5%8B%9F%E5%9F%BA%E9%87%91&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLlhazli5_ln7rph5EiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.B0SvtgBLqYKEeDTWE01yRKEyoEngx9XSl68kWdUM6ao&zhida_source=entity)首席经济学家告诉经济观察报记者，当前硬科技领域成为资本“聚集地”，[股权投资](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%82%A1%E6%9D%83%E6%8A%95%E8%B5%84&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLogqHmnYPmipXotYQiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.yn2mVBPXI2nQj3uDIYlcCazAyBGI37H6ctF4rTH-EWE&zhida_source=entity)向人工智能等战略[新兴行业](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%96%B0%E5%85%B4%E8%A1%8C%E4%B8%9A&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLmlrDlhbTooYzkuJoiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.wBZE7FzYkPWOBzUmmi4BjzSH1PPHHW3mcjCvLoumdCk&zhida_source=entity)倾斜。寒武纪和茅台估值鸿沟合理，因二者估值体系不同。寒武纪处于AI芯片高增长赛道，市场对其[未来技术](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%9C%AA%E6%9D%A5%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLmnKrmnaXmioDmnK8iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.feICCaYwXSNm6wR4okgT7_5NCbfnoPpcGmrovQgtixQ&zhida_source=entity)垄断和业绩爆发有高预期，故给予高估值；而茅台属于成熟消费行业，业绩稳定，市盈率较低，其估值更注重当下现金流和分红能力。

对于投资者而言，魏凤春表示，在惯性力量推动趋势前行并累积风险的当口，建议投资者关注两件事：一是各行业和各公司预期盈利的改善，二是抓紧转换投资思维，以适应新时代的基本要求。相对而言，业绩的观察是客观的，是后验的，数据出来后再坚定的投资者也无法否认。思维的转变则是主观的，是先验的，在变革和转型期，解放思想远比放开手脚重要，意识是行动的前提。

在杨德龙看来，就绝对股价而言，因[流通股](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%B5%81%E9%80%9A%E8%82%A1&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLmtYHpgJrogqEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.o8_fTyqzhfplGn1PNkqgmd82H9FDuDuzkLRvMnSKN9k&zhida_source=entity)本较小，寒武纪股价单价已高于贵州茅台；就总市值而言，与老牌龙头仍有显著差距。若以价值投资视角审视，传统[白马股](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%99%BD%E9%A9%AC%E8%82%A1&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLnmb3pqazogqEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.xvfbMXLYBeZey6KHnWOugd10dTfuJnBt1B9yvQUFtKg&zhida_source=entity)凭借高分红率与[低市盈率](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262380023&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%BD%8E%E5%B8%82%E7%9B%88%E7%8E%87&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjIzNzUsInEiOiLkvY7luILnm4jnjociLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzODAwMjMsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.bQ9lAPLz3h5ZdV4yoNoiKW-eL3CmDO4VSvgcdoph-ys&zhida_source=entity)，仍具稳健配置价值；而新兴产业则更注重未来成长性与想象空间。
