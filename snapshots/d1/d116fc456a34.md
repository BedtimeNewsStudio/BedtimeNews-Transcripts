Germany’s most popular gaming platforms: smartphones lead the way, with consoles catching up fast 📱🎮 Nearly 19 million people in Germany play video games on more than one platform, depending on context, preferences and convenience. Our latest data shows how the way Germany plays has changed in 2024: 📱 Smartphones: 22.9 million players (still the most popular platform!) 🎮 Consoles: 20.5 million players, a new record and +29% since 2019! 💻 PCs: 13.1 million players, continuing a downward trend 💡Tablets: 10.3 million players, slightly up year-on-year What is the driving force behind console growth? The strong appeal of the latest generation of consoles, with upgrades like the PlayStation 5 Pro and Nintendo Switch OLED Model. 'For years, the tremendous success of the smartphone as the most widely used gaming platform has been unsurpassed. It offers millions of Germans an accessible way to play, along with a huge variety of games for beginners and experienced players alike. But the gaming console is closing in on the top spot," says [Felix Falk](https://de.linkedin.com/in/felix-falk-749138b6?trk=public_post-text), Managing Director of game. The chart below shows how gaming preferences have evolved since 2019, based on representative [YouGov](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/yougov?trk=public_post-text) data (2024/25).
