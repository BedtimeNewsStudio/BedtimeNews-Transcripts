RD-270 (РД-270)

**RD-270** ( [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language "Russian language"): Ракетный Двигатель-270, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian "Romanization of Russian"):_Raketnyy Dvigatel-270_, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation "Literal translation")'Rocket Engine 270', [GRAU index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GRAU_index "GRAU index"): **8D420**) was a single-chamber [liquid-fuel rocket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid-fuel_rocket "Liquid-fuel rocket") engine designed by [Energomash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPO_Energomash "NPO Energomash") ( [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR "USSR")) in 1960–1970. It was to be used on the first stages of the proposed heavy-lift [UR-700](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Rocket#UR-700 "Universal Rocket") and [UR-900](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Rocket#UR-900 "Universal Rocket") rocket families, as well as on the [N1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N1_(rocket) "N1 (rocket)"). It has the highest thrust among single-chamber engines of the [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR "USSR"), 640 metric tons at the surface of Earth. The propellants used are a [hypergolic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypergolic_propellant "Hypergolic propellant") mixture of [unsymmetrical dimethylhydrazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unsymmetrical_dimethylhydrazine "Unsymmetrical dimethylhydrazine") (UDMH) fuel with [dinitrogen tetroxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinitrogen_tetroxide "Dinitrogen tetroxide") (N2O4) oxidizer. The chamber pressure was among the highest considered, being about 26 MPa. This was achieved by applying the [full-flow staged combustion cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staged_combustion_cycle#Full-flow_staged_combustion_cycle "Staged combustion cycle") for all the incoming mass of fuel, which is turned into a gas and passes through multiple turbines before being burned in the combustion chamber. This allowed the engine to achieve a [specific impulse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_impulse "Specific impulse") of 301s (2.95km/s) at sea level.

Engine testing was underway when the decision was made to cancel the program. Development was stopped with all other work on corresponding rocket projects on 11 December 1970.

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=RD-270&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

The development of the **RD-270** started on 26 June 1962. Preliminary investigations and development of the engine and its further production were performed under the guidance of [Valentin Glushko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentin_Glushko "Valentin Glushko") and finished in 1967. It became the most powerful engine in the world to date that used storable propellants. During 1967–1969, several test firings were performed with experimental engines that were adapted to work at sea level and had a short nozzle. In total, 27 test firings were performed with 22 engines; three engines were tested twice, and one of them was tested three times.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Harvey61-0)[\[2\]](#refnote-Harvey61)

The RD 270 was also considered for the [R-56](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R-56_(rocket) "R-56 (rocket)") rocket (although never formally adopted) until work on the design stopped in June 1964.[&#8202;](#ref-back-Harvey61-1)[\[2\]](#refnote-Harvey61)[&#8202;](#ref-back-Hendrickx-0)[\[3\]](#refnote-Hendrickx)

During development, Glushko studied the use of [pentaborane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentaborane(9) "Pentaborane(9)") "zip" propellants in a modified RD-270M engine. This would have created immense toxicity problems but increased the specific impulse of the engine by 42 seconds (0.41km/s).[&#8202;](#ref-back-L1-0)[\[4\]](#refnote-L1)

## Design

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=RD-270&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Design")\]

The engine [throttle range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Throttleable_rocket_engine "Throttleable rocket engine") was 95–105%, the thrust vector control range was ±12° (project [R-56](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R-56_(rocket) "R-56 (rocket)")) and ±8° for [UR-700](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Rocket#UR-700 "Universal Rocket") rocket family. The oxidizer-to-fuel ratio was 2.67 and could be changed by up to 7%.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NSPO-4)[\[1\]](#refnote-NSPO)

To achieve such a high [specific impulse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_impulse "Specific impulse") and pressure in [combustion chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combustion_chamber "Combustion chamber") as RD-270 has, two circuits of [full-flow staged combustion cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staged_combustion_cycle_(rocket)#Full-flow_staged_combustion_cycle "Staged combustion cycle (rocket)") were applied. The pair of turbines with preburners turns the fuel into the gaseous form and circulates all of the fuel components. One of the turbines uses the fuel-rich gas to power a fuel pump, another one uses the oxidizer-rich gas to power the oxidizer pump. As a result, the main combustion chamber burns only generator gas. The engine controller regulates the functions of the two independent fuel and oxidizer circuits.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NSPO-5)[\[1\]](#refnote-NSPO)

To cool the MCC, its wall structure is layered, with four internal belts of slots. Some parts of the nozzle are covered by [zirconium dioxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zirconium_dioxide "Zirconium dioxide") for thermal protection.[&#8202;](#ref-back-NSPO-6)[\[1\]](#refnote-NSPO)
