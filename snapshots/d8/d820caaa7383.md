Two ships operated by the [Indian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Navy "Indian Navy") have had the name **INS _Vikrant_**.

- [INS _Vikrant_(1961)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Vikrant_(1961) "INS Vikrant (1961)"), a British-built [aircraft carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_carrier "Aircraft carrier") which was in service from 1961 to 1997, operated as a [museum ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_ship "Museum ship") from 2002 to 2012 and was scrapped in 2014–2015.
- [INS _Vikrant_(2013)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Vikrant_(2013) "INS Vikrant (2013)"), a first Indian-built aircraft carrier which was launched in 2013, the first of two planned for the class, and commissioned on 2 September 2022.
