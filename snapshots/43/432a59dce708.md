#### Results

About the election

Short description of the context and results of the election.

[View field in the data dictionary](https://data.ipu.org/data-dictionary/about_the_election)

Following a half renewal in 2025, Prime Minister Shigeru Ishiba’s Liberal Democratic Party (LDP) and its coalition partner, Komeito (led by Mr. Tetsuo Saito), lost their majority in the 248-member House of Councillors. They won 47 seats of the 125 seats at stake (down from 76 in 2022), or 121 seats in all. The main opposition party, the Constitutional Democratic Party of Japan (CDP), led by former Prime Minister Yoshihiko Noda, won 22 seats. Two parties significantly increased their share of seats in the 2025 election: the Democratic Party for the People, led by Mr. Yuichiro Tamaki, from 5 to 17 seats; and Sanseito, led by Mr. Sohei Kamiya, from one to 14 seats.

A record 42 women were elected (33.6%), up from 35 (28%) in 2022. That brought the total number of women in the House of Councillors to 73 (29.4%), up from 64 (25.8%). Prime Minister Ishiba announced that he would remain in post despite a growing call from LDP members for him to step down. On 1 August, the House of Councillors held its first session and re-elected Mr. Masakazu Sekiguchi (LDP) as its President.

The main issues in the 2025 election campaign included measures to counter rising prices, negotiations on Japan-US tariffs, free education, and policies to address the declining birth rate.
