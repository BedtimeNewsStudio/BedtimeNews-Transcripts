# Patient Care for Preventing Rabies

 For Health Care Providers 

[Sept. 10, 2026, visit link for details.](https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/hcp/clinical-care/index.html?CDC_AA_refVal=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.cdc.gov%2Frabies%2Fhcp%2Fprevention-recommendations%2Fpost-exposure-prophylaxis.html#content-sources)

## Key points

*   CDC has recommendations for pre-exposure prophylaxis for certain people.
*   If your patient may have been exposed to rabies, contact your health department immediately for a risk assessment to determine eligibility for post-exposure prophylaxis.
*   Rabies post-exposure prophylaxis includes wound washing, human rabies immune globulin, and a four-dose series of vaccines.

![Image 7: vials and a needle](https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/images/viles_needle.jpg)

[](https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/hcp/clinical-care/index.html?CDC_AA_refVal=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.cdc.gov%2Frabies%2Fhcp%2Fprevention-recommendations%2Fpost-exposure-prophylaxis.html "PrEP, PEP, and biologics information")
## PrEP, PEP, and biologics information

Rabies can be prevented before and after exposure to rabies virus using rabies biologics including vaccine and immunoglobulins.

CDC has recommendations for people who are eligible for pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) and post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP), as well as more information about biologics.

[Rabies Pre-exposure Prophylaxis Guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/hcp/clinical-care/pre-exposure-prophylaxis.html)

Learn who's at risk for rabies and should receive pre-exposure prophylaxis for rabies prevention.

[Rabies Post-exposure Prophylaxis Guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/hcp/clinical-care/post-exposure-prophylaxis.html)

Necessary steps to take when a patient has been exposed to rabies.

[Rabies Biologics](https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/hcp/clinical-care/biologics.html)

More information about rabies biologics to prevent rabies, common errors. and adverse reactions.

[Rabies PEP Calculator](https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/hcp/clinical-care/pep-calculator.html)

Calendar tool for healthcare providers to create ACIP-based rabies PEP schedules.
