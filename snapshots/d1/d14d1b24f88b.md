##### Census 2022

The 2022 Census is the sixth since the establishment of the United Republic of Tanzania in 1964. The previous censuses were conducted in 1967, 1978, 1988, 2002, and 2012. The 2022 Population and Housing Census is the first to be conducted digitally in Tanzania and has been successfully carried out, enabling the timely release of results.

For more click <https://sensa.nbs.go.tz/>
