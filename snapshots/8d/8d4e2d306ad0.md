# Ashley St. Clair posted about using CRISPR to conceive Musk's child? Not true | Fact check

Joedy McCreary, USA TODAY

Add Yahoo as a preferred source to see more of our stories on Google.

## The claim: Image shows author's post about conceiving child with Elon Musk using CRISPR, 'enhancements from other organisms'

A Feb. 16 Facebook post ([direct link](https://www.facebook.com/TheLeftDotOrg/posts/pfbid02yy1YQugYULjoriy9mbRr3rnqaY7Si6rT2XzYXmfEiFNjP6zbQHjpQ8RBWdEqNBCRl), [archive link](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/25538336-21825-facebook-musk-st-clair/)) shows a screenshot of what appears to be an X post from conservative author Ashley St. Clair about a child she said she conceived with billionaire Elon Musk

"Elon and I used IVF with PGT-M & CRISPR to ensure our baby had the best traits," the text of the supposed post reads in part. "Our little one's DNA now includes enhancements from other organisms for superior intelligence & health and we couldn't be happier."

CRISPR is a technology that allows scientists to "selectively modify the DNA of living organisms," according to the [National Human Genome Research Institute](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/CRISPR#:~:text=CRISPR%20(short%20for%20%E2%80%9Cclustered%20regularly,editing%20systems%20found%20in%20bacteria.).

## Our rating: Manipulated media

The image is a fabrication, a spokesperson for St. Clair said. Her X profile contains no record of the post attributed to her.

## 'Counterfeit' post attributed to St. Clair

St. Clair – a conservative influencer, [author](https://www.amazon.com/Elephants-Are-Birds-Freedom-Island/dp/1955550492?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.aoeCwmdkVkVGtszcN2Rg2OyQgjQglbkdmgShBn7h_W62bh5F5gQF9gUJ3n7rUWJmyUXJW4w4t0Neu6mUFj0qY9jtwNwnPciZQNy_-O-kDKSym3JcEYO9aWAKNO3RhfTTPVISPeyw6pTUb65moxS0nV-boAaIJbVKhWF0XasMNesehldiVtMVnS5NI4kwGO-ntuCxJBeukfv5YNhHFUOxJb3zj2SlmZhBi7g7D_24_Sc.PryPOjuVB3dzaipCDSs3QjDAVb28_1crYZ6-M-TFYY8&dib_tag=se&keywords=ashley+st+clair&qid=1739907930&sr=8-1) and [commentator](https://www.foxnews.com/video/6336581116112) – said in [a Feb. 14 X post](https://x.com/stclairashley/status/1890554279749951687) that [Musk is the father of the child she gave birth to in 2024](https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/news/2025/02/17/elon-musk-ashley-st-clair-children-kids/78744733007/) and accused "tabloid media" of attempting to make it public knowledge. Musk – the world's wealthiest person who is already the [father of 12 children with at least three women](https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/news/2025/02/17/elon-musk-ashley-st-clair-children-kids/78744733007/#:~:text=Musk%20has%20previously%20fathered%2012%20children%20with%20at%20least%20three%20different%20women.), USA TODAY previously reported – had not responded to the claim as of Feb. 18.

The X post attributed to her in the Feb. 16 Facebook post, however, is a fabrication. St. Clair spokesperson [Brian Glicklich](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brianglicklich/) described it as "counterfeit." There is no record of that post [on her account](https://x.com/stclairashley/).

The image includes what appears to be a timestamp indicating it was shared Feb. 16 at "7:21" – without indicating whether that time is AM or PM, as [authentic posts](https://x.com/USATODAY/status/1891963525100147144) [on the platform do](https://x.com/USATODAY/status/1891955989655568511). Regardless, her profile contains no evidence that she shared a post at either time.

[Versions](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/25538517-21825-x-musk-st-clair/) [of the image](https://mvau.lt/media/9c530d6a-0393-4991-a644-0f9f0d138006) [circulating on social media](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/25538544-21825-facebook-musk-st-clair-2/) each indicate it had been viewed 173 times when it was captured – an indication it is a fabrication that traces to a single source. Were it real, it surely would have been captured and shared with varying numbers of views by her more than 1 million followers.
