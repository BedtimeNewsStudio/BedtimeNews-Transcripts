# ByteDance plans to spend $23 billion towards AI infrastructure in 2026, FT reports

By Reuters

December 23, 20251:33 AM UTCUpdated December 23, 2025

![Image 1: Illustration shows Bytedance logo](https://www.reuters.com/resizer/v2/S5KUZ6227FNSDF4TVYJUGXWVRI.jpg?auth=d349a01ae42c4d9f42cce4579da733d2a1f480519253736c6f41a0d52c60395c&width=1920&quality=80)

FILE PHOTO: Bytedance logo is seen in this illustration taken February 8, 2025. REUTERS/Dado Ruvic/Illustration/File Photo Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab

Dec 22 (Reuters) - TikTok owner ByteDance has made preliminary plans for capital expenditure of 160 billion yuan ($22.74 billion) in 2026 to build artificial intelligence infrastructure, the Financial Times reported on Monday, citing people familiar with the matter.

Reuters could not immediately verify the report.
