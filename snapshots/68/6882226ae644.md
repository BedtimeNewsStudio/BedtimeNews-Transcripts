光伏、储能（锂电池）、新能源汽车——这“新能源三剑客”，过去几年在政策的大力扶持下，一路狂飙突进，成为[资本市场](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%B5%84%E6%9C%AC%E5%B8%82%E5%9C%BA&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLotYTmnKzluILlnLoiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.z-NL9WLqodFmnkhwvtMkMf4ODA-iPbGPSvtz9pjxDLA&zhida_source=entity)炙手可热的香饽饽。

然而，繁荣背后，危机[暗涌](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%9A%97%E6%B6%8C&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLmmpfmtowiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.3V8dtQdd7a3aIMLo1-R1hgaW9sCFrMJ5BPcJ9YwZktg&zhida_source=entity)。

据清流工作室统计，截至2024年年底，国内117家光伏上市公司[总负债](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%80%BB%E8%B4%9F%E5%80%BA&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLmgLvotJ_lgLoiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.yuCpsXpJFSspvUw09H_A5-8jpPVwZvFOjUdKjLd8FD8&zhida_source=entity)规模合计达1.98万亿元，147家储能上市公司总负债规模合计达2.02万亿元，再加上29家新能源整车上市公司2.56万亿元的[总债务](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%80%BB%E5%80%BA%E5%8A%A1&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLmgLvlgLrliqEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.nJOMPDMXzizeTc2z7kqAKg_VUTtS82EktrKESd9atSQ&zhida_source=entity)，这三大行业负债规模合计高达6.56万亿元。

6.56万亿元是什么概念？这接近发达国家[瑞士](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%91%9E%E5%A3%AB&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLnkZ7lo6siLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.Y_hUk-jXT91o3L0_2-WMZmb7h7ayuk5nqgELf1f1pXI&zhida_source=entity)去年的GDP总量（约6.7万亿元人民币），超过两个越南去年GDP总量（约3.1万亿元人民币）；也大于去年国内GDP排名第五的省份——[四川省](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%9B%9B%E5%B7%9D%E7%9C%81&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLlm5vlt53nnIEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.lEduhi7H7qZxS1jcWcqGC5TqKAoNcpevy1X_qAJSu9c&zhida_source=entity)全年的[经济总量](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%BB%8F%E6%B5%8E%E6%80%BB%E9%87%8F&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLnu4_mtY7mgLvph48iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.S7pF2OD-fz5WzVeGed3a_PLODu6MLWUA0c2Kln3h8zE&zhida_source=entity)（6.47万亿元）。

但与此同时，截至去年，前述117家光伏上市公司[总资产](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%80%BB%E8%B5%84%E4%BA%A7&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLmgLvotYTkuqciLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.ZwNbAaf5zTPQ-zkCF5oMO7hFwTLk_URYvg8jMHOi4b0&zhida_source=entity)合计3.13万亿元，147家储能上市公司资产合计3.57万亿元，29家新能源车企资产合计3.98万亿元，三个行业加起来[资产规模](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%B5%84%E4%BA%A7%E8%A7%84%E6%A8%A1&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLotYTkuqfop4TmqKEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.RmKHeWtSGG35SNHH6a-1Yp9kWJZMnyun5iLPVb7aBNE&zhida_source=entity)达10.67万亿元。这意味着，三大行业资产完全可以覆盖其债务。

需要指出的是，这部分统计样本，还算不上覆盖了这三大行业的所有公司——既没有包括部分未被纳入统计范围的上市公司，也未包括未上市企业、已退市企业以及正在IPO的企业。这意味着，这三个行业的[真实债务](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%9C%9F%E5%AE%9E%E5%80%BA%E5%8A%A1&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLnnJ_lrp7lgLrliqEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.xwLHdAU8xLAdelIXgw9E8ljLjcLbkhwLP_J6OUffff0&zhida_source=entity)及资产规模，远远要大得多。

近年来，随着“新三样”内卷危机愈演愈烈，光伏、储能和新能源汽车行业常被拿来与房地产行业比较，“车圈是否有恒大”的讨论一度甚嚣尘上。但整体而言，制造业与房地产行业有着本质区别，其[资产流动性](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%B5%84%E4%BA%A7%E6%B5%81%E5%8A%A8%E6%80%A7&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLotYTkuqfmtYHliqjmgKciLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9._VSjczUzv_X7LxQP9hTdCN1dLTy3dvTcPmS58INVvYw&zhida_source=entity)更强，[风险结构](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%A3%8E%E9%99%A9%E7%BB%93%E6%9E%84&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLpo47pmannu5PmnoQiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.p8Ga26ASLiTb_BAlHxNsA_UdnbA4yZGZgfdVFbqocnY&zhida_source=entity)更分散，[现金流](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%8E%B0%E9%87%91%E6%B5%81&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLnjrDph5HmtYEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.gzBCBp56mgZX2sG2F6oOz4JAX4gdJbivXZNn9WcRSLw&zhida_source=entity)也更为稳定。不过，从具体的财务指标来看，“新三样”行业存在的部分风险与问题，仍不容忽视。

**光伏困于[内卷](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=2&q=%E5%86%85%E5%8D%B7&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLlhoXljbciLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MiwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.HYtZIb3VMoghuZOeA0gVX-qBIYSoiJSNzfrTEGGIkjs&zhida_source=entity)**

**储能表现各异**

所谓“光储不分家”，储能产业与光伏紧密相连，其债务规模同样在近几年迎来爆发式增长。

据清流工作室统计，2020年至2024年这五年中，147家储能上市公司的负债规模分别是0.72万亿元、1.06万亿元、1.69万亿元、1.86万亿元和2.02万亿元。

其中，在2024年，债务规模突破百亿的储能企业有41家，规模超500亿的有7家，规模超千亿的有2家。

从细分产业来看，负债规模前二十名储能企业中，包含5家系统设备企业，5家金属矿产企业，4家电池企业，4家储能材料企业，两家项目运营企业。

值得一提的是，相比海外车企，国内新能源车企[金融杠杆](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%87%91%E8%9E%8D%E6%9D%A0%E6%9D%86&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLph5Hono3mnaDmnYYiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.CAsnLZvu1-Oos7Ove4qe4VgGkdzkTlOwWwxLg4Qkv5g&zhida_source=entity)普遍较低。

清流工作室注意到，目前国内车企的[有息负债](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%9C%89%E6%81%AF%E8%B4%9F%E5%80%BA&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLmnInmga_otJ_lgLoiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.RzcYGJXc3kldjxWrR_tuoZ4T925TAvwauYfBwukXq9Y&zhida_source=entity)几乎都在30%以下，长安汽车、赛力斯、一汽解放的有息负债比例甚至低于1%。国产三巨头中，比亚迪有息负债比例低至6.9%，吉利汽车2.9%（母公司吉利控股为17.04%），上汽集团的有息负债比例也只有21.9%。与之相反的是，海外车企更愿意向金融机构借钱，杠杆放得更大，例如现代汽车的有息负债比例高达71.5%，丰田汽车及[通用汽车](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%80%9A%E7%94%A8%E6%B1%BD%E8%BD%A6&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLpgJrnlKjmsb3ovaYiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.Y-WAJ0ohIGbnpn0CoW4-1cpy8PIZFkJZNgOpuz0GRaw&zhida_source=entity)的有息负债比例均超过60%。

这是由于国内车企不爱向金融机构借钱，更倾向于通过“赊账”等手段优化[债务结构](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%80%BA%E5%8A%A1%E7%BB%93%E6%9E%84&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLlgLrliqHnu5PmnoQiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.5b8FD2-2LUA4DuHNGLcw_m6zOzxty7OeKrgSsZvO9Eg&zhida_source=entity)，因而对有息负债的依赖程度要低得多。

不过，这并不意味着新能源车企不存在[违约风险](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%BF%9D%E7%BA%A6%E9%A3%8E%E9%99%A9&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLov53nuqbpo47pmakiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.arxfB1-l7AFeGRUbze4B6bzyxnFuyvMXnca0p8jfTjc&zhida_source=entity)。相比海外车企，由于占用供应链上游的金额高、时限长，新能源车企普遍经营性负债高企、赊账比率高。

截至2024年，比亚迪和上汽集团的[应付账款](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%BA%94%E4%BB%98%E8%B4%A6%E6%AC%BE&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLlupTku5jotKbmrL4iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.IXU2EcWH4CDN_P1k-3qMKpvLRSs_QudtdNgCgLmSRQs&zhida_source=entity)及票据规模均逼近2500亿元大关。在29家车企中，应付账款及票据规模超过百亿的企业多达20家。

如果按应付账款及票据占营业收入的比例看，国内主流车企的赊账比例多数在30%至50%之间。其中零跑、小鹏、一汽解放、蔚来和长安2024年的应付账款及票据占比都在50%以上。相比之下，“海外同行”通用汽车、现代汽车、丰田汽车这个数值分别是13.7%、12.6%和11.65%，大众集团更是仅有9.17%。

6.56万亿元的[负债总额](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%B4%9F%E5%80%BA%E6%80%BB%E9%A2%9D&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLotJ_lgLrmgLvpop0iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.UfL01PQkj7Au5s9fxIC6OzfdIXV_HwXcSriJtNRZQuo&zhida_source=entity)不仅仅是一个单纯的数字，它背后折射出资本过度涌入[新能源产业](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%96%B0%E8%83%BD%E6%BA%90%E4%BA%A7%E4%B8%9A&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLmlrDog73mupDkuqfkuJoiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.jtzVyWr_A74Xt-hs2uxhfbkiQu2mKVQnGFqoI_XdxI0&zhida_source=entity)、产能过剩与内卷式竞争的一个个影子。

面对这把如同高悬的[达摩克利斯之剑](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=262375110&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%BE%BE%E6%91%A9%E5%85%8B%E5%88%A9%E6%96%AF%E4%B9%8B%E5%89%91&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMjc2NjMsInEiOiLovr7mkanlhYvliKnmlq_kuYvliZEiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyNjIzNzUxMTAsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.Ellv8XAL1LHXe18Wi5CbEJUcA0gI9_s5Q-gyeC0rvAQ&zhida_source=entity)，唯有通过行业自律，收缩产能，以及在政府、机构等各方协同支持下的“反内卷”，才有望逐步化解债务风险，实现行业的健康可持续发展。
