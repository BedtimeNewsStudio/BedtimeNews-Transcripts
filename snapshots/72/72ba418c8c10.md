| * * *<br>I'm the LEGO Papert Professor of Learning Research at the [MIT](http://web.mit.edu/) [Media\<br>Lab](http://www.media.mit.edu/),<br>where I lead the [Lifelong Kindergarten](http://llk.media.mit.edu/) research group.<br>Our Lifelong Kindergarten group developed [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/), the world's leading coding platform for kids. Recently, we launched [OctoStudio](https://octostudio.org/), a mobile coding app that enables kids to create projects anytime anywhere.<br>To learn more about my ideas and projects, please read my book<br>[Lifelong Kindergarten: Cultivating Creativity through Projects, Passions, Peers, and Play](https://mitpress.mit.edu/books/lifelong-kindergarten)<br>Or you can look at my [videos](http://www.media.mit.edu/~mres/videos.html), [publications](http://www.media.mit.edu/~mres/papers.html), or [press coverage](http://www.media.mit.edu/~mres/press.html).<br>* * *<br>Mitchel Resnick, LEGO Papert Professor of Learning Research at the MIT Media Lab, develops new technologies and activities to engage young people from diverse backgrounds in creative learning experiences. His Lifelong Kindergarten research group developed the [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/) programming software and online community, the world's leading coding platform for kids. Recently, the group launched [OctoStudio](https://octostudio.org/), a mobile coding app that enables young people to create projects anytime anywhere. The group has collaborated with the LEGO Company on the development of new educational ideas and products, including LEGO Mindstorms robotics kids. Resnick co-founded the [Clubhouse](https://theclubhousenetwork.org/) global network after-school learning centers, where youth from low-income communities learn to express themselves creatively with new technologies. Resnick earned an undergraduate degree in physics from Princeton, and a Masters and PhD degrees in computer science from MIT. He was awarded the McGraw Prize in Education in 2011 and the LEGO Prize in 2021. His book [Lifelong Kindergarten](https://mitpress.mit.edu/books/lifelong-kindergarten) won the 2017 Prose Award for Educational Practice.<br>* * *<br>Mitchel Resnick <br>MIT Media Lab (E14-445A) <br>75 Amherst Street <br>Cambridge, MA 02142 <br>617-253-9783 <br>mres at media.mit.edu<br>See [photos](http://www.media.mit.edu/~mres/mres-photos.html) of me, through the years. |
