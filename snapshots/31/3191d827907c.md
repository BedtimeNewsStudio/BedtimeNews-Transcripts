据华商报大风新闻报道：[陕西西安](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%99%95%E8%A5%BF%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLpmZXopb_opb_lrokiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.t3R2OuiHw36V74hGnLEIptBORhFg8vAblJDr3U9Vu3M&zhida_source=entity)的叶先生年过8旬，2022年5月，他购买了[山东潍坊滨城投资开发有限公司](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%B1%B1%E4%B8%9C%E6%BD%8D%E5%9D%8A%E6%BB%A8%E5%9F%8E%E6%8A%95%E8%B5%84%E5%BC%80%E5%8F%91%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLlsbHkuJzmvY3lnYrmu6jln47mipXotYTlvIDlj5HmnInpmZDlhazlj7giLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.UKq3PkQHQH0y3iekbAGgiHXndsMTlz4GOt_FOGVhzRA&zhida_source=entity)发行的“[城投债](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%9F%8E%E6%8A%95%E5%80%BA&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLln47mipXlgLoiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.TIVUyXnH0Du0BL9KjaWJleyAgS1Br1tela0JrIL_DRE&zhida_source=entity)”。认购了1年期100万元的项目，到期日2023年5月18日，预期[年化收益率](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%B9%B4%E5%8C%96%E6%94%B6%E7%9B%8A%E7%8E%87&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLlubTljJbmlLbnm4rnjociLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.L5LSxIGvBpMNU2LTUfmW1EiixW10mTOBb20Y0oWYBXs&zhida_source=entity)每年9.2%，约定按照自然季度付收益，最后一期收益随本金一起支付。

叶先生说，截至今年5月，他先后按期收到了3期利息，每期23000元，但最后一期利息以及返还本金出了问题，“我联系他们了很多次，结果对方仅支付了9900元左右，连最后一期利息都没有，更别提100万元本金了。”为了讨回钱款，他委托了律师进行起诉，结果[潍坊市寒亭区](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%BD%8D%E5%9D%8A%E5%B8%82%E5%AF%92%E4%BA%AD%E5%8C%BA&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLmvY3lnYrluILlr5Lkuq3ljLoiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.NaLFvGelRuckp_wz0UeQ3f3yjtBkHq7behohHC_eHDE&zhida_source=entity)法院始终拒绝立案，“律师告诉我，对方称为这件事已经成立了[专班](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%B8%93%E7%8F%AD&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLkuJPnj60iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.1zFB3fOJlELpvwX9CYvIdpYOvz-cyBTgRbkz0F02B-0&zhida_source=entity)，所以不能立案，但专班表示需要分期支付，今年年底前支付25%-30%，其余的本金没有固定返还期限，对此我无法接受。”

潍坊滨城投资开发有限公司成立于2005年8月，注册资本30亿元；该公司涉诉案件23起，其中43.5%的涉案由为[借款合同纠纷](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%80%9F%E6%AC%BE%E5%90%88%E5%90%8C%E7%BA%A0%E7%BA%B7&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLlgJ_mrL7lkIjlkIznuqDnurciLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.S4GgmiYQaEi0-RdYAP-JHqvkVmOaE_CyFw4y5HyTOsg&zhida_source=entity)；65.2%的案件身份为被告，39.1%的案件类型为[民事案件](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%B0%91%E4%BA%8B%E6%A1%88%E4%BB%B6&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLmsJHkuovmoYjku7YiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.P9BYchhAnuZpNvCWi1w8M0xtQ4xudAC_gwu4sz6lCB8&zhida_source=entity)。

记者联系到叶先生的律师[焦某](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%84%A6%E6%9F%90&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLnhKbmn5AiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.Egfodygtu2eJLntVOz0OvsbZR750xHWvlt39O1mGMFk&zhida_source=entity)。她说，曾代表叶先生发送了《[律师函](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%BE%8B%E5%B8%88%E5%87%BD&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLlvovluIjlh70iLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.k582xsjHxxnr08GfXKCtQxVgtXbqqcUk-43Sj415LAo&zhida_source=entity)》，随后向寒亭法院申请[一审立案](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%B8%80%E5%AE%A1%E7%AB%8B%E6%A1%88&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLkuIDlrqHnq4vmoYgiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.Mc3UNcYbqVhYwKD0qOmsECCS3yJQWr9FS55nG1GMlqk&zhida_source=entity)，但未通过。法官回复内容：[滨投](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%BB%A8%E6%8A%95&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLmu6jmipUiLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.fTUa-5ftizmiL_-9_Y_wAEbZxMVJ6wByNEATKiOP9ww&zhida_source=entity)已成立专班处理该类纠纷，请先到专班协调解决。

根据7月19日的专班回复录音显示，工作人员表示，因为他们集团的资金困难，计划七八月份先兑付25%，“涉及人数比较多，一下子付不了那么多钱，只能逐步兑付。”记者注意到，录音中工作人员多次强调，涉及人数众多，资金困难等。但专班工作人员表示，“肯定会付，需要逐步兑付。”对此，叶先生并不认可，“为什么法院拒绝受理[民间经济纠纷](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=231741146&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%B0%91%E9%97%B4%E7%BB%8F%E6%B5%8E%E7%BA%A0%E7%BA%B7&zd_token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJ6aGlkYV9zZXJ2ZXIiLCJleHAiOjE3OTQzMDIzMjAsInEiOiLmsJHpl7Tnu4_mtY7nuqDnurciLCJ6aGlkYV9zb3VyY2UiOiJlbnRpdHkiLCJjb250ZW50X2lkIjoyMzE3NDExNDYsImNvbnRlbnRfdHlwZSI6IkFydGljbGUiLCJtYXRjaF9vcmRlciI6MSwiemRfdG9rZW4iOm51bGx9.QrwQJC3E0EJo5zCIMLlo8xQExW_zIX2Hg_oFuIttbVU&zhida_source=entity)呢？司法程序相较于专班协调，明显是更有保障、且具有强制力的途径。”
