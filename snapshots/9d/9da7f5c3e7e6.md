# Mecca-Medina consortium gets go ahead for largest international contract by meeting conditions set by Saudi Government

[News](/lang/en/gobierno/news/Paginas/index.aspx "News") - 2012.9.17

The Al Shoula Group, a consortium of Spanish and Saudi companies, has received authorisation to begin work on the high-speed rail link that will connect Mecca and Medina. This announcement is a preliminary step towards the effective payment of the advance funding to be provided by the Saudi Railways Organization (SRO), representing 10% of the money to be used for building the superstructure.

The consortium of Spanish and Saudi companies, Al Shoula Group, winner of the tender for the second phase of the high-speed railway project between Mecca and Medina in Saudi Arabia, has received authorisation from the Saudi Railways Organization (SRO) to begin construction in the country.

This announcement is a preliminary step towards the effective payment of the advance funding to be provided by the SRO, representing 10% of the money to be used for building the superstructure.

The consortium received this authorisation after successfully meeting the demanding conditions imposed by the SRO to get final approval for the contract signed in January.

The conditions, which were organised into 15 sections, included the provision of bonds and guarantees necessary for the project to begin. The issue of these guarantees is a demonstration by the financial system of its support of the Spanish companies and one of the industrial sectors with the most potential for international expansion within the Spanish economy.

## Project progress

The progress made by the consortium in recent months, under supervision by the Spanish Ministry of Public Works and the Saudi authorities, has enabled the contract to be definitively approved for Spanish industry. The complexity and scope of this project will put Spanish high-speed railway technology at the forefront of the most cutting-edge railway engineering in the world.

One of the most important aspects of the progress made in recent months is the creation of a company to take responsibility for developing the project in Saudi Arabia. This company will be called Saudi Spain Train Project Company Limited (SSTPC).

Similarly, progress was also made on where to locate the company headquarters. It was decided that the company will share offices with the SRO in Jeddah.

## Mecca-Jeddah-Medina High-Speed Rail Link

The Mecca-Jeddah-Medina High-Speed Rail Link (450 kilometres of electrified double track) has an overall budget of 12 billion euros, of which 6.74 billion euros will be used on the second phase of the project awarded to the Spanish-Saudi business consortium.

This is the most important project of its kind ever undertaken by this sector outside of Europe and is the largest international contract ever to be awarded to Spanish companies.

This second phase includes the design and construction of the railway superstructure and installations (electrification, signalling, communications, etc.), the supply of 35 high-speed trains designed for speeds of over 300 km/h and an option to purchase a further 20 trains during the operation period, the maintenance of said rolling stock and the operation and maintenance of the line for a period of 12 years.

This high-speed rail link has been designed using the parameters of a European high-speed railway line, with a design speed of 320 km/h and a journey time of less than two and a half hours between the cities of Mecca and Medina.

The maximum operating speed will be 300 km/h on double UIC gauge track electrified at 25 kV 60 Hz and implementation of the European ERTMS Level 2 system, in which Spain is a world leader. These factors will lead to one of the safest high-speed railways in the world using the latest railway technologies that, together with the most modern equipment, will provide a service with the highest levels of safety and quality.

Estimated demand for the rail link is very high; approximately 160,000 passengers per day. To service that demand, three intermediary stops are planned along the route at the stations of Jeddah Central, the Jeddah International Airport and the King Abdullah Economic City, to which can be added the terminus stations of Mecca Central and Medina Central.

Besides bringing significant investment, this project will transform transport habits in Saudi Arabia by providing new travel options to the millions of pilgrims who visit the holy cities located in the west of the country every year.
