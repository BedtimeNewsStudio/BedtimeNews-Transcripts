## Hair Dryer Market Scope

The market is segmented on the basis of type, product type, price range, application, and distribution channel.

- **By Type**

On the basis of type, the hair dryer market is segmented into corded and cordless. The corded segment dominated the largest market revenue share of 83.06% in 2024, owing to its consistent power output and reliability during prolonged usage. Corded hair dryers are widely used in both household and salon settings, as they eliminate concerns regarding battery life and typically offer higher wattage for faster drying. The lower cost and greater variety of features in corded models, including multiple heat/speed settings and ionic technology, continue to drive their demand, particularly among consumers seeking performance-driven solutions.

The cordless segment is projected to witness the fastest growth rate from 2025 to 2032, driven by increasing demand for portable and travel-friendly grooming solutions. Cordless hair dryers offer enhanced convenience and mobility, making them ideal for on-the-go usage, gym bags, and professional stylists who require flexibility in movement. Advances in battery technology and faster charging capabilities are also contributing to the rising adoption of cordless models, especially among tech-savvy consumers looking for sleek, modern alternatives to traditional designs.

- **By Product Type**

On the basis of product type, the market is categorized into handheld hair dryers and hooded hair dryers. The handheld segment accounted for the largest revenue share in 2024, primarily due to its widespread usage in both personal and professional applications. Handheld hair dryers are valued for their compact size, ease of use, and ergonomic designs. Their broad availability across price ranges, coupled with continued innovation in drying technology such as tourmaline and ceramic components, enhances their appeal across consumer segments.

The hooded hair dryer segment is expected to register the fastest CAGR during the forecast period, owing to its resurgence in professional salons and increased use in at-home deep conditioning treatments. Hooded dryers are favored for their ability to evenly distribute heat without disturbing hair styles, making them ideal for treatments, color setting, and roller sets. The growing popularity of structured hair care routines and salon-such as results at home is boosting interest in hooded models, particularly in the premium product category.

- **By Price Range**

On the basis of price range, the market is segmented into less than USD 50, USD 50–100, and more than USD 100. The less than USD 50 segment held the largest market share in 2024, supported by strong demand from cost-sensitive consumers and the mass availability of basic-function models. These products cater well to entry-level users, students, and families looking for value-for-money solutions, especially in emerging economies.

The more than USD 100 segment is projected to witness the highest growth through 2032, driven by rising consumer preference for premium and salon-grade devices. This segment benefits from technological advancements such as infrared heating, noise reduction, automatic sensors, and smart temperature control. Consumers in this bracket are often more brand-conscious and seek products offering superior performance, longer durability, and added aesthetic value.

- **By Application**

On the basis of application, the hair dryer market is bifurcated into professional and individual. The individual segment accounted for the largest revenue share of 61.81% in 2024, owing to the vast base of home users across global households. Increased focus on grooming and self-care routines has accelerated the adoption of hair dryers for daily or occasional use. Online tutorials and beauty influencers have also played a role in encouraging personal usage of salon-style tools at home.

The professional segment is expected to grow at the fastest pace, supported by the global expansion of salon chains, beauty clinics, and grooming studios. Professionals demand high-performance tools that can endure continuous usage while delivering consistent results. Features such as lightweight designs, rapid drying, and ergonomic handling are especially important in commercial settings, contributing to higher adoption in this category.

- **By Distribution Channel**

On the basis of distribution channel, the market is segmented into offline and online. The offline segment held the largest market share in 2024, as brick-and-mortar stores including supermarkets, beauty retail outlets, and electronics chains continue to be primary purchase points. Physical stores offer hands-on product comparisons, demonstrations, and immediate availability, which remain important for many consumers.

The online segment is forecasted to witness the fastest growth through 2032, propelled by the increasing digitalization of shopping habits and the popularity of e-commerce platforms. Consumers are increasingly turning to online channels for convenience, wider product selection, competitive pricing, and access to customer reviews. Flash sales, influencer partnerships, and virtual try-on features on beauty platforms are also accelerating online adoption, especially among younger consumers.
